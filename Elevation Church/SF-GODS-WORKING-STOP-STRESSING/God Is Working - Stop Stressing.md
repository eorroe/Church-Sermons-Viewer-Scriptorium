# God's Working; Stop Stressing

## Overview

This sermon by Steven Furtick addresses the anxiety and fear that arise when we face uncertainty, waiting, or silence. Using Paul's experience in Troas and his own personal story, Furtick reveals that a devil at the door is often a sign that something important is on the other side. The message encourages believers to trust God's timing, resist hypothetical fears, and hold fast to the truth that when God opens a door, no devil can shut it.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are waiting for answers, news, or a breakthrough that hasn't come yet
- When your mind is flooded with worst-case scenarios about people, situations, or the future
- When you feel like giving up on an open door or opportunity because you lack peace
- When the user asks about overcoming stress, anxiety, or spiritual warfare during uncertain times

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Enemy's Strategy

The enemy uses silence, delay, and hypothetical scenarios to fill your mind with fear. He brings up past hurts and imagined futures to keep you from the opportunity God has placed before you. Understand that his goal is not to close the door, but to get your mind so overwhelmed that you walk away from it yourself.

### Step 2: Remember That a Devil at the Door Means Something Important Is Inside

If you are facing intense resistance, it is because what God has for you on the other side of that door matters. The size of the opposition often indicates the size of the assignment. Do not interpret the battle as a sign to quit; interpret it as confirmation that you are on the verge of something significant.

### Step 3: Choose Faith Over Fear While You Wait

Paul had no peace and left Troas, but God had already worked out the situation. While you wait for news, reconciliation, or resolution, hold on to God's character, not your circumstances. Speak life, declare that joy and restoration are coming, and refuse to let the enemy write a false story on the wall of your future.

### Step 4: Receive the Comfort God Has Already Sent

Titus arrived, and with him came the proof that God had been at work the entire time. The answer, breakthrough, or comfort you are waiting for may already be on its way. Keep your heart open so that when it arrives, you can receive it fully and allow your joy to be complete.

## Examples

### Example 1: Paul in Troas

Paul left an open door in Troas because he had no peace and could not find Titus. He traveled to Macedonia and waited, harassed on every side, with conflicts both outside and fears within. Months passed before Titus arrived with good news—news of the Corinthians' sorrow, longing, and concern for Paul. What Paul worried about for months, God had already resolved.

### Example 2: Steven Furtick's Breakdown Before a Significant Ministry Moment

Furtick describes breaking down crying before stepping into a major ministry assignment. He had no logical reason to be sad, but he believes the devil was at the door because what was on the other side was so important. The enemy had come to steal, kill, and destroy, but God's plan moved forward. The spiritual attack was an indicator of the significance of the calling.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Identify when the enemy is using hypothetical scenarios to control your emotions
- ✅ Remind yourself that God's opened doors cannot be shut by any force
- ✅ Speak faith-filled declarations over your situation even before you see the outcome
- ✅ Stay connected to community so that you are not isolated while you wait
- ❌ Don't let silence or delayed responses become the basis for your identity or decisions
- ❌ Don't walk away from open doors just because you feel fear or lack peace in the moment
- ❌ Don't assume that spiritual warfare means you are outside of God's will

## Keep In Mind

- If you or someone you know is struggling with suicidal thoughts, contact a trusted professional or emergency services immediately.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Interpreting every difficulty or delay as evidence that God has closed the door
  **Solution:** Remember that opposition often confirms the door is open; evaluate the situation through Scripture and wise counsel, not just feelings
- **Problem:** Getting stuck in cycles of worry while waiting for breakthrough
  **Solution:** Replace hypothetical scenarios with Scriptural truths and proactive prayer, and remain open to how God may already be working behind the scenes
- **Problem:** Isolating yourself when you feel misunderstood or overwhelmed
  **Solution:** Stay in community, share your burdens, and allow others to speak life and truth into your situation
