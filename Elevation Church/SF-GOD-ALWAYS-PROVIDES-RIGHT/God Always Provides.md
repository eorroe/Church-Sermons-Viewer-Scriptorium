# God Always Provides Right?

## Overview

This sermon challenges the assumption that God's removal of things from your life is punishment. Using the book of Haggai, Pastor Steven Furtick explains that God sometimes "blows away" what you have built to protect you from depending on resources disconnected from Him. The core message is that the real problem is not a lack of provision, but a lack of priority. When you seek first the kingdom of God, all things are added to you. The sermon outlines three actionable steps from Haggai: get into the right position, recognize the potential God has placed before you, and engage the process of building His house first.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel like God has taken something from you and you do not understand why
- When you are frustrated by a season of lack or interrupted plans
- When you are tempted to blame the devil for every difficulty instead of examining your priorities
- When you want to build your life on a foundation that sustains lasting significance, not just temporary success

## Steps

### Step 1: Stop assuming hardship is punishment

Refuse to default to the thought that bad things happen because the devil is attacking you. When something you built is taken away, ask God what He is protecting you from. Haggai shows that God removed the people's harvest not to punish them, but to keep them from depending on their own work instead of Him. Prayerfully pause before assigning blame and ask God to reveal whether what was lost was keeping you from Him.

### Step 2: Examine your priorities instead of your provision

Ask yourself whether you have been seeking the kingdom of God first. Jesus taught that if you seek first His kingdom and righteousness, all these things will be added to you. The problem is rarely that God lacks resources; the problem is that His provision cannot land where there are no priorities. Write down the areas where you have been anxious about provision, and then list whether those areas reflect God as your foundation or your own effort as your foundation.

### Step 3: Go up into the mountain — change your position

Haggai told the people to go up into the mountains. You must be in the right place to receive God's blessing. If you are living, working, or relating from a position disconnected from God's call, you will miss what He wants to provide. Move toward the place God has called you. This may mean changing your routine, your relationships, or your commitments so that you are positioned to receive from Him.

### Step 4: Bring down the timber — recognize and use the potential God has given you

Timber represents potential. The temple looked like simple timber until the people brought it down and built with it. God has already placed everything you need in your life, but it often looks unimpressive at first. Identify the gifts, skills, resources, and opportunities already in your hands. Instead of waiting for something better to appear, decide to use what God has already given you for His purposes.

### Step 5: Build My house — commit to the process of putting God first

The final step is the process: build God's house. This means ordering your finances, your family, your work, and your future around Him as the foundation. Bring your first fruits to God before you spend on yourself. When you lay a foundation of seeking Him first, He can build on it. If you wait for Him to send provision before you prioritize Him, you will remain stuck because God is waiting for you to lay the foundation first.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Widow and the Prophet

In First Kings, a widow lacked provision and told the prophet she had only a little cake left. The prophet told her to first make a small cake for him, then trust God to sustain her. Because she prioritized God's purpose over her own scarcity, her provision was multiplied. This illustrates that when you seek God's kingdom first, even your last resources become the seed for abundance.

### Example 2: The Boy with the Loaves and Fishes

A boy brought his lunch to Jesus instead of keeping it for himself. Jesus used that small offering to feed thousands, and there were leftovers. The miracle began with a decision to prioritize Jesus over personal security. When you bring your potential — even when it looks small — to God first, He multiplies it beyond what you imagined.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Ask God to reveal whether something lost was protecting you from dependence on anything other than Him
- ✅ Audit your priorities by listing where you spend your time, money, and energy before asking God for more provision
- ✅ Move toward the position God has called you to, rather than waiting for provision to appear in your current location
- ✅ Identify and use the gifts, resources, and opportunities God has already placed in your hands
- ✅ Bring your first fruits to God before you allocate resources to yourself
- ✅ Build your life on God as the foundation so that your blessings have a place to land

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal discernment, pastoral guidance, or environment-specific decisions about your life.
- Stop and ask for clarification from God if you are unsure whether a hardship is discipline, protection, or simply part of living in a broken world.
- Seek wise counsel before making major changes to your position, finances, or relationships.
- Do not use this sermon as an excuse to stay in unsafe environments or abusive relationships under the guise of "seeking God first." God's call to position includes wise and safe boundaries.
- Financial decisions, including tithing and giving, should be made with accountability and transparency, especially if you manage shared resources.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Blaming every difficulty on the devil without asking God what He might be teaching you
  **Solution:** Pause before assigning blame. Ask God to reveal whether the loss is protection, correction, or part of a larger redemptive purpose.
- **Problem:** Waiting for God to provide before you will prioritize Him
  **Solution:** Understand that God is waiting for you to lay the foundation of seeking Him first. Priority comes before the full manifestation of provision.
- **Problem:** Believing that financial success equals significance
  **Solution:** Remember that you can financially succeed without God, but you cannot have deep-seated significance without Him. Evaluate success by whether God is at the center, not by worldly metrics.
- **Problem:** Comparing your lack of visible provision to others and assuming God has abandoned you
  **Solution:** The problem is rarely provision; it is priority. If you seek the kingdom first, all these things will be added to you. Refocus on your position in God, not your current circumstances.
