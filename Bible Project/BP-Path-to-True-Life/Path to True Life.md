# Jesus Explains What the Path to True Life is Like

## Overview

This sermon explores Jesus' teaching on the path to true life from the closing section of the Sermon on the Mount. It explains the choice between the narrow gate that leads to life and the broad road that leads to ruin, how to recognize false prophets by their fruit, and the importance of building your life on the solid foundation of hearing and doing Jesus' words. The sermon calls listeners to respond to Jesus' invitation to enter the Kingdom of the skies through radical peacemaking, generosity, and authentic connection with him.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand Jesus' teaching on the narrow gate and the path to true life
- When working through questions about false prophets and how to recognize them by their fruit
- When the user asks about building a life on the solid foundation of Jesus' words rather than sand

## Steps

### Step 1: Enter through the narrow gate that leads to life

Jesus presents a clear choice: wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to ruin, and many enter through it. How narrow is the gate and how constricted is the road that leads to life, and those who find it are few. Choose the constricted road that leads to true freedom rather than the easy, popular path that leads to destruction. Accept that the path to life may feel confining at times and will likely involve difficult trials, but remember that it is the way to genuine freedom.

### Step 2: Recognize false prophets by their fruit

Watch out for illegitimate prophets who come to you in the clothing of sheep but inside are wolves who snatch the sheep. Evaluate teachers and leaders by whether their choices produce life and flourishing for themselves and other people. Pay attention to how they live and the outcomes of their decisions. Remember that good deeds, prophecies, and miracles done for wrong reasons will not fool God, and someone who imitates the actions of Jesus but lacks authentic connection with him will be rejected.

### Step 3: Build your life on the solid foundation of Jesus' words

Hear Jesus' words and put them into practice. Build your life on the rock of obedience rather than the sand of superficial hearing. Understand that "the house of God" represents a community where God's heavenly presence can reside here on Earth, just like the garden of Eden. Everyone who hears these words of Jesus and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock. When rain, rivers, and wind blow against it, the house will not fall because the foundation is on a rock.

### Step 4: Live as a community of peace and justice

Let your light shine so others can see. Create communities where God's wisdom and love overcome violence and death. Build places where God can reside and where Eden can spring to life because, ultimately, those places are the only ones that will last. Demonstrate the way back to God's life for the rest of the world through radical peacemaking and generosity.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Narrow Gate and Broad Road

Jesus teaches that wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to ruin, and many enter through it. How narrow is the gate and how constricted is the road that leads to life, and those who find it are few. This illustrates the choice between the easy, popular path that leads to destruction and the difficult, less-traveled path that leads to true life.

### Example 2: The Wise and Foolish Builders

Everyone who hears Jesus' words and does them is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. When rain came down and rivers came and wind blew and they fell upon that house, it did not fall because the foundation was on a rock. Everyone who hears these words and does not do them is like a foolish man who built his house on the sand. When rain came down and rivers came and wind blew and they fell upon that house, it fell, and its falling was huge.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Enter through the narrow gate and walk the constricted road that leads to life
- ✅ Evaluate teachers and leaders by the fruit of their lives and choices
- ✅ Build your life on the solid foundation of hearing and doing Jesus' words
- ✅ Live in authentic connection with Jesus rather than merely imitating his actions
- ✅ Build communities where God can reside and where Eden can spring to life
- ❌ Do not follow the broad road that leads to ruin just because it is easy or popular
- ❌ Do not trust prophets or leaders based on their words, prophecies, or miracles alone
- ❌ Do not build your life on superficial hearing without putting Jesus' words into practice
- ❌ Do not do the right thing for wrong reasons or rely on outward performance alone

## Keep In Mind

- Evaluate all religious teachers and leaders by their fruit, not by their title, platform, or miracles.
- Authentic connection with Jesus matters more than outward religious performance.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Following false prophets who disguise themselves as legitimate leaders
  **Solution:** Pay attention to whether their choices produce life and flourishing for themselves and others, not just their words or supernatural acts.
- **Problem:** Building life on superficial religious activity without authentic connection to Jesus
  **Solution:** Ensure your motives are connected to genuine love for Jesus, not just outward performance or desire for recognition.
- **Problem:** Choosing the easy path that feels good in the short term but leads to destruction
  **Solution:** Remember that the narrow gate and constricted road lead to true freedom even though they are difficult and unpopular.
