# WISDOM AND WONDER ｜ Where's Your Heart？

## Overview

This sermon exists to help anyone battling depression, heaviness of mind and heart, suicidal thoughts, or dark ideations find spiritual and emotional breakthrough. It also exists to help every listener examine the true condition of their heart through the Parable of the Sower (Matthew 13) and respond to God's Word in a way that produces lasting, fruit-bearing transformation. It serves as both a call to spiritual warfare against inner strongholds and an invitation to receive healing for buried trauma.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you or someone you know is battling depression, heaviness of mind, or suicidal thoughts and needs spiritual breakthrough
- When you are carrying past trauma, unaddressed pain, or buried emotional wounds and need healing and release
- When you want to understand the four types of responses to the gospel in the Parable of the Sower and identify which soil describes your heart
- When you are seeking a deeper, more receptive relationship with God's Word
- When you need encouragement to continue sharing the gospel even when met with rejection or hardship

## Steps

### Step 1: Come Against Mental and Emotional Strongholds

- Stand in the authority of Jesus' name and verbally reject depression, heaviness of mind, heaviness of heart, thoughts of suicide, and dark ideations
- Declare that you are taking every thought captive and bringing it into submission to Christ
- Renounce any demonic force operating against your mind and heart, and assert your authority over those areas in Jesus' name

### Step 2: Pray for a Consecrated and Healthy Interior Life

- Cry out to God specifically for a consecrated interior life and a healthy interior life; repeat this prayer persistently
- Ask God to illuminate the darkness in your mind with the truth of His Word, bringing clarity and conviction where there has been confusion
- Continue in prayer until anxiety, heaviness, or despair lose their grip and peace, assurance, and joy become your dominant disposition

### Step 3: Bring Buried Trauma to the Surface and Receive Healing

- Identify anything you have buried or avoided—such as abuse, betrayal, abandonment, loss, or deep emotional scars—and consciously bring it before God
- Receive by faith the power to forgive those responsible, release the pain, and move forward into what God has for you
- Receive prayer for healing over your mind, heart, and soul, asking God to break every chain and restore what was damaged

### Step 4: Examine Your Heart Using the Parable of the Sower

- Hear the Word of the kingdom and ask the Holy Spirit to reveal which type of soil your heart currently resembles:
  - **Hardened heart (path):** You automatically reject or ignore the Word of God and have no desire to engage with the gospel, the kingdom, or ministry
  - **Shallow heart (rocky ground):** You receive the Word with initial joy but have no depth; you fall away or disengage when difficulty, rejection, or persecution arises
  - **Preoccupied heart (thorns):** Your time and attention are consumed by worldly concerns and material pursuits, leaving no room for God, prayer, or Christian community
  - **Receptive heart (good soil):** You hear the Word, understand it, receive it, meditate on it, and produce lasting fruit in your life and the lives of others
- Honestly confess any area where you have resisted submission to Jesus as Lord, and ask Him to soften your heart

### Step 5: Sow the Word Intentionally and Persistently

- Read the Bible for transformative revelation, not merely for knowledge or information; treat Scripture as a direct means of healing and transformation for your heart
- In areas where you are shallow—such as stewardship, purity, relationships, marriage, sexuality, or finances—study and apply God's Word specifically in those areas until it takes root
- Share the Word of God intentionally in your marriage, relationships, family, conversations, social media, and every opportunity God provides
- Praise God by faith before you see visible change, trusting that God has already begun a new work in you

### Step 6: Respond to the Gospel Invitation

- If you recognize your heart as hardened, shallow, or preoccupied, acknowledge that today is the day to respond
- Confess that you are a sinner who has broken God's laws and deserves eternal separation from Him
- Receive the truth that Jesus Christ died a sacrificial death on your behalf, exchanging your unrighteousness for His righteousness
- Place your faith in Christ alone—not in church membership, religious activity, or your own efforts—and surrender your life to Him
- Respond to the Holy Spirit's call now, receive Christ as your Lord and Savior, and begin the new life He has for you

## Examples

### Example 1: Preaching While Depressed

The preacher shared his personal testimony of functioning depression—preaching every Sunday while on medication, with persistent heaviness and mental clouding. Through disciplined, repeated prayer for a consecrated and healthy interior life, he experienced breakthrough: the medication was no longer needed, the heaviness lifted, and peace and joy became his dominant experience.

### Example 2: From Hardened Heart to Sower

The preacher described his former life as someone involved in drug dealing and promiscuity from Queens, whose hardened heart caused the Word to bounce off him repeatedly. During a season of suicidal despair, his heart became receptive, the Word took root, and he was transformed from someone who consistently rejected the truth into someone now actively sowing that same Word into the lives of others.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Come against depression, heaviness, and dark ideations in Jesus' name immediately and consistently
- ✅ Pray specifically and repeatedly for a consecrated and healthy interior life
- ✅ Bring buried trauma to the surface honestly and receive prayer for healing and forgiveness
- ✅ Examine your heart seriously using the four soils of the Parable of the Sower and ask the Holy Spirit to reveal your true condition
- ✅ Read God's Word for personal revelation and transformation, not just to accumulate knowledge
- ✅ Sow the Word into every area of your life and into the lives of those around you
- ✅ Praise God by faith before you see the change, trusting that His work is already accomplished
- ✅ Respond to the Holy Spirit's call today—do not continue in hardness or delay

## Keep In Mind

- The seed in the Parable of the Sower is the Word of the kingdom—God's declared truth—and it targets the condition of the heart, not merely the intellect
- Jesus used parables both to reveal the secrets of the kingdom to those who genuinely seek Him and to veil truth from those who have willfully rejected Him
- Three of the four soil types in the Parable of the Sower represent false responses that do not lead to lasting fruit; only the receptive heart produces enduring transformation
- The issue is never the sower or the seed—it is always the condition of the soil, meaning each person is responsible for the state of their own heart
- Trauma or pain that you suppress rather than surrender to God will resurface and will require healing in a later season
- Forgiveness is not an endorsement of what was done to you; it is the decision to release the debt so that you can be free and experience God's peace
- A heart that can continue in sin without conviction has been given over to its own desires, which is a dangerous spiritual condition that requires immediate repentance

## Security & Safety Notes

- This sermon contains personal testimony of depression and suicidal ideation; listener discretion is advised
- If you or someone you know is in crisis or at risk of self-harm, seek immediate professional help and contact emergency services
- The spiritual practices described here are intended to complement, not replace, professional mental health care

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Remaining in a hardened heart and automatically blocking out conviction, correction, or the call of God
  **Solution:** Ask the Holy Spirit to soften your heart, humble yourself, and receive the Word with a teachable and open spirit

- **Problem:** Staying in shallow ground with no root system, so difficulty causes you to fall away or disengage from God for long periods
  **Solution:** Commit to consistent engagement with God's Word in the areas where you are most fragile, and remain connected to Christian community for accountability and support

- **Problem:** Letting the pressures and pleasures of this world consume your time and attention, choking out your relationship with God and His people
  **Solution:** Reprioritize your life around gathering with other believers, prayer, and Scripture; recognize that being too busy for God is a choice, not a necessity

- **Problem:** Reading the Bible only to gain knowledge, without allowing it to produce real change in your life
  **Solution:** Approach Scripture with a prayerful and receptive heart, asking God to speak to you personally through every passage

- **Problem:** Waiting for external circumstances to change before you believe God has moved in your life
  **Solution:** Trust by faith that God is already at work, and praise Him in advance of visible results

- **Problem:** Attempting to earn salvation through church attendance, ministry involvement, or moral effort
  **Solution:** Understand that salvation comes through faith in Christ alone, not through religious activity, and surrender your life to Him today
