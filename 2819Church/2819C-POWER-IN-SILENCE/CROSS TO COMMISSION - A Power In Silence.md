# CROSS TO COMMISSION | A Power In Silence

## Overview

This sermon explores Matthew 27:11-14, focusing on the trial of Jesus before Pilate—the most sensational trial in human history. Jesus remained silent before his accusers, fulfilling the prophecy of Isaiah 53:7, demonstrating godly submission to the Father's will even unto death. The message teaches believers to be filled with God's word so it can guide them in moments of trouble, to discern when to speak and when to remain silent, to reject the lies of the enemy, and to subject their will completely to God's will.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing trials, accusations, or opposition and need guidance on how to respond with godly wisdom
- When struggling with condemnation, guilt, or shame and need to stand firm in the truth of who you are in Christ
- When discerning God's will for your life and learning to submit your desires to His perfect plan

## Steps

### Step 1: Fill Yourself With God's Word

Immerse yourself in Scripture so that it becomes the foundation of your heart and mind. Just as Jesus knew His identity and mission, you must know who you are in Christ and what God has called you to do. Spend time daily reading the Bible, meditating on it, and letting it shape your responses to life's challenges. When trouble comes, the word stored in your heart will guide you—whether to speak or to remain silent.

### Step 2: Learn to Discern When to Speak and When to Be Silent

Not every situation calls for a verbal response. Like Jesus before Pilate, there are moments when silence is the most powerful witness. Evaluate each situation: Will speaking advance God's kingdom, or will it only waste words on those who do not want to hear? Do not engage with every critic or every lie. Reserve your words for the moments when God calls you to speak, and embrace silence when He calls you to stand firm in quiet confidence.

### Step 3: Stand Against the Lies of the Enemy

The enemy will try to fill your mind with condemnation, guilt, and shame—lies designed to derail your purpose. Recognize these attacks for what they are and reject them. Jesus stood silently against false accusations because He knew the truth. You must also know the truth of your identity in Christ and refuse to let the enemy's lies take root in your heart.

### Step 4: Subject Your Will to God's Will

Jesus' kingdom is not of this world; it is in the hearts of men. Christ is the only truth, and there is no other truth outside of Him. Lay down your own ambitions, titles, gifts, talents, and money at the feet of the Father. Submit your will completely to His will, trusting that His plan is perfect even when it leads to the cross. Like Jesus, be willing to go wherever the Father leads, even if it means silence in the face of injustice.

## Examples

### Example 1: Remaining Silent in the Face of False Accusations

A coworker spreads a false rumor about you at your job. Instead of immediately retaliating with anger or a lengthy defense, you choose to remain silent and trust God with your reputation. You continue to work with excellence and integrity, letting your actions speak louder than any words. Over time, the truth comes to light, and your quiet confidence becomes a testimony to those watching.

### Example 2: Submitting Personal Ambition to God's Calling

You have been gifted with talents and have been offered a lucrative opportunity that would bring you recognition and financial success. However, during your time in prayer and Scripture, you sense that God is calling you to a different path—one that requires sacrifice and obscurity. Like Jesus, who subjected His will to the Father's, you choose to decline the opportunity and walk in the purpose God has for you, even when it costs you everything.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Fill yourself with God's word daily so it guides you in moments of trouble
- ✅ Learn to discern when to speak and when to remain silent
- ✅ Stand firm against the lies of the enemy—condemnation, guilt, and shame
- ✅ Subject your will completely to God's will, even when it is difficult
- ✅ Do not waste words on people who do not want to hear the truth
- ✅ Boast only in Christ, not in your titles, gifts, talents, or money
- ❌ Do not speak just to defend yourself when silence would honor God more
- ❌ Do not let condemnation, guilt, or shame from the enemy take root in your heart
- ❌ Do not prioritize worldly success or recognition over obedience to God
- ❌ Do not treat Jesus' sacrifice as insignificant—He died so that you might live

## Keep In Mind

- Jesus' silence before Pilate was not weakness—it was prophetic fulfillment of Isaiah 53:7 and a demonstration of godly submission
- There is no truth outside of Christ; He is the only way, the only truth, and the only life
- Pilate found no fault in Jesus—an innocent man died so that guilty people could be saved
- Your ministry and purpose exist wherever you are, whether in a prison cell or a position of influence
- The enemy wants to keep you silent with guilt and shame, but God calls you to stand in the freedom He has already purchased for you

## Security & Safety Notes

- Guard your heart against the enemy's lies by staying rooted in Scripture
- Do not let condemnation from others—or from yourself—define your identity; your identity is found in Christ alone
- Be cautious about sharing your spiritual journey with those who will only mock or criticize; protect your peace and seek wisdom from God on when to open up
- Remember that your words have power—use them wisely and only when God leads you to speak

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Speaking out of anger or frustration when silence would have been more powerful
  **Solution:** Pause and ask God for wisdom before responding. Let His word guide your words.

- **Problem:** Allowing guilt, shame, or condemnation to keep you from fulfilling God's calling
  **Solution:** Remember that Jesus' sacrifice has already set you free. Stand in the truth of who you are in Christ and reject every lie from the enemy.

- **Problem:** Prioritizing worldly success, titles, or recognition over submission to God's will
  **Solution:** Daily surrender your ambitions to the Lord. Ask Him to align your desires with His perfect plan for your life.

- **Problem:** Wasting words on people who are not open to hearing the truth
  **Solution:** Not everyone is ready to receive what you have to say. Discernment from the Holy Spirit will help you know when to speak and when to remain silent.
