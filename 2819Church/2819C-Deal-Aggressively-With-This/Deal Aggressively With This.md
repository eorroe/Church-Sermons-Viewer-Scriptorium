# WISDOM & WONDER | Deal Aggressively With This | Matthew 18:7-9 | Philip Anthony Mitchell

## Overview

This sermon is a direct, urgent call from Matthew 18:7-9 for believers to stop treating sin as a pet and start dealing with it aggressively. Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell warns that sin is the root cause of all human suffering and destruction, and that believers who lead others into sin or harbor unrepentant sin in their own lives face severe eternal consequences. The message calls for repentance, humility, accountability, and a life of holiness empowered by the Holy Spirit.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you sense complacency or casualness about sin in your own life
- When struggling with habitual, willful patterns of unrepentant sin
- When you want to understand the true cost and severity of sin
- When seeking to live a genuinely holy life as a follower of Christ
- When you need to understand what biblical accountability looks like
- When you have been deceived into thinking grace gives you license for sin

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize and Name Your Sin

Identify the habitual, willful practices of sin in your life. Call them out by name — whether it is lust, pornography, unforgiveness, pride, anger, jealousy, rebellion, or any other sin. Do not minimize or make excuses for them. Acknowledge that every part of your being has been infected by sin, and that no one is exempt.

### Step 2: Deal Aggressively With Sins of Commission (The Hand and Foot)

Sins of commission are the active, outward sins you practice — watching pornography, lying, stealing, sowing discord, rebellion against authority, sexual immorality, adultery, and other unrepentant, outward sins. When you recognize these in your life, cut them off. This is not a call to physical self-mutilation, but to behavioral discipline — do whatever is necessary to stop practicing unrepentant sin. Deny yourself, suffer the discomfort of discipline, and remove the behavior.

### Step 3: Deal Aggressively With Sins of the Heart (The Eye)

Sins of the heart are the hidden sins that no one else sees — unforgiveness, pride, lust, anger, jealousy, envy, covetousness, self-inflation, idolatry. These are the internal corruptions that God sees. When you identify these in your heart, actively "ring them out" through prayer. Cry out to God to uproot them. Do not allow them to remain and grow. Go to God in your prayer room and confess each one specifically.

### Step 4: Increase Your Love for the Lord Jesus Christ

Actively grow your love for Jesus through prayer, devotion, reading the Word, and worship. The more you love Him, the harder it becomes to sin against Him. When you genuinely love someone, you do not want to keep hurting them. Make the pursuit of intimacy with Christ the primary driver of your fight against sin.

### Step 5: Pray for God to Give You a Hatred for Sin

Ask God to give you a hatred in your heart for what He hates. This is a specific prayer you can begin praying today: "God, give me a hatred for sin." The more you hate sin, the more you will recognize it in yourself, in the world, and in the church — and the more aggressively you will deal with it when you find it in your own life.

### Step 6: Establish Accountability Through Safety Circles and Partners

Do not keep your sin in the dark. Bring trusted believers into your life as accountability partners. Establish a "safety circle" in your home or community — a small group of trusted believers with whom you can share your struggles honestly without fear of condemnation. When you have a bad day or a bad week, call your accountability partners and tell them the truth. Darkness in your life is a lane for the devil to traffic; light drives it out.

### Step 7: Repent and Cry Out to God in the Moment

When conviction comes, do not delay. Bow your head, close your eyes, and be honest with God. Confess your sin specifically: "Lord, I am feeling lustful — ring that out. Lord, I am feeling jealous — ring that out. Lord, I am feeling covetous — ring that out." Cry out for grace to stop. Weep if you need to. Repentance is not a one-time event; it is a daily, moment-by-moment practice.

### Step 8: Live a Life of Holiness Monday Through Saturday

Holiness is not a song you sing on Sunday or a post you make on social media — it is the way you live every day. The Lord has called you to be a holy people: a royal people, a peculiar people, separated from the world. Do not be conformed to the world's patterns. Let your life be distinctly marked by the pursuit of moral purity, so that your witness to the world is not compromised.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Personal Testimony of the Preacher

Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell shares his own struggle with lust and pornography. Two years into his marriage, he had to go to his wife, Lena, and confess what had happened to him when he was 10 years old and why he struggled with lust throughout his life. He did not hide it — he brought it into the light through his safety circle. He says, "I don't want no dark areas in my life. Any area of darkness in your life is a lane for the devil to traffic." He established accountability partners who he calls when he has a bad day or a bad week, saying, "This is what I did. Pray for me. This is what I thought. Pray for me."

### Example 2: Lucifer's Fall — The Origin of Sin in the Universe

The first sin ever committed was not in the Garden of Eden — it was in heaven. Lucifer, an archangel responsible for worship, gazed at his own beauty and pride rose in his heart. He whispered to one angel, "I'm going to be better than God," then to another, and another, spreading rebellion through the heavenly host. The second sin born in his heart was rebellion, and war broke out in heaven. Satan was cast out, and one-third of the angels who were deceived were thrown out with him. These are the demons now roaming the earth, whispering into the ears of psychics, leading people away from Christ through tarot cards, sage burning, crystals, and other occult practices.

### Example 3: The Rich Man and Lazarus (Luke 16:19-31)

Jesus tells the story of a rich man who feasted sumptuously every day while Lazarus, a poor man covered in sores, lay at his gate desiring to eat what fell from the rich man's table. Both died — Lazarus was carried to glory, and the rich man went to Hades (hell). In torment, the rich man called out to Abraham, asking for Lazarus to cool his tongue with water. Abraham responded: "Son, remember that in your lifetime you received your good things and Lazarus received bad things, but now he is comforted and you are in anguish. Between us a great chasm has been fixed." The rich man begged Abraham to send someone to warn his five brothers. Abraham said: "They have Moses and the prophets; let them hear them." The rich man insisted: "If someone goes to them from the dead, they will repent." Abraham's final word: "If they do not hear Moses and the prophets, neither will they be convinced if someone rises from the dead." This is the fate of those who treat sin like a pet and refuse to repent.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Increase your love for Jesus Christ daily through prayer, devotion, reading the Word, and worship
- ✅ Pray specifically for God to give you a hatred for sin — ask Him to make you hate what He hates
- ✅ Establish accountability partners and a safety circle where you can share your struggles without judgment
- ✅ Ring out the sins of your heart through honest, specific prayer to God
- ✅ Read the Scriptures for yourself — do not rely solely on sermons or secondhand teaching
- ✅ Look for fruit and evidence of transformation in your life and in others, not just verbal claims of Christianity
- ✅ Deal with sin daily and moment by moment, not just when crisis strikes
- ✅ Be willing to suffer discipline and deny yourself rather than continue in unrepentant sin
- ✅ Let your life reflect holiness on Monday through Saturday, not just on Sunday
- ✅ Keep no secrets — bring every dark area of your life into the light

## Keep In Mind

- The "woe" in Matthew 18:7 is a double woe — it is simultaneously a declaration of unescapable punishment and a sorrowful expression that God feels grief for the person on the other side of it
- The first sin in the universe was pride, committed by Lucifer in heaven — sin did not originate on earth
- One-third of the angels were deceived and cast out with Satan; they are now demons roaming the earth
- Grace is not a license for licentiousness — the era of grace does not remove the call to holiness
- The Lord knows the deep damage and destruction sin brings to the hearts and lives of human beings, and He grieves when His children indulge in it
- The more you love Christ, the harder it becomes to sin against Him
- Accountability is not a sign of weakness — it is a sign of wisdom and a desire to be free from sin
- God has given you the power through the Holy Spirit to live a life free from unrepentant sin — you can do this
- "Those who endure to the end shall be saved" — a prayer of profession without a life of repentance is worthless
- A person can claim to be a Christian while living in unrepentant sin — we are called to check for fruit, not just labels
- The western church sings and preaches about holiness while failing to live it — this must change

## Security & Safety Notes

- Any area of darkness in your life is a lane for the devil to traffic — bring everything into the light
- Do not keep secrets about your sin, even from your spouse or closest believers — concealment gives the enemy a foothold
- Tarot cards, sage burning, crystals, and psychic readings are associated with demonic activity — flee from these practices
- Demons have been watching you since childhood and use occult practices to lead you away from Christ — do not dabble in the occult
- False prophets and compromised pastors will tell you what you want to hear rather than what the Bible says — test everything by the Word of God
- A false assurance of salvation — believing you are saved while continuing in unrepentant sin — is one of the most dangerous deceptions
- Do not trust someone's label ("Christian") without looking for fruit — evidence of grace and transformation is what matters

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating sin like a pet — keeping it on a leash, playing around with it, or making excuses for it
  **Solution:** Recognize sin immediately when you see it in your life, name it, and deal with it aggressively through repentance, accountability, and the power of the Holy Spirit

- **Problem:** Believing that grace gives you a license for licentiousness — thinking you can sin freely because you are under grace
  **Solution:** Read Ananias and Sapphira in Acts 5 — God struck them dead for deceitfulness of heart in the New Testament era. Grace empowers you to be holy, not to keep sinning

- **Problem:** Having a false assurance of salvation — believing you are saved because you prayed a prayer or went to an altar, but continuing to live in unrepentant sin
  **Solution:** Examine your life for fruit. Jesus said many will say "Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name?" and He will respond: "Depart from me, you worker of iniquity, you love sin, I never knew you." Endure to the end

- **Problem:** Looking for an earthly description of a "Christian" rather than looking for biblical fruit
  **Solution:** 1 John teaches how to know who the sons of God are and who the sons of the devil are. Check for evidence of grace and transformation, not a label or a social media profile

- **Problem:** Relying on sermons alone without reading the Scriptures yourself
  **Solution:** Read the Bible for yourself. Do not trust any preacher, including the one delivering this message, without verifying everything against the Word of God

- **Problem:** Keeping sin hidden in darkness rather than bringing it into the light
  **Solution:** Establish a safety circle with your spouse or trusted accountability partners. Share your struggles honestly and specifically. Darkness is where the devil traffics; light is where he cannot stay

- **Problem:** Thinking you cannot overcome sin — believing you are too weak, too far gone, or that sin has too strong a hold on you
  **Solution:** The Holy Spirit is the power of Christ on the inside of you. Jesus did not just die for your sin — He rose and gave you the Spirit so you could live a brand new life. You have been empowered. You can do this

- **Problem:** Judging others' sin while ignoring or excusing your own
  **Solution:** You are a problem too. Focus on the plank in your own eye before addressing the speck in your brother's. The call to deal aggressively with sin begins with you
