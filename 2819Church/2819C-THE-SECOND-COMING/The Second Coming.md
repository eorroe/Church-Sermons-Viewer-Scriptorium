# The Second Coming

## Overview

This sermon is a prophetic call to live with urgency and holiness in light of Christ's imminent Second Coming, based on Matthew 24:29-31. It exposes the danger of a complacent, entertainment-driven Christianity disconnected from the reality of Christ's return, and challenges every listener to realign their affections, priorities, and daily actions around the truth that this day is coming.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand the biblical sequence of the Rapture, Tribulation, and Second Coming
- When you feel your Christianity has become routine, disconnected, or merely performative
- When you want to realign your priorities, prayers, and resources around eternity rather than temporary things
- When the user asks how to live with a consciousness of Christ's return

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Prophetic Timeline

Know the order of end-times events as revealed in Scripture. Christ will return immediately after the seven-year Tribulation period (Matthew 24:29). Before that, the Church will be raptured out of the world (1 Thessalonians 4:17). Then the Antichrist will be revealed, sign a seven-year peace treaty with Israel, break it at the 3½-year mark, desecrate the rebuilt temple, and unleash wrath upon the earth. The Tribulation will involve global catastrophes, widespread death, and unparalleled suffering. Christ's Second Coming comes immediately after this period ends—not before, and not during it.

### Step 2: Recognize the Signs of Christ's Return

Understand the cosmic signs that will accompany Christ's return. The sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven (Matthew 24:29). Christ holds all things together by His power (Colossians 1:17), and when He returns He will loosen His grip from the universe, causing complete cosmic chaos—planets leaving orbit, stars flying through the solar system, total darkness covering the earth. Luke 21:25-26 describes people fainting from fear, with some dying of cardiac arrest from sheer terror at the visible, audible, and dreadful display of Christ's glory.

### Step 3: Fix Your Eyes on the Sign of the Son of Man

When the world is plunged into darkness and every nation is staring at the sky, a brilliant light will appear—not a planet or a star, but Jesus Christ Himself (Matthew 24:30). Every person on earth will see Him coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory (Acts 1:11). Every theological argument will end in that moment. Atheists, Muslims, Buddhists, Hindus, and every false religionist will know with certainty that Jesus is the Son of God. All tribes of the earth will mourn (Revelation 1:7) because their fate will be sealed—there will be no second chances.

### Step 4: Know That Christ Will Gather His Elect

Christ's return will also be the moment of gathering. He will send out His angels with a loud trumpet call to gather His elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other (Matthew 24:31). This includes those who were raptured before the Tribulation (who will return with Christ as the armies of heaven in white linen, Revelation 19:14) and those who came to faith during the Tribulation and survived the Antichrist's persecution. Both groups will be gathered together. Believers who are absent from the body will be present with the Lord (2 Corinthians 5:8), and when Christ appears, they will also appear with Him in glory (Colossians 3:4).

### Step 5: Reject the Doctrine of Universal Inclusion

Reject the false teaching that everyone will ultimately be saved. Christ is not coming for everyone—He is coming only for His elect, the redeemed, those saved by faith and grace alone (Matthew 24:31). Those who thought they were Christians while living disconnected from Christ will face eternal judgment. There are no second chances on the day Christ cracks the sky. The doctrine of universal inclusion is a lie and a heresy. Prepare accordingly.

### Step 6: Live With Consciousness of the Day

Since this day is coming, you must change how you live right now (2 Peter 3:11-12). Do not be tethered to temporary things. Everything you fight for in this life—your Instagram following, your podcast, your marriage, your business, your 401k, your ministry platform—will be dissolved (2 Peter 3:10-11). Set your affections on things above (Colossians 3:2). Live with a constant, sober awareness that Christ could return at any moment. Live holy, live godly, be set apart, and hasten the coming of the day of God. Your prayers, your marriage, your discipleship of your children, how you show up at work, how you speak on social media, and where you spend your money—all of it should flow like tributaries into the stream of the Great Commission.

### Step 7: Examine Your Motives and Allegiances

Ask yourself hard questions. What are you actually fighting for? What are you praying about? Are you praying for the lost, for your family members, for your coworkers? Or are you just begging God for stuff? Are the activities of your life flowing into the Great Commission? Where is your money going? If Christ returned today, would your life look like it was lived on mission, or would it look like you were building a kingdom for yourself?

## Examples

### Example 1: Getting Your House in Order

When a person knows they are going to die, they get their house in order. In the same way, when you know Christ is going to come back, you get your spiritual life in order. You stop delaying repentance. You reconcile broken relationships. You make sure your children have been discipled. You audit where your money goes. You stop treating Christianity as a Sunday hobby and start living like a missionary who knows his time is limited. Every day you live should have the kind of purpose that Christ demonstrated in His 33 years—even the fun He had was intentional and meaningful.

### Example 2: The Marriage Supper of the Lamb

Believers who are raptured before the Tribulation will be absent from the body and present with the Lord (2 Corinthians 5:8). The Tribulation lasts seven years—the same length as a Jewish wedding feast. While the world endures God's wrath, the Church will be at the marriage supper of the Lamb, eating and drinking with Christ, receiving rewards. Then Christ will return on His white horse, and the armies of heaven—all those who were raptured—will follow Him on white horses, clothed in white and pure linen (Revelation 19:14). You will be there. The question is whether you will be among the redeemed or among those who mourn.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Read Scripture regularly so you are not caught unaware by prophetic events
- ✅ Live every day with the consciousness that Christ could return at any moment
- ✅ Set your affections on things above, not on temporary possessions, platforms, or pursuits
- ✅ Pray for the lost—family members, friends, coworkers—with urgency
- ✅ Invest your time and money in the Great Commission
- ✅ Live holy and godly lives—different at home, at work, on social media, in your church
- ✅ Remember that everything in this life is temporary and will be dissolved
- ✅ Reject false doctrines of universal inclusion; hold fast to salvation by faith and grace alone

## Keep In Mind

- The exact day and hour of Christ's return is not known (Matthew 24:36), but the sequence of events is revealed, and the signs align with Jesus' teaching
- Christ's return will be visible, audible, and glorious—not a secret rapture but a cosmic event witnessed by every person on earth
- There will be no second chance after Christ appears; the fate of every person will be sealed
- The rapture removes the Church before the Tribulation—believers will not endure the seven years of wrath
- Every argument about theology ends on that day; all will know Jesus is the Son of God
- Believers will return with Christ as the armies of heaven

## Security & Safety Notes

- No one can predict the exact day or hour of Christ's return—reject anyone who claims to know the date
- The doctrine of universal inclusion (everyone will be saved) is a lie and a heresy that gives false security to the unprepared
- Playing church—going through the motions of Christianity without genuine saving faith—will not protect you on the day of Christ's return
- Do not be deceived by false Christs, false prophets, or false teachers who will arise and try to draw you away from the true Christ

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Living a Christianity disconnected from the consciousness of Christ's return—going through religious motions without genuine faith or urgency
  **Solution:** Let the truth of the Second Coming change how you pray, how you spend your money, how you disciple your children, and how you show up in every sphere of life

- **Problem:** Being tethered to temporary things—career, platform, possessions, status—as if this life is all there is
  **Solution:** Remember that everything will be dissolved; set your mind on things above and find your ultimate satisfaction in Christ alone

- **Problem:** Treating Christianity as a weekend activity or a social club rather than a life laid down for the King
  **Solution:** Live like Christ lived—with intentional, missionary purpose every single day, knowing your time is limited

- **Problem:** Believing the lie that everyone will ultimately be saved, so there is no urgency for the lost
  **Solution:** Hold fast to the truth that Christ is coming only for His elect. Those who reject Him will face eternal judgment—preach, pray, and reach the lost while there is still time

- **Problem:** Wasting time on ancillary pursuits, arguments, or entertainment instead of advancing the gospel
  **Solution:** Audit your life. If the activities you spend time on do not flow into the Great Commission, they are a waste of time in light of eternity
