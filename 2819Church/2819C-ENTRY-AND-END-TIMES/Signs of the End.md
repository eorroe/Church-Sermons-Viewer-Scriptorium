# Signs of the End

## Overview

This sermon explores the signs of the end times as taught by Jesus in Matthew 24:1-14. It emphasizes that God's prophetic plan, as recorded in scripture, moves toward its divinely appointed conclusion, and that the church must be ready for Christ's return by knowing the scriptures, maintaining faithful prayer and community, and actively sharing the gospel. The message calls believers to live faithfully, avoid deception, and remain steadfast while awaiting the fulfillment of all that God has spoken.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When studying the Olivet Discourse in Matthew 24
- When seeking to understand the signs of Christ's return and the end of the age
- When preparing for faithful living in light of biblical prophecy
- When teaching on end-time events and the church's calling in the present age

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That God's Prophetic Word Is Reliable

God has established God's prophetic plan as revealed in scripture that moves toward its intended conclusion. Unbelief, theological debate, and ignorance cannot alter what God has declared. The written Word of God contains prophecies that come to pass exactly as spoken, providing a trustworthy basis for understanding where we are in history.

### Step 2: Trust That Jesus' Prophetic Words Are Faithful and True

Jesus foretold the destruction of the temple, and within the first century Roman forces destroyed Jerusalem and its temple complex. His words of life, warning, and promise can all be trusted. The Bible records prophecies that have seen notable fulfillments in history.

### Step 3: Understand the Present Age and Its Significance

Christian interpreters understand that between Christ's first coming and His return there is a distinct era in which the church now lives, though the exact nature and duration of this era is interpreted differently across traditions. This understanding, while not explicitly detailed in the Old Testament, is recognized in New Testament teaching as the period in which believers now serve. The signs Jesus describes in Matthew 24:4-14 point toward events associated with the period leading up to His return.

### Step 4: Identify the First Sign — Global Deception

The first sign of the end times is widespread deception. Many will come claiming authority in Christ's name, and many will be led astray. Protect yourself by knowing the scriptures for yourself, gathering with other believers for encouragement and accountability, and maintaining a consistent life of prayer.

### Step 5: Identify the Second Sign — Global Conflict

Following deception comes global conflict. There will be wars and rumors of wars across the earth. Jesus tells those who follow Him not to worry, for those who set their minds on Him will have peace even in the midst of trouble — a principle rooted in His teaching to seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness (Matthew 6:33).

### Step 6: Identify the Third Sign — Global Suffering

Nation will rise against nation, and there will be famines and earthquakes in various places. Jesus described these events using the analogy of birth pains, indicating that they would intensify as the end draws near.

### Step 7: Identify the Fourth Sign — Global Persecution of Believers

Believers will face tribulation, be delivered over to suffering, and be hated because of Christ's name. Many will fall away, betray one another, and hate one another. This period of intense opposition calls the church to remain faithful and hold fast to Christ.

### Step 8: Identify the Fifth Sign — Global Chaos

Wickedness and evil will increase across regions, and because of this, the love of many will grow cold. The world will be marked by widespread wickedness, breakdown of moral order, and persecution of believers. Believers must remain faithful and not grow weary in doing good.

### Step 9: Identify the Sixth Sign — Global Gospel Proclamation

The final sign is that the gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed across the whole earth as a testimony to all nations. The message of Christ is to be shared widely before the end comes.

### Step 10: Live in Readiness for Christ's Return

The return of Christ can happen at any time. According to this interpretive framework, no other prophetic events must precede the rapture of the church. Believers must ask: If Christ returned next week, would the Lord find you faithful? Use your time, prayers, gifts, and resources to build the kingdom and make Christ known.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Destruction of the Temple

Jesus foretold that not one stone of the temple would be left upon another. Within the first century, Roman forces besieged Jerusalem, killed people, and dismantled the temple complex. This fulfilled prophecy confirms that Jesus' words can be trusted.

### Example 2: A Personal Prophecy Fulfilled

In June 2009, the pastor recorded a prophetic journal entry regarding his life. The entry noted a time of testing and refining, a major decision regarding church planting, a movement of young people following him, youth pastoring before pastoring, and a ministry that would be culturally significant. The major decision was to move to Atlanta, which he did against his will. Within six months of arriving, he became a youth pastor in South Atlanta. Within one year of that role, the Lord called him to start a church. And within two years of that call, Victory Church was born in October 2012. Today, 2819 Church exists with a community of young people actively involved in its ministry, confirming that prophetic declarations from the Lord come to pass.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Know the scriptures for yourself rather than relying solely on podcasts, books, or social media
- ✅ Read the Bible behind every preacher and verify teaching against the Word
- ✅ Maintain a consistent prayer life to develop spiritual discernment
- ✅ Gather with other believers for encouragement and accountability
- ✅ Be found faithful with your time, talents, prayers, and resources
- ✅ Prioritize the spread of the gospel above all other activities
- ✅ Live as an ambassador of the kingdom of God, not as someone playing church
- ❌ Do not trust every voice that appears godly without testing it against scripture
- ❌ Do not waste time on activities that do not advance the kingdom
- ❌ Do not assume that a large ministry, big following, or popular conference means God is present
- ❌ Do not fall away from the faith or betray other believers during difficult times
- ❌ Do not grow cold in love because of increasing lawlessness

## Keep In Mind

- God's prophetic plan moves toward its intended conclusion according to His purposes
- The present era in which the church now lives is recognized in New Testament teaching as the period between Christ's first and second coming
- The return of Christ can happen at any moment — it is the next promised event in God's redemptive plan
- No one will have an excuse at the judgment; every person will be held accountable for what they have heard
- The Olivet Discourse is the longest response Jesus ever gave to a question
- Daniel 9:27 is understood as speaking of a future covenant and time of tribulation, though this is one interpretive framework among several

## Security & Safety Notes

- Test every teaching, sermon, and prophetic word against the written Word of God
- Do not follow voices that claim to be Christ or claim special revelation outside of scripture
- Be vigilant against false prophets who arise during times of chaos and deception
- Guard your heart and mind from teachings that prioritize emotion, entertainment, or self-promotion over faithful exposition of scripture
- Ensure that your theology is rooted in scripture, not in podcasts, social media, or popular opinion

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being led astray by false prophets and deceptive voices
  **Solution:** Know the scriptures for yourself, maintain a prayer life, and remain grounded in fellowship with other believers

- **Problem:** Wasting time on entertainment and vain activities while the end approaches
  **Solution:** Evaluate how every area of your life — prayer, relationships, gifts, and talents — advances the kingdom of God

- **Problem:** Playing church without true surrender or faithfulness
  **Solution:** Ask the Lord to search your heart and give you a genuine desire to be found faithful when He returns

- **Problem:** Growing cold in love because of increasing lawlessness and wickedness
  **Solution:** Endure to the end by staying rooted in Christ, obeying His teachings, and keeping your eyes on His return

- **Problem:** Trusting every preacher or podcast without personal verification
  **Solution:** Read the Bible for yourself, study faithful teaching, and test all claims against the Word of God
