# Dealing Agressivly With Sin - The Pastor That Broke The Internet | Philip Anthony Mitchell

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers to deal aggressively with sin rather than treating it casually or hiding it. Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell emphasizes that Christians have been empowered by the Holy Spirit to live holy lives, and provides two practical steps: increasing love for Christ and developing a hatred for sin.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you find yourself tolerating known sin in your life
- When you want to grow in holiness and spiritual maturity
- When you need motivation to repent and turn from sinful patterns
- When you desire to live a life that honors God

## Steps

### Step 1: Increase Your Love for the Lord Jesus Christ

Engage in practices that deepen your love for Christ. Spend time in prayer, devotional reading, worship, and meditation on Scripture. The more you love Jesus, the harder it becomes to sin against Him because you do not want to hurt someone you love.

### Step 2: Ask God to Give You a Hatred for Sin

Pray and ask God to give you a hatred in your heart for sin. As you grow in your hatred for what God hates, you will begin to recognize sin in yourself, in the world, and in the church. This recognition leads you to deal with sin aggressively rather than treating it like a pet.

### Step 3: Repent and Turn From Sin

Bow your head, close your eyes, and repent. Ask God to give you the grace to ring out your heart of sin. The Lord has empowered you through the Holy Spirit to live a brand new life, so choose to turn away from unrepentant sin.

## Examples

### Example 1: Dealing With a Known Sin

If you know you are struggling with a particular sin, apply the steps above. Increase your love for Christ through extended prayer and worship, ask God to grow your hatred for that specific sin, and then actively repent and turn from it.

### Example 2: Overcoming Complacency

If you have grown comfortable with minor sinful habits, recognize that the Holy Spirit has given you power to live differently. Use that power to aggressively address those habits rather than making excuses.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Increase your love for Christ through daily prayer and devotion
- ✅ Ask God to grow a hatred for sin in your heart
- ✅ Actively repent and turn from known sin
- ✅ Remember that you have been empowered by the Holy Spirit
- ✅ Do not treat sin as something harmless or acceptable

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking you cannot overcome sin
  **Solution:** Remember that the Holy Spirit has given you power to live a holy life.
- **Problem:** Treating sin casually or hiding it
  **Solution:** Deal with sin aggressively by repenting and turning from it.
- **Problem:** Making excuses for sinful behavior
  **Solution:** Ask God to deliver you from excuses and empower you to live in holiness.
