# ENTRY & END TIMES | Stay Awake | Matthew 24:36-51 | Philip Anthony Mitchell

## Overview

This sermon declares that holding to the words and teachings of Christ and being aligned with the will of God is vital. Drawing from Matthew 24:36-51, Philip Anthony Mitchell urgently calls believers to wake from spiritual slumber, apathy, and indifference, and to live watchfully and faithfully in expectation of Christ's return. The message warns that only what is connected to the will of God will stand, and that the day and hour of Christ's return is known to no one — not angels, and not even the Son — making it imperative to be found faithful when He comes. It is a call to full surrender, bold repentance, and diligent stewardship of every area of life.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel spiritually apathetic, lethargic, or indifferent toward God
- When you have been attending church without a genuine, personal relationship with Christ
- When you have been "playing church" — going through religious motions without true surrender
- When you are consumed by worldly ambitions, finances, relationships, or busyness that crowd out intimacy with Christ
- When you are uncertain about your salvation or sense no conviction from the proclaimed word
- When you need to realign your priorities around what is truly eternal
- When you feel far from God due to sin, addiction, shame, or drifting away from the faith
- When you have been putting off repentance, obedience, or faithfulness to Christ
- When you desire to live with a genuine longing for the return of Christ
- When you need a reminder that your entire life is a test of stewardship over everything God has entrusted to you

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge the Unknown Day and Hour

Recognize that no human being, not even the angels of heaven, and not even the Son in His earthly ministry, knows the exact day or hour of Christ's return — only the Father. Reject any teaching, prophecy, or claim that assigns a specific date, year, or hour to Christ's return. Treat anyone who makes such a claim as a false prophet, regardless of their title, platform, or age. Center your faith on Christ's own words in Matthew 24:36 rather than human speculation.

### Step 2: Wake Up From Spiritual Slumber

Stop sleeping in spiritual apathy, lethargy, and indifference. Confess that you have been distracted, apathetic, or foolish in your walk with God. Pray and ask the Holy Spirit to awaken you, to open your blinded eyes, and to unstop your deaf ears. Make a deliberate decision to come out of your slumber and engage seriously with the things of God.

### Step 3: Hold to the Words and Teachings of Christ Above All Else

Evaluate every activity, pursuit, and ambition in your life against the standard of Christ's teachings and the will of God. Remove anything from your life that is not directly connected to the will of God. Nothing on social media, podcasts, career pursuits, or personal ambitions is more important than abiding in Christ's words. Let nothing take priority over your connection to God's will.

### Step 4: Live Every Day With Expectation of Christ's Coming

Adopt a posture of watchful readiness each day. Do not assume Christ will not return soon — the rapture of the church can happen at any moment, and nothing remains to be fulfilled before it takes place (1 Thessalonians 4). Live as though Christ could return at any moment, and let that expectation shape your decisions, conversations, and conduct.

### Step 5: Be Ready Now — Do Not Delay

Do not wait to get right with God, to be obedient, to serve faithfully, to give generously, or to pray earnestly. The moment to be ready is now. Every day of delay is a day you could be swept away in judgment unprepared. Surrender your life to Christ without further postponement. Confess your sins and receive His forgiveness today.

### Step 6: Come Boldly to Christ Regardless of Your Past

Come boldly before Christ as your high priest who understands everything you have been through — whether you have sinned, drifted, stopped praying, stopped reading the Word, or stopped attending church. The devil's lies may keep you from His presence, but you must reject those lies and run back to Christ. There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ (Romans 8:1). Return to fellowship boldly, no matter how long you have been gone.

### Step 7: Be a Faithful Steward of Everything God Has Entrusted to You

Understand that your entire life is a test of stewardship. You do not own anything — your time, money, relationships, opportunities, assignments, job, possessions, and influence are all entrusted to you temporarily. Be faithful with all of it. Handle your finances, marriage, children, career, and ministry with integrity. Do not fight over temporary things, but prove yourself faithful so that Christ may say, "Well done, good and faithful servant." Pursue maturity that releases your heart from over-attachment to this life.

### Step 8: Reject the Posture of a Wicked Servant

Do not say in your heart, "My master is delayed." Do not use Christ's apparent delay as an excuse to neglect obedience, to beat fellow believers, to take advantage of others, or to live in compromise with sin. The master will come on a day and at an hour you do not expect, and He will settle every account. Be found awake, watching, and faithful.

## Examples

### Example 1: Noah Building the Ark

Noah was 500 years old when God told him to build the ark, and he was 600 years old when he entered it — meaning he spent 100 years building a ship on dry ground while people mocked him, scoffed at him, and did not understand his obedience. He had no evidence of rain in his lifetime. Yet he had a word from the Lord, and he obeyed it regardless of whether anyone else understood. The world was busy doing life — eating, drinking, marrying — completely outside of God's will, right up until the flood came. Noah's example demonstrates that obedience to God does not require understanding from others, only a word from the Lord. Those who mocked him during those 100 years banged on the door of the ark when the waters rose, only to be turned away — because they ignored the warning when it was still being proclaimed.

### Example 2: The Houseowner Ready for the Thief

Jesus teaches that if the master of a house knew at what hour of the night a thief was coming, he would stay awake and keep watch, and would not let his house be broken into. The point is not to speculate on when Christ is coming, but to recognize that He is coming at an hour you do not expect — and to be ready now. The illustration is meant to provoke urgency: if you would be physically ready at a known time, how much more should you be spiritually ready at all times, since Christ's return is certain even if the timing is unknown.

### Example 3: The Faithful and Wise Servant vs. The Wicked Servant

Jesus closes with a sharp contrast between two servants. The faithful and wise servant is one whom his master finds doing his duty at his return — managing the household, giving food at the proper time, stewarding what was entrusted to him. That servant will be set over all of his master's possessions. The wicked servant, however, says in his heart, "My master is delayed," and begins to beat his fellow servants, eat and drink with drunkards, and live in disobedience. The master will return on a day he does not expect, cut that servant in pieces, and put him with the hypocrites, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth. This is the final outcome of those who call themselves Christians but live as careless, unfaithful stewards.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Wake up from spiritual apathy and engage the things of God with urgency
- ✅ Prioritize the words and teachings of Christ above every other pursuit
- ✅ Live every day with the expectation that Christ could return at any moment
- ✅ Be ready now — do not postpone repentance, surrender, or obedience
- ✅ Come boldly to Christ regardless of how far you have drifted or what you have done
- ✅ Be faithful with your time, money, relationships, opportunities, and assignments
- ✅ Treat your entire life as a stewardship test for eternity
- ✅ Reject false prophecies and anyone who claims to know the exact day or hour of Christ's return
- ✅ Maintain a prayer life, knowledge of the Word, and biblical community
- ✅ Long for Christ's return more than you long for any earthly success or possession

## Keep In Mind

- No one knows the day or the hour of Christ's return — not the angels, and not the Son in His earthly ministry — only the Father (Matthew 24:36)
- The rapture of the church can happen at any moment — nothing remains to be fulfilled before it takes place
- This life is only a test of stewardship; everything you have is temporarily entrusted to you, not owned by you
- Eternity is far greater than anything this world can offer — your reward is to be with Christ, not earthly success
- Christ understands every temptation, trial, and struggle you face because He lived as a full human being — come to Him boldly in times of need
- Even if you have been gone from the church or the faith for a long time, you can return and be restored
- Jesus confirms the historicity of Noah and the global flood — God's Word is true and trustworthy

## Security & Safety Notes

- Reject any person, teaching, or prophecy that claims to know the exact day, year, or hour of Christ's return — any such claim is false and contradicts the words of Christ Himself
- Do not trust visions, dreams, or angelic visitations that assign a date to Christ's return — the angels do not know the day or the hour
- Be cautious of those who use religious authority or large platforms to set dates for Christ's return; their credibility does not override Christ's clear statement
- Guard against self-deception — sitting in church, attending conferences, or having devotionals does not save you if you have not genuinely surrendered to Christ
- Do not delay your salvation or your surrender to Christ out of a false belief that you have unlimited time

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Living in apathy and spiritual lethargy — attending church without a genuine relationship with Christ
  **Solution:** Wake up, come out of slumber, and pursue an authentic, personal relationship with Jesus through prayer, the Word, and obedience

- **Problem:** Prioritizing worldly ambitions, finances, and relationships over Christ and His will
  **Solution:** Evaluate every area of your life against the will of God, and remove anything that is not directly connected to Christ's teachings

- **Problem:** Believing you have more time to get right with God, saying "My master is delayed"
  **Solution:** Be ready now — surrender today, because Christ's return is certain and the timing is unknown to all but the Father

- **Problem:** Being overly attached to temporary things — money, titles, possessions, career success
  **Solution:** Release your heart from these things by remembering they are only a test, and pursue eternal reward by being a faithful steward of what God has entrusted to you

- **Problem:** Falling into the trap of self-deception — thinking church attendance or religious activity equals salvation
  **Solution:** Examine your heart honestly — is it a real relationship with Christ or just a performance? True believers will care about His words and live accordingly

- **Problem:** Being influenced by false prophets who predict specific dates for Christ's return
  **Solution:** Anchor yourself to Matthew 24:36 — only the Father knows the day or the hour — and reject any teaching that contradicts Christ's own words
