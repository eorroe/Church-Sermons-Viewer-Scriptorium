# Fruit And Faith

ENTRY & END TIMES | Matthew 21:18-22 | Philip Anthony Mitchell

## Overview

This sermon calls believers to reject religious pretense and duplicity, approach Christ with raw honesty in prayer, and pursue a life of genuine spiritual fruit—transformed character and behavior—rather than empty religious activity performed for public approval.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel spiritually dry, bored, or indifferent toward Christ and need to rekindle awe and wonder
- When your public profession of faith does not match your private behavior
- When doubt, past disappointment, or injury is hindering your ability to believe God
- When you need to examine whether your Christianity produces real fruit or merely religious leaves
- When you are following spiritual leaders based on talent or platform rather than character

## Steps

### Step 1: Remember the Humanity of Christ

Remember that Jesus was fully God and fully human at the same time, and because He lived a human life for 33 years without sin, He is intimately acquainted with every emotion, suffering, and frailty you experience. There is nothing you will ever feel that Christ does not understand—betrayal, frustration, fatigue, divorce, difficulty in marriage, or the despair that God cannot hear your prayers. This truth should draw you near to Him rather than cause you to hide.

### Step 2: Go Before the Lord With Naked Honesty

Stop filtering your prayers and hiding your true feelings from God. Go into your secret place and be honest about everything happening in your heart—your anger, frustration, lust, vengeance, marriage struggles, pornography, or feelings of distance from God. The Lord can handle your real emotions, and when you approach Him with raw honesty rather than hiding like Adam and Eve with leaves, you will find grace, love, healing, and mercy.

### Step 3: Examine Your Life for Leaves Without Fruit

The fig tree in Matthew 21 had leaves but no fruit, representing religious activity without genuine salvation, character, or transformed life. Examine your own life honestly: Do you have social media posts about godliness but no evidence of a transformed heart? Do you preach, sing, or do ministry without the fruit of holiness, love, and integrity? Are you close to God with your lips but far from Him in your heart? A form of godliness without the power thereof is worthless and under judgment.

### Step 4: Stop Being Phony and Pursue Authenticity

Commit to living a life congruent with what you profess. Do not sing on Sunday and have a nasty attitude on Monday at Walmart. Do not post scriptures on Instagram while your marriage is falling apart or your children hate you. Be more impressed with character than with talent, and refuse to live a double life. Living a double life drains your energy and destroys your credibility with those who are watching. Unbelievers reject Christ not because of who He is, but because of the phoniness of His representatives.

### Step 5: Guard Your Heart Against Familiarity That Breeds Boredom

Protect your heart from becoming so familiar with Christ that you become bored with Him, His Word, church, and the Scriptures. The disciples marveled at the fig tree withering because they had awe and wonder for God; do not lose that sense of awe. When you maintain awe for Christ, your curiosity will lead you to ask honest questions of Scripture, and those questions will lead you to deeper revelation and understanding.

### Step 6: Pray With Faith and Expect God to Move

If you have faith in your heart and do not doubt, you can pray in alignment with God's Word and will and expect Him to move. This is not prosperity gospel or name-it-and-claim-it; it is agreeing with what God has already spoken in His Word. Speak to the mountains in your life—impossible circumstances, sickness, broken marriages, or barrenness—and believe that nothing is impossible for God when it aligns with His will. Your role is to pray with faith; God's role is to move according to His power.

### Step 7: Repent of Doubt and Unbelief

Doubt and unbelief are what hinder your faith. If life has injured your faith—through divorce, betrayal, pain, loss, or disappointment—bring that doubt before God in prayer. Cry out to Him for healing from wounded faith, for the removal of the evil of doubt, and for the rise of radical faith in your life. Repent of the unbelief that has kept you from believing God for the impossible, and receive the faith that comes by hearing, and hearing by the Word of God (Romans 10:17).

## Examples

### Example 1: The Barren Fig Tree

Jesus walked toward Jerusalem and saw a fig tree with leaves but no fruit. Because figs grow before leaves, a tree with leaves should always have fruit—so seeing leaves without fruit signaled something was wrong. Jesus cursed the tree, and it withered at once. This tree represented the nation of Israel: full of religious activity, sacrifices, and outward show, but no genuine repentance, love for God, or transformed character. The same applies to modern American Christianity—full of Instagram posts, sermons, programs, and platforms, but often void of authentic fruit.

### Example 2: The Church at Sardis

In Revelation 3, Jesus wrote to the church at Sardis: "I know your works; you have a reputation of being alive, but you are dead." This church had all the external activity of a living church—programs, singing, preaching—but no real spiritual life or fruit. Jesus' instruction was sharp: "Wake up, and strengthen what remains and is about to die." This is the same warning Jesus gives to any believer or church that has leaves without fruit.

### Example 3: The Barren Womb

A woman in the church was barren and could not have children after five years of marriage, yet she believed God's Word that commands fruitfulness and the laying on of hands for healing. During a Sunday service, the pastor's wife laid hands on the barren womb and prayed according to Scripture, and two weeks ago the woman gave birth to healthy twins. This demonstrates Step 6 in action: praying in faith and alignment with God's Word, and expecting Him to move in impossible circumstances.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Approach God with raw, unfiltered honesty in prayer, bringing your true emotions and struggles
- ✅ Prioritize character over talent, oratory skill, platform size, and public gifting
- ✅ Let your private life match your public profession—ensure congruence between what you say and how you live
- ✅ Cultivate awe and wonder for Christ so that curiosity leads you to ask questions of the Scripture
- ✅ Pray according to God's Word and will, expecting Him to move on your behalf
- ✅ Protect your heart from familiarity with Christ that breeds boredom and spiritual indifference
- ✅ Focus on being real and authentic rather than maintaining a perfect public image
- ❌ Don't sanitize your prayers or hide your sin, struggles, and emotions from God
- ❌ Don't be impressed by talent, oratory, or platform without evidence of transformed character
- ❌ Don't live a double life with religious public behavior and ungodly private behavior
- ❌ Don't follow spiritual leaders who have form without power, leaves without fruit
- ❌ Don't let doubt and unbelief remain in your heart, for they are what hinder faith

## Keep In Mind

- Jesus was fully God and fully human, and He understands every pain, emotion, and struggle you face—you can approach Him with confidence
- The fig tree withered immediately when cursed, showing the seriousness with which God judges fruitless religion and duplicity
- Leaves without fruit are deceptive: from a distance they appear to be a healthy tree, but up close there is no life or substance
- Authentic faith produces fruit—character, holiness, love, and transformed behavior—not just religious activity
- Faith that moves mountains is faith that aligns with God's Word and His will; your part is to believe, and God's part is to act
- Doubt is the single thing that hinders your faith, not your circumstances or other people

## Security & Safety Notes

- Be cautious of following spiritual leaders, pastors, or influencers who display impressive talent, oratory, or platform size without verifiable evidence of character, integrity, and a transformed private life
- Protect yourself from false prophets who prioritize building a personal brand, accumulating wealth, or growing a following over proclaiming the authentic gospel and making disciples
- Guard your heart against being drawn to religious spectacle or performance without the substance of genuine salvation and fruit
- Reject the pressure to maintain a perfect spiritual image on social media or in public while secretly living in contradiction to your profession
- If a spiritual leader's private life is marked by broken relationships, hypocrisy, or moral failure, remove yourself from their influence no matter how gifted they appear publicly

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Living a duplicitous life with outward religious activity but inward ungodliness, causing your testimony, witness, and credibility to wither away
  **Solution:** Repent of double-living and pursue radical authenticity and congruence between your public profession and your private life
- **Problem:** Becoming bored or indifferent toward Christ due to familiarity, causing you to stop asking questions, studying the Word, or pursuing Him with awe and wonder
  **Solution:** Guard your heart against familiarity by cultivating ongoing amazement at who Christ is, what He has done, and what He continues to do in your life
- **Problem:** Allowing doubt to cripple your faith after injury, disappointment, betrayal, or loss, causing you to shut down and stop believing God for the impossible
  **Solution:** Bring your wounded faith before God in prayer, ask Him to heal your heart and drive out doubt, and receive His Word by faith so that radical faith can arise in you
- **Problem:** Following spiritual leaders based on talent, gifting, or platform without verifying their fruit and character, leaving you under the tyranny of false prophets
  **Solution:** Be more impressed with character than talent, examine whether a leader's life matches their message, and refuse to follow leaves without fruit no matter how impressive they appear
