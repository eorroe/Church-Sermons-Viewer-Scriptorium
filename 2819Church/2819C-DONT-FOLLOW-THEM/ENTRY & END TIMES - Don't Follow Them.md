# ENTRY & END TIMES | Don't Follow Them | Matthew 23:1-12

## Overview

This sermon is the last public message Jesus ever preached, drawn from Matthew 23:1-12, delivered on Mother's Day 2018 by Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell. It warns believers to guard their hearts against false prophets, false teachers, and false authority figures who perform ministry for self-exaltation rather than true service. The core teaching is that true spiritual greatness is found in humility and servanthood, not in titles, outward show, or self-promotion. Righteousness comes from Christ's work on the cross, not from human behavior or religious performance.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to discern false prophets and false teachers in your life
- When you are tempted to value titles, positions, or public recognition in ministry
- When you want to understand what Jesus actually taught about true spiritual leadership
- When you feel pressure to perform spirituality outwardly to impress others
- When you are evaluating whether a teacher's fruit matches their message

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Threat of False Teachers

False prophets and teachers are the most dangerous threat to the church, not the devil alone. They bring destructive heresies, prophesy for profit, and exploit people with false words. Scripture warns about them in Jeremiah 23, 2 Peter 2, Jude, and Matthew 7. They appear on social media platforms like TikTok and Facebook, building online "churches" with no divine authority, no interaction with real sheep, and no accountability. If you follow them, they will "rot out your soul."

### Step 2: Identify the Characteristics of False Authority

Jesus warned that false religious leaders do all their deeds to be seen. They make their phylacteries broad and their fringes long to appear more righteous. They love the best seats in synagogues, being greeted in marketplaces, and being called "Rabbi" by others. Today this shows up as people who crave titles like apostle, prophet, or pastor without divine calling. They find their identity in their title rather than in Christ, and they become insecure or angry when their title is not used or respected.

### Step 3: Do Not Allow Human Titles to Replace God

There is only one true Father, one true Instructor, one true Master, and one who forgives sins and died on the cross. Do not allow any person who uses religious titles to become God to you. A title given to yourself is worthless. The Lord is not against calling a spiritual father or teacher, but He is against allowing that person to replace Him in your life. Your identity must be in Christ alone, not in any title you hold or are called.

### Step 4: Understand True Greatness

Jesus said the greatest among you shall be your servant. Whoever exalts himself will be humbled, but whoever humbles himself will be exalted. True ministry is about service to others, not being served. It is not about fancy clothing, outward show, or building a personal brand. True greatness is born in the fire of humility, not in self-promotion. Christ Himself came from glory, humbled Himself to become a servant, and was then highly exalted. Follow His example.

### Step 5: Evaluate Teachers by Their Fruit

You will know false teachers by their fruit. Do not follow someone based on their title, their social media presence, their conference appearances, or their books. Look at their character, their humility, their service to others, and whether their life matches their message. Authentic ministry flows from a humble heart, not from a desire for attention.

### Step 6: Protect Your Soul from Lies

Pray that God protects your ears, eyes, and soul from false preachers, false authorities, and false teachers. Ask God to purge your soul from lies and filth you may have already taken in. Be washed in the Word and in the blood of the Lamb. Pursue authentic ministry that comes from Christ's righteousness, not your own behavior or performance.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Pharisees in Matthew 23

The Pharisees are the primary example Jesus uses in this sermon. They made their phylacteries broad and their fringes long to draw attention to their supposed righteousness. They loved the best seats in the synagogue and being called "Rabbi" in the marketplace. They performed religious duties to be seen by others, not out of genuine love for God. Jesus condemned them for having clean exteriors while being full of hypocrisy and lawlessness within.

### Example 2: Modern Social Media "Apostles"

The sermon specifically calls out people on TikTok and Facebook who build "churches" online with no divine authority. They have no real interaction with sheep, no wounds from serving, and no accountability. They blaspheme and exploit people for profit. Their condemnation is from long ago, and their destruction is not asleep. Do not follow them or support their ministries.

### Example 3: Pastor Phillip's Own Example

The preacher shares his own testimony: he is a pastor by divine calling, but he ran away from that calling for four years. He does not throw his title around, does not introduce himself as "Pastor Phillip," and does not find his identity in his office. This demonstrates the humility and authenticity that should mark true spiritual leadership.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Test every teacher against Scripture to see if they stand in the counsel of the Lord
- ✅ Use the Word of God as your weapon against deception in your mind, heart, and home
- ✅ Evaluate spiritual leaders by their fruit, not their title, clothing, or social media presence
- ✅ Keep your identity in Christ alone, not in any role or title
- ✅ Pursue authentic ministry marked by humility and service to others
- ✅ Be willing to humble yourself so that God can exalt you in His timing
- ❌ Do not follow teachers who never warn about sin and only speak blessings
- ❌ Do not give your tithes or support to people prophesying for profit
- ❌ Do not allow any human leader to replace God in your life
- ❌ Do not find your identity in your title or position
- ❌ Do not perform ministry just to be seen and praised by others

## Keep In Mind

- This is Jesus' last public sermon before His execution, and He used it to warn His people about false teachers rather than to pronounce blessings
- The most dangerous threat to the church is not the devil alone, but false prophets and false teachers who ravage the flock from within
- False teachers can sit in your church, be liked and supported by you, and still be rotting out your soul
- Titles like apostle, prophet, and pastor must be divinely appointed, not self-appointed
- The Lord is not against special seating or calling someone a spiritual father or teacher, but He is against loving those things more than Him
- True righteousness comes from Christ's imputed righteousness on the cross, not from your own behavior or religious performance

## Security & Safety Notes

- False teachers are actively ravaging churches today through social media, conferences, and books
- They exploit people with false words and prophesy for profit
- They have no accountability and no real relationship with the sheep they claim to lead
- If you follow them, they will introduce destructive heresies and lead you away from the true Christ
- Protect your soul by testing everything against Scripture and by praying for discernment
- Ask God to tear down strongholds built by false preachers in your mind
- May God anoint your ears to discern wolves among the sheep

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You follow a teacher because they are popular on social media or have a large platform
  **Solution:** Test their message against Scripture and examine their fruit. Popularity does not equal divine authority.
- **Problem:** You find your identity in your religious title or position
  **Solution:** Your identity is in Christ alone. Titles can change or be taken away. Only your identity in Him is secure.
- **Problem:** You perform spiritual duties to be seen and praised by others
  **Solution:** Examine your motives. True ministry is done in secret and for God's glory, not your own. Humility, not attention, is the mark of true greatness.
- **Problem:** You only listen to blessings and never hear warnings about sin
  **Solution:** Find teachers who stand in the counsel of the Lord, who pray and study, and who proclaim the full Word of God including its warnings.
- **Problem:** You allow a human leader to become a replacement for God in your life
  **Solution:** There is only one Father, one Instructor, one Master. No human leader should ever take the place of Christ in your heart or life.
