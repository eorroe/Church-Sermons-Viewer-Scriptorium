# Hand God the Keys and Watch Him Work

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the pathway to experiencing God's kingdom power and presence in your life is through surrendering your own will and submitting to God's will. It explains that God has both an unconditional will (purposes He will accomplish regardless of human cooperation) and a conditional will (purposes He desires to accomplish through human cooperation via prayer, submission, and obedience). You must be willing to discover, understand, submit to, and actively accomplish God's revealed will in order to partner with Him and experience His glory. The core message is that you exist for God, not the other way around, and that handing God the keys to your life allows Him to work on your behalf.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are facing a crisis, uncertainty, or a situation where you see no human help, hope, or way out
- When you are struggling to understand God's plan or purpose for your life
- When you feel disconnected from God's presence or power
- When you are tempted to rely on your own understanding or will rather than God's
- When you need to make a major life decision and want to align it with God's will
- When you are wrestling with questions like "Where were you, God?" in the midst of pain or disappointment
- When you desire to experience more of God's kingdom manifesting in your daily life
- When you want to grow in confidence that God knows what He is doing even when you do not understand His ways

## Steps

### Step 1: Surrender Your Will to God's Will

Make the conscious decision to submit your will to God's will, recognizing that you exist for Him and not the other way around. Acknowledge that God's will must supersede your will, and commit to being adjustable and willing to change your plans, desires, and preferences to align with His purposes. Pray "Not my will, but thy will be done" and mean it, understanding that this surrender is the gateway to experiencing God's kingdom power.

### Step 2: Understand the Two Wills of God

Recognize that God has an unconditional will (purposes He has determined to accomplish regardless of whether any human cooperates) and a conditional will (purposes He desires that require your active cooperation). Understand that God's unconditional will will always be accomplished, but you can choose to cooperate with or resist His conditional will. Realize that if you resist His conditional will, you may be bypassed while God accomplishes His ultimate purposes through others.

### Step 3: Discover God's Revealed Will

Seek to discover God's revealed will through Scripture. Ask God to open your eyes to see what He says about your situation, what biblical principles apply to what you are facing, and what guidance He has already provided in His Word. Do not expect to learn God's secret will (personal guidance not explicitly written in the Bible) if you are ignoring or rebelling against His revealed will.

### Step 4: Ask God for Wisdom

Pray and ask God for wisdom to understand how to handle your specific situation. Ask Him how He wants you to deal with your circumstances, what decisions to make, and how to navigate your challenges. Trust that He will give you wisdom when you ask in faith, according to James 1:5.

### Step 5: Submit to God's Will

Once you have discovered and understood God's will for your situation, make the decision to submit to it. Say "Yes, Lord" to His will, even when it is difficult, painful, or contrary to what you want. Trust that He knows better than you do, even when you do not like what He is doing or do not understand His ways.

### Step 6: Walk in and Accomplish God's Will

Take action to walk in the wisdom and direction God has given you. Make decisions based on what you have learned from Him, and act on His will in your daily life. Bear fruit in every good work and increase in the knowledge of God as you follow His leading. Continue to pray "Thy will be done" as your daily practice, making it the basis of your decisions and actions until your circumstances change or even if they do not change.

## Examples

### Example 1: Martha and Lazarus

When Lazarus died, Martha expressed her pain by asking Jesus, "Where were you?" yet she also demonstrated deep confidence by saying, "Even now, when I see no help, no hope, no victory, no way out, I believe God can still work." Jesus honored her faith and raised Lazarus from the dead, demonstrating that even in the midst of seemingly hopeless situations, God can work when you surrender to His will and trust in His power. Martha had to first submit to Jesus' instruction to move the stone before she could see the miracle, illustrating the principle that obedience to God's revealed will precedes experiencing His secret will.

### Example 2: The Hebrew Boys in the Fiery Furnace

The Hebrew boys (Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego) declared, "We know God can deliver us, but if He does not, we will still praise Him." This demonstrates total surrender and trust in God's will regardless of the outcome. They were willing to face death rather than disobey God, and they experienced God's miraculous presence in the fire. Their story shows that true submission to God's will means trusting Him even when you cannot see the outcome, and that God honors those who exist for Him rather than for their own comfort or safety.

### Example 3: Jesus' Submission to the Father's Will

Jesus demonstrated perfect submission to the Father's will, even when it meant suffering on the cross. He said, "I exist to do the Father's will," and made that the foundation of His life and ministry. He shows that true satisfaction and fulfillment come not from doing your own will, but from accomplishing the will of God.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Pray "Thy will be done" daily as a practice, making it the foundation of your decisions and perspective
- ✅ Be honest with God about your pain, confusion, and disappointment, while also trusting in His sovereignty
- ✅ Study Scripture to discover God's revealed will before seeking personal, specific guidance
- ✅ Ask God for wisdom and trust that He will give it generously when you ask in faith
- ✅ Say "Yes, Lord" to God's will even when it is difficult or contrary to your desires
- ✅ Trust that God knows what He is doing even when you do not understand or like your circumstances
- ✅ Give God the keys to every area of your life - your plans, your relationships, your finances, your future
- ✅ Remember that you exist for God's glory and purposes, not the other way around
- ✅ Worship and praise God even when He does not change your circumstances, because He is worthy regardless
- ✅ Cooperate with God's conditional will so that Heaven can be manifested on earth through your life

## Keep In Mind

- God's unconditional will will always be accomplished, regardless of whether you cooperate with Him or not
- You can cooperate with God's will or resist it, but you cannot stop Him from achieving His ultimate purposes
- If you resist God's conditional will, you may be bypassed while He accomplishes His purposes through others
- God desires a voluntary partnership with you in fulfilling His will, but He will not force you to cooperate
- Pride is the root sin that leads you to rebel against God's will and pursue your own agenda
- Satan's strategy is to make your will seem better than God's will, causing you to drift from God's plan
- You cannot know God's secret will if you are ignoring or rebelling against His revealed will
- God's will is comprehensive and all-encompassing - nothing of eternal significance sits outside of it
- God has both the right and the power to accomplish whatever He determines to do
- Even when you see no human help, no hope, no victory, and no way out, God can still work

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not use the phrase "Thy will be done" as an excuse for passivity or inaction - God expects you to actively seek, discover, understand, and accomplish His will
- Do not claim to know God's secret will for specific personal matters without first grounding yourself in His revealed will in Scripture
- Do not use God's sovereignty as a reason to ignore personal responsibility or the consequences of your choices
- Be careful not to confuse God's unconditional will with His conditional will - the former will happen regardless of you, but the latter requires your active cooperation
- Do not become fatalistic or conclude that your choices do not matter - your willingness to cooperate with God's conditional will determines whether you experience His best for your life

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Praying for God's kingdom but being unwilling to surrender your own will
  **Solution:** Make the decision to be adjustable and willing to change your plans, desires, and preferences to align with God's purposes

- **Problem:** Wanting to know God's secret will while ignoring or rebelling against His revealed will in Scripture
  **Solution:** Ground yourself in God's revealed will first, and submit to His known commands before seeking personal guidance

- **Problem:** Losing confidence in God when circumstances seem hopeless or when you do not understand what He is doing
  **Solution:** Remember that God is sovereign and knows what He is doing, even when you cannot see it. Trust Him with tears, like Martha, but do not give up on Him

- **Problem:** Letting culture, people, programs, or your own distractions prevent you from pursuing God's will
  **Solution:** Prioritize God's kingdom purposes above all else, and refuse to let anything draw you away from submitting to His will

- **Problem:** Thinking that God exists for you rather than recognizing that you exist for Him
  **Solution:** Embrace the truth that you were created for God's purposes, and make it your daily goal to seek His will above your own

- **Problem:** Resisting God's will because it conflicts with your own desires or preferences
  **Solution:** Remember that pride is the root sin that leads you to rebel, and that true satisfaction comes from doing the Father's will, not your own. Trust that God's ways are better than yours

- **Problem:** Giving up on God when He does not intervene in the way or timing you expect
  **Solution:** Like the Hebrew boys, determine to worship and praise God regardless of whether He delivers you from your circumstances, because He is worthy of your trust and devotion

- **Problem:** Assuming that because God is sovereign, your choices and cooperation do not matter
  **Solution:** Understand that while God's unconditional will will be accomplished, your willingness to cooperate with His conditional will determines whether you experience His best and are part of His kingdom work on earth
