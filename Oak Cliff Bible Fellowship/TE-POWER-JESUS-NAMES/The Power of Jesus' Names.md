# The Power of Jesus' Names: Son of God and Son of Man

## Overview

This sermon teaches that Jesus Christ equally partakes of two natures—divine and human—unmixed and resident in one person, making him both the Son of God and the Son of Man. Through the virgin birth, the Holy Spirit provided both the divine substance and divine protection so that Jesus could bear all the attributes of God and all the attributes of humanity apart from sin. This dual nature enables Jesus to fulfill a ministerial responsibility on behalf of God and on behalf of humanity, connecting heaven to earth and rendering the devil powerless over those who trust in him.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand the theological significance of Jesus being both Son of God and Son of Man
- When studying the doctrine of the hypostatic union (two natures in one person)
- When teaching about the virgin birth and why it matters
- When explaining how Jesus can be fully God and fully man without contradiction
- When preparing a message on the power and authority of Jesus' name
- When seeking to deepen your understanding of why Jesus' death and resurrection are effective
- When learning about the believer's union with Christ and the indwelling of both natures

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Meaning of "Son"

Recognize that the word "son" in Scripture does not primarily refer to biological origin from a previous source. Instead, it refers to sharing the same nature and fulfilling a particular task or role. This understanding applies both to Jesus as Son of God and Son of Man, and to ministry relationships like Paul calling Timothy "his son in the ministry."

### Step 2: Accept the Dual Natures of Christ

Believe the Bible's declaration that Jesus Christ is both the Son of God and the Son of Man equally. Accept that he has a divine nature (sharing all the attributes of God: omnipotent, omnipresent, omniscient, truthful, gracious, merciful, just, and wrathful) and a human nature (sharing all the attributes of humanity: hunger, thirst, tiredness, sleep, emotion, temptation, and death) apart from sin.

### Step 3: Understand the Virgin Birth as the Means

Study how the virgin birth made the incarnation possible. The Holy Spirit fertilized Mary's egg, providing the divine substance for Jesus' human birth, while simultaneously protecting Mary's sin nature from transferring to Jesus. This is why Joseph was not part of the process—the seed came directly from the Holy Spirit.

### Step 4: Trust the Inerrancy of Scripture

Because the Holy Spirit oversaw the writing of Scripture just as he protected Jesus from sin, you can trust the Bible as the inerrant Word of God. What the Bible declares about Jesus' nature and work is true and reliable.

### Step 5: Receive Jesus as Your Only Begotten Son

Understand that "only begotten" means uniquely one-of-a-kind, class by himself. There is only one Jesus who is both Son of God and Son of Man. This uniqueness is why there can be no other messiahs or deities—Jesus is the sole mediator between God and man.

### Step 6: Recognize Jesus' Ministerial Responsibility

Understand that Jesus not only possesses both natures but carries out a responsibility for both. As Son of God, he fulfills what the Father requires. As Son of Man, he fulfills what humanity needs. This enables him to operate as the bridge between heaven and earth.

### Step 7: Believe Jesus' Word and Expect Greater Things

When Jesus reveals something about himself or your situation through his word, believe what he says. Because you believe his word, you will see greater things—heaven opening and angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man, connecting divine resources to your earthly needs.

### Step 8: Go Get the Water

When Jesus gives you instruction or direction, act on it. Do not question the process or wait for the miracle to appear before you obey. Like the servants who filled the water jars, you must take the step of obedience even when the outcome is not yet visible.

### Step 9: Walk in the Fullness of the Kingdom

Move beyond merely entering the kingdom through salvation. Live in the kingdom atmosphere, exercising the authority and dominion Jesus has provided. Recognize that you have both the Son of God and the Son of Man operating within you, enabling you to experience both divine power and perfect human design.

### Step 10: Cultivate Intimacy with the Father

Call God "Abba Father" rather than merely a distant deity. The closer you walk with Jesus, the more intimate your relationship with the Father becomes. Do not settle for knowing God as only a powerful creator—accept his invitation to relationship as your Father.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Wedding at Cana

When Jesus' mother asked him to intervene at the wedding where the wine ran out, Jesus instructed the servants to fill six stone water jars with water. They obeyed and drew out the water, which had turned to wine. This demonstrates Step 8: the servants went and got the water (obedience before the miracle), and as a result, they experienced the Son of Man opening heaven and delivering something they could not explain. The best wine came last, showing that when you get right with Jesus, your best times can still be ahead even after you have run out.

### Example 2: Philip and Nathanael

Philip found Nathanael and told him they had found the one Moses and the prophets wrote about—Jesus of Nazareth. Nathanael was skeptical because of Jesus' hometown, but Philip invited him to "come and see." When Jesus saw Nathanael coming, he revealed that he had seen Nathanael under the fig tree before Philip called him. Nathanael then declared, "Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are the King of Israel." Because Nathanael believed Jesus' word based on this small revelation, Jesus told him he would see greater things—heaven open and angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man. This demonstrates Steps 7 and 8: believing Jesus' word leads to experiencing the greater things he promises.

### Example 3: Paul and Timothy

Paul called Timothy his "son in the ministry," though Timothy was not Paul's biological son. Timothy had been ordained by the church to minister in another location, and Paul viewed him as a son because of the ministerial function he fulfilled. This illustrates Step 1: the concept of "son" refers to function and role, not just biology. In the same way, Jesus is the Son of God and Son of Man because of the natures he possesses and the responsibilities he fulfills.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Study the names and titles of Jesus to understand the full scope of his identity and work
- ✅ Believe the whole counsel of Scripture regarding the incarnation, virgin birth, and dual natures
- ✅ Act on Jesus' word before you see the miracle—obedience precedes manifestation
- ✅ Cultivate intimacy with God the Father through the Son, calling him "Abba Father"
- ✅ Live in the fullness of the kingdom authority Jesus has provided, not just the salvation he has purchased
- ✅ Trust that the Holy Spirit protects both the person of Christ and the written Word of God from error
- ❌ Do not reduce Jesus to merely a good man or prophet—he is God in the flesh
- ❌ Do not separate the two natures of Christ—they coexist unmixed in one person
- ❌ Do not limit your experience of Christ to his deity only—benefit from his humanity as well
- ❌ Do neglect the authority and dominion that belongs to you as a kingdom citizen

## Keep In Mind

- The title "Son of God" means Jesus possesses the divine nature with all its attributes and eternality
- The title "Son of Man" means Jesus possesses the human nature with all its experiences and limitations apart from sin
- Both natures operate side by side in one person without mixing or confusion
- The virgin birth was the means by which divine and human natures were united without sin transferring
- Jesus is uniquely the only begotten Son—there is no other like him
- The Son of Man is the connecting point between heaven and earth, the new Jacob's Ladder
- Angels ascend and descend on the Son of Man, delivering heaven's resources to earth
- You have both the Son of God and the Son of Man living inside you as a believer
- The goal of God is to replicate Jesus in you—conforming you to the image of his Son
- The Holy Spirit protected Jesus from sin in his humanity at the virgin birth, just as the Holy Spirit protected the written Word of God from human error during inspiration
- You can have full confidence in both the living and written Word of God because the same Holy Spirit oversaw both
- The devil has been rendered powerless to hold any legitimate claim on believers through Christ's death and resurrection
- The only way the devil can still affect believers is through trickery—making them believe they are not free when they actually are

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking Jesus is only God and not truly human
  **Solution:** Study the incarnation and virgin birth, recognizing that Jesus experienced real human limitations like hunger, thirst, tiredness, and temptation apart from sin

- **Problem:** Thinking Jesus is only a human prophet or teacher
  **Solution:** Study the divine attributes ascribed to Jesus in Scripture and recognize that all that is true of God is true of Jesus because he shares God's nature

- **Problem:** Experiencing God as only a distant, powerful deity without intimacy
  **Solution:** Walk closely with Jesus as the Son of Man who understands your humanity, and through him, call God "Abba Father" and experience the intimate Father-child relationship Jesus has with the Father

- **Problem:** Waiting for heaven's blessing without obeying Jesus' instructions
  **Solution:** Take practical steps of obedience like filling the water jars—act on Jesus' word even when you do not yet see the result

- **Problem:** Limiting your understanding of salvation to merely going to heaven
  **Solution:** Recognize that you are saved for kingdom purpose—to live out God's kingdom on earth, exercising authority and dominion over the devil's works in your life
