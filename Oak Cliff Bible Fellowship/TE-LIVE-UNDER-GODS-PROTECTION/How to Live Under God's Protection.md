# How to Live Under God's Protection

## Overview

Dr. Tony Evans teaches that God's protection is released when believers live under covenant covering, stay aligned beneath God's authority, and face every threat with spiritual confidence that the battle belongs to the Lord. Using the story of David and Goliath from 1 Samuel 17, this sermon explains that protection comes through covenant alignment, using the specific skills, experiences, and resources God has placed in your hands, recognizing the spiritual nature of battles, and trusting that God will deliver you in His way and timing.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing a seemingly impossible obstacle or "giant" problem that feels well-fortified and threatening
- When feeling afraid, overwhelmed, or threatened by circumstances that won't go away
- When you need to understand why God's protection may appear absent and how to release it through covenant alignment
- When you want to learn how to fight spiritual battles with the right perspective and authority
- When serving in ministry or leadership and facing opposition that requires divine intervention

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize When You Are Facing a Giant

Identify that you are facing a major threat when:
- The problem looms large, is well-fortified, and feels impossible to tackle
- It controls your emotions, causing fear, dismay, and a desire to flee
- It won't leave you alone, persisting morning and evening day after day
- It threatens to hurt, defeat, or destroy you and your well-being

### Step 2: Shift Your Perspective to See the Missing Ingredient

Like David looking at Goliath and seeing an uncircumcised Philistine instead of just a 9-foot giant, you must change how you view your threat:
- Look for the spiritual source, battle, or authority behind the physical problem
- Identify what is missing in the threat's life or position that makes it vulnerable
- Remember that everyone else may see only the size of the problem, but you must see what God sees

### Step 3: Ensure You Are Under Covenant Covering

God's protection is released through covenant alignment:
- Understand that a covenant is a divinely created relational bond that administers God's kingdom rule
- Enter into covenant through salvation and baptism, which signifies alignment underneath Christ
- Stay aligned under God's authority so you operate under kingdom connection, kingdom covering, kingdom influence, and kingdom protection
- Know that one reason believers don't find the protection they want is that they operate uncovered, even if they are Christian

### Step 4: Use What God Has Placed in Your Hands

When under attack, do not rely on someone else's anointing or weapons:
- Identify the specific gifts, skills, and resources God has given you to use against the threat
- Reject Saul's armor if it doesn't fit your experience or calling
- Pick up your own slingshot - the tool God designed you to use
- Take initiative and confront the problem through prayer, action, and spiritual authority instead of running from it

### Step 5: Make the Battle Spiritual and Engage God

Shift the conflict from a physical fight to a spiritual one:
- Recognize that your enemy has a spiritual source behind the challenge, person, or circumstance
- Call upon the name of the Lord of hosts, Jehovah Sabaoth, for authority and identification
- Declare that God will deliver the threat into your hands so that all may know there is a God in Israel
- Trust that the battle belongs to the Lord and He will give victory into your hands

### Step 6: Understand That Protection Comes in Different Forms

Know that God's protection does not always mean deliverance out of trouble:
- God may deliver you out of the threat, or He may join you in it
- Like the three Hebrew boys thrown into the fiery furnace, you may face the fire, but God will be with you
- The fire may still be present, but it will no longer have control over you
- Even in suffering or death, God's promise is "God's got me" - His presence is ultimate protection

## Examples

### Example 1: David and Goliath (1 Samuel 17)

David faced a 9-foot giant that terrified the entire Israelite army. While everyone else saw an unbeatable champion, David saw an uncircumcised Philistine without covenant covering. He used his slingshot - the tool God had placed in his hands - and approached the giant on offense. He declared that the battle belonged to the Lord, and God delivered Goliath into his hands.

### Example 2: The Three Hebrew Boys (Daniel 3)

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego faced a fiery furnace for refusing to worship an idol. They declared that their God was able to deliver them, but even if He did not, they would not bow. God joined them in the fire, and the flames lost their power. They were protected not by escape, but by God's presence in the midst of the threat.

### Example 3: Personal Threats and Perseverance

Any real threat - illness, financial crisis, relationship breakdown, or persecution - can be approached with the same principles. Acknowledge the reality of the threat without dismissing it. Seek the spiritual perspective. Align yourself under covenant. Use your God-given gifts. Engage the spiritual battle. Trust God's protection, whether He delivers you out of it or walks with you through it.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Acknowledge the reality of your threat without minimizing it
- ✅ Shift your perspective to see the spiritual dimension behind major threats and obstacles
- ✅ Stay aligned under covenant through regular prayer, Bible study, worship, and obedience to Christ
- ✅ Use the specific skills, experiences, and resources God has placed in your hands
- ✅ Take initiative against the threat through prayer, action, and spiritual authority rather than running from it
- ✅ Engage the spiritual battle by calling on the name of the Lord
- ✅ Trust that God's protection may come as deliverance or as presence in the fire
- ✅ Share your testimony so others know there is a God in Israel

## Keep In Mind

- Goliath was real, and so are your threats. Do not dismiss them as imaginary.
- You can be in the kingdom but not operating under the covenant covering.
- Baptism is the covenantal act for New Testament believers to signify alignment underneath Christ.
- You cannot fight with someone else's anointing or weapons if they haven't been tested in your hands.
- The enemy wants you to serve the threat; covenant alignment means you only serve the Lord your God.
- God's protection doesn't mean the absence of threat, but the presence of God's power and covering.
- Even if God does not deliver you out of the threat, He will sustain you, transform you, and bring you through it with His presence.

## Security & Safety Notes

- This sermon is for spiritual formation and encouragement and does not replace professional counseling, medical advice, or legal assistance for serious life situations.
- Always verify scriptural teachings against the full counsel of God's Word.
- Be cautious of teachings that dismiss real problems as merely spiritual or that promise physical healing as guaranteed in this life.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Minimizing the threat by claiming it's not real or just a "spiritual attack" without addressing practical needs.
  **Solution:** Acknowledge the threat's reality while maintaining perspective that God is greater than the threat.

- **Problem:** Trying to use someone else's anointing, gifts, or methods to fight your battle.
  **Solution:** Discern what God has specifically placed in your hands to use against the threat.

- **Problem:** Believing God will always remove the threat immediately.
  **Solution:** Understand that protection comes in multiple forms - deliverance out of trouble or presence through it.

- **Problem:** Fighting the battle in the natural realm only, ignoring the spiritual dimension.
  **Solution:** Engage the spiritual battle by calling on the name of the Lord and recognizing the spiritual source behind the threat.

- **Problem:** Operating outside of covenant alignment and expecting God's protection.
  **Solution:** Ensure you are under covenant covering through salvation, baptism, and ongoing alignment with Christ.
