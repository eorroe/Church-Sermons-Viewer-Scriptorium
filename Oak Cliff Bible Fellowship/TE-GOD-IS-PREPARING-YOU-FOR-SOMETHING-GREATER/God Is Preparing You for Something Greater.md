# God Is Preparing You for Something Greater

## Overview

This sermon explores how God uses wilderness experiences, contradictory situations, and His presence to prepare and position believers for the assignments He has planned for them. Through the story of Moses and the burning bush, it reveals that encounters with God always come with a purpose and that He is the self-sufficient "I AM THAT I AM" who has not forgotten His plans for you. A wilderness season is any extended period of waiting, dryness, or unfulfilled expectation where God seems silent or delayed in His promises. A divine encounter is a direct, personal experience of God's presence where He communicates with you, reveals something about Himself, or gives you direction for your life.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are in a dry or wilderness season and need encouragement
- When you are facing contradictory situations that don't make sense
- When you want to understand how God prepares people for assignment
- When you need to be reminded that God has not forgotten His plans for you
- When you desire a deeper encounter with God beyond His provision

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize that you are already in God's vicinity

Position yourself in His presence through prayer and the Word. Set aside dedicated time daily for prayer (talking with God and listening) and study the Bible (reading Scripture to know His character and promises). This positions you to recognize Him when He calls. You must be in His presence to encounter Him.

### Step 2: Respond to contradictory situations that don't make sense

Contradictory situations are circumstances that defy natural explanation or contradict what you expected - situations that don't make logical sense but draw your attention because they seem supernatural or divinely orchestrated. These are signs you are on the precipice of a divine encounter. When life presents you with contradictions that don't align with normal patterns, pause and reflect on unusual circumstances in your life. Ask God what He might be showing you through situations that seem out of the ordinary - these are often God's invitation to encounter Him.

### Step 3: Turn aside to investigate the unusual

Don't ignore contradictions, move closer to God when they occur. Like Moses, when you see something that doesn't make sense, turn aside to investigate it further rather than walking past it.

### Step 4: Listen for God calling your name

A rhema word is a specific, personal message from God that applies general Scripture directly to your current situation. It feels like God is speaking specifically to you, not just teaching a general lesson. It is a personal word from God that speaks directly to your situation. Be sensitive to His voice calling you specifically in your current circumstance.

### Step 5: Deal with sin before approaching God

Remove what is unholy to stand on holy ground. You cannot have an encounter with God if you are unwilling to deal with sin. Repent and separate yourself from unholy practices - these include known sin in your life, unrepentant attitudes, relationships or habits that contradict God's Word, and anything you are holding onto that keeps you from fully surrendering to God. Repent and separate yourself from unholy practices to prepare for His presence.

### Step 6: Know that God is "I AM THAT I AM"

The self-sufficient One who needs nothing outside Himself. God defines Himself by Himself. He is not dependent on anything outside of Himself, and He is your all-sufficient source in every situation.

### Step 7: Trust that God has not forgotten His plans for you despite delays or wilderness seasons

Whether you have waited 2 years, 20 years, or 40 years like Moses, the same principle applies - God has not forgotten His promise. The length of the wait does not change God's faithfulness. Even though there may be a 40-year gap or longer, God has not forgotten what He planned for you. If you are still here, there is still hope for a fresh encounter with God.

### Step 8: Pursue the Person of God, not just His provision

Desire relationship with Him, not just what He can give. Many people want God's power and provision but don't want His person. Seek to know Him intimately rather than just seeking what He can do for you.

### Step 9: Be ready for the assignment that comes with the encounter

An assignment from God is a specific calling, task, or mission He gives you that aligns with His purpose for your life. It may involve ministry, career, relationships, or personal obedience. An encounter with God always comes with a purpose. When you have an encounter with God, He will give you an assignment. Be prepared to say "Here I am" and accept the mission He has for you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Moses tending sheep for 40 years after trying to deliver Israel his own way

Moses tried to deliver Israel his own way and had to flee to the wilderness. For 40 years he tended sheep, humbled and prepared. God used this wilderness season to remove the "Egypt" from him before the divine encounter at the burning bush. The assignment came after the preparation, not before it. Modern Application: A person today might experience a similar situation when they attempt to pursue a calling through their own strength, fail, and then spend years in a "desert season" of waiting before God sovereignly prepares them and calls them to the same mission on His terms.

### Example 2: The burning bush that burned but was not consumed

The bush was an ordinary object invaded by supernatural presence. God spoke to Moses from an ordinary bush that was on fire but not consumed. This contradictory situation caught Moses' attention and led to a divine encounter where God revealed Himself and gave Moses his assignment to deliver Israel from Egypt. Modern Application: A person today might notice a random opportunity, an unexpected conversation, or a set of circumstances that seem too coincidental to ignore - like a job posting that appears exactly when they were praying for direction, or a stranger saying the exact words they needed to hear - and recognize it as God's invitation to encounter Him.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Set aside dedicated time daily for prayer and Bible study to position yourself for encounter
- ✅ When you notice something unusual that doesn't fit normal patterns, pause and ask God what He might be showing you
- ✅ Deal with sin honestly to prepare for God's presence
- ✅ Listen for God's personal call on your life
- ✅ Pursue relationship with God above His blessings
- ❌ Don't ignore the unusual or contradictory in your life
- ❌ Don't try to do God's work your own way
- ❌ Don't want God's power without knowing His person
- ❌ Don't let past failures or long waits make you give up

## Keep In Mind

- God had a 40-year gap between calling Moses and the burning bush encounter
- Moses was already at the mountain of God before the supernatural manifestation
- The bush was an ordinary object invaded by supernatural presence
- Holy ground is where you stand, not necessarily the miraculous thing you see
- If there is nothing in your life that only God can do, you will forever be tending sheep - "only God can do" refers to situations, callings, or transformations that are impossible through human effort alone - situations where your own strength, resources, or connections cannot accomplish what God has planned
- Whether you have waited 2 years, 20 years, or 40 years like Moses, the same principle applies - God has not forgotten His promise. The length of the wait does not change God's faithfulness.

- Always verify teachings against Scripture
- Apply biblical principles with wisdom and discernment

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Missing divine encounters by ignoring contradictory situations
  **Solution:** Turn aside and investigate unusual circumstances in your life

- **Problem:** Wanting God's provision without pursuing His presence
  **Solution:** Seek the Person of God first, not just what He can do for you

- **Problem:** Trying to fulfill God's assignment before having an encounter with Him
  **Solution:** Allow God to prepare you in the wilderness before the assignment
