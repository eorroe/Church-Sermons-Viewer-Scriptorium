# When God Speaks We Must Listen

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God is speaking through the chaos and turmoil of our world, and we must stop listening to news media, social media, political commentary, and secular human wisdom long enough to hear His voice. Pastor Tony Evans explains that when God's written Word is not enough for a culture that has turned away from Him, God allows or uses disruptive events—such as hurricanes, political upheaval, and social unrest—to get our attention and bring correction to people and nations. The central message is that we must start with the spiritual reality of God's written Word rather than just reacting to the visible circumstances we see in the news.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel overwhelmed by cultural chaos, news cycles, or worldly turmoil and need God's perspective
- When you have been trusting in news media, social media, political commentary, or secular wisdom more than Scripture
- When God has allowed a significant interruption in your life, family, church, or community and you need to understand its spiritual purpose

## Steps

### Step 1: Start With the Unseen, Not the Seen

Begin with the Bible (God's written Word) rather than what you can see, touch, feel, or hear in the physical world. Paul says in 2 Corinthians 4:18 that Christians do not start with what is seen but with what is unseen. If you only start with your physical senses, you will miss what God is doing.

### Step 2: Zero In on the One Who Knows What He Is Talking About

When you look at what is happening in the world, stop watching pundits, news media, and social commentary. Fix your attention on God, who sovereignly knows and controls all that happens. You and I are merely observers, but God is sovereign over all events.

### Step 3: Understand That God Speaks Through Shaking

God's primary way of speaking is His written Word, but when people do not listen, He speaks by allowing or orchestrating upheaval. Hebrews 12:25-29 teaches that God shakes the earth and heaven to remove things that can be shaken so that the things which cannot be shaken may remain. When God shakes, disturbs, or interrupts the natural order, He is communicating.

### Step 4: Don't Refuse the One Who Is Speaking

Hebrews 12:25 says, "See to it that you do not refuse him who is speaking." When God allows an interruption—a hurricane, a political upheaval, a social crisis—do not assume it is merely a natural storm, merely an election, or merely a social conflict. Recognize that God is trying to tell you something. If you refuse to hear Him, you will be like those who ignored God's warning through His messengers and missed the opportunity to escape judgment.

### Step 5: Ask God What He Is Saying

When your world is shaken, ask: "What are you doing? What are you trying to tell me? What are you trying to teach me? What are you trying to clarify?" A funeral, a natural disaster, or a social crisis can be an opportunity God uses to disturb your natural existence and show you something eternal. Look for the spiritual purpose behind the physical event.

### Step 6: Respond to God's Correction Personally

Start with yourself and the community of believers before trying to fix the world. God is trying to correct the fact that the physical has trumped the spiritual. Let spiritual matters be more important than physical matters. When you respond to God's correction in your own life, He can use you to affect a world in chaos.

### Step 7: Recognize There Are No Impersonal Forces

There is no such thing as impersonal forces of nature, luck, chance, or fate. Job 37:3-13, Jeremiah 10:13, Lamentations 3:31, and Amos 4:6-8 all show that God commands the lightning, rain, snow, and wind. Scripture teaches that creation obeys God's commands; historical storms like Harvey and Katrina demonstrate this truth. Whether for correction, for the world, or for loving-kindness, He purposes these events.

## Examples

### Example 1: Hurricane Harvey

When Hurricane Harvey devastated Texas, people sought out meteorologists because they wanted inside information about what was coming. Pastor Evans suggests that just as people turned to meteorologists because they had specialized knowledge about that sphere, when you look at world chaos you need to turn to God, who really knows what is happening. The hurricane was an example of God using upheaval to get people's attention back to Him.

### Example 2: Charlottesville and Racism

When the racial tensions in Charlottesville erupted, people were divided, taking sides, and fighting about color, class, and culture. But when Hurricane Harvey hit shortly after, people found themselves united by shared need rather than divided by racial or cultural differences. In one event, God addressed racism, separatism, and division—showing that when He moves, He can unite people in a moment, without waiting generations for cultural change, seminars, or workshops.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do start with God's written Word before you look at the news, politics, or circumstances
- ✅ Do ask God what He is saying when your world is shaken or interrupted
- ✅ Do recognize that God is behind the events of history and your personal life
- ✅ Do respond to God's correction personally before trying to fix society
- ✅ Do let the spiritual trump the physical in your priorities
- ❌ Don't put your confidence in the news media more than the Word of God
- ❌ Don't put your confidence in politics more than the power of Scripture
- ❌ Don't assume disasters, storms, or upheavals are impersonal or random
- ❌ Don't refuse God when He interrupts your regular programming
- ❌ Don't start with what you see, feel, or experience instead of what God says

## Keep In Mind

- Nothing happens that is not either caused by God or allowed by God for His purposes—there is no luck, chance, or happenstance
- God is sovereign over all creation, including weather, governments, and social movements
- When God allows upheaval, it is always for the purpose of correction and bringing spiritual priorities back to their rightful place
- The community of believers must get right with God before the world will listen to us
- God is not a distant or optional deity; He will make His presence known whether we acknowledge Him willingly or through forced circumstances
- The pain and discomfort of God's discipline are like labor pains—separation is occurring so new life can come
- Always verify biblical claims by checking Scripture yourself rather than relying solely on a sermon

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You look only at what you see in the news and miss what God is doing behind the scenes
  **Solution:** Start every circumstance with the question "What is God saying?" before forming an opinion
- **Problem:** You assume natural disasters, political upheaval, or social chaos are impersonal forces or random events
  **Solution:** Remember that God commands the weather and controls history; nothing is outside His sovereign purpose
- **Problem:** You refuse God when He shakes your world because the discomfort is too painful
  **Solution:** Like unpleasant medicine is messy but necessary to expel poison, God's shaking is uncomfortable but necessary to bring correction and healing
- **Problem:** You try to fix the world before you have submitted to God's correction in your own life
  **Solution:** Get right with God personally and in your church first; then God will use you to affect the world
- **Problem:** You let politics, race, or culture divide you instead of seeking God's unity
  **Solution:** Remember that God is not on any political side; He is the King of kings with His own agenda of spiritual correction
