# Finding Victory Over Satan's Tricks and Traps

## Overview

This sermon teaches how Satan's initial deception of Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden established a pattern of tricks and traps that continues to affect every area of human life today. Pastor Tony Evans explains that sin produces death—not as an end, but as a separation from God—and that this death manifests in four distinct forms: spiritual, emotional, relational, and physical or economic. The sermon exists to help believers recognize these deceptive patterns, understand their origin, and find the hope and victory available through Jesus Christ, the "last Adam," who reverses the curse and enables believers to reign in life rather than be ruled by sin and death.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel spiritually disconnected from God despite attending church or participating in religious activities
- When you are experiencing emotional turmoil, guilt, shame, or fear that dominates your life
- When you are facing ongoing conflict in your marriage, family, or close relationships
- When you are struggling with financial hardship, career frustration, or economic setbacks that reflect the curse of thorns and thistles
- When you want to understand the root cause of recurring sin and how to break free from its cycle
- When you need hope that your situation can change through the power of Jesus Christ
- When you are ready to stop hiding behind good works or religious routines and face the truth of your condition before God

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That Death Means Separation, Not Cessation

Understand that biblical death is not the end of existence but the result of separation. Sin always produces death, and this death begins the moment sin occurs. Adam and Eve did not die physically the day they ate the fruit, but they died spiritually because their relationship with God was broken. This same principle applies to every person who sins: at the moment of sin, a separation occurs.

### Step 2: Identify the Four Types of Death at Work in Your Life

Examine your life for evidence of the four deaths that result from sin:
- **Spiritual death:** A disconnected or distant relationship with God, going through religious motions without true fellowship
- **Emotional death:** Guilt, shame, fear, anxiety, or emotional instability resulting from unconfessed sin or unresolved guilt
- **Relational death:** Conflict, control struggles, or broken relationships in marriage, family, or community, stemming from rebellion against God's order
- **Physical or economic death:** Frustration, hardship, toil, and financial burden resulting from disobedience to God's instructions

### Step 3: Stop Hiding Behind Fig Leaves

Like Adam and Eve, people try to cover their shame with substitutes—religion, popularity, entertainment, medication, relationships, or busyness. These fig leaves only mask the problem but never solve it. God sees through every substitute. Stop running from God and from the truth of your condition. Confess that you have been hiding, and come out into the open before Him.

### Step 4: Answer God's Three Questions Honestly

When God comes looking for you, He asks three questions designed to bring self-examination, not because He lacks information. Ask yourself honestly:
- Where are you?
- Who told you that you were naked or exposed?
- What is this you have done?
Do not deflect, minimize, or blame others. Adam blamed Eve, and Eve blamed the serpent. Own your sin and your rebellion against God's revealed will.

### Step 5: Understand That the Issue Is Deity, Not Just Behavior

Sin is not merely about breaking rules. It is about rejecting God's authority and asserting your own independence. Satan's original temptation was not about a piece of fruit; it was about becoming like God, knowing good and evil, and operating autonomously from Him. Every sin is fundamentally a god issue: you are choosing to be your own god rather than submitting to the living God.

### Step 6: Receive the Hope of the Last Adam

The only answer to the death caused by the first Adam is the life offered by the last Adam, Jesus Christ. Jesus came to crush the head of the serpent and to reverse the curse. Through faith in Him, you are no longer under the reign of death. You can reign in life through Jesus Christ. This means your emotions, circumstances, relationships, and physical life come under His lordship and are restored.

### Step 7: Live as a Kingdom Man or Woman Under Christ's Authority

Stop letting human relationships or personal desires overrule God's commands. If you are married, understand that the closest human relationship does not override your submission to God. Men are responsible before God to lead their households according to God's order, not their wives' wishes or their own preferences. Women are called to support that God-ordained order rather than seeking to usurp it. Both are called to submit to Christ first.

### Step 8: Practice Honest Self-Examination Daily

Do not wait for crisis to force you to face your condition. Regularly ask God to show you where you are hiding, where you have been deceived, and where you are still fig-leafing your way through life. Bring everything into the light. Confession and repentance are the pathways to restored fellowship and victory.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Married Couple Battling Over Everything

A husband and wife fight constantly over small decisions—what temperature to set the thermostat, how to spend money, how to raise their children, and even what to eat for dinner. Every conversation escalates into World War III. The wife wants to control the home because of a controlling upbringing, and the husband reacts by asserting a harsh, domineering authority that feels abusive. This curse did not start with their arguments. It started when both of them placed their human relationship above God's order, leading to relational death. The solution is not better communication techniques alone but returning both spouses to rightful submission to Christ, recognizing that their marriage is dying because of separation from God's design, not merely personality conflicts. When both submit to Christ first, the curse is broken and the relationship can be restored.

### Example 2: The Overwhelmed Professional

A person works long hours, earns a good salary, and appears successful to the outside world, but inside feels anxious, empty, and disconnected from God. They medicate with busyness, entertainment, and occasional alcohol to numb the feeling that something is wrong. They attend church on Sundays but avoid genuine fellowship because they are wearing fig leaves—trying to look like they have it together. They are experiencing spiritual death, which has led to emotional death and leads to relational and economic death. The sermon teaches that their productivity has been interrupted by the thorns and thistles of disobedience. The solution is to stop hiding, to come out from behind the fig leaves of performance and appearance, to answer honestly before God about their condition, and to receive the life that Jesus offers as the last Adam.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Confess sin and rebellion honestly before God rather than hiding, minimizing, or blaming others
- ✅ Regularly examine your heart and relationships for evidence of spiritual, emotional, relational, or economic death
- ✅ Submit every area of your life—marriage, career, emotions, relationships—to the lordship of Jesus Christ
- ✅ Recognize that Jesus Christ, the last Adam, has defeated Satan and offers you victory and abundant life
- ✅ Allow God's Word to expose your condition rather than relying on your own perception or the approval of others
- ❌ Don't attend church or perform religious activities while remaining spiritually disconnected from God
- ❌ Don't blame others for your sin or disobedience, just as Adam blamed Eve and Eve blamed the serpent
- ❌ Don't think that a good-looking exterior or respectable reputation covers the spiritual death inside
- ❌ Don't assume that emotional distress or relationship conflict is only a psychological or communication problem when it is rooted in spiritual separation
- ❌ Don't let your closest human relationship override your obedience to God, no matter how much you love that person

## Keep In Mind

- Death in the Bible means separation, not cessation of existence
- Every sin produces death at the moment it occurs
- Spiritual death precedes emotional, relational, and physical death
- Adam and Eve's fig leaves represent every human attempt to cover shame without dealing with sin
- God asks questions to promote self-examination, not because He lacks information
- The root of sin is not bad behavior but rebellion against God's authority
- Jesus Christ is the last Adam who came to reverse the curse and give you life abundantly
- Reigning in life means your emotions, circumstances, and relationships are under Christ's control, not the other way around
- The cherubim and flaming sword guarded the Tree of Life after the Fall, showing that access to God was cut off by sin—but Jesus has now reopened that access

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not attempt to overcome spiritual death or Satan's deception through human effort, religion, or willpower alone
- The only safe covering for sin is the righteousness of Jesus Christ, not fig leaves of your own making
- Hidden sin creates spiritual separation that opens you to further deception and destruction
- If you are experiencing abusive relationships, remember that abuse is never God's design, but healing begins with submission to Christ, not retaliation
- Genuine repentance brings forgiveness and restoration, but superficial confession without true heart change will not produce victory
- Satan's goal is to keep you disconnected from God by keeping you busy, religious, or successful-looking on the outside while remaining dead on the inside

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Getting stuck on the fruit (the specific sin) and missing the root issue (rebellion against God's authority)
  **Solution:** Ask God to show you the deity issue behind your behavior. Every sin is ultimately about whether you will submit to God or be your own god.
- **Problem:** Using good things—church, counseling, relationships, medication, work—as hiding places rather than turning to God
  **Solution:** Recognize when you are using God's blessings or good resources as fig leaves. Come out from hiding and face God directly.
- **Problem:** Blaming others for your sin instead of owning it, just as Adam blamed Eve and Eve blamed the serpent
  **Solution:** When God asks you where you are, answer honestly. Do not deflect. Own your disobedience and your rebellion.
- **Problem:** Treating relationship conflict or emotional distress as merely psychological or relational problems without recognizing the spiritual root
  **Solution:** Examine whether the conflict stems from spiritual separation and rebellion against God's order. Seek reconciliation with God first, then with others.
- **Problem:** Trying to patch together a solution with your own effort rather than receiving the finished work of Christ
  **Solution:** Stop sewing fig leaves. Accept that only Jesus Christ can restore what sin has broken. You cannot fix yourself; only He can.
- **Problem:** Believing that being a "good person" or being active in church compensates for unconfessed sin and spiritual separation
  **Solution:** God's standard is not being nice or religious. His standard is perfect fellowship with Him through Christ. Come out of hiding and receive the life He offers.
