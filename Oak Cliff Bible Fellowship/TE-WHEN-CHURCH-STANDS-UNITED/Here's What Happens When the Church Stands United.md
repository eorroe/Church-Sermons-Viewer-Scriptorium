# Here's What Happens When the Church Stands United

## Overview

This sermon teaches that true kingdom power is released when believers resist division and stand together in their identity in Christ. Dr. Tony Evans explains that Christians are called to be reconcilers who operate by a different value system than the world, because they belong to a new kingdom and a new race through Jesus Christ. The message reveals that Satan's primary strategy is to divide people—starting in the mind and spreading to families, churches, and nations—because he knows God's preferred work happens in unified environments. When the church pursues biblical unity, God commands a blessing and His power is manifested. The cross of Jesus Christ serves as the ultimate emulsifier that brings together what could not naturally connect, enabling believers to reconcile across racial, social, and cultural divides.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you see division and divisiveness in your family, church, or community and want to understand how to respond biblically
- When you are struggling with conflict in your home, marriage, or parent-child relationships
- When you want to understand why racial, class, and political division continues to plague society
- When you feel called to be a reconciler but don't know how to practically live out that calling

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand That Christians Are Called to Be Reconcilers

Recognize that you and I as Christians should be reconcilers. We operate by a whole different value system because we have a brand new identity. Now we're part of a new kingdom. Now we belong to a new race because we belong to the kingdom of God. This is not optional—it is the fundamental calling of every believer. Because we want to stand and have His power manifested in us, through us, for us, and by us, we must embrace our identity as reconcilers rather than contributors to division.

### Step 2: Transform Your Mind to Think Biblically

The Bible calls for the transformation of the mind. This means learning to think biblically, learning to think kingdom-wise, learning to think Christocentrically. It is about a spiritually informed way of thinking, a biblically centered way of thought. Because Satan's power is when he gets to your mind, you must renew your mind with the word of God so that whatever controls your mind will guide your feet in the right direction. Your spiritual battle is between your two ears—Satan gets into your mind and changes how you think and function. Renewing your mind with Scripture is how you defeat this strategy and walk in the unity God calls you to.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Tower of Babel

In Genesis 11, God told humanity to spread out over the whole world, but they said, "We're not doing that. We want to become one civilization." They built a city and a tower to reach heaven, unifying without God to do their own thing. God came down and said, "If we don't stop this, there will be nothing impossible for man to do." Because man had turned over the earth to Satan, and if they kept unifying without God, they would destroy the planet. So God disrupted their attempt at unity by creating different languages—that's why it's called the Tower of Babel. The lesson is that God will disrupt any attempt at unity that excludes Him, and He will also disrupt Satan's attempts to divide His people.

### Example 2: The Cross as the Emulsifier

Dr. Evans explains that the cross of Christ is like an emulsifier. Oil and water don't get along. You can mix them, stir them, and try to force them, but they separate because their components just can't get along. The cross is the egg that grabs the oil and grabs the water and says, "Y'all come over here." The blood of Jesus Christ brings together what could not naturally connect. Ephesians 2:13 says, "But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ." Whether it's a marriage conflict, racial division, or family strife, the cross is what brings divided people together. This illustrates how Christ's sacrifice enables unity among people who naturally could not get along.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Embrace your identity as a new creation in Christ who belongs to a new kingdom and a new race
- ✅ Recognize that spiritual issues are at the root of social, cultural, political, and economic problems
- ✅ Pursue biblical unity, which is uniqueness headed in the same direction, not uniformity
- ✅ Keep your focus on the kingdom goal rather than getting distracted by differences
- ✅ Place your Christianity in the adjectival position and your culture in the noun position
- ✅ Transform your mind by learning to think biblically, kingdom-wise, and Christ-centrically
- ✅ Bind the strong man (Satan) in your mind, home, family, and church through the authority of Jesus Christ
- ✅ Remember that Satan's power is broken at the cross—he no longer legitimately holds you hostage
- ✅ Pursue unity in your home, church, and community so that God can command a blessing

- Your spiritual battle is between your two ears—Satan gets into your mind and changes how you think and function

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Joining the illegitimate divisions of culture, race, gender, or politics that are not rooted in kingdom values
  **Solution:** Remember that you now operate by a whole different value system. You have a new identity, new commitment, and new authority in Christ. Do not take cultural sides that advance division rather than kingdom unity.

- **Problem:** Confusing biblical unity with uniformity—expecting everyone to be the same
  **Solution:** Understand that biblical unity is uniqueness headed in the same direction, not everyone being identical. Differences are allowed and even celebrated as long as everyone is moving toward the same kingdom goal under Christ.

- **Problem:** Allowing Satan to divide your mind, home, family, or church through wrong thinking
  **Solution:** Your spiritual battle is between your two ears. Transform your mind by learning to think biblically, kingdom-wise, and Christ-centrically. Renew your mind with the word of God so that your thoughts and actions align with kingdom principles.

- **Problem:** Giving Satan permission to keep what he has stolen in your life
  **Solution:** Satan has already been disarmed and defeated at the cross. He no longer legitimately holds you hostage unless you give him permission. Bind the strong man in your mind, home, and circumstances through the authority of Jesus Christ and take back what the enemy has stolen.

- **Problem:** Focusing on differences and obstacles rather than the kingdom goal
  **Solution:** Satan makes us so focused on our differences that we don't even see the goal. Keep your eyes on Jesus and the kingdom purposes of God. When you focus on what unites you in Christ, division loses its power.

- **Problem:** Abandoning a biblical standard or kingdom focus to fit in with culture
  **Solution:** When the church abandons biblical standards and kingdom focus, it becomes ineffective, like an ant hill on the grass. Stand firm in your identity in Christ and refuse to compromise kingdom values for cultural acceptance. God's power is released when the church pursues biblical unity.
