# How The Devil Uses Disunity To Defeat Believers

## Overview

This sermon teaches that unity is not uniformity but oneness of purpose amid God-given uniqueness. It exposes the devil's primary strategy of creating disunity in individuals, families, and churches, and shows why Satan's tactic works against the very nature of God. By understanding the Trinity as the model of perfect harmony and applying biblical principles from 1 Corinthians 1 and 1 Peter 3:7, believers learn to fight for the unity of the faith so that God's power can be experienced and Satan is thwarted.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When your church or small group is experiencing division, quarrels, or personality-based cliques
- When you feel confused about your identity or sense disunity within yourself
- When family conflict, disconnection, or competing agendas are disrupting your household
- When you want to understand why disunity is a spiritual battle rather than just a relational issue
- When you need biblical clarity on what true unity looks like compared to mere uniformity

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand What Unity Is and Is Not

Unity is not sameness. Unity has to do with same purpose within the uniqueness God has given each person. Unity always involves uniqueness, but it is a variety of uniquenesses headed toward the same purpose. The absence or presence of unity has everything to do with the absence or presence of God in the midst of his people. Do not confuse unity with uniformity. A football team is unified not because everyone plays the same position, but because everyone is headed to the same goal line. An orchestra is harmonious not because every musician plays the same instrument, but because they all play the same song. The sameness is not in who you are; the sameness is in where you are going.

### Step 2: Recognize the Devil's Strategy of Disunity

The devil's primary tactic is disunity. His goal is to destabilize believers through separation. Satan first disunified the angels, getting one-third to rebel against God. He then disunified mankind from God by deceiving Adam and Eve. He disunified the first family by bringing conflict between Adam and Eve, and then between Cain and Abel. This is his consistent modus operandi. He wants to disunify you with yourself by creating identity confusion. He wants to disunify your family by amplifying small issues into large ones. He wants to disunify the church by making secondary matters like race, culture, class, education, or personalities override the cause of Christ. The devil is the amplification master. He can take a small disagreement and blow it up so that it becomes bigger than the gospel.

### Step 3: Apply the Trinity as the Model for Unity

The nature of the Christian God is triune. There is one God composed of three co-equal persons who are distinct in personhood yet one in essence. The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit work in perfect harmony. They are like a pretzel with three holes: the first hole is not the second hole, and the second hole is not the third hole, but all three holes together make one pretzel because they are tied together by the same dough. This is the model for human unity. Whenever there is disharmony or lack of unity, the devil knows that God cannot bless that situation. The Father turned away from the Son on the cross because of sin, demonstrating that God turns away from disunity because his very nature requires perfect harmony. Therefore, pursuing unity is pursuing the very nature of God.

### Step 4: Act to Restore and Maintain Unity

When disunity is present, take practical steps to restore harmony. First, check for competing goals and agendas. Are you or others crafting your own direction instead of pulling in the one direction God has set? Second, refuse to let secondary issues override the cause of Christ. The ground is level at the cross. It does not matter what your economic status is, what your education level is, or what your notoriety is. You are just a sinner saved by grace. Third, fight for the unity of the faith. You must fight for the unity of the church because only in its unity will Satan be thwarted and God's power be experienced. Fourth, remember that disunity blocks answered prayer. 1 Peter 3:7 states that when there is disharmony in the life of a couple, their prayers are hindered. Disharmony blocks answered prayer. This is no small issue.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Corinthian Church Divided by Personalities

The church at Corinth had a multiplicity of problems, but Paul attacked their lack of agreement first. Some said "I am of Paul," others said "I am of Apollos," others said "I am of Cephus," and still others said "I am of Christ." They developed schisms based on personalities, backgrounds, and spiritual pride. Paul did not tell them to start a Paul church, an Apollos church, a Cephus church, or even a Christ church. He exhorted them to be of the same mind and the same judgment. The solution was not more churches based on preferences, but unity in the same purpose.

### Example 2: A Family Fighting Over Small Issues

A married couple begins arguing about something small, perhaps finances, in-laws, or household responsibilities. The devil amplifies the issue until it becomes bigger than the cause of Christ in their marriage. One or both spouses consider divorce. What they are fighting about has been made bigger than their shared purpose in Christ. The solution is to remember that the ground is level at the cross. Both are sinners saved by grace. They must fight for the unity of their marriage by refusing to let a secondary issue override their primary purpose as one flesh before God.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Define unity as oneness of purpose, not sameness of people or methods
- ✅ Use the Trinity as the model: distinct persons, one essence, perfect harmony
- ✅ Refuse to let secondary issues like race, culture, class, education, or personalities override the cause of Christ
- ✅ Remember that the ground is level at the cross regardless of status or background
- ✅ Fight for unity in your family, church, and personal life as a spiritual discipline
- ✅ Keep your focus on the goal line rather than your own position or preferences
- ✅ Check for competing agendas that pull people away from the shared purpose

## Keep In Mind

- Unity always involves uniqueness. God created you uniquely, and true unity requires your distinct contribution toward the same goal.
- Disunity is not a small issue. It blocks answered prayer and causes God to turn away, just as the Father turned away from the Son on the cross due to sin.
- Satan's MO is disunity. He first attacked the angels, then mankind, then the first family, and he continues to attack families and churches today.
- Putting the label "Christian" over disunity does not make it right. Even singing scripture out of step with the conductor creates chaos.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Guard your heart against identity confusion. If you are confused about who you are, others who follow you will also be confused. Know your identity in Christ.
- Do not allow disunity to become a pattern in your family or church. Destabilization through ongoing conflict opens the door for further spiritual attack.
- Be aware that the devil uses pseudo-spirituality and religious language to disguise pride and create division. Examine motives when someone claims to be "more spiritual" than others.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Creating separate churches or groups based on preferences, backgrounds, or personalities instead of pursuing unity in diversity.
  **Solution:** Remember that Paul did not tell the Corinthians to start separate churches. He called them to be unified in the same mind and judgment despite their differences.
- **Problem:** Treating secondary issues as more important than the cause of Christ, allowing race, culture, class, or education to divide believers.
  **Solution:** Keep the main thing the main thing. The ground is level at the cross. Secondary issues should never override the shared purpose of advancing the gospel.
- **Problem:** Thinking that singing the same songs, using the same methods, or looking the same is what makes a church unified.
  **Solution:** Unity is about direction, not uniformity. Ask whether everyone is heading toward the same goal, not whether everyone looks or acts the same.
- **Problem:** Ignoring disunity in marriage or family because it feels like a small issue.
  **Solution:** Understand that disharmony blocks answered prayer and distances you from the Father. Fight for unity as if your spiritual life depends on it, because it does.
