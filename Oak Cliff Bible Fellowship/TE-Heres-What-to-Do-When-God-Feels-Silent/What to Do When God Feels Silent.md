# What to Do When God Feels Silent

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God is light with no darkness at all, and that genuine fellowship with Him only happens when we walk in His light. Tony Evans explains that God's light exposes our impurity not to condemn us, but to cleanse us through the blood of Jesus Christ, deepening our personal, day-to-day fellowship with God and growing in daily fellowship with God and obeying His will.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel distant from God and sense a lack of spiritual intimacy
- When you actively participate in or tolerate unconfessed sin or recurring sinful behaviors in your life
- When you need to understand the difference between confession and repentance

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize that God is light and has no darkness at all

Understand that God is pure light with not even the smallest element of darkness. He only operates in light and will not be found in any place of moral, spiritual, or emotional darkness where truth is avoided. This is foundational to increasing spiritual intimacy with Him.

### Step 2: Walk in the light by giving God freedom to expose your sin

Turn on the light in your life and invite God to show you the pride, racism, jealousy, hatred, and other hidden sins in your heart. Say to the Lord, "Show me my prideful ways, my hatred, my evil heart." Allow His light to expose impurity so that you can be cleansed and maintain fellowship with Him.

### Step 3: Do not deny, reject, or hide from what God reveals

When the light exposes specific sins, attitudes, or behaviors that conflict with God's character, affirm it rather than running from it. Believers live in darkness when they do not give God the freedom to show them the pride, racism, jealousy, hatred, and other hidden sins in their hearts. If you deny what the light reveals, you move to darkness where God does not operate.

### Step 4: Confess the specific sins God reveals

Confess means to agree with God, to say the same thing He says about your sin. When God shows you a specific attitude or action that conflicts with God's revealed character as light, agree with Him that it is sin. Confession is tied to a specific revealed act, not always a recurring pattern that requires a change of direction.

### Step 5: Understand the distinction between confession and repentance

Confession is agreeing with God about what He has revealed to you. Repentance is turning away from a recurring sinful behavior or habit that you have tolerated over time. You need confession to get to repentance, but your confession does not always need repentance if you agree with God about the specific sin and do not continue in it until it becomes a recurring habit.

### Step 6: Trust Jesus as your advocate and defense attorney

Jesus Christ is our advocate (paracletos) with the Father. Satan is the prosecuting attorney who presents our sins before God to disrupt or destroy the close, shared relationship and communion with God. Jesus, the Son of the Judge, is righteous and has no sin, and He defends us against Satan's legitimate accusations.

### Step 7: Receive cleansing through the blood of Jesus Christ

When you confess your sins, the blood of Jesus Christ is applied, put to work, or made effective to cleanse you from all sin. 1 John 1:7 promises that walking in the light as He is in the light, the blood of Jesus cleanses us from all sin. 1 John 1:9 adds that if you confess, He is faithful and just to forgive your sins and cleanse you from all unrighteousness, including the sins you are not aware of.

### Step 8: Maintain fellowship by staying in the light

Continue to walk in the light so that the blood of Jesus keeps doing what it is designed to do: cleanse you from sin and maintain your fellowship with God. God wants to have fellowship with you and grow your intimacy. He will cover the sins you have not yet recognized if you are willing to walk in the light with Him.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Legal Courtroom - Satan as Prosecuting Attorney vs. Jesus as Defense Attorney

Satan functions as the prosecuting attorney who presents our sins before God in order to deny us fellowship because he knows God is pure. He brings legitimate charges against us, as seen in passages like Zechariah 3, Job 1, and Revelation 12:10. However, we have a defense attorney: Jesus Christ the righteous. Jesus paid the bill for our sins on the cross, satisfying God's wrath and propitiating the Father. The Son of the Judge is your defense attorney, and He has no sin.

### Example 2: The Unpayable Bill Paid by Another

Our sin is a debt we could never repay before a perfect, holy God. But Jesus came and paid the full price that we owed. Once Jesus paid the bill, the creditor is satisfied (propitiated), not because we paid it, but because Jesus paid it even though it was our bill. This is the good news of the gospel: you don't have to pay for your sins because Jesus has already paid the full price. His propitiation covers the sins of the whole world, making every human being eligible for salvation through faith in Christ.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do walk in the light and give God permission to show you specific sins, attitudes, or behaviors that conflict with God's character
- ✅ Do confess specific sins when God reveals them to you
- ✅ Do agree with God about the pride, racism, jealousy, hatred, and other specific hidden sins in your heart because He sees what you cannot see
- ✅ Do remember that Jesus is your advocate who defends you against Satan's accusations
- ✅ Do trust that the blood of Jesus cleanses from all sin when you walk in the light
- ❌ Don't say you have no sin, for you deceive yourself and the truth is not in you
- ❌ Don't ignore or reject what God's light exposes in your life
- ❌ Don't hide from God like Adam and Eve when confronted with sin
- ❌ Don't expect God to help you while you walk in darkness
- ❌ Don't run from the light because you do not want to hear how God feels about areas where you resist His conviction or correction

## Keep In Mind

- God is light and in Him is no darkness at all - there is not even a trace of moral, spiritual, or ethical darkness when it comes to God
- You will never find God in the dark; you will only find Him in the light
- If you claim fellowship with God but walk in darkness, you are a liar because you are operating in a realm where He does not exist
- The light exposes impurity but has no impurity itself - sins, impurities, and hidden motives in your heart are revealed by the light, not because of the light
- Darkness allows impurity and prevents you from seeing yourself, your sin, and God's standard clearly as they actually exist
- Believers live in darkness when they do not give God the freedom to show them the pride, racism, jealousy, hatred, and other specific hidden sins in their hearts.
- If you regard iniquity in your heart, the Lord will not hear you (Psalm 66:18)
- He who covers his sin shall not prosper (Proverbs 28:13)

## Security & Safety Notes

- Believers run from the light when they do not want to hear how God feels about areas where they resist His conviction.
- "Darkness sermons" that only emphasize God's love, kindness, and forgiveness without His holiness keep people in darkness
- If you only hear preaching that only emphasizes God's love and forgiveness while never confronting sin, you are hearing darkness sermons
- God won't help you while you walk in darkness because He doesn't hang out in places of moral and spiritual darkness
- You cannot ask God to help you overcome a specific sin, struggle, or pattern that needs to be overcome if you are operating outside of the light where God does not dwell or work

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking you have no sin, which deceives yourself and makes the truth absent from you
  **Solution:** Humble yourself and acknowledge that you are more messed up than you know, allowing God's light to expose the pride, racism, jealousy, and other specific hidden sins like pride, racism, jealousy, or hatred that you were not aware of

- **Problem:** Running from the light when God exposes impurity because it feels uncomfortable
  **Solution:** Remember that the light exposes impurity to cleanse you, not to condemn you. The exposure is good news because it means God wants to have fellowship with you and grow your intimacy

- **Problem:** Confusing confession with repentance and feeling condemned when a pattern emerges
  **Solution:** Confession is simply agreeing with God about a specific revealed sin. Repentance is reversing a pattern. Agree with God about the specific sin He revealed without denying, minimizing, or making excuses for it so it doesn't become a pattern requiring repentance. Confess the sins you know, and God will take care of the ones you don't know

- **Problem:** Expecting God to fix specific sins, struggles, or broken sinful habits in your life while continuing to walk in darkness
  **Solution:** Move into the light. God will not operate in self-deception, denial, or pretending sin is not present, and He will not hear or answer your prayers when you pray from a place of darkness. He only works in the light where He reveals specific sins, attitudes, or behaviors that conflict with God's character as light so that cleansing can take place
