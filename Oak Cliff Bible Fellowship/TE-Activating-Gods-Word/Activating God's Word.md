# Activating God's Word

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God's Word is meant to be experienced, not merely read or heard. Using the analogy of a restaurant menu, Dr. Tony Evans explains from James 1:19-25 that believers must resign themselves to the Word, receive it with humility, and respond to it as doers. The sermon emphasizes that true spiritual transformation occurs when the Word moves from external knowledge to internal experience.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you want to move from merely hearing God's Word to experiencing its transformative power
- When you struggle with applying scripture to daily life decisions
- When you recognize that knowledge of the Bible has not produced lasting change

## Steps

### Step 1: Resign Yourself to the Word

Be quick to hear what God has to say, slow to speak your own opinion, and slow to anger when God's Word conflicts with your perspective. Allow God's truth to take precedence over social media, family opinions, or personal preferences. Go to the Word of truth first in every situation rather than relying on human opinion or public media.

### Step 2: Receive the Word Implanted

Put aside all filthiness and wickedness in humility. Confess sin and repent so the Word can penetrate your soul. Like a spiritual colonoscopy, this cleansing allows the Word to reach the implant of the Holy Spirit within you. Do not be a spiritual anorexic who never receives the Word, or a spiritual bulimic who receives it but spits it back out. Welcome the Word internally even if you do not like it, and let it be implanted in your soul.

### Step 3: Respond to the Word as a Doer

Take action based on the Word you have embraced. Do not be like the man who looks in a mirror and quickly walks away, forgetting what he saw. Be like the woman who intently studies her reflection until she sees what needs to change. Treat the Word like a crockpot, not a microwave — let it abide and marinate in your life. As you act on the Word, it activates and produces transformation.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Restaurant Menu

Dr. Evans compares reading and hearing God's Word to reading a restaurant menu. You can read the menu, hear the waiter describe it, and even see pictures, but if you do not actually eat the meal, you leave nutritionally malnourished. The purpose is the experience of the meal, not just the description. Likewise, you did not come to church merely to read or hear about God's Word; you came for the experience of it.

### Example 2: The Mouse Trap

A mouse warns a chicken, pig, and cow that Farmer Brown bought a mouse trap. They dismiss it as not their problem. When a snake gets caught in the trap, the farmer's wife gets bitten, and the chicken, pig, and cow are each killed to provide for the sick woman and her funeral. The mouse's final words: "I tried to tell them about the mouse trap, but they didn't pay attention to the word." This illustrates how God's Word has repercussions beyond what we immediately see.

## Best Practices

- Be quick to hear God's Word before seeking advice from social media or friends
- Receive the Word with humility and welcome it even when it is uncomfortable
- Allow the Word to abide and marinate rather than seeking quick spiritual experiences
- Act on the Word immediately rather than being a forgetful hearer
- Examine yourself in the Word like looking intently in a mirror

## Keep In Mind

- God's Word is the word of truth — an absolute standard by which reality is measured, not merely a collection of facts
- Your spirit is saved instantly at conversion, but your soul must be progressively saved through receiving and acting on the Word
- The implanted Word inside you needs the external Word to activate it, like an egg needs sperm or popcorn needs heat

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being quick to speak and slow to hear
  **Solution:** Make God's Word the first place you go when facing life's issues, before social media, friends, or family opinions
- **Problem:** Receiving the Word but not keeping it
  **Solution:** Avoid being a spiritual anorexic who never receives the Word or a spiritual bulimic who receives it but rejects it. Welcome and embrace the Word fully
- **Problem:** Hearing the Word but not doing it
  **Solution:** Take action on what you learn. Do not be like the man who looks in a mirror and immediately forgets his reflection
- **Problem:** Treating the Word like a microwave instead of a crockpot
  **Solution:** Let the Word abide and marinate in your life. Do not just seek the temporary juice of a sermon but let it transform you over time
