# How the Enemy Tries to Distract You From God's Plan | Tony Evans Sermon

## Overview

This sermon teaches that life is a dangerous journey filled with hidden landmines, explosives, and pitfalls that the enemy strategically places to destroy your purpose and keep you from completing God's plan for your life. Using the Genesis 3 account of the serpent's deception of Eve, Pastor Tony Evans reveals the enemy's specific strategies: using good things to produce bad outcomes, making you believe God is holding out on you, causing you to doubt God's Word, and tempting you through physical, emotional, and intellectual appetites toward independence from God. The central message is that God has given you a map — His Word — to navigate every danger on the journey, and how you treat that map determines whether you reach your God-ordained destination or fall victim to enemy schemes.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel distracted from your God-given purpose or calling
- When you are facing repeated negative consequences and don't understand why
- When you struggle with temptation and want to understand the enemy's strategy
- When you question whether God's Word is trustworthy or relevant to your life
- When you desire to develop spiritual discernment between good and evil
- When you feel trapped by sin patterns and want to break free
- When you are tempted to be independent from God and rely on your own understanding

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge That You Are on a Journey With Hidden Dangers

Understand that from the moment you are born, you are on a journey called life, and this journey contains landmines, explosives, and pits designed to destroy you. These dangers are not always obvious, but they are real and present throughout your entire life. The enemy has a tailor-made strategy to defeat you based on your specific weaknesses, tendencies, and sensitivities — you are spiritually "on tape."

### Step 2: Accept That God Has Given You a Map

God has provided His Word as your map for this journey. It reveals exactly where every danger lies and how to navigate around it. The map shows every landmine, explosive, and pit that would seek to bring you harm. Your life depends on this map, so it must become the most precious thing in your life. Rejecting the map or treating it casually — reading it once a week, occasionally, or ignoring it entirely — is the enemy's first victory.

### Step 3: Recognize the Enemy's First Strategy — Crafty Distraction

Satan does not appear as a red creature with horns and a pitchfork. He is crafty, sneaky, and uses something good to create something bad. He enters your world through legitimate, good things — relationships, opportunities, desires — and twists them to bring destruction. Like an illusionist who distracts you with one hand while performing the real trick with the other, Satan makes you focus on what looks good so you miss what he is actually doing. Watch for things that look appealing but lead away from God's plan.

### Step 4: Recognize the Enemy's Second Strategy — "God Is Holding Out on You"

Satan wants you to believe that God's restrictions are unfair and that God is keeping something good from you. He selectively focuses on the one thing God says no to while omitting the vast freedom God actually gives you. He reduces God's clear command to a question to lessen its impact. Remember: God's "no" is always to protect His "yes." When you focus on what God has forbidden, you miss the abundant blessings He has freely offered.

### Step 5: Recognize the Enemy's Third Strategy — "God's Word Cannot Be Trusted"

Satan plants doubt about the reliability and relevance of Scripture. He whispers that the Bible was written too long ago, that it doesn't apply to modern life, or that you can determine truth on your own. Counter this by standing on Romans 3:4 — "Let God be true and every man a liar." No matter how convincing a lie sounds or who presents it, God's Word is the only trustworthy foundation. Do not let anyone or anything rearrange the truth of Scripture in your mind.

### Step 6: Recognize the Enemy's Fourth Strategy — The Appeal to Independence

Satan tempts you to want to be like God on your own terms — self-sufficient, independent, and answerable to no one but yourself. This is the root of rebellion: the desire to determine good and evil for yourself without submitting to God's authority. This leads to every form of death — economic, relational, emotional, and spiritual. Submit every area of your life — your education, your success, your possessions — to God. No achievement, degree, or accomplishment makes you equal to Him.

### Step 7: Recognize the Enemy's Fifth Strategy — Erasing the Evil and Highlighting the Good

Satan blinds you to the long-term consequences of sin by making you focus only on the immediate pleasure or gain. He increases your physical appetite, then your emotional appetite, and finally your intellectual appetite — making you feel that you know better than God's Word. If you could see the full picture of the destruction that sin brings — addiction, broken relationships, financial ruin — you would not be so quick to indulge. Ask God to give you discernment to see beyond the temporary pleasure to the eternal outcome.

### Step 8: Develop Spiritual Discernment Through the Word

The biggest problem facing believers today is a lack of discernment. We are impressed with what looks good on the outside without discerning what lies beneath. Spiritual people, according to Hebrews 5:11-14, are those who through practice have trained their senses to distinguish between good and evil. This discernment does not come from human wisdom, education, or philosophy — it comes from eating from the tree of life, which is Jesus Christ, and studying God's Word. When you know God's Word, you cannot be faked out by something that looks good on the surface but leads to destruction.

### Step 9: Stop Walking Into the Same Trap Repeatedly

When you discover where a trap is, you do not keep walking into it. The enemy has switched the value tags in your life, causing you to spend your time, energy, and life on cheap, temporary things while ignoring the eternal treasures God offers freely. Identify the areas where you keep repeating the same destructive patterns, and with God's help, break the cycle. When you find a trap, address it and move on.

### Step 10: Qualify for God's Grace by Turning From Consequences

If you are tired of living with the consequences of sin — regret, debt, broken relationships, emotional pain — you qualify for God's abundant grace. James 1:13-16 teaches that temptation comes from our own desires, not from God. Acknowledge that the consequences you are experiencing are the result of sin, not bad luck. Turn back to God's map (His Word), follow it deliberately, and let His mercy meet you in the areas where you have been harmed by enemy deception.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Credit Card Limit Increase

Pastor Evans shares a personal example of receiving a congratulatory letter from a credit card company announcing a $5,000 credit limit increase, making him feel like a "preferred customer" worthy of platinum status. On the surface, this looked like a blessing. But behind it was a trap: the company wanted him to feel good about himself so he would spend more money and remain trapped in minimum payments for decades. This illustrates the enemy's strategy perfectly — he wraps destruction in something that looks good, makes you feel valued and entitled, and leads you deeper into bondage while you think you are being blessed.

### Example 2: The Farmer and the Banker (Duck Hunting Story)

A banker went duck hunting and shot a duck that fell onto a farmer's property. When they argued over who owned the duck, the banker proposed a solution: "You hit me as hard as you can, then I'll hit you as hard as I can, and whoever screams the least gets the duck." The farmer agreed and hit the banker so hard he fell to the ground groaning. When it was the farmer's turn, he simply said, "You can have the duck." The enemy wants to hurt you and then let you keep the very thing that is destroying you. You think you're winning, but you're actually losing. The duck (the sinful pleasure, the toxic relationship, the bad decision) costs you far more than you realize.

### Example 3: The Teenager Seeking Independence

A teenager demands independence — "Don't tell me what to do" — but still expects to be provided for, driven around, and clothed by their parents. This mirrors the human condition: we want independence from God while still expecting Him to bless, protect, and provide for us. True independence from God is not freedom — it is spiritual death. The moment you try to separate yourself from God's authority, you step outside His protection and provision. You cannot have it both ways.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Read God's Word (the map) daily, not just once a week or occasionally
- ✅ Treat Scripture as the most precious thing in your life because your survival depends on it
- ✅ Practice spiritual discernment by studying the Word so you can distinguish between good and evil
- ✅ Submit every area of your life — education, success, money, relationships — to God's authority
- ✅ Address traps and landmines immediately when you discover them rather than repeatedly stepping in them
- ✅ Remember that God's "no" is always designed to protect His greater "yes"
- ✅ Accept consequences as signals that you have deviated from the map, then return to it
- ✅ Qualify for God's grace by turning away from repeated sin patterns and running to Him

## Keep In Mind

- Satan cannot force you to sin — he can only deceive you into wanting to
- The enemy uses something good to create something bad; he does not need something evil to tempt you
- God's Word is the only reliable map for your life journey — human wisdom and philosophy are insufficient
- When you eat from the "tree of independence" (living by your own rules apart from God), every area of your life begins to die — economically, relationally, emotionally, and spiritually
- Consequences are not bad luck — they are the natural result of stepping off God's path
- The enemy has already studied your tendencies and weaknesses; do not underestimate his knowledge of you
- If you could see the full consequences of a sin before committing it, you would never do it — but the enemy hides the end from you
- You become like what you submit to — submit to God and become like Him; submit to the world and become like it

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not trust any message — no matter how convincing, who delivers it, or how good it feels — that contradicts God's Word
- If a teaching, philosophy, or idea conflicts with Scripture, reject it completely, even if it seems educated, modern, or logical
- Do not put anyone on a pedestal — pastors, celebrities, intellectuals, or successful people — because no human being is equal to God
- Do not make an idol of knowledge, degrees, or human wisdom; when human truth conflicts with divine revelation, divine revelation wins
- When you feel the pull toward self-sufficiency or independence from God, recognize it as the enemy's strategy and immediately submit back to Him
- Do not rely on your own understanding of right and wrong — Proverbs 14:12 warns that a way that seems right to man leads to death

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating God's Word as optional — reading it once a week or only when it is convenient, while expecting God's guidance and protection
  **Solution:** Make God's Word your most precious priority. Your life depends on it. Read it daily, study it, and apply it to every decision. The map only helps if you actually reference it before you step.

- **Problem:** Believing the lie that God is holding out on you — focusing on what God forbids while ignoring the vast freedom and blessings He offers
  **Solution:** Shift your focus to all the things God has freely given you rather than the one thing He has restricted. God's "no" is always to protect His greater "yes." Count your blessings, not your restrictions.

- **Problem:** Doubting the Bible because it seems outdated, culturally irrelevant, or intellectually inferior to modern knowledge
  **Solution:** Stand on Romans 3:4 — "Let God be true and every man a liar." The Bible is not a product of its time; it is the eternal, reliable Word of the God who created time. No human philosophy, scientific theory, or cultural trend can override it.

- **Problem:** Wanting to be independent from God while still wanting His blessings, protection, and provision
  **Solution:** Submit fully. You cannot have it both ways. True freedom is found in submission to God, not independence from Him. Like a teenager who wants to be treated like an adult but still expects their parents to pay the bills, your rebellion only cuts you off from the very things you need.

- **Problem:** Being deceived by things that look good on the surface without considering the long-term consequences
  **Solution:** Develop discernment by saturating yourself in God's Word. Before making any major decision — about relationships, finances, career, or behavior — ask: "What does God's map say about this?" If you cannot see the end result, seek counsel from the Word and the Holy Spirit rather than relying on how something feels in the moment.

- **Problem:** Repeatedly stepping into the same sin patterns, traps, and destructive cycles without breaking free
  **Solution:** When you find a trap, stop walking into it. Identify the specific area where you keep failing, acknowledge it honestly, and with God's help, remove yourself from that environment, relationship, or pattern. The enemy has switched your value tags — he has made cheap things look expensive and expensive things look cheap. Renew your mind through the Word to see things as they truly are.
