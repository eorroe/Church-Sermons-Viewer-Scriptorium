# How to Overcome Your Addictions

## Overview

This sermon by Tony Evans teaches that addiction is not merely a bad habit or a psychological issue, but a spiritual stronghold that requires a spiritual solution. The enemy creates a mental partition that blocks God's truth from penetrating, making people feel trapped in cycles they cannot break. The path to freedom is found not in fleshly effort, but in abiding in God's truth through His Word and developing a personal relationship with Jesus Christ, who alone can set you truly free.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you or someone you know feels trapped by an addiction they cannot break
- When church attendance and prayer alone have not brought release from sin
- When you want to understand the spiritual nature of addiction as a stronghold
- When you are trying to overcome a recurring sin through your own effort without lasting victory

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize Addiction as a Stronghold

Understand that the biblical word for what the world calls addiction is "stronghold." A stronghold is a spiritually-based addiction that cannot be overcome by worldly methods alone. It is a fortress built in the mind by the enemy, using wrong thinking and double-mindedness to keep God's truth from fully reaching your heart.

### Step 2: Identify the Partition in Your Mind

The enemy builds a "lofty thing" — a mental partition — that keeps God's truth on one side and worldly thinking on the other. This double-mindedness means you hear sermons but cannot apply them, so you have victory one moment and defeat the next. Acknowledge that this partition is why your efforts to change have failed.

### Step 3: Stop Trying to Fix It With Fleshly Effort

Do not rely on willpower, New Year's resolutions, counseling alone, medication, or self-scrubbing to overcome the stronghold. The flesh cannot fix the flesh. These methods may offer temporary relief but will not bring lasting freedom because they do not address the spiritual root.

### Step 4: Abide in the Truth

Continue in God's Word daily. Do not treat church as a weekly visit — abide in it. Read Romans chapters 6, 7, and 8 every day this week. Let the Word of God sink into your soul through repetition. Like medicine you do not fully understand, ingest it and let it do its work. The truth will make you free, but you must stay in it.

### Step 5: Understand Your New Identity in Christ

From Romans 6, learn that you are no longer your old self. You have been buried with Christ in baptism and raised into a new life. Sin is lying to you when it tells you that you are your addiction. You are a blood-bought, completely converted son or daughter of the Living God who has a problem with [addiction]. Consider your old person dead.

### Step 6: Press Into the Power of the Holy Spirit

From Romans 7 and 8, understand that even the Apostle Paul struggled with things he did not want to do. The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus is greater than the law of sin and death. When you abide in the truth, the Holy Spirit gives you the supernatural power to overcome. You are no longer obligated to the flesh.

### Step 7: Allow the Word to Divide Soul and Spirit

Hebrews 4:12 says the Word of God is alive and sharper than any two-edged sword. When taken in, it divides soul and spirit, separating what is of God from what is not. The Living Word (Jesus) connecting with the written Word in your heart posts bail and brings the key to the lock — setting you free indeed.

### Step 8: Walk in the Freedom Christ Purchased

Remember that Jesus already paid for your freedom on the cross — the emancipation proclamation has been signed. Satan wants you to believe a partition that keeps you from walking in that freedom. Declare your freedom by continuing in the Word and calling on the Son. Walk out of the prison; do not stay on the plantation just because it feels familiar.

## Examples

### Example 1: Overcoming Alcoholism

If you struggle with alcoholism, you may have tried 12-step programs where you stand and say, "Hello, my name is [name] and I am an alcoholic." The Bible disagrees with that identity. Romans 6 says you are a new creation in Christ. Your old self, the alcoholic self, died with Christ. You are not an alcoholic — you are a child of God who is dealing with the sin of alcohol. Abide in Romans 6-8 daily, and let the Holy Spirit give you victory over the master called alcohol.

### Example 2: Breaking Free From Anger

If you struggle with uncontrolled anger and wrath, you may have tried anger management classes or promised yourself you would change. These are fleshly efforts. The stronghold is a partition in your mind that keeps God's truth about patience and self-control from penetrating. Read Romans 6-8 every day, asking the Holy Spirit to divide your soul and spirit so that God's nature replaces your anger.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Read Romans 6, 7, and 8 every single day until the truth becomes part of your soul
- ✅ Continue in the Word — abide in it, don't just visit it on Sundays
- ✅ Renounce your old identity based on your sin and adopt your identity in Christ
- ✅ Call on Jesus as the Son who has the key to your lock
- ✅ Recognize that knowing the truth is not enough — you must know the Author of truth

## Keep In Mind

- Not all truth is the same: there is your truth, cultural truth, and absolute truth. Only the absolute truth of God's Word can set you free.
- Being made free and being set free are different: the Word posts bail, but the Son unlocks the door.
- Just because you use Christian language or attend church does not mean you are not being controlled by Satan's partition in your mind.
- Satan does not mind you hearing God's truth on Sunday as long as he keeps his partition in place Monday through Saturday.
- Individuals struggling with severe addictions should seek both spiritual guidance and professional help.
- The biblical approach presented here addresses the spiritual root but does not exclude the need for medical or therapeutic support.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Relying on self-effort, willpower, or worldly methods to overcome addiction
  **Solution:** Stop trying to fix the flesh with the flesh. Abide in God's truth and rely on the Holy Spirit's power.
- **Problem:** Treating church as a weekly event rather than a daily abiding relationship
  **Solution:** Read the Word every day and let it penetrate your soul through repetition.
- **Problem:** Identifying yourself by your sin rather than your identity in Christ
  **Solution:** Read Romans 6 daily and remind yourself that your old self died with Christ; you are now a new creation.
- **Problem:** Thinking that knowing the truth is enough for freedom
  **Solution:** It is not enough to know about truth — you must know the Author of truth through a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.
