# You Were Created to Love Like Christ | Tony Evans Highlight

## Overview

This sermon teaches that biblical love, or agape, is a deliberate decision rather than a passing emotion. Tony Evans explains that because God is love, He only dwells with those who choose to love others, and this kind of love is what unlocks a deeper experience of God's presence. The message calls believers to stop merely attending church and start loving one another as a lifestyle, because without a love-centered community, even the best preaching, singing, and praying cannot bring about true spiritual transformation or answered prayers.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel distant from God or wonder why your prayers seem unanswered
- When you find it difficult to love certain people in your church or family
- When you are tempted to judge, look down on, or refuse to embrace others who are broken, forgiven, or different from you

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Nature of Biblical Love

Recognize that agape is not a feeling. "Like" is a feeling—you may like chocolate cake or a person—but biblical love is a deliberate choice to act in the other person's best interest regardless of your emotions—for example, speaking kindly, meeting a need, or forgiving when it is undeserved. You can choose to eat vegetables you do not like because you love healthy nutrition. In the same way, you can choose to love people you do not naturally like.

### Step 2: Position Yourself in God's Store

Realize that your environment shapes you. If you hang out in a flower shop, you start smelling like flowers. God's store is called love. If you are a loveless Christian, it is because you are shopping in the wrong store. Spend time in God's presence and in community with other believers so that His love shapes you. The more you love others, the more you dwell in the place where God's presence is experienced.

### Step 3: Love the Family of God

Shift your focus from "me, myself, and I" to loving others. Ask yourself daily: "How can I love today? Who can I love today in the family of God? What can I do today?" You cannot love everybody, but you can love somebody. Choose one person and take a specific action this week to bless them—such as listening, encouraging, or serving them.

### Step 4: Speak Truth with Love

Avoid two extremes: emotional sentimentalism (love without truth) and cold orthodoxy (truth without love). When you love without truth, you become mushy and ineffective. When you have truth without love, you become harsh and judgmental. The correct balance is to speak the truth in love so that people know you care, without compromising the teachings of Scripture.

### Step 5: Embrace the Restored and the Broken

Do not think you are better than others who come into the church with messy pasts, ragged clothes, or shameful histories. You were no better when Jesus died for you. If it were not for Jesus, you would be the one walking through the door looking the same way. Love people while holding to God's moral standard, but do not look down on them for failing to meet it.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Flower Shop Analogy

Tony Evans uses the illustration of a flower shop and a perfume shop to show how environment shapes you. If you spend time in a flower shop, you will start smelling like flowers. If you spend time around perfume and cologne, you will take on that scent. Likewise, if you hang out in God's store—love—His love will shape your attitudes and actions as you remain in Him and in community with believers. If you are not loving, it is because you are hanging out in somebody else's store, not God's.

### Example 2: The Prodigal Son and the Older Brother

In Luke 15, the prodigal son returns home after wasting his life, and the father throws a celebration. The older brother, who had remained faithful, becomes angry and refuses to join the party. The story ends with the older brother standing before his father, still unresolved, showing that each listener must choose whether to embrace love or remain resentful. Some Christians today act the same way: they do not want to hang out with people who have been forgiven, redeemed, or whose lives have been broken and beaten. The sermon challenges listeners to choose love like the father did.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Decide to love regardless of your emotions or preferences
- ✅ Ask daily how you can love someone in the family of God
- ✅ Speak the truth with love so people know you care
- ✅ Remember that you were no better than anyone else when Jesus saved you
- ✅ Hold a biblical standard while loving people toward that standard
- ❌ Don't confuse "like" (a feeling) with biblical "love" (a decision)
- ❌ Don't expect answered prayers or deep spiritual experiences if you are not loving others
- ❌ Don't preach, sing, or shout your way into God's presence without love
- ❌ Don't look down on broken or restored people who come into the church
- ❌ Don't choose truth without love, which creates cold orthodoxy
- ❌ Don't choose love without truth, which becomes emotional sentimentalism

## Keep In Mind

- God is love, and He only dwells with those who love others—if you are not loving, you cannot remain in close relationship with Him
- Love is a decision, not an emotion; you can love people you do not naturally like
- God measures His responsiveness to you by your willingness to love and care for other people.
- If you do not love others, you do not know God; and if you do not love your brother, you cannot claim to love God.
- You cannot have an intimate relationship with God if you do not love others.
- God demonstrated His love to us while we were still sinners—love others the same way
- The church is called to be known for its love, not its preaching, music, or programs

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Confusing like with love, treating love as a feeling rather than a decision
  **Solution:** Understand that biblical agape love is a choice to seek another's well-being regardless of emotion, just as you can choose healthy food you do not enjoy

- **Problem:** Being a loveless Christian who attends church regularly but never truly connects with God or others
  **Solution:** Stop shopping at the wrong store and start abiding in love; ask daily who you can love and how you can bless someone else

- **Problem:** Looking down on new, broken, or restored believers while feeling self-righteous about your own faithfulness
  **Solution:** Remember that you were no better when Jesus died for you; love people with a standard but never with pride or contempt

- **Problem:** Balancing truth and love incorrectly by leaning to one extreme or the other
  **Solution:** Always speak the truth in love, avoiding both cold orthodoxy and emotional sentimentalism; let people know you care while never compromising God's Word
