# Uncovering the Spiritual Treasure That Surpasses All Wealth

## Overview

This sermon challenges believers to prioritize spiritual legacy over material legacy, calling them to seek a double portion of God's spirit rather than a double portion of worldly possessions. Using the story of Elisha asking Elijah for a double portion of his spirit, Tony Evans explains that God wants to transfer His power and presence through generations, but this requires leaving behind old commitments, surrendering attachments to the world, and walking through a discipleship process. The core message is that spiritual inheritance — more of God Himself — is the only legacy that carries eternal value.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stuck or delayed in your relationship with God
- When you sense God calling you to a deeper level of commitment
- When you are evaluating what kind of legacy you are leaving for your family or community
- When you are dissatisfied with a shallow or surface-level faith
- When you want to understand the cost and process of discipleship
- When you are searching for a mentor who can impart spiritual legacy
- When you have been asking God for more but feel like you have not received it

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify What You Must Leave Behind

Examine your life honestly and identify what you are still committed to that leaves God out. This could be relationships, habits, priorities, or attachments that keep you spiritually stagnant. You cannot carry yesterday's world into today's move of God. If you are still looking back at what God has delivered you from, you are not positioned to receive more of Him.

### Step 2: Assess Your Commitment Level

God tests commitment before He releases a double portion. Like Elijah testing Elisha three times — at Gilgal, Bethel, and Jericho — God will observe whether your faith is real under pressure. Commit to following Him even when it is difficult. Be willing to go further than the minimum requirement. Do not settle for being a spectator when God is calling you to a participant.

### Step 3: Walk Through the Discipleship Process

There is a divinely ordained process before the double portion is released:

- **Gilgal** — Leave the world behind. Stop carrying old mindsets, old sins, and old commitments that keep you from fully entering God's plan.
- **Bethel** — Hear God's voice. Once you have turned away from worldly distractions, God can now speak to you directly. Listen for His voice and let Him change your character.
- **Jericho** — Obey God's instructions even when they seem strange or impossible. The walls of Jericho fell because the people obeyed an unusual command. Trust that what seems ridiculous from heaven is the very thing that brings breakthrough.
- **Jordan** — Receive the double portion. After you have passed through Gilgal, Bethel, and Jericho, you arrive at the place where God releases the greater measure of His spirit and power upon your life.

### Step 4: Find a Spiritual Mentor Who Has Seen God

You cannot receive a double portion from someone who does not have one. Seek out someone who has personally experienced God's supernatural presence — someone who can impart spiritual legacy through their life, not just their words. This will often be a grandparent, a pastor, or a mature believer who walks in consistent faith. Hang around them long enough to catch what God has placed in them.

### Step 5: Ask God for the Double Portion

Once you have identified what to leave behind, assessed your commitment, and walked through the discipleship journey from Gilgal to Jordan, make the specific request: ask God for a double portion of His spirit. Do not ask for a bigger house, a nicer car, or a higher position first. Ask for more of God Himself. The double portion is His to give, not something any person can manufacture for you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Elisha's Request for a Double Portion

Elisha witnessed Elijah's extraordinary ministry — fire from heaven, drought by prophetic word, and supernatural provision. Instead of asking for wealth or status, Elisha asked for a double portion of Elijah's spirit. Elijah replied that it was a hard thing, but if Elisha remained committed and saw Elijah taken up, it would be granted. Elisha saw it, received it, and went on to perform twice as many miracles as Elijah. This illustrates that the double portion belongs to those who are committed and spiritually perceptive.

### Example 2: Leaving the World Behind at Gilgal

When the Israelites left Egypt, they struggled to leave Egypt in their hearts. They kept looking back. It took forty years in the wilderness before they were free from Egypt's pull. Gilgal was the place where God removed the reproach of Egypt from Israel. Similarly, many believers today are still mentally and emotionally tied to the world they have been saved from. Until they truly leave Egypt behind at Gilgal, God cannot release a new level of His presence in their lives.

### Example 3: A Life That Leaves a Spiritual Legacy

A believer who prioritizes intimacy with God over material accumulation leaves behind something that outlasts possessions. When that person passes on, the power of God continues to work through the next generation because of the spiritual heritage deposited in them. Psalm 71:18 declares that God's power is to be transferred from one generation to another. This is the kind of legacy that surpasses all wealth.

### Example 4: The Doctor's Sign

Tony Evans tells the story of a man on his honeymoon who suffered a head-on collision. His bride was bleeding out, and he found a doctor's office nearby. The old man who answered the door said he had stopped practicing medicine years ago. The old man had a sign that said he could help, but there was no power behind it. Many Christians carry signs — "I'm a Christian," "I love Jesus" — but when crisis hits, there is no substance. God is looking for people who practice what they preach, not just people who carry signs.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Ask God specifically for more of Himself before asking for more material things
- ✅ Examine your life regularly for worldly attachments that still have your heart
- ✅ Seek mentors who have genuinely experienced God's supernatural power
- ✅ Walk through the full discipleship process — do not skip Gilgal, Bethel, or Jericho
- ✅ Be willing to obey God even when His instructions seem unusual or uncomfortable
- ✅ Leave yesterday behind so you can experience new moves of God today
- ✅ View spiritual legacy as more valuable than any material inheritance

- ❌ Do not ask God for a double portion of material things while ignoring a double portion of His spirit
- ❌ Do not try to receive from people what only God can give
- ❌ Do not skip the process and expect instant results
- ❌ Do not follow mentors who have material success but no evident spiritual power
- ❌ Do not remain attached to the world while expecting God to entrust you with more of Himself
- ❌ Do not assume that church attendance alone equals spiritual commitment

## Keep In Mind

- The double portion is a spiritual inheritance that comes with greater responsibility. Elisha, as the firstborn, incurred greater responsibility, which is why he needed a greater portion. You cannot carry the double portion if you are not willing to be used by God for the well-being of others.
- Spiritual legacy is transferred through relationship, not just through words. You need to be close enough to someone who has seen God to actually catch what God has placed in them.
- God will test your commitment. He is not interested in lip service. He wants to know if you will keep going when it costs you something.
- You will receive exactly what you see. If your focus is on the material world, you will receive more of the material world. If your focus is on the spiritual, you will receive more of God.
- Only God can give the double portion. No human being has the authority or power to release that kind of inheritance upon your life.
- Leaving the world behind is not a one-time event. It is a daily choice to turn your back on old attachments and follow Christ forward.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Asking God for more material things instead of more of God.
  **Solution:** Reorder your prayers. Seek the kingdom of God and His righteousness first, and He will add the material needs (Matthew 6:33). Ask specifically for a double portion of His spirit.

- **Problem:** Getting stuck at Gilgal — still emotionally and mentally attached to the world.
  **Solution:** Honestly name the worldly attachments that still have your heart. Make a deliberate decision to leave them behind. You cannot carry old baggage into a new move of God.

- **Problem:** Following the wrong mentors — people with material success but no evident spiritual power.
  **Solution:** Evaluate the people you are following. Do they have a genuine experience of God? Can they point to supernatural fruit in their lives? Spend time with those who have seen God for themselves, regardless of their education or social status.

- **Problem:** Skipping the process and expecting instant breakthrough.
  **Solution:** Understand that Gilgal, Bethel, Jericho, and Jordan are sequential. You cannot jump straight to Jordan. Walk through each stage honestly, and do not rush what God is doing in you.

- **Problem:** Waiting for someone else to give you the double portion instead of receiving it directly from God.
  **Solution:** Remember that only God can give the double portion. Mentors can point the way and impart spiritual legacy, but the actual release comes from God alone.
