# It's Time to Grow into Who God Called You to Be

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God will not lower His standards to accommodate anyone — instead, He calls every believer to grow into the person He has destined them to be. Through the example of the church at Thyrotyra in Revelation 2, Tony Evans illustrates that an overcomer must have clear toleration limits and must refuse to accept what falls outside of God's boundaries. Spiritual growth and discipleship are about reaching God's standard through intentional, daily transformation.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stagnant in your spiritual walk and desire growth
- When you are unsure where God's boundaries should be in your life
- When you want to move from being an undercomer to an overcomer
- When you need clarity on what it means to live victoriously rather than in defeat
- When you are seeking to understand your God-given identity and calling

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That God Will Not Lower His Standard

Understand and accept that God does not compromise His standards to please people. He expects growth, not passivity. This is the foundation of all spiritual development.

### Step 2: Embrace the Goal of Spiritual Growth

Make the decision that you want to grow into who God called you to be. Spiritual growth and discipleship are not optional — they are the process of reaching the standard God has set for your life.

### Step 3: Establish Your Toleration Limits

Like the overcomer described in Revelation 2, identify the sinful patterns, unbiblical values, and worldly compromises you will not tolerate or accept. Set clear boundaries that align with God's Word. These boundaries define what is inside and outside of God's will for your life.

### Step 4: Study the Example of Thyrotyra

Read Revelation 2:18–29 to understand how God addresses the church at Thyrotyra. Reflect on how their context — a city filled with trade guilds that required idolatrous participation and endorsed Jezebel's false teaching — mirrors the pressures believers face today to conform to worldly systems at the expense of faithful obedience.

### Step 5: Learn from Lydia's Surrender

Study Acts 16:14 and the story of Lydia, a successful seller of purple dye who surrendered her life to Christ. Let her example inspire you to surrender your own ambitions, wealth, and identity to God's calling.

### Step 6: Live as an Overcomer

Reject the identity of an undercomer or loser. Choose victory. Refuse defeat. Walk in the confidence that God has called and equipped you to overcome through Christ.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Church at Thyrotyra

The city of Thyrotyra was known for its trade guilds — organized groups that provided economic benefits to members but required participation in idolatrous feasts and the worship of pagan deities. In this worldly environment, a church was established. The church faced the temptation to compromise with idolatry and accept Jezebel's false teaching rather than stand firm in Christ. God's message to them in Revelation 2 was a call to hold fast, reject compromise, and pursue overcomer living.

### Example 2: Lydia — From Business Success to Kingdom Calling

Lydia was a dealer in purple dye — a lucrative trade that supplied couture clothing to the wealthy. She was a manufacturer, distributor, and wholesaler with considerable social and economic influence as a wealthy merchant. Yet when Paul the Apostle preached the gospel, she responded in faith (Acts 16:14). Lydia's story shows that God calls people in every vocation, social class, and cultural context, and calls them to surrender even their most successful identities to follow Him.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Make spiritual growth a daily priority — discipleship is a process, not an event
- ✅ Regularly examine your life against God's standard rather than comparing yourself to others
- ✅ Establish clear, Scripture-based boundaries for what you will and will not tolerate
- ✅ Study the lives of biblical overcomers to model your own journey after them
- ✅ Surrender your successes, ambitions, and identity to God so He can reshape them for His glory
- ✅ Surround yourself with a community that encourages growth, not complacency

## Keep In Mind

- God's love for you does not mean He lowers His call to holiness, Christlikeness, and victorious living — it means He empowers you to meet them
- Spiritual growth takes time; do not become discouraged by slow progress
- An overcomer is not someone who never fails, but someone who persistently returns to Christ and refuses to accept defeat as permanent
- Your calling is uniquely yours — do not compare your growth journey to anyone else's
- The presence of worldly trade guilds and idolatrous systems around you does not mean you must conform to them

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Believing that God's grace means you don't need to grow or change.
  **Solution:** Understand that grace empowers growth — it does not remove the call to pursue holiness and spiritual maturity.
- **Problem:** Setting toleration limits based on personal preference rather than Scripture.
  **Solution:** Ground every boundary you set in the Word of God, not cultural norms or personal comfort.
- **Problem:** Comparing your spiritual growth to others and feeling discouraged.
  **Solution:** Focus on your own journey with Christ. Growth is personal, and God is committed to your unique development.
- **Problem:** Thinking being an overcomer means never experiencing struggle or defeat.
  **Solution:** Being an overcomer means rising after defeat — it is about perseverance, not perfection.
- **Problem:** Letting worldly affiliations and systems shape your identity more than your calling in Christ.
  **Solution:** Like Lydia, bring every part of your life — including your career and social circles — under Christ's lordship.
