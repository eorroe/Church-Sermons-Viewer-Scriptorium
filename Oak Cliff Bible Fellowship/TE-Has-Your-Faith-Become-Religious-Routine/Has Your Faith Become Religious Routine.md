# Has Your Faith Become Religious Routine?

## Overview

This sermon challenges listeners to move from empty religious routine into authentic relationship with the living God. Tony Evans identifies five patterns — traditionalism, externalism, ritualism, narcissism, and legalism — that appear religious and spiritual but, as Tony Evans teaches in this sermon, are actually inspired by demonic forces and designed to keep people from genuine spiritual transformation. The core message is that religious activity without the goal of spiritual intimacy produces no real change, no power, and no growth.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you sense your spiritual life has become a series of routine activities without heart engagement
- When you attend church, read the Bible, and pray, yet feel unchanged and unmoved
- When you want to discern between legitimate spiritual disciplines and empty religious performance
- When you are struggling with addiction, broken relationships, or pressure that religious routine alone is not helping you overcome

## Steps

### Step 1: Examine Your Motivations

Ask God to reveal whether your religious activities are rooted in genuine relationship or in habit, obligation, or self-interest. Be honest about whether you are "sitting at two tables" — meaning you participate in religious activities while your heart and loyalty remain distant from God, trying to serve both religious tradition and God at the same time — going through religious motions while your heart remains distant from God. James 4:1-10 warns that you cannot have both the religious thing and God Himself.

### Step 2: Move From Religion to Relationship Through the Word, Prayer, and Faith

Commit to a three-part process: (1) Read the Word and let it sink into your heart, not just glance at it; (2) Pray in agreement with the Word, inviting the Holy Spirit to apply it; (3) Take a step of faith that demonstrates you are serious about what you agreed with in the Word. This invites the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit to work in your life because you have aligned your heart and actions with God's Word through prayer and faith.

### Step 3: Reject the Five Camouflaged Isms

Intentionally identify and reject the five "isms" that can secretly replace authentic faith: traditionalism (following practices without biblical basis), externalism (prioritizing outward appearance over heart condition), ritualism (performing spiritual routines without expectation of God's action), narcissism (using faith for personal gain while ignoring those who cannot repay you), and legalism (obeying rules out of fear rather than love for God).

## Examples

### Example 1: Ritualism — Saying Grace Without Expecting God

Tony Evans uses the example of praying over food. Many people say grace out of religious obligation, but 1 Timothy 4:4-5 teaches that when you pray over your meal, you are to *sanctify* it — setting it apart through prayer and thanksgiving so that it is received as a gift from God, rather than treating it as merely a physical commodity. The sermon teaches that this prayer invites God to intervene supernaturally because the physical world contains things that can physically harm you, and God's blessing on your food is an invitation for His protection. The ritual becomes meaningful only when you genuinely expect God to act, not when you repeat words out of routine.

### Example 2: Legalism — Keeping the Ten Commandments Without Power

The Ten Commandments are good, but trying to keep them in your own strength cannot change you. Evans explains that the law can tell you the standard, but it cannot give you the power to meet it. Galatians 3:23-24 says the law was a tutor to drive you to Christ, not a system to save you. If you are obeying out of fear or obligation, you remain in a state of continual frustration and spiritual bondage — a condition in which a person tries to obey God's law through their own effort without relying on Christ's power, and therefore misses the joy of transformed relationship with God.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Let every spiritual activity be directed toward knowing God personally, not checking a religious box
- ✅ Engage the Word deeply — read it, reflect on it, and let it change how you think and speak
- ✅ Combine agreement with the Word by praying for the Holy Spirit to apply it, then act on it
- ✅ Serve people who cannot repay you as evidence that your worship is genuine, not narcissistic
- ✅ Measure your faith by transformation, not by attendance or religious performance
- ❌ Don't treat spiritual disciplines as a checklist to complete for God's approval
- ❌ Avoid measuring your faith by how many religious activities you perform
- ❌ Don't expect God to act when your heart is distant from Him
- ❌ Avoid serving only those who can benefit you in return

## Keep In Mind

- Religious activity without the goal of spiritual intimacy is like taking painkillers for a broken bone — it may mask the discomfort temporarily, but the underlying spiritual fracture remains unhealed unless you turn to God for true healing
- When religious practices become idols — when you rely on them instead of relying on God Himself — they can become tools that keep you from genuine relationship with God, because the focus shifts from God to the activity
- God is looking for those who will worship Him in spirit and in truth, not merely with their bodies
- Suffering is an invitation to intimacy with God; draw near when life is hard, not only when it is easy

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not dismiss all tradition, ritual, or structure as evil — the issue is not the activity but the motivation and outcome
- Evaluate every practice against Scripture to ensure it is rooted in God's Word and not merely human tradition
- Guard against the pride that can come from religious achievement; true faith produces humility, not self-righteousness

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Confusing religious busyness with spiritual growth
  **Solution:** Ask whether your activities are producing measurable heart change, transformed relationships, and increasing love for God and others
- **Problem:** Holding onto traditions that keep God's Word from working in your life
  **Solution:** Bring every tradition and routine before the Lord and ask Him to show you whether it is drawing you closer to Him or creating a rut
- **Problem:** Showing up to receive blessings rather than to be changed by God
  **Solution:** Examine whether you show up to church or prayer primarily to receive blessings, healing, comfort, or status from God rather than to be changed by Him
- **Problem:** Trying to obey God in your own strength and becoming miserable
  **Solution:** Stop striving and turn to Christ, who gives the power that the law cannot provide; let obedience flow from relationship, not rule-keeping
