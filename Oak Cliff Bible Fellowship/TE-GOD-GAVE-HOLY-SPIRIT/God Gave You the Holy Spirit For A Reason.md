# God Gave You the Holy Spirit For A Reason

## Overview

In this sermon, Tony Evans teaches that God has given every born-again believer the Holy Spirit as an internal source of divine guidance. Evans refers to the Holy Spirit as "the anointing." This anointing is a gift from God that operates as a form of internal navigation, providing direction in specific life decisions, transitions, and daily choices. Evans teaches that the world contains many influences and people who oppose Christ and seek to lead believers away from God's intended path. These influences cannot remove a believer's salvation, but they can cause a believer to make decisions that deviate from God's purposes. The anointing provides divine direction when a believer actively engages with it. To receive this guidance, Evans teaches that a believer must abide in Christ through specific, regular spiritual disciplines. The Holy Spirit then provides guidance that is tailored to the believer's individual circumstances, rather than general principles that apply to everyone.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are facing a specific major life decision—such as choosing a career path, deciding whether to marry someone, or considering a major purchase—and you do not know which option aligns with God's will for your life
- When you have stopped praying and reading the Bible on a regular basis and feel a lack of clarity or purpose in your spiritual life
- When you want specific, practical steps for discerning whether a thought, impulse, or piece of advice comes from the Holy Spirit or from another source
- When you are experiencing direct pressure from coworkers, friends, or family members to make choices that conflict with biblical teaching
- When you have made a decision in the past that led to negative consequences and you want to understand how to avoid repeating that pattern

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That You Have the Anointing

As a born-again believer, you have received the Holy Spirit. This is a specific gift, not a general feeling. The Holy Spirit lives within you and is available to provide direction. Evans teaches that ignoring this gift leads to poor decision-making. To apply this step, begin each day by verbally affirming that the Holy Spirit is present within you and asking Him to guide your decisions that day. Write down one specific decision you are facing and ask the Holy Spirit for clarity on that decision before you proceed.

### Step 2: Identify the Deception Around You

Evans teaches that the world contains many people and influences that oppose Christ and seek to lead you away from God's direction for your life. These influences cannot remove your salvation, but they can cause you to make decisions that move you away from God's purpose. To apply this step, evaluate the sources of information and advice you receive on a daily basis. Make a list of the five people and five media sources you listen to most frequently. Compare their advice and influence against biblical teaching. If a source consistently encourages choices that conflict with scripture, reduce or remove that influence from your life.

### Step 3: Abide in Christ

Evans teaches that you must maintain a daily, active connection with Christ to receive guidance from the Holy Spirit. "Abiding in Christ" means engaging in specific spiritual disciplines on a regular basis. To apply this step, establish a daily routine that includes reading a specific portion of scripture and praying before you begin your day's activities. Evans specifies that this should not be an occasional practice but a consistent daily habit. If you are unsure how to begin, start with five minutes of reading the Bible and five minutes of prayer each morning, and increase the time as you are able.

### Step 4: Allow the Anointing to Work Through Spirit, Soul, and Body

Evans teaches that the Holy Spirit provides guidance by working through your complete being—your spirit, your thoughts, and your physical circumstances. This means the Holy Spirit may provide direction through an inner conviction, through a specific thought that comes to mind, or through a change in your external circumstances. To apply this step, pay attention to all three areas. When you feel an inner conviction about a decision, write it down. When a specific scripture verse comes to mind during your day, note it and consider how it applies to your situation. When circumstances change in an unexpected way, evaluate whether that change may be providing direction. Do not rely on only one of these three areas; consider all three when making a decision.

### Step 5: Expect Personal Guidance for Your Unique Situation

Evans teaches that the Holy Spirit does not provide a one-size-fits-all message for every person. Instead, the Holy Spirit provides guidance that is tailored to your specific circumstances. Evans uses the analogy of "local news" versus "national news." National news is general information that applies to everyone. Local news is specific information that applies to your particular situation. The Holy Spirit provides "local news" for your life. To apply this step, ask the Holy Spirit for specific guidance for your situation, not general principles. Look for specific people to talk to, specific scripture verses that apply to your decision, and specific changes in your circumstances. Do not accept general advice as a substitute for specific guidance from the Holy Spirit.

### Step 6: Act on the Guidance When Given

Evans shares a personal story about getting lost because he did not follow directions properly. He teaches that when the Holy Spirit provides direction through people, circumstances, or inner conviction, you should follow it promptly and carefully. To apply this step, when you receive specific guidance, write down exactly what you believe the Holy Spirit is telling you to do. Then take action on that guidance within 24 to 48 hours. Do not delay indefinitely while waiting for additional confirmation. Evans teaches that careless delay after receiving clear guidance is equivalent to ignoring the guidance entirely. If you are unsure whether the guidance is from the Holy Spirit, verify it through scripture and counsel from mature believers before acting.

### Step 7: Walk in the Assurance of His Return

Evans teaches that when you abide in Christ and follow the anointing, you will not feel shame when Jesus returns. This means you can live with confidence that you are following God's direction for your life. To apply this step, at the end of each week, review the decisions you made and evaluate whether you sought the Holy Spirit's guidance before making them. If you followed the guidance you received, thank God for His direction. If you did not follow the guidance, confess that to God and ask for His help to follow His direction in the future. Let this review process motivate you to stay connected and obedient on a consistent basis.

## Examples

### Example 1: Navigating a Career Decision

You have received a job offer. The salary is higher than your current position, but the company culture conflicts with your values. Before accepting, you spend 30 minutes praying and asking the Holy Spirit for guidance. You read a specific passage of scripture—such as Proverbs 3:5-6—and ask the Holy Spirit to apply it to your situation. Over the next two days, a specific friend who knows your work situation contacts you and asks how the job search is going. When you mention the offer, they share a specific concern about the company's practices that you had not considered. At the same time, you receive an unexpected email from a different company that aligns with your values and offers a comparable salary. You interpret the friend's contact and the new email as confirmation from the Holy Spirit to decline the first offer and pursue the second opportunity. You make your decision based on these specific inputs rather than on the salary alone.

### Example 2: Facing Worldly Pressure

Your coworkers regularly make remarks that mock your faith and encourage you to compromise on ethical standards. You feel pressure to go along with them to avoid conflict. Instead of conforming, you recall Evans' teaching that many influences in the world oppose Christ and seek to lead believers away from God's best. You establish a specific boundary: you will not participate in conversations that mock your faith, and you will politely redirect the conversation or remove yourself from the situation. Each morning, you spend 10 minutes praying for wisdom and reading a specific passage of scripture—such as 1 Corinthians 15:33—and ask the Holy Spirit to give you specific words to say when pressured. When a coworker next pressures you, you recall the scripture and respond with a specific, prepared statement that honors your faith without starting a confrontation. You then discuss the situation with a mature believer at your church who helps you evaluate whether your response was consistent with biblical teaching.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Each morning, spend a specific amount of time—such as 10 to 15 minutes—praying and asking the Holy Spirit to guide you for that day
- ✅ Read a specific portion of scripture daily, such as one chapter of a New Testament book, so that the Holy Spirit has specific truth to bring to your attention when you face decisions
- ✅ Keep a written record of specific promptings, thoughts, scripture verses, and circumstances that you believe come from the Holy Spirit, and evaluate them against scripture at the end of each week
- ✅ When you receive what you believe is specific guidance from the Holy Spirit, take action within 24 to 48 hours rather than delaying indefinitely
- ✅ Identify five people in your life who consistently encourage your faith, and seek their counsel when you face a major decision
- ❌ Do not ignore promptings from the Holy Spirit and expect to make good decisions without divine guidance
- ❌ Do not attempt to make major decisions entirely on your own without prayer, scripture, and counsel
- ❌ Do not maintain close relationships with people or media sources that consistently encourage you to make choices that conflict with biblical teaching

## Keep In Mind

- The anointing is available to every born-again believer, but it only benefits those who engage in specific daily spiritual disciplines and actively apply the guidance they receive
- Influences that oppose Christ cannot remove your salvation, but they can cause you to make decisions that result in tangible negative consequences—such as damaged relationships, financial loss, or missed opportunities to serve God
- If you have made a decision that took you away from God's path, God will provide grace to restore you. The process of restoration involves returning to the practices outlined in this sermon: daily prayer and Bible reading, evaluating your influences against biblical teaching, seeking the Holy Spirit's specific guidance for your current situation, and acting on that guidance when it is received. This process takes time and requires consistent, intentional effort on your part
- The Holy Spirit provides specific guidance for your individual circumstances, not general principles that apply to everyone. The guidance you receive will differ from the guidance another believer receives, even if you are facing similar situations

## Security & Safety Notes

- Not every thought that enters your mind comes from the Holy Spirit. Before acting on a prompting, compare it to specific passages of scripture and seek the perspective of at least one mature believer who knows you well
- Some influences that present themselves as helpful or friendly are intentionally designed to lead you away from Christ. Evaluate every source of advice by comparing it to the Bible, not by how reasonable or kind it seems
- Do not make major life decisions—such as changing careers, moving to a new city, or ending a relationship—without first seeking confirmation through prayer, specific scripture, and counsel from at least two mature believers who are committed to biblical teaching

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You ignore the Holy Spirit and attempt to make decisions based only on your own reasoning
  **Solution:** Before every significant decision, pause for 30 minutes to pray, read scripture, and ask the Holy Spirit for specific guidance before proceeding
- **Problem:** You maintain relationships with people or regularly consume media that consistently encourages choices that conflict with biblical teaching
  **Solution:** Conduct a monthly review of your five closest relationships and your five most consumed media sources. Reduce or remove any source that consistently contradicts scripture
- **Problem:** You read the Bible and pray occasionally, but you do not do so on a consistent daily basis, and you expect the Holy Spirit to provide clear guidance anyway
  **Solution:** Establish a specific time each day—such as first thing in the morning—for prayer and Bible reading. Use a calendar or reminder app to track your consistency and adjust your schedule if you miss the appointed time
- **Problem:** You receive what appears to be clear guidance from the Holy Spirit, but you delay acting on it or deviate from it without seeking further clarification
  **Solution:** Write down the specific guidance you have received. Set a specific date by which you will either act on it or seek further clarification. Do not deviate from the guidance without a specific, scriptural reason and confirmation from mature believers
