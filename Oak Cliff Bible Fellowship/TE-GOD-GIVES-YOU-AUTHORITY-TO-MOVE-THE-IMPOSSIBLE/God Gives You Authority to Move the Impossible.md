# God Gives You Authority to Move the Impossible

## Overview

Kingdom authority is the specific right and responsibility God gives every believer to exercise His power on earth, as described in Mark 11:22-25 and Matthew 17:20. This sermon teaches you how to operate in that authority by moving from a passive "coach level" of faith—where you are saved but do not actively exercise authority over problems—to a "first class" experience where you speak to mountains, believes God has already answered, and actively exercises kingdom authority. Through the story of the fig tree in Mark 11 and the boy delivered from demonic oppression in Matthew 17, you will learn a clear, actionable framework for engaging with God about your problems while speaking directly to those problems with the truth of who God is.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are facing a problem, obstacle, or "mountain"—any situation that is beyond your natural ability to fix, such as a health crisis, financial shortage, broken relationship, or emotional pain that you cannot resolve on your own
- When you feel stuck in a passive Christian experience and want to move into a deeper, more powerful walk with God
- When your prayers seem unanswered and you need to understand how faith, authority, and God's timing work together
- When you are struggling with doubt, mixed messages, or a belief system that cancels out your faith
- When you need to forgive someone before you can experience breakthrough in a difficult situation
- When you are ready to stop merely surviving and start exercising the authority Jesus died to give you

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify Your Mountain and Speak to It

A mountain is any problem, obstacle, or situation that is beyond your natural ability to fix—whether it is financial lack, health issues, broken relationships, or emotional pain. The first action God calls you to take is not to talk to God about the mountain; it is to speak directly to the mountain itself. To speak to the mountain means to verbally declare God's character, truth, and Word directly to the problem—out loud, not merely in your thoughts, and not merely by talking to someone else about it. Say who God is in relation to your situation—His power, His faithfulness, His ability to fix what you cannot. verbally declare specific scriptures and truths about who God is directly to the problem itself. This is the first conversation God calls you to have.

### Step 2: Talk to God and Believe You Have Already Received

After you have spoken to the mountain, turn to God in prayer about the same situation. This is the second conversation, and it requires a different posture. Speaking to God means praying to Him about the same mountain while believing the answer is already accomplished by God in heaven before it becomes visible on earth. Believe that the answer has already been received, even if you cannot see it yet. what God has declared is already true in heaven, even if it is not yet visible on earth, and faith connects what God has already declared in heaven to what you will eventually see on earth. Faith in God means trusting in the specific character and promises of God as revealed in Scripture, not trusting in your own ability, other people, or vague positive thinking. Expect that there may be a gap of time between what God has already accomplished before it becomes physically visible and what becomes visible physically, but stand firm in knowing it is already done.

### Step 3: Examine Your Belief System and Remove Unbelief

Place your trust in God Himself, not in the amount of faith you have or in your own ability to believe. Examine whether you are mixing belief with unbelief—saying "He is able" with one side of your mouth while doubting with the other. Confess that faith accesses what God has already purposed to do; it does not force God's hand or make Him act outside of His will. Make your thoughts, beliefs, and spoken words consistent with what God has said, removing any thoughts that contradict His Word so that you are fully trusting in who God is and what He has already established. Anything less than complete trust without wavering or mixed messages in God's Word cancels the effectiveness of your declaration.

### Step 4: Forgive Anyone You Are Holding a Debt Against

Before you pray, forgive anyone who has wronged you. Forgiveness means choosing to no longer require a debt to be paid by the person who wronged you. It does not require reconciliation or trust, only the release of your demand for justice. When you forgive others, you release God to forgive you and to remove the problem that exists because of your own choices, actions, or sin. This is a non-negotiable qualification for exercising kingdom authority and seeing breakthrough.

## Examples

### Example 1: Overcoming a Financial Crisis

You are facing a severe financial shortage that seems impossible to resolve. First, identify your mountain and speak to it: "Finances, you are a mountain, but God is my Provider. He supplies all my needs according to His riches in glory. I speak to this lack and declare that God has already provided for me." Then, talk to God and believe you have already received: "Father, I thank You that You have already made a way for me. I believe I have received the provision I need, even though I do not see it yet." Forgive anyone you have bitterness toward, identify and reject any thoughts that contradict what God has said, and choose to believe God's Word instead, continue to speak God's truth and believe His Word even when circumstances look unchanged, knowing that the provision is already done in heaven and will manifest on earth.

### Example 2: Healing from a Long-Term Illness

You are dealing with a health issue that doctors cannot resolve. Identify your mountain and speak directly to it: "Sickness, I speak to you. By His stripes I am healed. God has already provided healing for me, and I declare that His power is greater than this diagnosis." Then, talk to God and believe you have already received: "Lord, I thank You that my healing is already secured. I believe I have received it, and I trust Your timing to make it visible." Ensure your heart is free from unforgiveness by forgiving anyone you are holding a debt against, make your beliefs match exactly what God has said, without compromise, and continue to speak God's truth and believe His Word even when circumstances look unchanged, knowing that God's Word has already accomplished the work.

### Example 3: Restoring a Broken Relationship

You are grieving over a relationship that has ended or become estranged. Identify your mountain and speak to the situation itself: "Brokenness, I speak to you. God is a restorer of what is lost. I declare that His plan for healing and reconciliation is already at work." Then, talk to God and believe you have already received: "Father, I thank You that You are already working behind the scenes. I believe the restoration I need has already been received in Your sight." Forgive the other person, release any demand for justice or payment, make your beliefs match exactly what God has said, without compromise, and hold fast to the truth that God's authority can turn even this around.

## Best Practices

- Place your trust in God Himself, not in the size of your faith, your circumstances, or other people
- Speak directly to the mountain, bringing God's character, truth, and Word verbally to the problem itself
- Believe you have already received the answer when you talk to God, even if you cannot see it yet
- Forgive anyone you are holding a grudge against before you pray for breakthrough; forgiveness means releasing your demand for a debt to be paid, not requiring reconciliation
- Expect a gap between what God has already accomplished before it becomes physically visible and what you see manifested physically
- Stand on God's truth rather than your feelings or current circumstances
- Examine your belief system regularly to remove any unbelief that cancels your faith

## Keep In Mind

- God does not do for you what He has already told you to do. He has instructed you to speak to the problem, so take authority over it directly.
- There is often a gap of time between what God declares and what you see. The gap of time is the period between when God's Word takes effect in the unseen spiritual realm before it becomes visible in the physical world and when it becomes visible in the physical world. Like the fig tree that withered at the root before it was visibly dead above ground, God's Word may be working invisibly before it becomes obvious.
- God's sovereign will that operates independently of human faith or action means that He may choose not to fix, heal, or change certain things. Do not let this stop you from obeying what He has called you to do, because in the situations where He does want to move, you will be ready to see it.
- Forgiveness releases God's hand. It does not mean you must reconcile or ignore consequences; it means you stop demanding that a debt be paid.
- You are exercising kingdom authority in an authorized manner when you meet the qualifications God has laid out in Mark 11:22-25: faith in God, speaking to the mountain, speaking to God, and forgiving others.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not use this teaching as a reason to blame others for their suffering. Kingdom authority is about what God has authorized believers to do, not a condemnation of those who are still waiting on breakthrough.
- Avoid using this message to claim healing or provision for someone else without their consent or without clear biblical direction. Authority is given to the individual believer for their own situation.
- Be cautious not to confuse faith with presumption. Faith rests in what God has already said; presumption assumes God will act outside of His revealed will.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Putting faith in faith itself, constantly trying to increase the amount of faith you have rather than placing your trust in God.
  **Solution:** Stop focusing on the size of your faith and focus on the object of your faith—God Himself. Faith only works when it rests in a worthy object.
- **Problem:** Only talking to God about the mountain instead of speaking to the mountain about God.
  **Solution:** Follow the biblical pattern in Mark 11:23—speak directly to the problem, delivering God's truth and character to it verbally.
- **Problem:** Mixing unbelief with faith, which cancels out the effectiveness of your declaration.
  **Solution:** Align your words and beliefs so that you are fully convinced of what God has already done. Do not say "He is able" with one breath and "I don't know if it will happen" with the next.
- **Problem:** Refusing to forgive, which blocks the very breakthrough you are asking God for.
  **Solution:** Forgive anyone you have anything against before you pray. Forgiveness is not optional when it comes to moving mountains. It means releasing your demand for a debt to be paid, not requiring reconciliation or trust.
- **Problem:** Becoming disappointed with God when He does not act in the way or timing you expect.
  **Solution:** Remember that God may have reasons sovereignly outside your understanding. Obey what He has told you to do, and trust Him with the results.
