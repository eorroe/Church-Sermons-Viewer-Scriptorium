# Living Under God's Authority Is the Secret to Freedom

## Overview

Dr. Tony Evans teaches that true spiritual power and freedom come when believers choose to obey a specific command of scripture when it conflicts with their preference, study God's truth daily, and practice yielding to the Holy Spirit's direction. The Holy Spirit's direction will (1) align with scripture, (2) produce peace rather than anxiety, and (3) lead you toward loving others — not merely toward personal comfort. The kingdom of God is the reign of Jesus Christ over every area of life, expressed through obedience to His commands as revealed in scripture. When you come under His authority through faith in Christ, you receive the Holy Spirit's power and prepare a specific story about what God has done in your life — including the situation, the scripture that guided your response, and the outcome. Just as bank agents learn to spot counterfeit money by studying authentic currency, believers must be grounded in kingdom truth to recognize deception and experience the Spirit's enabling power in everyday life.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel powerless as a believer and want to experience the Holy Spirit's power in your daily life
- When you are confused about how to make decisions and want the Holy Spirit to guide you into truth
- When you encounter ideas in culture, media, or relationships that contradict what you know about God
- When you want to develop a personal testimony of God's work but feel you have nothing to share
- When you sense you are being shaped more by values that prioritize self-gratification, material success, or social approval over obedience to Christ than by God's kingdom values
- When you want to know how to practically submit every area of your life to God's authority

## Steps

### Step 1: Study and Declare the Kingdom of God's Authority

Read through the Gospels to see how Jesus taught about the kingdom of God. Write down what "kingdom" means in your own words — the reign of Jesus Christ over every area of life, expressed through obedience to His commands as revealed in scripture. Memorize key kingdom-focused verses such as Matthew 6:33 and Mark 1:15. Look at the book of Acts and list every reference to the kingdom of God you can find. Tell someone this week what you learned about God's kingdom authority.

### Step 2: Confirm Your Submission to God's Jurisdiction

If you have not yet been baptized, speak with your pastor about taking this step. If you have been baptized, verbally renew your commitment today: "I live in this world, but I submit to God's rule over every area of my life — my thoughts, my relationships, my work, my finances, and my future." Write down three specific areas where you will choose to obey a specific command of scripture when it conflicts with your preference this week.

### Step 3: Receive the Holy Spirit as Your Power Source

Pray and ask Jesus to fill you with the Holy Spirit. If you are already a believer, thank God that the Spirit lives within you. Read Acts 1:8 and Acts 2:1-4 to understand the Spirit's purpose. Write down one area of your life where you need the Holy Spirit's power right now. Ask God to give you that power today and tell a trusted believer what you asked for.

### Step 4: Practice Daily Connection to the Holy Spirit

Set a specific time each day to pray and invite the Holy Spirit to fill you. Write down the difference between having the Holy Spirit at salvation and experiencing His power daily. A prompt from the Holy Spirit will (1) align with scripture, (2) produce peace rather than anxiety, and (3) lead you toward loving others. Ask God to show you any area where you feel spiritually disconnected. Confess any sin that may be blocking your connection. Read Galatians 5:16-26 and write down one way you can walk in the Spirit today instead of walking in your own strength.

### Step 5: Practice Listening to the Holy Spirit's Guidance

Before making a decision today, stop and pray: "Holy Spirit, guide me into the truth of this situation." Read John 16:13 and write down what it means to you. Keep a short journal this week of every time the Holy Spirit prompts you. Write down what the prompt was, what you did, and what the result was. A prompt from the Holy Spirit will (1) align with scripture, (2) produce peace rather than anxiety, and (3) lead you toward loving others. Look for patterns in how the Spirit speaks to you.

### Step 6: Commit Daily to Kingdom Truth

Choose one area of your life where you have been compromising with values that prioritize self-gratification, material success, or social approval over obedience to Christ. Write down God's perspective on that area from scripture. Make a specific commitment to align that area with kingdom truth starting today. Memorize one verse that defines truth for that area. Share your commitment with an accountability partner who can pray for you and check in on your progress.

### Step 7: Study Authentic Truth to Recognize Counterfeit Values

Set a goal to read through the entire Bible in one year, or join a Bible study group at your church. When you encounter a cultural message that feels true but contradicts scripture, write it down and compare it against the Bible. Ask yourself: "Does this align with God's kingdom values?" Practice explaining to a friend why a popular cultural idea that prioritizes self-gratification, material success, or social approval is counterfeit compared to the reign of Jesus Christ over every area of life, expressed through obedience to His commands as revealed in scripture. The more you know God's word, the faster you will spot deception.

### Step 8: Share Your Testimony of God's Work

Write down three specific things God has done in your life — a specific event, the scripture that guided your response, and the outcome — ready to share in under two minutes. Practice sharing one of these testimonies with a friend or family member this week. When you see God moving in your life, write it down immediately so you remember it later. Tell someone about what God is doing in your home, your work, or your church. Your testimony becomes powerful when you share it.

## Examples

### Example 1: Philip Preaching the Kingdom in Samaria

Philip went to Samaria and preached about the kingdom of God and Jesus Christ. The people believed and were baptized, entering a new sphere of God's authority in their lives (Acts 8:12). Action: This week, share the gospel with someone by explaining both who Jesus is and what it means to live under God's kingdom rule. Do not share only facts about Jesus — share what it means to submit to His authority.

### Example 2: Paul Testifying About the Kingdom

Paul stayed in Rome for two years, preaching the kingdom of God and teaching about Jesus Christ with boldness and without restriction (Acts 28:23, 30-31). Action: Pick one person you can consistently share the kingdom message with over the next two weeks. Tell them about God's rule and how Jesus has authority over every part of life. Be open and unhindered in your sharing, just as Paul was.

### Example 3: Counterfeit Money Detection

Bank agents learn to spot counterfeit money by studying real currency — not by studying fakes. They handle authentic bills until they know the feel, the color, the texture, and the watermarks so well that any fake is immediately obvious. Action: Spend 15 minutes today reading a Psalm or a passage from the Gospels. Do this every day this week. By day seven, you will be more familiar with God's truth and better able to spot ideas that contradict His word.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Study the kingdom of God consistently so you can recognize error when it comes. Self-check: Did I read or listen to scripture about God's kingdom this week?
- ✅ Stay plugged into the Holy Spirit to experience His power in your life. Self-check: Did I pray and invite the Holy Spirit's power today?
- ✅ Allow the Holy Spirit to guide you into all truth progressively. Self-check: Did I pause today to ask the Holy Spirit for guidance before making a decision?
- ✅ Submit every area of your life to God's authority and rule. Self-check: Is there any area of my life where I am making decisions about relationships, finances, or time without consulting scripture or the Holy Spirit?
- ✅ Develop your testimony by letting God work through you. Self-check: Did I share what God is doing in my life with someone this week?
- ❌ Don't abandon kingdom truth for secular thinking or cultural values
- ❌ Don't expect the power of the Holy Spirit without committing to kingdom truth
- ❌ Don't try to make the kingdom of God conform to the kingdom of culture
- ❌ Don't be satisfied with just having the Holy Spirit without being plugged into Him
- ❌ Don't try to bear a testimony when you haven't been tethered to kingdom truth

## Keep In Mind

- God's kingdom is His rule over every area of your life, not just your church attendance. Check whether you have submitted your work, finances, relationships, and thoughts to His authority.
- The Holy Spirit activates the new nature you received at salvation. If you feel powerless, check your connection — are you praying, reading scripture, and yielding to Him daily?
- Having the Holy Spirit inside you is not the same as walking in His power. Ask yourself: "Am I trying to solve problems through willpower, self-reliance, or anxiety without prayer or scripture today, or am I depending on the Spirit?"
- God guides you into truth at the pace you can handle. Do not become overwhelmed by trying to understand everything at once. Trust the Spirit's timing.
- Cultural values often look attractive but lead away from God. Test every idea, goal, and desire against scripture before accepting it as truth — ask: Does this align with God's word? Does it produce peace? Does it lead me to love others?
- Your testimony grows when you experience God's power and then share it. Write down what God does so you do not forget, and tell someone about it.
- When God does something real in your life, tell someone. Do not keep it to yourself — your testimony encourages others and brings glory to God.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Wanting the power of the Holy Spirit without being committed to kingdom truth
  **Solution:** Before asking God for power, first choose to obey a specific command of scripture by reading one chapter of the Bible, confessing any sin you are aware of, and praying specifically: "Holy Spirit, I yield to Your guidance today." Do this daily for one week and observe the difference in your daily experience.

- **Problem:** Being culturalized rather than kingdomized by adopting secular values
  **Solution:** When you hear a cultural message, write down its main claim. Then find one Bible verse that speaks to that topic — specifically, one that aligns with the reign of Jesus Christ over every area of life. Compare the two. If the cultural message contradicts scripture, it is a counterfeit. Practice this once a day this week with one idea from social media, news, or conversation.

- **Problem:** Not having a testimony because you're not plugged into the Holy Spirit
  **Solution:** Check your connection to the Holy Spirit each morning by asking: "Am I choosing to obey a specific command of scripture when it conflicts with my preference today?" If yes, expect the Spirit to work through you. Write down one thing God does through you today — a kind word, a moment of patience, or a conviction to share the gospel. That is your testimony.

- **Problem:** Trying to make the kingdom of God conform to the kingdom of culture
  **Solution:** Recognize that there is a competing influence that values that prioritize self-gratification, material success, or social approval over obedience to Christ. When you face pressure to compromise, identify the specific pressure, name the kingdom value that opposes it, state your commitment out loud, and tell one person who can hold you accountable. Each morning, review one area where culture pressures you to compromise. Write down one kingdom value that opposes that pressure and choose today to stand by it.
