# How to Get Your Prayers Answered

## Overview

This sermon explains how prayer functions as the mechanism by which God's conditional will is released from heaven into earth. Tony Evans teaches that prayer is not a religious ritual but relational communication that draws eternity into time, and that effective prayer requires faith, righteousness, and persistence. The message uses James 5:13-18 and the story of Elijah to show how believers can partner with God through prayer to see His will accomplished in history.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand why some prayers seem unanswered
- When working through seasons of suffering, weariness, or illness
- When the user asks about how to pray with power and effectiveness
- When building a foundation for a consistent prayer life

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That Prayer Accesses God's Conditional Will

Understand that God has both an unconditional will and a conditional will. His conditional will requires human cooperation through prayer. Before you pray, God has already answered (Isaiah 65:24), but you must pull that answer from heaven into history through prayer. Prayer is not convincing God to do something He does not want to do; it is partnering with what He already desires.

### Step 2: Pray Without Ceasing as a Lifestyle

Make prayer a continual posture, not a single event. Whether you are suffering or cheerful, you should be in communication with God. Pain is an invitation to pray, and blessings are an invitation to praise. A lifestyle of prayer keeps you connected to heaven so you do not miss what God is doing.

### Step 3: Use the Word of God as Your Foundation

Pray with your Bible open. Point God back to His promises and align your prayers with Scripture. Elijah prayed based on God's Word, not his own imagination. The Bible is full of prayers you can pray for specific situations—Psalm 42 for struggle, Psalm 51 for repentance, Psalm 34 for fear.

### Step 4: Pray with Earnestness and Expectation

Approach God with focused, energized faith. Do not pray casually or out of habit. Elijah crouched down with his face between his knees—a posture of labor and urgency. Faith is not based on what you see; it is based on hearing God's voice and trusting Him before the manifestation appears.

### Step 5: Persist Through Opposition

Do not give up when the answer is delayed. Elijah sent his servant to look for rain seven times before he saw even a fist-sized cloud. Opposition and delay are not signs that God is not moving. Keep praying until you receive a clear yes, no, or wait.

### Step 6: Seek Spiritual Cover and Confession

When you are too weary to pray on your own, call on the elders of the church. Do not be a lone ranger Christian. Confess any unrepented sin to a trusted spiritual person who will pray for you, because sin can block healing and keep you in weariness. Allow the church to minister to you through prayer, anointing, and practical help.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Healing of the Weary

James 5:14-15 teaches that when someone is sick or weary, they should call the elders of the church to pray over them and anoint them with oil. The prayer offered in faith will restore the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up. If there is sin involved, it will be forgiven. This demonstrates the community nature of prayer and the power of focused faith to bring restoration.

### Example 2: Elijah and the Rain

Elijah was a man with a nature like ours, yet he prayed earnestly that it would not rain, and it did not rain for three and a half years. Then he prayed again, and the sky poured rain. He did not pray out of wishful thinking; he prayed based on the Word of God. His servant had to look seven times before seeing the first sign of the storm. This shows that ordinary people can accomplish much through effectual, fervent prayer aligned with God's will.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Treat prayer as a 24/7 lifestyle, not a religious event
- ✅ Pray with your Bible open and align your requests with Scripture
- ✅ Expect God to answer before you see the physical result
- ✅ Persist in prayer until you receive a clear yes, no, or wait
- ✅ Confess sin to trusted spiritual leaders who will pray for you
- ✅ Be an "oil provider" for others who are weary or suffering
- ❌ Do not treat prayer as a meaningless tradition disconnected from your life
- ❌ Do not pray casually or without faith
- ❌ Do not try to be a lone ranger Christian
- ❌ Do not ignore unconfessed sin that may be blocking your prayers

## Keep In Mind

- God's conditional will requires your active participation through prayer
- You are not praying to make God do something He does not want to do; you are pulling down what He has already determined
- Weariness is not a sign to quit praying; it is an invitation to seek help and pray more
- The church exists so that believers can carry each other when they cannot carry themselves
- Faith is hearing God's voice and acting on it before you see the full manifestation

## Security & Safety Notes

- Only confess sin to trusted spiritual leaders who will pray for you, not gossip about you
- Do not share intimate prayer needs with those who have not proven themselves to be prayer warriors
- Anointing with oil and prayer are spiritual practices meant for deliverance and healing, not harm or manipulation
- Spiritual authority should always be exercised in Jesus' name and according to His character

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating prayer like the national anthem before a sporting game—something you do out of tradition but that has no connection to real life.
  **Solution:** Understand that prayer is the passport to heaven. It changes your location from the physical to the spiritual, and it is the primary way God releases His will on earth.

- **Problem:** Giving up when prayer is delayed or when weariness sets in.
  **Solution:** Remember that pain and weariness are invitations to pray more, not less. Call on the elders and the body of Christ to stand with you when you cannot stand alone.

- **Problem:** Praying without the Word of God or without faith.
  **Solution:** Pray with your Bible open. Point God back to His promises. Faith comes by hearing the Word of God, so let Scripture anchor your prayers.
