# Trust God's Plan When It Doesn't Make Sense

## Overview

This sermon explains why God allows believers to face dilemmas that seem to contradict His promises and character. Using Exodus 13-14, Dr. Tony Evans shows how God led Israel into a trap at the Red Sea to reveal His power and transform them from slaves into overcomers. You will learn that a dilemma is not evidence of God's absence but a classroom where He teaches you to trust Him when nothing makes sense. The core message is that God will turn your impossible situation into a testimony so long as you stop complaining against His appointed leaders, submit to spiritually mature counsel, and move forward in faith when He gives clear direction.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are caught between two impossibilities and cannot see an exit strategy
- When you have prayed for deliverance but the situation has gotten worse instead of better
- When you are tempted to complain against spiritually mature leaders or return to past bondage because the present pain is too great

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Trap Is Purposeful

Understand that God has allowed this dilemma because He is ready to give you a deeper trust in Him. A dilemma has no visible exit, and every direction looks like a trap, but that is exactly where God reveals Himself. If you are in the will of God and walking by faith, this confusion is not punishment—it is preparation. Stop trying to escape the situation through your own wisdom and instead expect God to show up in the impossible.

### Step 2: Stop the Mixed Signals

Do not cry out to God while simultaneously complaining to the spiritual leaders God has placed in your life. Complaining reinforces the frustration of your dilemma and blinds you to the biblical perspective that mature believers can offer. Silence your complaints and submit to those who walk with God, know Scripture, and can speak truth into your situation. When you are confused, only God can give you clarity, and He often speaks through spiritually mature authority figures.

### Step 3: Stand Still Until God Tells You to Move

When God puts you in a dilemma and there is nothing you should or can do, wait on the Lord for His timing and instructions rather than taking matters into your own hands. When God finally tells you to move forward through Scripture, prayer, or godly counsel, obey immediately. Faith is in your feet, not in your feelings, and you must move even when fear is still present. Fear immobilizes, but faith overrides fear and advances when God gives the command.

### Step 4: Watch God Honor His Word

When you obey in faith, God will harden the hearts of your enemies and make them pursue you so that His power can be displayed. He will fight for you while you keep silent and trust Him. In the Exodus account, the Egyptians who pursued Israel were destroyed, and Israel witnessed God's power for the first time. God uses the trap to deliver you from your past and move you into a new tomorrow that only He can provide.

## Examples

### Example 1: Red Sea Trap

Israel had just escaped Egypt and found themselves trapped between the Red Sea and Pharaoh's advancing army. They cried out to God but simultaneously blamed Moses, wanting to return to slavery. God hardened Pharaoh's heart so that the pursuit would force Israel to witness God's power. When Moses stretched out his rod, the sea opened, Israel crossed on dry ground, and the Egyptian army was destroyed. This illustrates how God uses a no-exit situation to transition His people from yesterday's bondage into tomorrow's promise.

### Example 2: Modern Career Dilemma

A believer is fired from a job despite praying for provision and acting with integrity. Every job application leads to rejection, and financial pressure mounts. The circumstance looks like a trap with no way out. Instead of complaining or returning to old unhealthy patterns, the believer submits to spiritual counsel, stands still in prayer, and waits for God's direction. When God opens the right door, the new position is not just a job but a divine promotion that could only have come through the impossible valley.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Cry out to God alone without shifting blame to the spiritual leaders He has placed in your life
- ✅ Submit to spiritually mature authority when you lack biblical perspective
- ✅ Move by faith when God speaks, even if fear is still present
- ✅ Keep silent before the Lord so He can fight your battles
- ❌ Do not complain about spiritual leaders while claiming to trust God
- ❌ Do not try to force an exit from a dilemma before God says to move
- ❌ Do not let fear immobilize you when faith gives you a next step

## Keep In Mind

- God is not caught off guard by your dilemma—He allowed it for a purpose.
- Your position as an overcomer in Christ must become your personal experience through trust.
- A dilemma is not evidence that God has abandoned you; it is evidence that He is about to do something new.
- Spiritual authority can see what you cannot see when you are trapped in confusion.
- Faith does not eliminate fear, but it overrides fear when you act on God's word.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not ignore legitimate warning signs of abuse or danger under the guise of "trusting God." A dilemma is a God-ordained circumstance for spiritual growth, not a license for others to harm you.
- If you are in a dangerous situation, seek immediate help from law enforcement and trusted authorities while also seeking God in prayer.
- Spiritual authority should never pressure you to stay in harm's way or violate your conscience.
- Test every leading and instruction against Scripture and the character of Christ before acting on it.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You confuse a God-ordained dilemma with a toxic situation and try to fix it yourself before God says move.
  **Solution:** Submit the situation to prayer and spiritually mature authority. Wait for clear direction from God before making drastic changes.
- **Problem:** You cry out to God while simultaneously blaming and complaining to the spiritual leaders God has placed in your life.
  **Solution:** Separate your petitions to God from your responses to people. Silence your complaints so God can speak through the spiritual authority He has provided.
- **Problem:** You look at the impossibility of the trap and become immobilized by fear instead of moving when God gives you a command.
  **Solution:** Remember that faith is in your feet. Fear is normal, but it must not stop you from obeying God's specific instruction to move forward.
