# It's Your Time to Rise Above Every Challenge

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God's plan is greater than human planning, and that God provides the power of choice to rise above life's challenges. Through Steve Harvey's personal testimony of losing everything in 2005 and experiencing restoration in God's higher purpose, Steve Harvey's message emphasizes trusting God's higher purpose, recognizing the role of supportive relationships, and understanding that aligning your mind with victory positions you for divine elevation.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing unexpected setbacks or losses that seem to wipe you out
- When your own plans have failed and you need to surrender to God's greater design
- When you need encouragement to trust that restoration and promotion are still possible

## Steps

### Step 1: Surrender Your Plan to God's Plan

Trust that God's plan is always better than your own. Steve Harvey reveals he wanted to be a premier standup comedian, but God had a bigger plan—making him part of the Kings of Comedy, the biggest comedy tour in history. Accept that God's elevation may come in forms you did not anticipate, and His intention is to bring you greater success than you imagined for yourself.

### Step 2: Exercise the Power of Choice

God gives you the power of choice and allows you to work your plan. Even when circumstances seem impossible, you have the authority to choose your response. Steve Harvey emphasizes that your choices determine your trajectory—choose to believe in restoration rather than defeat. Say with conviction, "I'll do whatever God tells me to do," and release your need to control the outcome.

### Step 3: Rise with Support and Experience Victory

Lean on the people God places around you and experience victory firsthand. When Steve Harvey was at his lowest point with only $1,700 left, his wife Margie stood with him and helped him climb out. Do not be afraid to ask for help from trusted people, and invest in positive experiences—such as first-class travel, luxury items, or victory celebrations—that condition your mind for success. Let those who believe in you pull you up when you cannot pull yourself up.

### Step 4: Activate the Law of Attraction

Align your mindset with the level of success and influence God has promised you. Just as buying a first-class ticket or an expensive outfit conditions your mind to think and act from a mindset of abundance and success, you must mentally position yourself in victory. Your subconscious will then produce the thoughts and actions that lead to your desired outcome. Experience first class in your mind before you see it in your life, because what you consistently think about, you will attract.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Comedy Career That Surpassed Every Expectation

The speaker dreamed of being a premier standup comedian, and achieved the Kings of Comedy tour—the biggest in comedy history. Yet in 2005, God allowed a devastating setback where he lost everything, including his marriage and finances, down to only $1,700. This was not the end but a repositioning for a greater comeback. From 2005 to the present, Steve Harvey's life and career were restored beyond what he imagined, moving from $1,700 to reaching millions through television, radio, and live events. God took Steve Harvey to ministry and influence opportunities he never thought he would go.

### Example 2: The Wife Who Would Not Let Him Stay Down

Facing divorce and financial ruin with only $1,700 to his name, the speaker told his wife Margie he did not even want to get married right now because he had to recover from the divorce. She replied, "We'll recover together." She climbed down into that hole with him, refused to let him stay defeated, and her belief in him became the foundation of his restoration. From 2005 to the present, Margie's unwavering support proved that no one achieves lasting success without trusted people standing beside them.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Trust God's plan even when it differs from your own
- ✅ Exercise the power of choice to believe in restoration
- ✅ Lean on the people God has placed in your life
- ✅ Condition your mind through experiences of victory
- ❌ Don't rely solely on your own understanding or plan
- ❌ Avoid isolating yourself when facing challenges

## Keep In Mind

- God's plan is always better than your plan
- True restoration often comes through the people God places around you
- Your mindset and experiences condition your future success
- The law of attraction—the principle that consistently held thoughts attract corresponding experiences—operates when you align your thoughts with victory
- No one achieves lasting success without the support of trusted people standing beside them

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Relying on your own plan and understanding rather than surrendering to God's higher purpose
  **Solution:** Yield your plans to God, trusting that His design will surpass your expectations and lead to greater outcomes. Say, "I'll do whatever God tells me to do," and release control.

- **Problem:** Giving up when setbacks wipe you out financially or relationally
  **Solution:** Remember that God provides the power of choice and the ability to recover. Stand with those who believe in you and choose restoration over defeat. Do not let pride keep you from climbing down into the hole with the person who is willing to help you rise.
