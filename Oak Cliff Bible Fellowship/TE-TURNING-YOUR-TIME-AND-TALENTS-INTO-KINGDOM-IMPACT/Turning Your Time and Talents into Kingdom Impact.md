# Turning Your Time and Talents into Kingdom Impact

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers to live with an eternal perspective by faithfully stewarding the time, talents, and treasures God has entrusted to them. Using the parable of the ten minas, Dr. Tony Evans explains that salvation is by grace through faith, but once saved, Christians are called to actively do business for God's kingdom until Christ returns. The message emphasizes accountability at the judgment seat of Christ and the eternal rewards that come from expanding what God has given.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand the difference between salvation by grace and rewards for service
- When working through feelings of spiritual aimlessness or passiveness
- When seeking to use your time, talents, and treasures for eternal impact
- When preparing for the judgment seat of Christ and understanding biblical accountability

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize You Have Been Given Three Things

Every believer has been given time, talents, and treasures. You do not have the same amount as others, but everyone has some portion of each. These are God's assets entrusted to you for kingdom use.

### Step 2: Understand Your Assignment

Your assignment is to do business for God until He returns. This means actively using your time, abilities, and resources to advance His kingdom and benefit others. You are not called to be passive or aimless while waiting for Christ's return.

### Step 3: Operate as a Steward

A steward protects and expands the assets of another. You are a steward of God's time, talents, and treasures. Your job is to multiply what He has given, not merely preserve it or return it unchanged.

### Step 4: Do Not Fear the Master's Expectations

Do not let fear of failure or fear of God's high standards keep you from acting. The worthless servant did nothing because he was afraid. Faithful stewardship requires risking and investing what you have been given.

### Step 5: Live With an Eternal Perspective

Your earthly life is brief compared to eternity. How you invest your time, talents, and treasures now will determine your eternal reward. Live every day knowing that God is measuring how your life impacts His eternal kingdom.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Parable of the Ten Minas

A nobleman gave ten servants ten minas each and told them to do business until he returned. Two servants invested and multiplied their minas. One servant hid his mina out of fear and did nothing. When the nobleman returned, the two who multiplied were rewarded with authority over cities, and the one who did nothing had his mina taken away.


### Example 2: Matthew 25 Comparison

Jesus tells a similar story in Matthew 25, but there the servants receive different amounts based on their ability. This sermon highlights that regardless of how much you receive, the expectation is to multiply what has been entrusted to you.

## Best Practices

- ✅ View your time, talents, and treasures as God's assets entrusted to you
- ✅ Actively invest and multiply what God has given
- ✅ Live with the eternal perspective that Christ may return at any moment
- ✅ Use your gifts to serve others and advance God's kingdom
- ❌ Do not be passive or aimless while waiting for Christ's return
- ❌ Do not let fear of failure prevent you from using your gifts
- ❌ Do not think you get credit merely for being a Christian or attending church
- ❌ Do not waste your life building only for this temporary world

## Keep In Mind

- You are saved by grace through faith, not by works (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- Once saved, you are saved for good works that God prepared beforehand (Ephesians 2:10)
- Heaven is not eternal idleness; it is participating in God's rule with responsibilities based on your earthly stewardship
- The judgment seat of Christ evaluates what you did with what was on loan to you

## Security & Safety Notes

- Your salvation is secure by grace, but your rewards are not guaranteed
- Being a servant of God does not automatically mean you will receive commendation; faithful service is required
- Do not rely on religious performance or church attendance as a substitute for genuine kingdom business

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being passive and aimless while waiting for Christ's return
  **Solution:** Recognize that God expects you to do business for His kingdom using your time, talents, and treasures
- **Problem:** Fearing failure or God's high expectations and doing nothing with what you have been given
  **Solution:** Trust God with the outcome and invest what you have, even if it feels risky
- **Problem:** Confusing salvation with rewards and thinking church attendance or a prayer of faith earns eternal rewards
  **Solution:** Understand that salvation is a free gift, but rewards come from faithfully serving and expanding what God entrusted to you
- **Problem:** Living only for this life and investing everything in temporary pursuits
  **Solution:** Keep an eternal perspective and invest in what lasts forever
