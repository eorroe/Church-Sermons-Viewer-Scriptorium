# This Is What Makes The Beatitudes So Important

## Overview

This sermon by Tony Evans explains the Beatitudes as a kingdom values system from Jesus Christ, revealing what kingdom people look like and the divine benefits that come when those values are embraced. It emphasizes that the Beatitudes are not merely quotes to memorize but a kingdom blueprint for living that brings heaven's rule into earthly reality. Each Beatitude is unpacked to show how recognizing spiritual insufficiency, mourning sin, practicing meekness, hungering for righteousness, showing mercy, maintaining purity of heart, making peace, and enduring persecution for righteousness align a believer with God's kingdom and invite His favor.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand what it truly means to live as a citizen of God's kingdom
- When working through personal struggles with self-sufficiency, pride, or spiritual dryness
- When the user asks about the meaning and application of the Beatitudes in daily Christian life
- When seeking to deepen your spiritual dependency on God rather than earthly sufficiency

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize Your Spiritual Insufficiency

Acknowledge before God that you are spiritually poor and cannot achieve righteousness or sufficiency on your own. Confess that without divine provision, you are in trouble, and adopt dependency on God as a lifestyle, not merely a reaction during crises. This means refusing to look to earthly resources as your primary source of strength and instead opening the door for heaven to rule your earthly circumstances.

### Step 2: Cultivate a Mourner's Heart Over Sin

Allow yourself to feel genuine sadness over your own sin and the destructive effects of sin in the world around you. Do not dismiss evil, injustice, or moral decay as trivial. Ask God to give you a heart that hurts when His heart hurts, so you can respond to brokenness with the same sorrow Jesus felt at the tomb of Lazarus and when He saw the pain sin caused.

### Step 3: Practice Meekness Through Submission to God's Will

Choose to submit your plans, relationships, and responses to the will of God rather than insisting on your own way. Meekness is not weakness; it is strength under control that trusts God to defend and intervene on your behalf. When you refuse to fight your own battles and instead yield to Him, you position yourself to receive the earthly inheritance God has already bequeathed to you.

### Step 4: Hunger and Thirst for Righteousness

Replace spiritually unhealthy consumption with a craving for what pleases God. Feed your soul with God's standards, not secular culture's values. Evaluate what you are feeding your mind, emotions, and time, and shift your appetite toward the righteousness that satisfies the soul's true requirement, just as physical food satisfies the body.

### Step 5: Show Mercy to Others Freely

Extend undeserved compassion, patience, and relief to those who have wronged you or are in need, remembering that mercy means not giving people what they deserve. Track the mercy you show others, because when you need mercy from God, He will measure out to you what you have offered. Release the tendency to demand justice while refusing to show grace.

### Step 6: Maintain Purity of Heart Before God

Examine your motives and remove double-mindedness, pretense, and hypocrisy from your relationship with God. Pursue authenticity so that your inward reality matches your outward profession. A pure heart is not sinless perfection but an unmixed, genuine devotion that allows God to manifest His presence in your life in tangible ways.

### Step 7: Become a Peacemaker, Not a Peacekeeper

Actively seek to resolve conflicts and rebuild broken relationships rather than merely keeping the peace through avoidance or compromise. Be a bridge builder who pursues reconciliation because the call of the cross is to reconcile, not divide. Accept that this role may bring public recognition as a son or daughter of God, but do not seek peace merely for reputation's sake.

### Step 8: Endure Persecution for Righteousness with Joy

Hold firmly to biblical standards and your public identification with Christ even when facing rejection, insults, or false accusations. Rejoice when the world pushes back against your stand for righteousness, because every rejection for Christ's sake increases your eternal reward. Refuse to be canceled into silence or ashamed of the name of Jesus.

## Examples

### Example 1: Overcoming Self-Sufficiency Through Spiritual Poverty

A believer who has always prided themselves on independence and hard work reaches a point where their career collapses and their resources are exhausted. Instead of falling into despair or frantic self-reliance, they honestly acknowledge their insufficiency before God and begin to pray and depend on Him daily rather than their own strategies. Over time, they experience God's provision and direction in ways they never could have engineered, confirming that heaven has become their sufficiency.

### Example 2: Choosing Meekness in Family Conflict

A family member publicly criticizes and undermines a believer's marriage and ministry decisions. Rather than retaliating with harsh words or mounting a defense, the believer submits the situation to God, refuses to fight their own battles, and continues walking in integrity. God intervenes on the believer's behalf, the accuser's credibility is discredited, and the believer experiences the inheritance of peace and vindication that meekness brings.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Treat the Beatitudes as a kingdom lifestyle system, not as inspirational quotes
- ✅ Practice spiritual dependency by beginning each day with prayer and Scripture, confessing your dependence on God before every major decision, conversation, and challenge, and refusing to rely on your own strategies
- ✅ Allow the Holy Spirit to convict you of sin so you can mourn its effects genuinely
- ✅ Submit your will to God's will before crisis forces you to do so
- ✅ Audit your heart each morning and evening for double-mindedness, pretense, and hypocrisy by asking God to reveal any hidden motives before you begin the day and before you rest
- ✅ Replace worldly media, entertainment, and input with Scripture and God-centered content at every opportunity throughout the day
- ✅ Confess your dependence on God aloud before every major decision, conversation, and challenge rather than relying on your own strategies
- ✅ Offer mercy to others in every conflict or need before asking God for mercy in your own situation
- ✅ Pursue reconciliation actively even when it is costly or uncomfortable, starting with the closest relationship that needs restoration
- ✅ Tell others about your faith in Christ in conversations, social media, and community settings so your identification with Him is unmistakable

## Keep In Mind

- Blessed means divine favor designed to flow through you, not just personal happiness
- The kingdom of God is the realm of divine rule that God wants to transplant onto Earth
- Kingdom values often contradict worldly values and will require counter-cultural choices
- Inheritance in the Beatitudes refers to present, earthly deliverance, not only future heaven
- God's reward for persecution is real and is being recorded continuously for those who stand firm
- A mourner's heart aligns you with God's own heart and invites His comfort
- Peacemakers reflect the reconciling nature of the cross

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not confuse kingdom meekness with being a doormat; meekness submits to God, not to people's manipulation
- Mercy does not mean enabling ongoing sin or removing natural consequences; it means undeserved compassion within wise boundaries
- Publicly identifying as a Christian carries social risk, but hiding your faith compromises your witness and your reward
- Mourning sin should not drive you to despair but to repentance and dependence on God's comfort
- Persecution for righteousness is not an excuse for reckless behavior or seeking conflict unnecessarily

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Reading the Beatitudes as general moral advice rather than kingdom values with divine consequences
  **Solution:** Read each Beatitude in the context of Matthew 5 and ask what kingdom benefit is promised for each value
- **Problem:** Mistaking meekness for weakness and allowing people to walk all over you without godly boundaries
  **Solution:** Remember Moses was the meekest man on earth, yet God Himself defended Moses when he was wronged
- **Problem:** Wanting mercy from God but refusing to extend it to others
  **Solution:** Recognize that Luke 6:38 measures your receiving by your giving; audit your mercy account regularly
- **Problem:** Feeding the soul secular values and wondering why you feel spiritually empty and unsatisfied
  **Solution:** Hunger for righteousness intentionally by replacing worldly media and input with Scripture and God-centered content
- **Problem:** Fearing rejection and hiding your Christian identity in public settings
  **Solution:** Accept that if Christ went public on the cross for you, you can go public for Him, and rejection for righteousness is a cause for rejoicing
