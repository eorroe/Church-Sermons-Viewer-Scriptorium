# Become the Man God Created You to Be

## Overview

This sermon confronts the modern crisis of masculine identity and calls men to abandon cultural confusion and step into the God-defined role of a "kingdom man." Tony Evans explains that true manhood is not about passivity, violence, or cultural performance, but about three God-given responsibilities: providing, protecting, and spiritually leading. When men embrace these roles under divine ownership rather than cultural pressure, they become stabilizing forces for their families, churches, communities, and ultimately the world.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are a man seeking biblical clarity on what it means to be a man in today's culture
- When you feel pressure from conflicting messages about masculinity and want a godly standard
- When you want to understand how your personal walk with God impacts your family and community
- When you are struggling with passivity, anger, or confusion about your role as a husband, father, or leader
- When you desire to lead your household spiritually but don't know where to start
- When you recognize that broken men produce broken families, churches, and communities

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That God Owns Your Manhood

Understand that your manhood does not belong to society, culture, your father, or your own feelings. God created you and therefore defines what it means to be a man. Reject the false definitions of "toxic masculinity" and cultural passivity. God's ownership of your manhood means he sets the standard.

### Step 2: Reject Both Cultural Extremes

Avoid the passivity of men who have been "nuted and decaffeinated," domesticated, and silent while their families and communities suffer. Also avoid the violent, abusive extreme where men hurt others and refuse responsibility. Neither extreme reflects the kingdom man God created you to be.

### Step 3: Embrace Your Three God-Given Responsibilities

God gave Adam three specific roles that define biblical manhood:

1. **Cultivate (Provide/Produce)** - God put Adam in the garden to work and produce. An physically capable man who is unwilling to work is not fulfilling his purpose. You are called to be a provider for those under your care.
2. **Keep (Protect)** - Guard and protect the realm God has entrusted to you. Make your home and sphere of influence a secure, safe place where people know you are on the front lines defending them.
3. **Pastor (Spiritual Leader)** - Be the spiritual leader of your household. Receive God's Word and transfer it to those in your house. Lead your family to serve the Lord.

### Step 4: Position Yourself Under Divine Authority

A kingdom man operates under the authority of Christ. Christ is under God, every man is under Christ, and a woman is under her husband. When you refuse to submit to Christ and God's Word, you become subject to the corrupting influences of culture. Submit to God's authority so you can lead others rightly.

### Step 5: Speak and Act on God's Word

Like the wise man who built his house on the rock, hear God's Word and act on it. Do not ask everyone in your house for their opinion. Declare: "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." Execute this standard with love and obedience, and your leadership will be established by what people see, not by your title.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Provider Role

A man who refuses to work or provides only when convenient is like Adam before Eve was created. God wanted to know Adam would be willing to get up and go to work before bringing anyone into his life. A modern example is a husband who stays in bed while his wife works and children fend for themselves. This man has not graduated to biblical manhood because he is not producing and providing.

### Example 2: The Protector Role

Adam was told to "keep" the garden, meaning to guard it. When Satan entered the garden, Adam was present but silent. A contemporary example is a father who allows harmful media, ungodly influences, or dangerous relationships into his home without speaking up. The kingdom man is the filter of his house—he guards what enters so his family remains secure.

### Example 3: The Spiritual Leader

When Eve was deceived, Adam was with her but failed to lead spiritually. A modern example is a husband who leaves spiritual decisions to his wife or never discusses God's Word with his family. The kingdom man takes initiative to learn Scripture, pray with his family, and lead them to serve the Lord, even when he doesn't have all the answers.

### Example 4: The Wise Man vs. The Fool

Jesus described two men building houses in Matthew 7. The wise man built on the rock (God's Word) and his house stood through every storm. The fool built on sand (cultural opinion, personal desire) and his house collapsed. A kingdom man builds his marriage, family, and life on the unchanging Word of God rather than shifting cultural values.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Accept that God created you and owns your manhood; let his definition, not culture's, guide you
- ✅ Cultivate by working diligently and providing for your household
- ✅ Keep your home by guarding against harmful influences and standing as a protector
- ✅ Be the spiritual leader who reads, speaks, and lives by God's Word
- ✅ Position yourself under Christ's authority so you can lead others well
- ✅ Act on God's Word instead of merely hearing it
- ✅ Declare "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord"
- ✅ Lead with love and obedience, not demanding respect but earning it through kingdom living
- ✅ Never apologize for your biblical manhood

## Keep In Mind

- God's question "Adam, where are you?" was about position, not location
- You may not be personally responsible for past failures or broken patterns you inherited, but you are responsible for your sphere
- God always holds the man responsible first in the covenant chain
- A messed up man produces a messed up family, church, community, city, county, state, nation, and world
- When men step up biblically, the entire culture benefits
- God's Word is the only sure, solid, nonchanging standard for men
- You don't need everyone's approval to lead your house

## Security & Safety Notes

- Protect your household from harmful influences, ungodly media, and destructive relationships
- Guard your position as spiritual leader by staying connected to Christ
- When you submit to God's authority, you become safe for those under your care
- Your family should know that when they are under your umbrella, they are in a secure, safe place

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You believe cultural messages that masculinity is toxic or that men should be passive.
  **Solution:** Return to Genesis 2 and Matthew 7. God defines manhood. Be a kingdom man owned by God, not culture.

- **Problem:** You are angry, violent, or abusive and call it "being a man."
  **Solution:** This is the wrong extreme. Biblical manhood is not domination or harm. It is loving, godly leadership under Christ.

- **Problem:** You stay silent while harmful things happen in your home or community.
  **Solution:** Silence is passivity. God called Adam to keep the garden. Speak up, guard your realm, and lead.

- **Problem:** You delegate spiritual leadership to your wife because you don't have the answers.
  **Solution:** You are called to be the pastor of your home. Get the answer, study the Word, and lead your family to serve the Lord.

- **Problem:** You build your life on cultural opinion, feelings, or popular vote rather than God's Word.
  **Solution:** Build on the rock. Hear God's Word and act on it. Declare that your house will serve the Lord.

- **Problem:** You blame your father's failures or your past for your current passivity.
  **Solution:** Your father does not own your manhood. God does. Regardless of yesterday's failures, move to a different tomorrow.
