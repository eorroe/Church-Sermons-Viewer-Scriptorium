# You're Closer to Victory Than You Think

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God's promises of victory are within your reach but require your active cooperation and faith to move them from promise to experience. Through the story of King Joash and Elisha in 2 Kings 13, Dr. Tony Evans explains how God sanctifies ordinary actions when you take responsibility and step out in faith, and how partial commitment results in partial victory while full commitment unlocks complete victory.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are facing a crisis, battle, or situation that seems impossible to win on your own
- When you have received a promise from God but have not yet seen it manifested in your life
- When you feel stuck, defeated, or overwhelmed and need to understand how to cooperate with God for breakthrough

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize You Need a Promise of Victory

Acknowledge that you have a situation you cannot fix on your own. Admit that you need divine intervention. This is the starting point for pursuing God's promise of victory in your life.

### Step 2: Seek a Prophetic Word

Go to God in prayer and seek His word for your situation through scripture and the Holy Spirit. Just as King Joash went to the dying prophet Elisha for a word when he was in a desperate military situation, you must seek God's specific promise for your circumstance through prayer and Bible study.

### Step 3: Pick Up Your Bow (Take Action)

God will not do what He has called you to do until you take the first step. Elisha told the king to pick up the bow and arrows. You must take hold of the practical steps, resources, and opportunities God has placed before you before He can sanctify them.

### Step 4: Allow the Hand of Heaven to Be Placed on Your Hand

Let God's authority and power be transferred to your situation. This happens through prayer, faith, and surrender. Elisha placed his hand on the king's hand to sanctify the action and bring heaven's power into the natural battle.

### Step 5: Shoot Through the Window (Act in Faith)

Take your sanctified practical action and direct it toward your specific challenge. The king shot the arrow through the window toward the east where the enemy was. You must direct your faith-filled action at the specific problem or obstacle you are facing.

### Step 6: Strike the Ground (Follow Through Completely)

After receiving the promise, continue to act on it fully. Elisha told the king to strike the ground with the remaining arrows. The king only struck three times instead of striking the ground five or six times as instructed, limiting his victory because of partial commitment. You must follow God's instructions completely to receive complete victory.

### Step 7: Trust God to Fulfill the Promise

Believe that God will do what He has promised. Once you have taken the steps of faith and cooperation, surrender the outcome to God and trust Him for the results. The promise is already settled in heaven; your cooperation brings it to earth.

## Examples

### Example 1: King Joash and Elisha (2 Kings 13:14-21)

King Joash was in a losing battle against Aram and went to the prophet Elisha for help. Elisha told him to pick up a bow and arrows, then placed his hand on the king's hand to sanctify the action. The king shot an arrow through the window toward the east, and Elisha declared it the Lord's arrow of victory. However, when Elisha told him to strike the ground with the remaining arrows, the king only struck three times. Elisha was angry because the king had partial commitment, resulting in partial victory instead of complete victory over Aram.

### Example 2: Moses and the Rod (Exodus 14-17)

Moses had a shepherd's rod that God told him to pick up. When he held it out over the Red Sea, God parted the waters. When he held it up during the battle against the Amalekites, Israel prevailed. The rod was sanctified when Moses obeyed God's instructions, and the supernatural power of God flowed through the ordinary rod. God would not act until Moses took his position of obedience and faith.

### Example 3: David and the Five Stones (1 Samuel 17)

David picked up five regular stones from the brook and placed them in his shepherd's bag. These ordinary stones were sanctified when David stepped out in faith to face Goliath. One stone was enough because God had sanctified the stones, but David was prepared with five because Goliath had four brothers. The natural stones became supernatural weapons when David acted in faith and obedience.

### Example 4: Peter and the Miraculous Catch of Fish (Luke 5:1-11)

Peter was a fisherman who had caught nothing all night. Jesus got into Peter's boat, taught the people, and then told Peter to let down the nets for a catch. Peter obeyed, even though he thought it was pointless. The nets were sanctified by Jesus' word, and they caught so many fish that the nets began to break. The ordinary nets became supernatural when Peter cooperated with Jesus' instructions.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Acknowledge your need for God's intervention when facing impossible situations
- ✅ Seek God's specific word and promise for your situation through prayer and scripture
- ✅ Take immediate action on the practical steps God has placed before you
- ✅ Allow God's hand to be placed on your situation through prayer and surrender
- ✅ Act in faith, directing your actions toward the specific problem or obstacle
- ✅ Follow through completely on God's instructions, avoiding partial commitment
- ✅ Trust that God has already decided to bless you and is waiting for your cooperation
- ✅ Distinguish between blessings (require cooperation) and miracles (do not require cooperation)

## Keep In Mind

- God's promises are in your reach, but they are not automatically in your hand. You must cooperate with God to experience them.
- Blessings require your participation and cooperation, while miracles are supernatural interventions that do not depend on your actions.
- Partial commitment leads to partial victory. If you want complete victory, you must be fully committed and obedient.
- God has already decided what He will do, but He is waiting for you to take your position of faith and action before He moves.
- Your natural abilities, resources, and tools can become supernatural when sanctified by God's presence and your obedience.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not confuse faith with passivity. Faith without works is dead (James 2:26). Taking action is not a lack of faith; it is the expression of faith.
- Do not blame God for lack of victory when you have not taken the steps He has called you to take. God is not obligated to bypass your responsibility.
- Do not seek miracles as an excuse to avoid cooperation and obedience. Miracles are rare and operate outside natural laws; blessings are God's normal pattern for His children who walk in obedience.
- Do not limit God through unbelief, fear, or partial commitment. Your attitude and actions can affect whether God's promises are fulfilled in your life.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting passively for God to move without taking any action yourself
  **Solution:** Understand that God's normal pattern is to work through your cooperation. Take the practical steps of faith that God has placed before you, knowing that He will sanctify them and bring supernatural results.

- **Problem:** Partial commitment to God's instructions, leading to partial victory
  **Solution:** Examine your heart and actions to ensure you are fully committed. Ask God to reveal any areas where you are holding back, and be willing to go all in. Remember that the king only struck the ground three times instead of completing the full action, and that limited his victory.

- **Problem:** Confusing a promise with an experience
  **Solution:** A promise from God is true and reliable, but it becomes an experience only when you take the necessary steps of faith and obedience. Do not settle for just knowing the promise; pursue the experience by cooperating with God.

- **Problem:** Asking God for blessings without fulfilling your part of the equation
  **Solution:** Recognize that blessings require cooperation. If you want a job, you must job hunt while praying. If you want healing, you must take your medicine and follow the doctor's advice while praying. God will not do your part for you; He will sanctify it and make it supernatural when you do your part.
