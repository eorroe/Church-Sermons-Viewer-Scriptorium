# Running to God in Hard Times Changes Everything

## Overview

This sermon teaches that during difficult seasons—referred to as the "evil day," meaning a period of severe hardship where human solutions have failed after sustained effort—you must run to God rather than away from Him. Through Ephesians 6:10-18, Dr. Tony Evans explains that the battle is not against flesh and blood but against principalities, powers, and world forces in the spiritual realm. The message teaches believers to put on the armor of God, simplify by putting on Christ, engage in consistent prayer, walk in the spirit, and maintain continuous contact with heaven, recognizing that prayer activates angelic help and engages God's power on your behalf.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are experiencing severe hardship where human solutions have failed after sustained effort
- When attempts to solve the problem through doctors, medicine, financial planning, therapy, legal means, or personal effort have not produced results
- When you feel tempted to avoid God during difficult times because of fear, anger, doubt, or shame
- When you need to understand spiritual warfare and the armor of God
- When you want to move from emergency-response prayer to a lifestyle of prayer

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the True Source of Your Problem

Understand that your struggle is not against flesh and blood. People may be conduits for your pain, but they are not the ultimate source of your struggle. Your battle is against principalities, powers, and world forces in the spiritual realm (Ephesians 6:12). These include God, angels, and demonic spirits—personal, intelligent beings who act in the world. The spiritual realm is a real dimension where God, angels, and demonic forces exist and act, distinct from the material world humans can see and touch.

### Step 2: Run Toward God, Not Away From Him

In the midst of the evil day—a period of severe hardship where human solutions have failed after sustained effort—do not run from God. Run to Him. This is not the time to evade Him; it is the time to embrace Him. Stand firm in the faith and strength of the Lord Jesus Christ.

### Step 3: Put On the Armor of God

Dress for spiritual battle with the six pieces of armor: truth, righteousness, peace, faith, the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit (the word of God). These are practical tools for taking a stand when under spiritual attack. To engage in spiritual resistance means actively opposing demonic influence through prayer, speaking scripture, exercising faith in Jesus, and refusing to accept defeat or despair.

### Step 4: Simplify by Putting On Christ

If you cannot remember all six pieces of armor, remember one thing: deliberately choose to think about situations through the truth that Jesus is the Son of God who died for your sins and rose again, and that His finished work on the cross has secured your victory, rather than evaluating through fear, emotion, or reasoning that intentionally excludes God, ignores the Bible, or contradicts clear biblical teaching. Define yourself by understanding yourself as a forgiven, loved, and redeemed child of God, defined by what Jesus has done rather than by your circumstances or achievements.

### Step 5: Use Prayer to Put On Christ

Prayer is the practical way to utilize Christ and equip yourself for battle. Prayer is relational communication with God. The way you put on Christ is with prayer (Ephesians 6:18). Engage in consistent, persevering prayer for all saints, and maintain regular moments of prayer at morning, midday, and evening; intentionally reflecting on a specific Bible passage, asking God what it means for your life, and memorizing key verses to recall during the day.

### Step 6: Pray in the Spirit

Don't just pray with words—pray with a mindset aligned with the Holy Spirit, clarified as thoughts that are consistent with the clear teachings of scripture, seek to honor God above self, and are willing to change your mind or direction when shown from the Bible or through the Holy Spirit. This is distinguished from personal desire or emotional reaction that contradicts specific biblical teaching or that lacks confirmation from scripture and mature believers.

### Step 7: Walk in the Spirit Daily

Make walking in the spirit a daily pattern of when facing a decision, first consulting the Bible for specific, clear teachings from the Bible that are agreed upon by most historic Christian traditions, praying for the Holy Spirit's direction, pausing before acting until you have a specific Bible verse or principle that applies and a calm that persists even when the situation is painful or uncertain, and affirmation from Christians who have demonstrated consistent faith over years, biblical knowledge through regular scripture study, and godly character as observed by their community. Choose the option that aligns with scripture even when it requires sacrifice of money, time, reputation, or comfort.

### Step 8: Understand How Prayer Activates Angelic Help

When you pray, you engage God the Holy Spirit, to which God answers by sending or activating spiritual resources, including one or more angels, according to the sermon's teaching, each believer has at least one angel assigned by God; the exact number is known only to God. These angels actively oppose demonic forces: the sermon teaches that demonic forces actively cause suffering, pain, and defeat, and that believers must resist them through spiritual means. Angels are spiritual beings with the ability to engage and overcome demonic spirits; the conflict takes place through prayer, faith, and the authority of Jesus in the spiritual realm.

### Step 9: Maintain Continuous Contact With Heaven

Pray without ceasing (1 Thessalonians 5:17)—meaning keeping in mind and actively remembering that God is with you throughout the day, choosing to include Him in your thoughts, conversations, and decisions. Don't treat prayer or the spirit like a Sunday visit. Stay connected to God through ongoing prayer and submission to His will.

## Examples

### Example 1: Medical Diagnosis With No Answers

When doctors cannot explain your symptoms after multiple medical visits and relevant testing, or have no treatment options available and medicine fails—this is an evil day scenario. Do not panic and run from God. Run to Him. Put on the armor of God, using the practical tools of speaking truth, choosing right, pursuing peace, exercising faith, recalling your salvation, and applying scripture. Pray with a mindset aligned with the Holy Spirit, and walk in the spirit daily.

### Example 2: Financial Collapse

When bankers, financial advisors, or creditors have no viable options to resolve the financial crisis and financial doors are closing—losing a job or income source, having a business decline, being denied a loan or credit, facing foreclosure or eviction, or experiencing a significant reduction in income that threatens basic needs—do not panic and run from God. Run to Him. Put on the armor of God, pray with a mindset aligned with the Holy Spirit, and maintain continuous contact with heaven.

### Example 3: Family Crisis

When family conflict increases to the point where you cannot have normal conversations, resolve conflicts, or maintain the relationship without outside help and human solutions such as mediation or counseling have not resolved it, this is an evil day scenario. Run to God. Put on the armor of God, pray in the spirit, and walk in the spirit daily.

- ✅ Walk in the spirit—a daily pattern of when facing a decision, first consulting the Bible for specific, clear teachings, praying for the Holy Spirit's direction, pausing before acting until you have a specific Bible verse or principle that applies, a calm that persists even when the situation is painful or uncertain, and affirmation from mature believers

## Keep In Mind

- The evil day is real and involves demonic opposition—the sermon teaches that demonic opposition is a real spiritual cause of suffering, not merely a human psychological state or social dynamic
- According to the sermon's teaching, each person who has placed their faith in Christ has one or more angels, as God determines for each believer, appointed by God specifically for their protection and spiritual support
- Prayer engages the power and right of God to act, which believers access through prayer, faith, and the name of Jesus
- Walking in the spirit results in experiences that are perceptible either to yourself or to people around you through observable changes in circumstances, behavior, or experience, and attributable to God's intervention
- You may not feel like praying, but praying repeatedly over time prepares you to receive experiences that are perceptible either to yourself or to people around you through observable changes in circumstances, behavior, or experience, and attributable to God's intervention

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not attempt to confront or engage demonic forces without first deliberately choosing to think about situations through the truth that Jesus is the Son of God who died for your sins and rose again, and that His finished work on the cross has secured your victory, rather than evaluating through fear, emotion, or reasoning that intentionally excludes God, ignores the Bible, or contradicts clear biblical teaching
- Do not rely solely on human wisdom or medical/financial solutions during spiritual attack—continue to believe God, pray, and act on faith despite external or internal pressure that causes you to abandon your faith, act in fear, or disobey God
- The sermon teaches that demonic forces actively cause suffering, pain, and defeat, and that believers must resist them through spiritual means, not through physical combat or human debate

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Running from God during hardship instead of running to Him
  **Solution:** Recognize that difficulty is the time to embrace God, not evade Him. Continue to believe God, pray, and act on faith despite external or internal pressure.

- **Problem:** Treating prayer as an emergency-response tool only
  **Solution:** Build a practice of communicating with God in both scheduled and unplanned, brief prayers or thoughts directed to God throughout the day—such as praying before a meeting, thanking God for a small blessing, or asking for help in a sudden difficulty—praying at morning, midday, and evening; reading scripture daily; and making a deliberate decision to actively seek God's guidance through prayer and scripture before making decisions, rather than relying solely on your own analysis, advice, or resources.

- **Problem:** Praying driven by the flesh instead of praying with a mindset aligned with the Holy Spirit
  **Solution:** Deliberately choose to think about situations through the truth that Jesus is the Son of God who died for your sins and rose again, and that His finished work on the cross has secured your victory, rather than evaluating through fear, emotion, or reasoning that intentionally excludes God, ignores the Bible, or contradicts clear biblical teaching.

- **Problem:** Forgetting that people are not the source of your problems
  **Solution:** Remember Ephesians 6:12—the underlying origin of the problem is spiritual, not merely physical or human; therefore spiritual weapons are needed, not just physical or medical treatment, financial planning, legal action, therapy, personal effort, or advice from others that does not include prayer, faith, or reliance on God.
