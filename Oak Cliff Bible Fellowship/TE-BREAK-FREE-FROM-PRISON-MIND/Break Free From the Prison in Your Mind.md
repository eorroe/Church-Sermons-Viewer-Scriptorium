# Break Free From the Prison in Your Mind

## Overview

This sermon teaches that your victory in life is tied to your mind, and the shift in your perspective will determine the redirection of your life. Based on Colossians 3:1-3, it explains that when you came to Christ, you received a "hookup" — a spiritual connection to Christ's resurrection life — but you must intentionally set your mind on things above to experience the freedom God has already declared yours. Through daily practice of considering yourself dead to sin, putting off the old self, and putting on the new self, God will renew your mind and you will begin to live out the new creation you truly are.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel trapped by past patterns, habits, or generational struggles
- When you are struggling with thoughts like "I'll never get better" or "This is just who I am"
- When you want to move from merely knowing about Christ to having Christ integrated into every part of your daily life
- When you need to break free from a "plantation mentality" of defeat and step into the freedom God has already given you

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Hookup — Accept Your New Identity in Christ

Recognize and believe mentally that at salvation, you were raised up with Christ. Your life is now hidden with Christ in God. You are no longer the same person you were before Christ. Write down and declare your new identity: you have died to your old life and your life is now hidden in Christ. This mental acknowledgment is the starting point for all transformation.

### Step 2: Intentionally Set Your Mind on Things Above

Each day, purposefully set your mind on heavenly things — not as an escape from earth, but as a way to live on earth with heaven's perspective. Like setting an alarm clock, this is an intentional, planned act. Do not wait for it to happen automatically. Speak it out loud: "Today I set my mind to live and make decisions from heaven's point of view."

### Step 3: Consider Your Body Dead to Sin and Put Off the Old Self

Each morning, go through the areas of your life that are outside the will of God — immorality, impurity, anger, slander, greed, and any other sinful patterns — and declare them dead. Write the obituary for those habits. Take off the old clothes of your former way of thinking and operating.

### Step 4: Put On the New Self and Invite Heaven's View

After declaring the old dead, put on the new self — the new way of thinking, speaking, and operating. Just as a shower is useless without changing into clean clothes, forgiveness is not enough without a change of conduct. Speak righteousness, peace, and the will of God. Stir Jesus into the totality of your existence so that every part of your day is influenced by Him.

## Examples

### Example 1: Breaking Generational Cycles of Abuse

A person who grew up in a home with abuse, drunkenness, and anger may think, "This is just who I am. I'll always be defeated by this." By applying Step 1, they acknowledge that in Christ, they have died to that old identity. By applying Step 3, they write the obituary for generational anger and declare it dead. By applying Step 4, they put on patience and self-control, speaking peace instead of anger. Over time, God renews the mind and the cycle is broken.

### Example 2: The Christian Who Only Attaches Jesus to the Surface

A believer who only says a quick prayer in the morning and thinks that is enough to get through the week is like someone who showers on Sunday but wears the same dirty clothes all week. By applying Step 2, they intentionally set their mind to bring heaven's perspective into every decision at work, at home, and in relationships. By applying Step 4, they stir Jesus into every part of their day — not just a little on top, but integrated into every part of their existence from the bottom to the top.

### Example 3: The Football Player Who Reaches High While Staying Grounded

A football player catches the winning touchdown by reaching high to catch the ball while keeping his feet firmly planted on the ground. Without both actions, it would have been an incomplete pass. Likewise, the Christian must reach high to heaven in perspective while keeping their feet firmly planted on earth in daily life. You are not meant to be so heavenly minded you are no earthly good, nor so earthly minded you are no heavenly good.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do set your mind on purpose, like setting an alarm clock — intentionally and deliberately each day
- ✅ Do consider yourself dead to sin daily, writing the obituary for old patterns
- ✅ Do put off the old self and put on the new self — both actions are required together
- ✅ Do stir Jesus into every part of your existence, not just a surface-level attachment
- ✅ Do reach high to heaven while keeping your feet planted on earth
- ✅ Do remember that your life is hidden with Christ in God — your identity is now in Him

## Keep In Mind

- Your victory in life is tied to your mind — it is in the shift of your mind that will determine the redirection of your life
- You only do what you do because you think what you think — changing your actions starts with changing your thoughts
- The hookup at salvation is real, but the hookup without turning on the ignition will still leave you dead, stuck, and going nowhere
- Setting your mind must be done daily — just as you shower daily, you must daily declare yourself dead to sin and put on the new self
- God will renew your mind as you practice the habit of setting it — what was once traumatic becomes more and more natural over time
- You are seated with Christ and your life is hidden in Him — this is your new point of reference

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not rely solely on feelings to confirm your freedom — you have been set free simply because God said so, even if you do not feel free
- Guard against a "plantation mentality" — the belief that you will never overcome something or that a struggle is just who you are is a lie from the enemy
- Do not confuse the "hookup" (salvation) with the daily work of sanctification — both are necessary and work together
- Do not attempt to put on the new self in your own strength — God shifts and shapes the mind as you cooperate with Him through the daily practice

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Putting a little Jesus on top of your life without stirring Him into the totality of your existence
  **Solution:** Intentionally integrate Christ into every part of your day — from the moment you wake up to when you go to sleep — so that your life is consumed with Him
- **Problem:** Taking the shower (salvation) but not changing the clothes (daily sanctification)
  **Solution:** Each day, consider yourself dead to sin, take off the old self, and put on the new self through the power of Christ
- **Problem:** Being so heavenly minded you are no earthly good, or so earthly minded you are no heavenly good
  **Solution:** Reach high to heaven with your mind while keeping your feet firmly planted on earth — both happen simultaneously
- **Problem:** Waiting for transformation to happen automatically without intentionally setting your mind
  **Solution:** Set your mind on purpose daily, like setting an alarm clock — until it becomes a natural habit built into your system
- **Problem:** Believing the lie that your current struggles define who you are
  **Solution:** Remember that your life is hidden with Christ in God — you are a new creature in Christ, and your identity is found in Him, not in your past or current struggles
