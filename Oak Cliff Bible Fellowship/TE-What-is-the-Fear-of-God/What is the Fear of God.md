# What is the Fear of God?

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the fear of God is not about trembling in terror, but about taking Him seriously with a daily orientation to please Him. Tony Evans explains how believers should make it their ambition each day to live in a way that pleases God, using the analogy of electricity to illustrate how we should handle God's power with reverence. The message also emphasizes the importance of influencing others through church accountability and recognizing that everyone will stand before the Lord.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand what it truly means to fear God
- When working on developing a daily spiritual discipline to please God
- When someone needs to understand living with an eternal perspective and accountability

## Steps

### Step 1: Take God Seriously

The fear of God means taking Him seriously rather than casually. It means respecting His thoughts, views, and perspectives and not treating His name lightly.

### Step 2: Make It Your Daily Ambition

Make it your goal each day to please God in word and deed. Start your day declaring that your ambition is to please Him, and end your day reflecting on whether you pleased God in your words and actions.

### Step 3: Pursue God's Pleasure as a Practice

Pleasing God should become a daily practice and orientation. Ask yourself throughout the day whether your choices will make God happy, and let this pursuit shape your thinking and actions.

### Step 4: Recognize the Weight of God's Name

Knowing the fear of the Lord means recognizing the substance and authority behind His name. Don't throw His name around casually or use it for empty invocations and benedictions.

### Step 5: Influence Others Through Church Community

Influence others to fear God by being part of a church community where believers can hold one another accountable, correct each other, and persuade one another to stay on the path of discipleship and righteousness.

### Step 6: Avoid Using God's Name Casually

Don't thank God for blessings while living in contradiction to His principles. Using His name casually while engaging in evil or profanity is taking His name in vain.

### Step 7: Remember the Coming Judgment

Recognize that you will stand before the Lord and give an account for your life. Every knee will bow and every man's work will be examined, good and bad.

### Step 8: Let the Church Challenge and Correct You

Our job as a church is not just to preach but to persuade and challenge one another. Allow yourself to be called back when you've moved too far in either the direction of legalism or the direction of moral compromise.

## Examples

### Example 1: Electricity Analogy

Electricity is a blessing that powers our lives, but we handle it carefully because the same power that gives us good things can also harm us. Similarly, God's power and presence should be revered and not taken casually.

### Example 2: Parental Authority

Just as parents expect their children to take them seriously and not play with their instructions, God expects us to take Him seriously. When a child defies a parent, it insults the parent's authority—likewise, disregarding God's clear principles insults Him.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Start each day with the goal of pleasing God in word and deed
- ✅ Make pursuing God's pleasure a daily practice and orientation
- ✅ Take God's name seriously and avoid using it casually or in vain
- ✅ Be part of a church community that holds you accountable
- ✅ Influence others to fear God through persuasion and challenge
- ❌ Don't treat God casually or throw His name around without meaning
- ❌ Don't avoid accountability because you don't want others in your business
- ❌ Don't use God's name to bless your own achievements while living contrary to His principles

## Keep In Mind

- The fear of God is about taking Him seriously, not trembling in terror
- You are not without sin, but God wants it clear that you are pursuing His pleasure
- The church exists so believers can love, correct, serve, and help one another
- Every knee will bow and every man's work will be examined on the day of judgment

## Security & Safety Notes

- You will stand before the Lord and give an account for your life
- God's judgment is coming, and every man's work will be examined, good and bad
- Insulting God by rejecting His clear teachings in favor of your own opinions has eternal consequences

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating God casually by using His name without meaning or substance
  **Solution:** Take God's name seriously in both speech and life, recognizing it carries real authority
- **Problem:** Avoiding accountability and church community
  **Solution:** Be part of a church where believers can persuade and challenge you to stay on the right path
- **Problem:** Being satisfied with a superficial, convenience-based faith rather than pursuing God's pleasure daily
  **Solution:** Make it your daily ambition to please God, starting each morning with that goal and ending each day in reflection
