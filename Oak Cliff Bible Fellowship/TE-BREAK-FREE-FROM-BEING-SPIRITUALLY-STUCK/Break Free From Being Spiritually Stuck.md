# Break Free From Being Spiritually Stuck

## Overview

This sermon identifies why many Christians remain spiritually stagnant and provides a biblical framework for progressing from spiritual infancy to maturity. Using 1 John 2:12-14, Tony Evans explains the three levels of spiritual development—children, young men, and fathers—and teaches how to move from being stuck to becoming a spiritual parent who actively disciples others.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel like your spiritual life is not growing despite attending church
- When you want to understand the stages of spiritual maturity described in 1 John
- When you are a new Christian and want to grow beyond infancy
- When you feel stuck in spiritual battles and need victory over temptation
- When you want to become a spiritual parent and invest in others' faith
- When you need to break free from worldly influences controlling your decisions

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify Your Current Spiritual Stage

Examine your life against the three stages of spiritual development in 1 John 2:12-14:
- **Infant/Child**: You have recognized God and come to faith, but you are not growing or reproducing spiritually. You may be new to the faith, or you may be a long-time Christian with stunted growth.
- **Young Man/Teenager**: You are actively battling the devil's influence and learning to use God's Word to overcome temptation. You are developing spiritual muscles through conflict.
- **Father/Mature**: You have walked with God over time and are actively investing in the spiritual lives of others through discipleship. You reproduce spiritually by leading others to Christ and raising them in the faith.

Ask yourself: Where am I currently? Do I see spiritual growth and multiplication in my life?

### Step 2: If You Are an Infant, Develop by Abiding in God's Word

If you are an infant spiritually—either because you are new to the faith or because you have stunted growth—you must move from mere recognition of God to active engagement with His Word.

- Spend time in scripture daily, not just to read or memorize, but to **utilize** it
- Learn to "abide" in the Word—hang out with it, meditate on it, and let it dwell in you
- Understand that Satan is "allergic" to scripture; when you use God's Word against him, he must leave
- Follow Jesus' example in Matthew 4: when tempted, He responded with "It is written" three times
- Move from being a receptacle of truth to a conduit of truth

### Step 3: If You Are a Young Man, Use the Word to Overcome the Devil

If you are in the teenage/battling years spiritually, this is the time when spiritual muscles develop. You are facing peer pressure, cultural pressure, and Satan's attempts to draw you away from God.

- Recognize that the teenage years are a time of **spiritual conflict**—this is normal and expected
- Use God's Word to defeat the devil during these struggles
- Do not rely on feelings, opinions, or social pressures; rely on scripture
- Remember: Satan can handle your opinions and feelings, but he cannot handle the Word of God
- Keep hammering the devil with the truth of God's Word until he leaves the vicinity
- You are becoming an overcomer when you consistently use the Word to resist temptation

### Step 4: Graduate to Fatherhood by Investing in Others

To move to the next level of intimacy with God, you must become a spiritual parent who invests in the spiritual life of somebody else.

- Biblical discipleship means you are growing in the faith AND transferring that faith to others through evangelism and spiritual growth
- Ask yourself: Who have I led to Christ? Who am I developing in the faith?
- A good spiritual father not only "sires" children but raises them
- If you only come to church for yourself with no investment in others, at best you are still a teenager or infant
- Ministry to others is essential for your own spiritual growth; it moves you from being a receptacle to a conduit

### Step 5: Break Free from Worldly Control by Developing an Eternal Perspective

Do not love the world or the things in the world. The world is the system headed by Satan that leaves God out. When you embrace worldly standards, you become disconnected from fellowship with God.

- Understand that the world is temporary and passing away; God's will lasts forever
- Do not let time become more important than eternity
- Keep a heavenly perspective so you can get along better on earth
- Do not put all your marbles in the world's basket—you will end up out on a limb
- Live in the world, but do not let the world control you or govern your decisions
- When bad times come, the world cannot help you—only the Lord can

## Examples

### Example 1: The Infant Christian

A new believer comes to faith and begins attending church. They love God and have a genuine relationship with Him, but they only attend church and never grow or share their faith. After five years, they are still acting the same way—no spiritual reproduction, no change in character. This is a Christian with stunted growth, still functioning as an infant spiritually.

### Example 2: The Teenage Believer

A Christian in high school or college faces pressure from friends to compromise their faith. They feel the tension between wanting to fit in and wanting to follow Christ. They begin to use scripture to resist temptation—when tempted to lie, cheat, or compromise, they respond with "It is written" and stand on God's Word. They are in the battle, but they are overcoming. This is a young man or woman spiritually, developing spiritual muscles.

### Example 3: The Spiritual Father

A mature Christian who has walked with God for many years begins to intentionally invest in others. They lead someone to Christ, then disciple them, teaching them how to walk with God. They raise spiritual children. This is a father or mother spiritually—they have graduated from infancy and teenage years to maturity, and they are now reproducing for God.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Spend time in God's Word daily, not just to read it but to use it against temptation
- ✅ Use "It is written" when facing temptation, just as Jesus did in Matthew 4
- ✅ Invest intentionally in the spiritual growth of others through discipleship
- ✅ Keep an eternal perspective—never let temporary things become more important than forever
- ✅ Recognize your spiritual stage and take the next step toward maturity
- ✅ Share biblical truth with someone else so you can grow and so they can benefit

## Keep In Mind

- Spiritual development is a process from babyhood to maturity—don't expect instant perfection
- The Greek word for "children" in 1 John 2:13 specifically means "infant," indicating a stage of life, not just a relationship status
- You can be an infant by age or by function—stunted growth is a real problem even for long-time Christians
- The teenage years spiritually are marked by conflict and battle, but that is when spiritual muscles develop
- Being a biological parent does not make you a spiritual parent; spiritual parenthood requires actively raising others in the faith
- Ministry to others is not optional for spiritual growth—it is the path to maximum intimacy with God

## Security & Safety Notes

- Your salvation is secure in Christ, but your fellowship with God can be broken by loving the world
- You are not losing your salvation if you become worldly, but you are separated from the intimate fellowship with God that you could have
- Satan cannot operate where the Word of God is actively used and abided in
- When you abide in the Word, you are hanging out in a realm where Satan cannot be comfortable

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking you are mature because you have been a Christian for many years, but you have not developed spiritually.
  **Solution:** Examine your life for evidence of growth and spiritual reproduction. Are you investing in others? If not, you may still be an infant.

- **Problem:** Trying to overcome spiritual battles with feelings, opinions, or social pressure instead of God's Word.
  **Solution:** Learn to use scripture when facing temptation. Satan cannot handle the Word of God. Respond to temptation with "It is written" and specific scripture.

- **Problem:** Loving the world and its temporary pleasures, thinking they will satisfy.
  **Solution:** Remember that the world is passing away. Keep an eternal perspective. The world can only offer Disneyland—a temporary visit. God offers a permanent home.

- **Problem:** Coming to church only for yourself and never investing in others' spiritual growth.
  **Solution:** Ask God to show you who you can disciple. Ministry to others is the pathway to your own spiritual growth. You cannot reach maximum intimacy with God without becoming a spiritual parent.

- **Problem:** Staying stuck in the teenage years by not learning to abide in the Word during times of battle.
  **Solution:** When you face temptation and conflict, this is your signal to grow. Use the Word to deal with the devil. Don't stay stuck—abide in the scripture and you will become an overcomer.
