# Don't Forget Eternity When You're Planning For Tomorrow

## Overview

This sermon addresses the danger of letting material planning and personal ambition overshadow our eternal perspective. Through Jesus' teaching in Luke 12, it warns against greed and the temptation to secure our future while neglecting God. It calls believers to integrate faith into every plan, recognizing that earthly life is temporary and eternity is certain. The message challenges us to be "rich toward God" rather than merely rich in possessions.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When making major life decisions or financial plans
- When tempted to prioritize career or material success over spiritual commitment
- When struggling with procrastination in serving God or ministry

## Steps

### Step 1: Evaluate Your Plans Through God's Lens

Examine your current plans—career goals, financial targets, family decisions—and ask whether God is central to them. If you can complete your plans without mentioning God, they may need realignment. Bring each major decision before God in prayer and seek His will above your own ambition.

### Step 2: Practice Eternal Thinking in Daily Decisions

Before making commitments, ask "What does eternity say about this?" Recognize that possessions cannot follow you after death, so invest in things that last forever: generosity, discipleship, and faith. Do not delay serving God while waiting for the "right time" or more resources.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Rich Fool

Jesus told of a man who built bigger barns to store his abundant crops, thinking he had secured his future. However, God called him a fool because he planned independently of God and forgot his soul. That night, his life was demanded of him, and all his stored wealth was useless.

### Example 2: James 4's Warning to Businessmen

James 4:13-16 warns those who boast about their business plans: "If the Lord wills, we will live and also do this or that." This teaches humility in planning—acknowledging that every day and every outcome belongs to God.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Include God in every major decision by praying and seeking His will
- ✅ Practice generous stewardship, especially as you are blessed
- ❌ Don't postpone serving God waiting for the "right time" or more resources
- ❌ Don't measure success by possessions or self-made security

## Keep In Mind

Death is not a period but a conjunction—you transition directly into eternity. You cannot take your possessions with you.

## Security & Safety Notes

While planning for tomorrow is wise, spiritual security comes from being "rich toward God," not from earthly wealth. Avoid false security in material accumulation.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Planning life as if it will last forever, hoarding resources and delaying ministry.
  **Solution:** Regularly reassess plans through the lens of eternity; give generously and serve now, trusting God with the future.
