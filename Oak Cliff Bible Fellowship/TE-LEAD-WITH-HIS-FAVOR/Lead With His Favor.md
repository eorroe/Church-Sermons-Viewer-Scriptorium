# Lead With His Favor

## Overview

This sermon teaches ministry leaders and believers how to lead with God's divine favor by establishing personal righteousness, strengthening family relationships, and maintaining specific ministry boundaries—including protected time blocks, dedicated family spaces, emotional availability limits, and family-first scheduling. Based on Psalm 128 and Tony Evans' teaching from the sermon "Lead With His Favor," it explains that God's blessing flows through covenant relationship and proper order across four spheres: individual, family, church, and society. The message emphasizes that ministry effectiveness depends on family health, not the reverse, and provides practical steps for husbands, wives, and parents to build kingdom-focused homes.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to reset your biblical priorities and distinguish genuine spirituality from "religious camouflage"—defined as ministry activity used to mask a lack of personal spiritual authenticity
- When balancing ministry responsibilities with family life
- When seeking to understand how personal favor with God flows through every area of life
- When the user asks about building a multi-generational legacy of faith
- When studying covenant theology across the four biblical spheres of individual, family, church, and society
- When working on marriage restoration or strengthening spousal roles

## Steps

### Step 1: Evaluate Your Personal Relationship With God

Examine whether you fear God seriously or take Him casually. Fearing God means practicing concrete behaviors: consistent prayer, Scripture obedience, moral choices, and reverent decision-making that honors Him in every choice. Walk in His ways through consistent action, not merely feelings or words. Personal righteousness is the foundation for every other sphere of influence.

### Step 2: Secure Your Marriage Covenant

Husbands, lead your home by providing spiritual stability—defined as consistent prayer, Scripture engagement, and moral integrity—and creating an environment where your wife can flourish, meaning a home where she is respected, supported in her God-given roles, and free to exercise her gifts. Wives are essential participants in kingdom purpose, not reduced to domestic substitutes. Practice the two-word marriage formula: husbands love sacrificially, wives respect honorably. Resolve marital conflict quickly because disunity shuts down prayer. As Scripture teaches in 1 Timothy 3:5, "if anyone does not know how to manage his own household, how will he care for the church of God?"

### Step 3: Establish Family Devotions At The Table

Make the dinner table your primary leadership environment. Be present consistently without distractions to pray, teach, correct, bless, and inspire your children. Let children lead devotions when appropriate. Table time shapes values more than programs because it is relational and consistent, not programmatic. This intentional, daily gathering builds spiritual patterns that structured programs cannot replicate.

### Step 4: Raise Children As Olive Plants

Nurture children deeply so their roots run down and they produce lasting fruit for subsequent generations. The olive metaphor refers to a legacy of faith measured in the faithfulness of those who come after you, not a literal span of thousands of years. Properly rooted children who maintain their faith and raise their children in the Lord create a generational legacy. Protect them from cultural domination by maintaining stronger family influence than media or peers.

### Step 5: Balance Ministry And Family Without Compromise

Ministry is critical but functions as a reflection of your family, not a replacement for it. Do not allow ministry demands to rob your family of your presence. Paul instructs that if you cannot manage your household, you cannot manage the church. Enter your children's world by showing up at their events while keeping them genuinely connected to the local church community through active participation, not merely occasional attendance.

### Step 6: Build Multi-Generational Faith

Live for your grandchildren, not just your children. Establish family traditions like monthly gatherings where multiple generations pray, quote Scripture, and reinforce shared values. Gather regularly while family members are alive and able to participate, not waiting for perfect health conditions. Your consistent example creates a legacy that outlasts your lifetime.

### Step 7: Lead Your Sphere Of Influence

Understand that your personal condition flows outward in concrete ways: a person's spiritual condition directly impacts their marriage, parenting, church involvement, neighborhood witness, and civic engagement. Individual health affects family, family health affects church, church health affects neighborhood, neighborhood health affects city, city health affects state, state health affects nation, and national health affects the world. Improve yourself to improve every larger sphere around you.

### Step 8: Communicate The Gospel Clearly

Guard against message loss in translation, which includes both literal translation errors and the more common problem of miscommunication between sender and receiver. As a messenger, ensure what is encoded matches what is decoded. Avoid ministry camouflage where activity replaces authenticity. Be honest about your target by being truthful about your specific spiritual goals and the actual condition of your heart, rather than projecting an image of success. Let others see genuine transformation, not just painted bullseyes—pretending to hit spiritual targets without actual growth.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Barn Target Painter

A man admired his friend's perfect marksmanship on a barn with twenty bullseyes until he learned the truth: the shooter fired first, then painted the target around the hole. This illustrates how ministry can create the appearance of spirituality without actual accuracy. The solution is authentic spiritual formation through repentance, obedience, and accountability, not post-hoc justification of empty activity.

### Example 2: The Mistaken Email

A man in Chicago emailed his wife saying the weather was bad and she should not return. The email reached a pastor's widow in Iowa whose husband had died the day before. The miscommunication caused her to faint. This demonstrates how critical clear encoding and decoding are in ministry communication, and how even simple messages can fail when clarity is absent.

### Example 3: The Walmart shopper

Everyone wants God, but only when He is on discount—meaning people want God's blessings without submitting to God's authority or commands. When God's requirements feel costly, people shop elsewhere. This explains why discipleship that teaches people to fear God and operate under divine authority has been neglected, producing casual Christianity instead of covenant relationship.

### Example 4: The Father's Midnight Bible

Tony Evans' father dropped out of high school, worked hard, and could only read the Bible late at night after his family slept. He fought for his family through prayer and persistent love, eventually leading his wife and children to Christ. This one man's personal covenant with God produced four children in ministry, multiple grandchildren following Christ, and broadcast influence across 130 countries through the ministry's reach—not a measure of his personal spiritual impact.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Fear God by living in conscious awareness of His presence through concrete behaviors: prayer, Scripture obedience, moral choices, and reverent decision-making, not through emotional rituals alone
- ✅ Start with your own spiritual condition before attempting to fix larger institutions
- ✅ Make the dinner table your family's primary training ground
- ✅ Let your wife be an essential collaborator in kingdom purpose, not a replacement for your leadership
- ✅ Show up at your children's events to earn the right to speak into their world
- ✅ Establish monthly multi-generational gatherings while family members are alive and able to participate, not waiting for perfect health
- ✅ Correct marital conflict quickly because unity opens the door for prayer
- ✅ Take your family to church so they know their faith is part of a community
- ✅ Be a preview of the coming kingdom in your marriage, family, and ministry

## Keep In Mind

- God's blessing is designed to flow through you to others, not merely accumulate in you
- Ministry is a reflection of family health, not a substitute for it
- Satan attacks the family because it is the primary unit of kingdom advancement
- The husband bears ultimate covenantal responsibility as the primary accountability to God for the spiritual direction of the home, while both spouses share responsibility for the marriage covenant
- Children need parents more than they need programs, peers, or social media
- Cultural dominance at the family table produces generational loss of faith
- The church should reinforce what families teach, not contradict it
- Legacy is measured in generations, not just in your own children
- Spiritual stability rests on consistent prayer, Scripture engagement, and moral integrity

## Security & Safety Notes

- Maintain confidentiality when ministering to families in crisis
- Protect children by ensuring they are present in healthy environments, not exposed to domestic strife or ministry dysfunction
- Do not use ministry platform to abuse authority over family members
- Guard against using spiritual language to camouflage manipulation or control
- Ensure marriage counseling maintains biblical boundaries and professional safety

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Allowing ministry to consume family time and energy
  **Solution:** Treat family as your first ministry; protect specific boundaries including protected time blocks, dedicated family spaces, emotional availability limits, and family-first scheduling

- **Problem:** Treating Christianity casually rather than fearing God
  **Solution:** Evaluate whether your decisions reflect the concrete behaviors of prayer, Scripture obedience, moral choices, and reverent decision-making, or mere religious routine

- **Problem:** Reversing God's designed roles in marriage
  **Solution:** Husbands lead sacrificially through love, wives honor through respect; neither dominates nor abdicates. Recognize the husband's primary accountability to God for the home's spiritual direction while both spouses share responsibility for the marriage covenant

- **Problem:** Expecting the church to parent your children
  **Solution:** Parents bear primary responsibility for spiritual formation; the church supports but does not replace the home

- **Problem:** Painting bullseyes around ministry holes to look spiritual
  **Solution:** Measure authenticity by transformation over time through genuine spiritual formation, not by activity or title

- **Problem:** Letting culture dominate your children's values
  **Solution:** Establish stronger family influence through consistent presence, table devotions, and multi-generational traditions

- **Problem:** Focusing only on individual blessing without family impact
  **Solution:** Recognize that personal righteousness flows outward to influence every larger sphere of life
