# Put God First and Watch Him Handle the Rest

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God knows your needs and has no insufficiency problem. When you put God's rule and righteousness first in your life, He handles the rest. The antidote to worry is repositioning God to first place. Seek His kingdom and His righteousness first, and all these things will be added to you.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are struggling with worry, anxiety, or stress about life circumstances
- When you need to remember that God is aware of your situation and your needs
- When you want to learn how to prioritize God's rule over your own plans
- When you are facing decisions and need to seek God's perspective first

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge That God Knows Your Needs

Your heavenly Father knows what you have need of and has no insufficiency problem. He is well aware of your situation and what you are lacking. Stop trying to handle everything yourself and recognize that God is already aware of your circumstances.

### Step 2: Stop Worrying and Reposition God

The antidote to worry is repositioning God to first place in your life. Worry is concern on steroids where the issue controls you. God says do not worry when He has been repositioned. When God is not in first place, you have to take care of yourself because He bears no responsibility for your second-place location of Him.

### Step 3: Seek God's Kingdom First

Seek ye first the kingdom of God and His righteousness. The Greek word for kingdom means "rule" or "authority." God wants to be in charge. Pledge allegiance to His kingdom and operate under His rules, laws, and authority. This means pursuing His perspective before you ask everybody else what they think.

### Step 4: Seek God's Righteousness

Righteousness is the standard God has established by which His people are made acceptable to Him. Seek what is right based on His standard, not the standards of the society in which you live. What food is to the body, righteousness is to the soul. Without righteousness, the soul starves. Feed on God's standards instead of filling yourself with the world's unrighteousness.

### Step 5: Trust That God Will Handle the Rest

Put God first and He will handle the rest. "All these things will be added to you." God is eternal and already in the future. When you get to tomorrow, He will already be there. Do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will care for itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Pilot Illustration

A man was rushing to catch an airplane, worried he would miss his flight. While running, he bumped into a man in a pilot's uniform. The pilot told him to slow down because he was the pilot of that flight. The plane wasn't going anywhere without him. So the man could relax and fellowship with the pilot because he wasn't going to lose the flight when he hung out with the pilot. God says if you let Him be the Pilot, the Ruler, the Righteousness, you can chill. Nothing is going anywhere until He arrives.

### Example 2: Pledge Allegiance to God's Kingdom

When people become citizens of the United States, they learn the history and make a commitment, a public declaration of loyalty to the kingdom called the USA. They operate under its rules and laws with the goal of pursuing the American dream. What God is asking for is to pledge allegiance to His kingdom, of which He is King. Place your hand over your heart and say, "I am willing to function under your rule and under your authority. I am willing to conform to you. And I am willing to do it first."

## Best Practices

- ✅ Put God's rule and righteousness first before making decisions
- ✅ Seek God's perspective before asking everybody else what they think
- ✅ Pursue God's standard rather than society's standards
- ✅ Feed your soul with righteousness rather than worldly unrighteousness
- ✅ Trust that God is already in your future and will handle tomorrow
- ❌ Do not seek God's perspective only after everything else has been done
- ❌ Do not try to use God's name while operating under your own or society's standards
- ❌ Do not worry about tomorrow when you can trust God's eternal nature
- ❌ Do not locate God in second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, or last place

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You are worried about tomorrow and future circumstances
  **Solution:** Remember that God is eternal and already in the future. Trust that He will handle tomorrow and focus on today.
- **Problem:** You seek God's perspective only after seeking everyone else's first
  **Solution:** Seek ye first the kingdom of God. Look at His decision before you ask everybody else what they think.
- **Problem:** You operate under society's standards instead of God's righteousness
  **Solution:** Righteousness is the standard God established. Feed your soul with God's standards rather than worldly unrighteousness.
- **Problem:** You try to handle everything yourself because God is not first in your life
  **Solution:** Reposition God to first place. Put His rule and righteousness first and let Him handle the rest.
