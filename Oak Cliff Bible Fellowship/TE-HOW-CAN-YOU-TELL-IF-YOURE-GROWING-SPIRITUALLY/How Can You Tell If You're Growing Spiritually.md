# How Can You Tell If You're Growing Spiritually?

## Overview

Dr. Tony Evans teaches that the true motivation for ministry is helping believers mature in Christ. This sermon explains the difference between milk Christians and meat Christians, emphasizing that maturity comes from using the Word of God to discern good and evil. It calls every believer to pursue spiritual completeness so that on the day they stand before the Lord, they will be presented mature and blameless.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to assess your spiritual maturity and growth
- When working through spiritual growth challenges or life decisions
- When the user asks about how to discern spiritual development

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Goal of Spiritual Maturity

Recognize that God's goal for every believer is to be presented complete in Christ. Spiritual maturity means reaching a mature stage in your Christian experience. This means becoming more like Christ in character and conduct, attitudes, and actions.

### Step 2: Discern Between Milk and Meat Christians

A milk Christian is one who has not developed the habit of going to the Word of God to answer life's issues. They rely on friends, family, or secular wisdom first. A meat Christian has learned through practice to discern good and evil by using the Word. Like a baby drawn to a shiny marble over a diamond, milk Christians gravitate to appearances while meat Christians see true value.

### Step 3: Embrace the Role of the Church in Admonishment

You need the church not just for sermons or teaching, but for community and admonishment. Admonishment is when someone stronger than you comes alongside to encourage, motivate, challenge, and correct you in using God's Word. Without it, you will drift from the truth because you cannot force-feed yourself spiritual growth alone.

### Step 4: Allow Christ to Dwell Within You

The secret to maturity is Christ in you, the hope of glory. Jesus came not only to take you to heaven, but to bring heaven to you by indwelling you. Your change comes from the inside out. When the written Word connects with the living Word inside you, transformation happens. Welcome life's challenges as practice sessions where you apply Scripture and grow.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Milk Christian

A person attends church every Sunday, hears 52 sermons a year, memorizes Bible stories, and can quote Scripture. But when faced with a real-life decision, they immediately call their friends, search the internet, or follow their feelings instead of opening the Bible. They remain defeated because they have never developed the habit of letting God's Word inform their choices. Like a baby grabbing a shiny marble, they settle for what looks good rather than what has eternal value.

### Example 2: The Meat Christian

A believer faces a difficult opportunity at work that seems shiny and promising but conflicts with biblical principles. Instead of immediately saying yes, they pause, search the Scriptures, and use the Word to discern the true nature of the opportunity. They may also ask a mature believer to admonish them and help apply God's truth. Because they have practiced using the Word in smaller situations, they can now see through the shine and choose the diamond. They are growing from the inside out because Christ is actively living through them.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Develop the daily habit of appealing to God's Word before making decisions or facing challenges
- ✅ Welcome admonishment from stronger believers who can help you apply Scripture correctly
- ❌ Don't rely on secular wisdom, friends, or feelings as your first source of guidance
- ❌ Don't treat Christianity as merely attending church, hearing sermons, or knowing Bible stories without application

## Keep In Mind

- Spiritual maturity typically takes about 5 years if it is a priority, not 30 years of staying on milk
- Christ in you is the hope of glory, transformation comes from the inside out, not from managing external circumstances
- The church exists for community and admonishment, not just to provide sermons, teaching, or music
- You will stand before the Lord either complete or incomplete, a victor or a victim, eternal perspective should influence your daily choices

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not replace personal Bible study and application with merely consuming content through media, sermons, or teaching
- Ensure that admonishment comes from mature, grounded believers who are themselves rooted in Scripture
- Beware of "shiny" churches or teachings that look impressive but lack the solid food of genuine biblical depth

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Remaining a milk Christian by consuming teaching without applying the Word to real life
  **Solution:** Develop the habit of using Scripture to address life's realities, and invite mature believers to admonish and walk alongside you
- **Problem:** Resisting admonishment because it feels intrusive or uncomfortable
  **Solution:** Understand that without outside accountability, you will drift from the truth, embrace community as God's means of keeping you tethered to His Word
