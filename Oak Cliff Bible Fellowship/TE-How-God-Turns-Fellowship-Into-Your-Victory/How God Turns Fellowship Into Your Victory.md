# How God Turns Fellowship Into Your Victory

## Overview

This sermon explains how intimacy with God transforms believers into overcomers. It teaches that while believers are already declared overcomers by position in Christ, they must also become overcomers through practice. The message emphasizes that vertical fellowship with God must connect to horizontal love for other believers, and it provides three override buttons from Revelation 12:11 to help believers experience victory in their daily lives.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand the difference between being an overcomer by position and becoming an overcomer by practice
- When working through challenges that seem to have the final say in your life
- When the user asks about how fellowship with God leads to victory over circumstances

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand Your Position as an Overcomer

Recognize that if you are a believer in Jesus Christ, you are already declared an overcomer by position. Just as a Supreme Court decision overrules a lower court, your standing in Christ means that challenges, difficulties, and struggles do not have the final word. You are an overcomer because you are born of God (1 John 5:4-5). Recite this truth regularly: "I am an overcomer. I have stuff to overcome, but I am an overcomer."

### Step 2: Connect Vertical Fellowship with Horizontal Love

Intimacy with God must be connected to love for other believers. John teaches that you cannot want to be close to God without also being connected to His family. The vertical relationship with the Father must flow through you to others. When your intimacy with God is connected with love for others, you experience the "rechargeable battery" of God's overcoming power in your life. Pursue the well-being of others as a practical expression of your love for God.

### Step 3: Push the Three Override Buttons

To experience overcoming in your daily life, activate these three override buttons from Revelation 12:11:
1. **The Blood of the Lamb** - Call on the blood of Jesus to speak into your circumstances. Just as the blood of Abel spoke and brought judgment, the blood of Jesus speaks better things and asks God to address and judge the situations coming against you.
2. **The Word of Your Testimony** - Be willing to publicly identify with Jesus Christ. Do not be a secret agent Christian. Your testimony must be visible and verbal, clearly showing that you are a follower of Christ.
3. **Not Loving Your Life Even Unto Death** - Be fully committed, not a casual or cultural Christian. Give complete allegiance to Christ rather than only part-time faith.

### Step 4: Focus on Jesus Instead of Your Pain

Fix your eyes on Jesus, the author and finisher of your faith (Hebrews 12:2-3). Like Kerri Strug in the 1996 Olympics, who focused on her coach's voice instead of her excruciating ankle pain to complete her final vault and win the gold medal, you must focus on Jesus rather than your circumstances. When you shift your focus from your pain to Jesus, He propels you forward and gives you the ability to accomplish what you could not accomplish on your own strength, just as Kerri Strug completed her vault when she focused on her coach's voice rather than her ankle pain. This produces the peace that passes understanding even in the midst of chaos.

## Examples

### Example 1: Kerri Strug at the 1996 Olympics

During the 1996 Olympics, gymnast Kerri Strug twisted her ankle on her first vault and could barely stand. With one jump remaining that would determine whether America won the gold medal, she was in excruciating pain and had disappointed her team. Her coach, Bella Karolyi, encouraged her: "Kerri, you can do it. I believe in you. Come on, girl. Don't quit now. I know you're hurting, but don't throw in the towel." Kerri focused on her coach's voice rather than her pain, completed the vault, and secured the gold medal for her team. She later said, "I kept focusing on my coach, not my pain. I kept focusing on the voice over there and not the circumstance I was in."

### Example 2: The Blood Speaking Better Than Abel's Blood

At the Passover, the blood of the lamb caused the death angel to pass over Israel's homes. In Hebrews 12:24, the blood of Jesus is described as speaking better things than the blood of Abel. When Cain murdered Abel, Abel's blood cried out for judgment, and God responded by judging Cain. The blood of Jesus speaks even more powerfully into circumstances that need to be addressed. When you call on the blood of Jesus, you are asking God to judge and address the things that are coming against you, releasing His divine intervention into your situation.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Declare daily that you are an overcomer by position in Christ
- ✅ Pursue intimacy with God and love for other believers simultaneously
- ✅ Use the blood of the Lamb, your testimony, and full commitment as override buttons against the enemy
- ✅ Focus on Jesus and His voice rather than your pain and your situation
- ✅ Remember that God's commandments are not burdensome when rooted in love

## Keep In Mind

- Being an overcomer is both a position and a practice—you are an overcomer by status, but you must also become an overcomer through experience
- Rules without relationship lead to rebellion; intimacy with God lightens the burden of commandments
- The world is any system that keeps God out of the equation, and it operates under Satan's jurisdiction
- Love is not just an emotion but a decision to seek the well-being of another in God's name
- You are more than a conqueror through Christ who loves you (Romans 8:37)

## Security & Safety Notes

- The sermon does not encourage reckless behavior; full commitment to Christ means surrendering to His will, not taking unnecessary physical risks
- Public identification with Christ should be done wisely and in alignment with God's guidance for your specific context
- The override buttons are spiritual practices, not substitutes for seeking medical, legal, or professional help when needed

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to overcome through self-effort without recognizing your position in Christ
  **Solution:** Remind yourself daily that you are already an overcomer by position; overcoming flows from this identity, not from your own strength
  
- **Problem:** Separating fellowship with God from love for other believers
  **Solution:** Pursue both vertical intimacy with God and horizontal love for His family; neglecting either one limits your ability to experience overcoming
  
- **Problem:** Being a casual, cultural, or secret agent Christian
  **Solution:** Be fully committed and publicly identify with Jesus Christ; your testimony must be visible and verbal, not hidden or only cultural
  
  - **Problem:** Focusing on your pain and your situation instead of Jesus
   **Solution:** Like Kerri Strug focusing on her coach, fix your eyes on Jesus and listen to His voice rather than being dominated by your circumstances
