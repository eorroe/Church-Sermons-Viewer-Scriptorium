# Is Something Blocking You from Seeing More of God?

## Overview

This sermon addresses the pattern that prevents people who have professed faith in Christ from experiencing a measurable reduction in time spent in prayer, Scripture reading, or worship (worship is defined as singing, praying, or listening to worship music for a minimum of 5 minutes), and a corresponding increase in distraction. The problem occurs when prayer and Scripture reading drop below your established baseline, defined as the average daily minutes of prayer plus Scripture reading across the most recent 4-week period. A decrease of at least 25% means your current daily average falls to at most 75% of that baseline, provided the drop persists for at least three consecutive weeks. This is measured by confirming that, in at least three of the following five domains — (1) daily schedule, (2) major decisions, (3) financial choices, (4) relationships, and (5) discretionary hours not allocated to work, sleep, or obligations that would result in significant negative consequences if not fulfilled, as documented by a scheduled commitment or legal requirement — prayer and Scripture reading receive the largest single continuous sitting of at least 15 minutes of discretionary time, or are the primary input guiding the decision, where primary input means the factor whose removal would result in the largest measurable shift in the decision's expected outcome, as assessed by comparing the decision outcome with and without that factor present, where measurable shift means a change of at least 25% in the decision's outcome score on a 1-to-10 scale where 1 = clearly wrong and 10 = clearly right, assigned by the decision-maker and documented with a brief written justification in a journal entry. Either criterion suffices per domain. The baseline is calculated once at the start of each 28-day period and held fixed for the duration of that period. If at least two blocks are equal in duration, the block containing prayer or Scripture reading counts as the largest.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you experience any of the following measurable patterns: inability to complete a 5-minute prayer or Scripture session without checking a smartphone, tablet, computer, smart watch, or any electronic screen, leaving the room where the session began for a non-emergency reason, or engaging in an activity that is not an emergency confirmed by a medical professional or first responder, a pre-scheduled commitment with prior written agreement, or a time-sensitive obligation with documented financial, legal, or physical consequences evidenced by a signed document, official notice, or verified electronic communication (a dated email from the obligee, a text message with sender verification, or an official in-app notification), meaning activities that are not (a) an emergency requiring immediate action to prevent serious injury or death, as confirmed by a medical professional, law enforcement officer, or first responder, (b) a pre-scheduled commitment with prior agreement between all parties involved in the prayer or Scripture session, or (c) a time-sensitive obligation whose failure to act within the same day would result in documented financial loss such as an overdue bill, penalty fee, or contract breach, evidenced by a signed document, official notice, or verified electronic communication (a dated email from the obligee, a text message with sender verification, or an official in-app notification) from the obligee, legal consequence, or physical harm in at least three sessions out of a minimum of six total sessions attempted over a two-week period; the ability to verbally recount the request, passage, or reflection point in at least one complete sentence without external prompting by another person for a continuous period of at least 15 days; completing fewer than 15 minutes of prayer or fewer than 15 minutes of Scripture reading on a given day more than 3 times in any rolling 7-day period; or changing jobs, making a purchase exceeding 10% of monthly net income, entering or ending a romantic relationship, or relocating without completing at least one 5-minute prayer or Scripture session and without completing at least one scheduled conversation of 15 minutes or more with a spiritual mentor (a spiritual mentor is a professing Christian recognized by the senior pastor or elders of your home church as mature, who meets with you regularly for spiritual accountability) the decision must be the stated purpose of the meeting and documented in writing by both parties
- When your current daily prayer and Scripture reading total falls below 75% of your 4-week rolling average, or failing to attend previously accepted and documented roles within your local church or a ministry recognized by your local church leadership as doctrinally aligned and accountable, confirmed in writing or by a posted schedule, on at least 3 occasions over a 30-day period that you accepted within the past 90 days
- When prayer and Scripture reading do not receive the first available block of at least 30 minutes of discretionary time in the daily schedule, and no conflicting commitment is accepted during that block, on at least 4 days in any 7-day period (Monday through Sunday)

## Steps

### Step 1: Remember Where You Fell

Identify the specific date, event, or time period (e.g., a job change, the start of a romantic relationship, or a project deadline period) when you began to reduce or eliminate your established daily prayer and Scripture reading routine, defined as 15 minutes of prayer and 15 minutes of Scripture reading per day. Name the exact person, activity, goal, or specific belief or narrative such as 'I don't have time,' 'I deserve a break,' or 'God will understand' that now occupies the time and choices previously allocated to your daily 15 minutes of prayer and 15 minutes of Scripture reading.

### Step 2: Repent (Reverse Course)

Discretionary time = time not allocated to work, sleep, meals, hygiene, or documented obligations with financial, legal, or physical consequences. Take at least one concrete action within 7 days, evidenced by a dated journal entry describing the declined commitment or reallocation, to remove or reduce the named person, activity, goal, or specific belief or narrative identified in Step 1 and reinstate the displaced spiritual practice. Record this action in a dated physical or digital journal entry. Repentance means changing direction and taking at least one concrete action within 7 days, such as (but not limited to) declining a conflicting commitment, adjusting your daily schedule, or redirecting financial resources, demonstrated by declining a conflicting commitment or reallocating at least 15 minutes of discretionary time to prayer or Scripture toward your local church, a ministry with independently audited financial statements by a CPA, chartered accountant, or licensed public accountant in the ministry's jurisdiction, audited in accordance with GAAP or the ministry's local jurisdictional standard, or a needs-based giving initiative approved in writing by the senior pastor or elders of your home church and listed on the church's official website or bulletin.

### Step 3: Repeat the Deeds You Did at First

Resume the following specific practices from your most recent continuous 365-day period during which you maintained a documented record of intentional daily practice, where intentional daily practice is defined as a minimum of 15 minutes of prayer and a minimum of 15 minutes of Scripture reading per day and a documented record is a dated journal entry, digital log that records date, start time, end time, and discipline type for each session, or calendar entry with a minimum of start time, end time, and discipline type: daily prayer, daily Scripture reading, weekly worship defined as at least 5 minutes of singing, praying, or listening to worship music, obedience to one command from Scripture that you have not practiced in the past 30 days, as evidenced by a specific observable action such as reconciling with a brother or sister rather than merely thinking about forgiving them, written explicitly in your journal, and at least one weekly accountability meeting with a fellow believer who professes faith in Christ and is actively practicing the disciplines described in this sermon. The accountability partner must present their own log or journal entry from the past 7 days as evidence of their practice. If you do not have a documented record from your first 365-day period of intentional daily practice, use a daily minimum of 15 minutes of uninterrupted prayer and 15 minutes of Scripture reading as your starting standard. If you do have a documented record, return to that same frequency and duration completed on at least 26 of 30 days. If multiple qualifying periods exist, use the most recent one.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Church at Ephesus

The Ephesian church was commended for its service, sacrifice, steadfastness, separation, and suffering for the faith. Yet Jesus had a grievance against them: they had left their first love. This demonstrates that serving in ministry, attending church, or performing religious duties without accompanying personal time with God in prayer and Scripture does not constitute a healthy relationship with Him.

### Example 2: A Season of Busyness

A believer may spend so much time on career, family obligations, or ministry activities that personal prayer and Scripture reading fall below their 4-week average baseline, provided the drop persists for at least three consecutive weeks. Remember: any decline in frequency or duration of prayer and Scripture reading that meets the threshold defined in the 'When to Follow This Sermon' section, repenting of the misplaced priority, and returning to a consistent, daily minimum of 15 minutes of uninterrupted prayer and 15 minutes of Scripture reading, results in a documented pattern of at least 26 of 30 days in prayer, Scripture reading, and specific loving actions such as volunteering, giving, or serving others.

## Best Practices

1. Examine your heart at the start of each week to ensure God remains your first priority. Specifically, answer this question for each of the five domains:
   - (1) daily schedule
   - (2) major decisions
   - (3) financial choices
   - (4) relationships
   - (5) discretionary hours not allocated to work, sleep, or obligations (documented by a scheduled commitment or legal requirement) that would result in a measurable impact score of 7 or higher on a 1-to-10 scale where 1 = negligible, 7 = loss of income or serious property damage, 8 = serious physical injury requiring medical attention, 9 = permanent disability, and 10 = death, as assessed by the individual and documented with a brief written justification in a journal entry if not fulfilled
   Do prayer and Scripture reading receive the largest single block of discretionary time or are they the primary input guiding the decision? Record your findings in a journal.
2. Record daily in your journal, log, or tracking app your actual time spent in prayer, Scripture reading, worship, and obedience; review weekly against a rolling 28-day average of daily prayer and Scripture minutes, recalculated daily, and Either your current 4-week rolling average declines to at most 75% of your established baseline OR your 14-day total falls below 75% of the preceding 14-day total. This triggers Step 1 (Remember) immediately and requires completion of Step 2 (Repent) within 7 days.
3. Identify at least two accountability partners who meet ALL of the following criteria:
   - profess faith in Christ as evidenced by a verbal or written account of one's conversion or call to faith shared in the presence of at least two other people, baptism, or membership in a local church (any one of these suffices)
   - actively practicing as measured by a documented pattern of at least 15 minutes of daily prayer and 15 minutes of daily Scripture reading
   Each accountability partner should also be discipling at least one person who meets the same criteria, defined as meeting regularly with a professing Christian for the purpose of spiritual growth, Scripture study, and accountability, with a documented meeting schedule and written spiritual growth goals, with a meeting schedule of every two weeks, shared Scripture study of at least 30 minutes per meeting, and written or digital spiritual growth goals reviewed together. If no currently available partner meets all criteria, seek to partner with the closest qualifying individual and begin mutual discipleship within 30 days.
4. ❌ Don't equate busyness in ministry with intimacy with Christ
5. ❌ Don't assume that past service guarantees present closeness with God
6. ❌ Avoid delaying repentance by justifying competing priorities

## Keep In Mind

- God makes His will, peace, and presence known to those who demonstrate any of the following:
  - dated written or digital records showing at least one reallocation of at least 15 minutes of discretionary time to prayer or Scripture
  - a financial giving record showing an absolute increase of at least 5 percentage points compared to the trailing 3-month average as a percentage of monthly net income. If fewer than 3 months of giving history exist, use the available months of history; if none exist, this criterion does not apply until 3 months of history are recorded.
  - a documented relationship decision such as reconciling with a family member or ending a relationship characterized by at least two separate days on which harmful behavior occurred within a 90-day period, where harmful behavior means action or inaction causing measurable emotional injury diagnosed within 90 days of the incident(s) and documented in a clinical record by a licensed mental health professional, physical injury documented in medical records by a licensed health care provider, or financial injury documented by dated journal entries or witness accounts from individuals who directly witnessed the incident(s) and are not immediate family members of either party
  - A documented relationship decision qualifies only if each instance of harmful behavior was followed by a refusal to acknowledge the harm or make restitution when directly confronted in a face-to-face or video conversation with the harm-doer present, taken after prayer with at least one spiritual mentor and counsel from a licensed professional or spiritual leader
- A documented commitment to put God first in major life decisions, evidenced by seeking prayer and counsel from a licensed professional or spiritual leader before changing jobs, making purchases exceeding 10% of monthly net income, entering or ending relationships, or relocating
- A believer can gradually reduce personal time with God while maintaining outward religious activity, even after years of a documented pattern of at least 26 of 30 days in prayer and Scripture reading, maintained over the preceding 6 months, combined with specific loving actions such as volunteering, giving, or serving others
- Repentance requires a demonstrable change in behavior — not just feeling sorry — evidenced by resuming the displaced spiritual practice to at least your defined daily minimum (or 15 minutes of uninterrupted prayer and 15 minutes of Scripture if no baseline was previously established) within 7 days
- The three-step process of Remember, Repent, Repeat is designed to return you to your previously recorded frequency and duration of prayer and Scripture reading, not to gain additional merit, status, or blessing beyond what you already have in Christ

## Security & Safety Notes

- This sermon is for personal spiritual growth and does not replace pastoral counseling
- If you are experiencing spiritual distress lasting at least two weeks, characterized by persistent doubt, despair, or disconnection from God that does not resolve with normal spiritual practices (prayer, Scripture, counsel), or by inability to maintain employment, attend to personal hygiene, or care for dependents, or by thoughts of self-harm documented in writing, or by inability to eat or sleep for at least three consecutive 24-hour periods, or if you are in physical danger, seek immediate help from trusted spiritual leaders who profess faith in Christ and are recognized as mature by having served in a recognized leadership or discipleship role for at least one year or emergency services
- Do not use the call to repentance as a tool for guilt or manipulation of others

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating the three steps as completing Remember, Repent, Repeat once and considering the matter closed, rather than returning to the practice each time any decline in frequency or duration of prayer and Scripture reading that meets the threshold defined in the 'When to Follow This Sermon' section is detected. Perform this review monthly.
  **Solution:** Set a recurring monthly 30-minute review in which you compare your current spiritual disciplines (prayer, Scripture, worship, obedience) against your most recent documented 4-week baseline, and document any decline in frequency (If you separate sessions every day, expected = 28 sessions per 28 days. If you combine sessions every day, expected = 14 sessions per 28 days. If you mix, add combined days + 2 × separated days to get expected total, and apply the threshold of missing more than 3 sessions to that total. A session is missed if no prayer or Scripture reading occurs on that day; missing more than 3 of your expected 28 combined prayer and Scripture sessions in any rolling 28-day period) or quality (inability to identify at least one prayer request by the name of a person, topic, or situation prayed for, one Scripture passage by book, chapter, and verse, or one reflection point of at least 10 words from the session)
- **Problem:** Measuring your own spiritual health by comparing the quantity or visibility of your ministry activity to that of other believers, rather than evaluating your own love for Christ and consistency in private disciplines
  **Solution:** Evaluate your spiritual health by your own consistency in prayer, Scripture reading, and obedience, rather than by comparing your ministry output or public spiritual activities to those of others
- **Problem:** Assuming that serving in ministry, attending church, or performing religious duties pleases God when personal prayer and Scripture reading have been neglected for more than 30 consecutive days
  **Solution:** Evaluate whether your public spiritual actions are motivated by a desire to honor Christ, as evidenced by continuing the practice even when no one observes it, no one thanks you, and it is recorded only in a private journal. If the motivation is a need for approval, recognition, or religious status, this indicates a misplaced priority.
