# Manage Sexual Desire in a Godly Way

## Overview

This sermon teaches unmarried believers how to honor God with their sexuality by practically managing sexual desire through spiritual disciplines, self-control, and planned behaviors. It affirms that sexual drive is a God-given gift, not inherently sinful, while calling Christians to channel that desire toward godly purposes and reserve its expression for marriage. The goal is purity, not guilt—empowering believers to live with integrity, accountability, and dependence on Christ's power.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When an unmarried Christian struggles with managing sexual temptation or tension
- When seeking biblically rooted guidance for sexual purity
- When learning to redirect sexual energy into ministry, discipleship, and service to others
- When preparing for marriage or wanting to submit your body and mind to Christ

## Steps

### Step 1: Appreciate Your God-Given Sexuality

Gratefully accept your sexual nature and the purposes God has designed for it within marriage. Do not deny, repress, or feel guilty about having sexual feelings—they are normal and God-planned. Offer your sexuality to God by dedicating your body, mind, and desires to Him as a living sacrifice (Romans 12:1), refusing to use them for self-gratifying or sinful purposes, and trusting Him to guide you in honoring Him with your whole person. Redirect your energies into ministry, discipleship, and service for the Lord, and seek to glorify Him in your choices and actions.

### Step 2: Cultivate a Mindset That Honors God

Make honoring God and exercising self-control your aim, even with your imagination. Recognize that much of today's media overemphasizes sexuality and promotes instant gratification. Practice self-discipline through daily prayer and regular Scripture reading, putting on His spiritual armor by practicing the disciplines of Ephesians 6:10-18 (truth, righteousness, peace, faith, salvation, the Word of God, and prayer), and trusting Jesus to fight the battle for you.

### Step 3: Yield to the Holy Spirit's Power

Remember that the Holy Spirit dwells within every Christian. Your body is God's temple (2 Corinthians 6:16). Submit your desires to the Spirit and resist temptation by praying specifically for the Spirit's guidance and self-control, submitting every temptation to Him the moment it arises, and walking in His strength rather than relying on your own willpower to resist.

### Step 4: Follow Jesus' Example of Self-Discipline

Jesus was "tempted in every way, just as we are—yet he did not sin" (Hebrews 4:15), which means He managed His sexual drives for the glory of God. He directed all His energies toward serving the genuine needs of others through tangible ministry. Serve alongside Him by ministering to people in tangible ways—at church, in your community, and in your relationships—and let Him serve through you by offering you as an instrument of His love, compassion, and truth to a world in need.

### Step 5: Redirect Your Thoughts and Body When Urges Intensify

When a sexual urge grows, actively redirect your thoughts and reduce the intensity of the desire by removing the mental and physical fuel feeding it. Specifically: remove yourself from triggering situations, engage in physical activity, pray, or speak with a trusted person—any action that shifts your focus and reduces the intensity of the desire so you can think rationally and make God-honoring choices.

### Step 6: Ruthlessly Avoid Unnecessary Sexual Stimulation

Be intentional about what you watch, read, and think. Men should especially avoid visual media that shows nakedness or sexual activity, as visual stimuli frequently trigger desire. Both men and women can be affected by visual stimuli, and both genders should be ruthless about eliminating unnecessary sexual stimulation—defined as anything that intentionally or recklessly arouses sexual desire outside of marriage, including pornography, sexually explicit content, media depicting romantic or sexual intimacy that tends to arouse lust, and lingering thoughts that stir desire once recognized.

### Step 7: Guard Your Eyes and Heart Against Lust

Follow Jesus' command not to look at anyone lustfully (Matthew 5:28). Follow Job's example: "I made a covenant with my eyes not to look lustfully at a young woman" (Job 31:1). Practice chastity by training your mind, thoughts, imagination, and eyes to refrain from lustful thoughts and actions.

### Step 8: Get Up When You Wake Up

For many people, lying in bed while drowsy and mentally unfocused opens the door to sexual arousal and increases desire. When you wake up in the morning, get up immediately. If you struggle with nighttime temptation in bed, apply the same principle: do not linger in bed when arousal begins—get up, leave the room, and engage in a different activity within moments.

### Step 9: Work Vigorously

Work with your full physical effort during the day. Physical exertion helps you fall asleep quickly at night, reducing nighttime vulnerability to temptation.

### Step 10: Manage Your Mind with God's Truth

Fill your mind with what is true, noble, right, pure, lovely, and admirable (Philippians 4:8). Intentionally choose thoughts that Philippians 4:8 describes as true, noble, right, pure, lovely, and admirable—thoughts that crowd out sexual cravings.

### Step 11: Channel Sexual Energy Into Meaningful Service

Sublimate sexual energy into service for others in Christ's name by intentionally redirecting the physical and emotional energy of sexual desire into active, God-glorifying pursuits—such as ministry, discipleship, physical labor, or creative work—so that the energy that would fuel lust instead fuels love and service. Channel it into projects and activities that fully occupy your mind.

### Step 12: Keep Your Focus on Marriage and Its Godly Purpose

Prepare for the responsibilities of marriage and family, keeping your attention on the benefits of sexual purity. Understand that marriage is not primarily about sex (Ephesians 5:21-33), so do not rush into marriage merely to satisfy sexual desire.

### Step 13: Replace Lustful Imagination with Pure Memories

Use your imagination and memory to recall physical sights, sounds, smells, feelings, and tastes experienced in ways that bring no shame—such as a beautiful landscape, a beloved hymn, the smell of fresh bread, a warm embrace from a family member, or a delicious meal God has provided. Choose to fill your mind with memories that Philippians 4:8 describes as true, noble, right, pure, lovely, and admirable—memories you can reflect on without shame—rather than lustful ones.

### Step 14: Change Your Environment When Urges Intensify

When a sexual urge intensifies, change your environment immediately—ideally within seconds of recognizing the temptation. Safe environments include: taking a walk with a trusted friend, going to a public space such as a coffee shop or church, joining a group activity, or stepping outside for fresh air. Do not remain in the same room or situation where the temptation began.

### Step 15: Develop a Hands-On Hobby

Cultivate a hobby that requires active use of your hands and mind—something that occupies your mind and body in God-honoring, non-sexual ways such as exercise, service, or creative work.

### Step 16: Identify and Avoid Triggers

Take note of what triggers your sexual urges, and avoid those triggers. If certain lustful thoughts reliably follow a specific activity—such as browsing certain websites, watching a particular type of show, or being alone in a certain place—stop engaging in that activity and replace it with a healthier alternative. Do what you can to change the situation, routine, or relationship associated with the desire.

### Step 17: Talk to God About Your Struggles

Share every situation and feeling with the Lord Jesus. Prayerfully bring your temptations before Him and trust Him with your weaknesses.

### Step 18: Confess Sin Immediately and Receive Forgiveness

If you do sin, confess it to Christ without delay and receive His forgiveness by honestly acknowledging your sin before God (1 John 1:9), turning away from it through repentance, and trusting in the finished work of Jesus Christ on the cross—not your own efforts—to cleanse you from all unrighteousness. Do not let shame control you. Thank God that Christ already died for all sin, that God's love is unconditional, and His mercy is new every morning. Ask God for victory, relying on prayer, Scripture memorization, the Holy Spirit's power, Christian community, and the specific passages of Ephesians 6:10-18, 1 Peter 5:6-11, and James 4:7-8 as His means of victory.

### Step 19: Pursue Accountability With Other Believers

Cultivate close, honest relationships and accountability with other mature Christians who are grounded in Scripture, accountable to church leadership, and have demonstrated the fruit of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22-23). Ask them to pray for you and be available for weekly conversations, quick texts when temptation strikes, or meeting in person—so they can encourage you, hold you accountable, and walk with you through the battle for purity.

### Step 20: Acknowledge Struggles to a Trusted Leader

Confide in someone you can trust, such as your pastor. You are not the first person to struggle with managing sexual nature. Hiding your temptation and sin from a trusted pastor or mature Christian by concealing the truth about your temptation, faking spiritual maturity by pretending you have no temptation, and acting hypocritically by presenting yourself as blameless while secretly sinning will each damage your character, undermine your witness, and ultimately destroy your integrity. If you do slip, your confidante can support you with prayer, exhortation, and encouraging guidance.

### Step 21: Be Patient and Persistent in the Process

Realize that striving for obedience in managing sexual desire may not be easy or quick. Practice self-control through the means described in this document. As 1 Corinthians 9:26-27 teaches, discipline yourself so that you will remain effective in the ministry God has prepared for those who faithfully follow Him.

### Step 22: Use Music to Shift Your Mind

When you feel sexual cravings, listen to music with lyrics that align with Philippians 4:8—such as hymns, worship songs, or music from Christian artists whose lyrics are rooted in Scripture and promote godly living—to redirect your thoughts and lift your heart to the Lord.

### Step 23: Take It One Day at a Time

Depend on God's grace daily and keep your eyes on Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith (Hebrews 12:2). Trust God for the strength you need right now, taking today's challenges one moment at a time.

### Step 24: Consider Marriage If Control Is Not Possible

If you consistently find yourself unable to control your sexual urges through prayer, discipline, accountability, and the other means described in this sermon—and you have sought wise counsel from mature believers—it is good and right to marry, specifically when your motive aligns with God's design for marriage as a Christ-centered partnership (a marriage where both spouses prioritize following Christ together, loving each other as Christ loved the church, and building their relationship on biblical principles), marriage will help you flee from ongoing, unrepentant temptation rather than merely escaping discomfort, and you are prepared to love and serve your spouse sacrificially. Paul writes, "It is better to marry than to burn with passion" (1 Corinthians 7:8-9). Singleness is a good gift, and so is marriage. However, do not rush into marriage merely to have sex—understand God's design for marriage and sex so you build a strong, biblically grounded relationship with your spouse.

## Examples

### Example 1: A Single Young Man Battling Visual Temptation

A young unmarried man finds his sexual desire intensified by social media and movies. He applies these steps by deleting apps that trigger lust, scheduling morning workouts to avoid lying in bed, listening to worship music when cravings hit, and meeting weekly with an accountability partner. Over time, he experiences greater peace and self-control.

### Example 2: A Single Young Woman Managing Emotional Triggers

A single woman realizes her sexual urges are often triggered by certain romantic conversations or media. She begins to avoid those conversations, redirects her energy into serving at church and volunteering, changes her environment by calling a friend when she feels tempted, and confides in her pastor for ongoing guidance.

### Example 3: Repentance and Recovery After a Fall

A believer struggles with recurring sin and feels burdened by shame. After reading these instructions, he confesses his sin to Christ that same day, receives His forgiveness, and opens up to a trusted Christian mentor. Together they identify triggers, establish new habits, and walk in the freedom and grace God provides.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Accept your sexuality as a God-given gift, not something shameful
- ✅ Redirect sexual energy into ministry, discipleship, physical labor, and creative work
- ✅ Be ruthless in eliminating unnecessary sexual stimulation from your environment
- ✅ Confess sin without delay and receive God's forgiveness
- ✅ Cultivate honest accountability relationships with other believers
- ✅ Meditate on Philippians 4:8 and fill your mind with pure, godly content
- ✅ Take practical action—exercise, physical activity, change of environment—when urges intensify
- ❌ Do not repress or deny your sexual feelings—this is not the path to purity
- ❌ Do not rely solely on willpower without the Holy Spirit's power
- ❌ Do not hide your struggles or live in shame and hypocrisy
- ❌ Do not rush into marriage merely to satisfy sexual desire

## Keep In Mind

- Sexuality is God's good gift, not inherently sinful or dirty
- Self-control is a command, not a suggestion (1 Corinthians 9:24-27; 2 Peter 1:6)
- Wet dreams (nocturnal emissions) are a natural, involuntary relief of sexual pressure and not sinful. However, while they are involuntary and do not constitute sin, they should not be used as an excuse for maintaining repeated, chosen sinful behaviors during waking hours. Take responsibility for the thoughts and behaviors you can control, and seek God's grace for the areas you cannot.
- Sexual release through self-gratification reinforces lust rather than providing fulfillment
- Fulfillment in marriage comes from serving your spouse, not from self-gratification
- Sexual purity is a lifelong calling for both unmarried and married believers (1 Corinthians 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3-8; Hebrews 13:4)
- Marriage is a good gift, but it is not primarily about sex—it is about Christ-centered partnership

## Security & Safety Notes

- Guard your heart and mind against sexual temptation by monitoring your media consumption (tracking what you watch, read, and browse) and thought patterns (noticing when lustful thoughts arise, what triggers them, and how often they occur), and setting specific boundaries—such as deleting triggering apps, using content filters, and redirecting your mind the moment an impure thought enters
- Use accountability partners to help you stay safe and honest
- Do not share explicit details of temptation with those who are not spiritually mature or trustworthy—defined as those who are grounded in Scripture, accountable to church leadership, demonstrate the fruit of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22-23), and have a proven record of handling sensitive matters with discretion and biblical wisdom
- Establish and maintain specific boundaries in relationships—such as limits on physical contact, private time alone together, and topics of conversation—to protect both yourself and others from lust

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Feeling guilty about having sexual desires as an unmarried person
  **Solution:** Remember that sexual desire itself is not sinful. God designed you with these drives. The issue is not the desire, but how you handle it. Accept it as God's gift and submit it to Him.

- **Problem:** Relying only on willpower to resist temptation
  **Solution:** Self-discipline is commanded, but it must be empowered by the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 9:24-27). Stay close to God, wear His armor, and invite Him to direct your desires.

- **Problem:** Falling into sin and being consumed by shame
  **Solution:** Confess immediately to Christ (1 John 1:9), receive His forgiveness, and walk in freedom. Shame is not from God. His mercy is new every morning.

- **Problem:** Hiding your temptation and sin from a trusted pastor or mature Christian and living a double life
  **Solution:** Open up to a trusted pastor or mature Christian. Concealing your temptation from those who can help you damages your character, while confession brings healing, support, and accountability.

- **Problem:** Considering marriage solely to escape sexual tension
  **Solution:** Marriage is a good gift, but rushing into it for sexual release alone creates a poor foundation. Understand God's design for marriage first, then pursue it with wisdom.
