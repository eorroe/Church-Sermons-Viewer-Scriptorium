# 6 Ways the Holy Spirit Guides and Strengthens You Every Day

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the Holy Spirit serves as a personal guide for every believer, providing divine direction, truth, and strength to live a life that fulfills God's purpose. Through six specific ways, the Holy Spirit helps you know what you need to know, go where you need to go, say what you need to say, wait for God's perfect timing, resist temptation, and avoid dangers and mistakes. Understanding how to listen to and follow the Holy Spirit's guidance is essential for making decisions that align with God's will and accomplishing the purpose for which God created you.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are waiting on God for a specific answer or direction in your life
- When you need to make a difficult decision and want to know God's will
- When you feel lost or uncertain about your next step
- When you struggle with temptation, addiction, or compulsive behavior
- When you want to avoid mistakes and dangers in your relationships, career, or ministry
- When you desire to hear God's voice more clearly and consistently
- When you are preparing for a major life change, such as marriage, career shift, or relocation
- When you need to know what to say in a conflict, interview, or witnessing situation

## Steps

### Step 1: Talk to the Holy Spirit While You Wait

When you are waiting on God for something, do not simply wait in silence. Engage the Holy Spirit in conversation during your waiting period. Ask Him to help you understand the specific direction God wants you to take, the specific knowledge you need for the situation ahead, the specific words you should say when the opportunity arises, and the specific length of time God intends for you to wait. Waiting without praying is passive and unproductive. Talking to the Holy Spirit while you wait turns your waiting time into preparation time. God will use that time to develop your character, teach you His ways, and position you for what He has planned next.

### Step 2: Allow the Holy Spirit to Help You Know What You Need to Know

The Holy Spirit teaches you essential truth and information at the moment you need it. He brings to mind Bible verses you have read, songs you have heard, articles you have read, and insights from sermons that apply directly to your current situation. This is called revelation. The Holy Spirit will not contradict the Bible, but He will illuminate its meaning for your specific circumstance. Read God's Word daily so the Holy Spirit has a full library of truth to draw from when you need it. The more Scripture you store in your heart, the more the Holy Spirit can remind you of it when a specific need arises.

### Step 3: Allow the Holy Spirit to Help You Go Where You Need to Go

The Holy Spirit opens specific doors of opportunity and directs your path toward places human planning alone cannot reach. This includes your career direction, your relationships, your ministry opportunities, and your living situation. When the Holy Spirit nudges you to go somewhere or do something specific, obey immediately. God has placed dreams and visions in your heart that can only be fulfilled through His guidance. The Holy Spirit will show you the specific doors to enter and keep you from the specific ones to avoid. Trust that He is leading you to the exact places God has prepared for you.

### Step 4: Allow the Holy Spirit to Help You Say What You Need to Say

The Holy Spirit gives you the right words at the right time and in the right way. Whether you are in a conflict with your spouse, a job interview, a witnessing conversation, or a conversation with someone who is grieving, the Holy Spirit knows exactly what needs to be said. Do not panic when you do not know what to say. Pray immediately and ask, "Holy Spirit, what do You want me to say?" Then wait for the specific words to come to your mind. The longer you walk with God, the more quickly you will recognize His voice. You will stop saying words that make problems worse and start saying the wise, healing words that bring resolution.

### Step 5: Allow the Holy Spirit to Help You Wait for God's Perfect Timing

Timing determines whether an action succeeds or fails. You can do the right thing at the wrong time and it will fail. A vision from God comes in three parts: what God is going to do, how God will do it, and when God will do it. Most people stop at part one and rush ahead, only to fail and then wonder if they missed God's will. The "how" typically differs from how you would naturally do it. The "when" is the most important part of all. God's timing is exactly right for every situation. If you had moved before God's timing arrived, you would have received less than God intended to give you. Wait confidently. Delays are not denials. Delays mean God is preparing you for what He has planned.

### Step 6: Allow the Holy Spirit to Help You Resist What You Cannot Normally Resist

The Holy Spirit's power is stronger than your willpower. Temptations, compulsions, desires, and addictive patterns will be present throughout your entire Christian life. In fact, the more you grow spiritually, the more Satan will target you with temptation. But the Holy Spirit does not remove the temptation; He gives you the power to resist it. Having an attraction is not the same as committing a sin. An attraction is not an action. An action is what constitutes sin. The Holy Spirit gives you the strength to say no and to choose what God says is truly important. Rely on the Holy Spirit's power, not your own willpower.

### Step 7: Allow the Holy Spirit to Help You Avoid Dangers and Mistakes

The Holy Spirit warns you, cautions you, and protects you from disaster. He speaks to your mind with clear warnings, saying in effect, "If you go down that road, it will lead to destruction." He does not only offer encouragement; He also gives specific warnings. The less you listen to Him, the more mistakes you will make and the more dead ends you will face. You will hear the Holy Spirit most clearly when you are relaxed and calm. Fear-based thoughts are never from the Holy Spirit. If your thought is rooted in fear, anxiety, or panic, it is not God speaking. Trust His specific warnings and you will spare yourself from unnecessary pain.

### Step 8: Pause and Be Quiet

You cannot hear the Holy Spirit when you are rushed, busy, or distracted. You must intentionally pause and be quiet. Psalm 37:7 says, "Be still and know that I am God." Isaiah 30:15 says, "In quietness and confidence will be your strength." Find a private place where you can be alone, shut out noise and distraction, and sit in silence for at least five to ten minutes. If you cannot find a private room, go into the bathroom and sit in a stall. Create a consistent practice of solitude so you can hear God's voice clearly.

### Step 9: Humbly Ask the Holy Spirit to Guide You

You have not because you ask not. But you must ask with a humble spirit, because your attitude is what the Holy Spirit is looking for. Psalm 25:9 says, "God guides the humble in what is right. And He teaches them His way." God guides those who are humble, not those who are proud, not those who are arrogant, and not those who think they have every answer already. Psalm 27:11 says, "Teach me, Lord, what You want me to do. Lead me down the right path." Come before God with the genuine cry, "I cannot do this on my own. I have to know the specific next step. I cannot move forward without direction. Guide me."

### Step 10: Be Willing to Do What He Says Before He Tells You

If you approach God with the attitude, "I will do what You tell me to do, but You must tell me first, and then I will decide whether I want to do it or not," you will hear nothing. You must start with desire, longing, and commitment. Say to God, "I want to do Your will. I choose to do Your will before I know what it is. Now reveal to me what it is." John 7:17 says, "Whoever is willing to do what God wants and chooses it, will know what I teach if what I teach comes from God. The knowing comes after the choosing." Tell God you are already committed to obeying before He reveals the specific instruction.

### Step 11: Look to God's Word

God has given you both a Guide and a Guidebook. The Guide is the Holy Spirit. The Guidebook is the Bible. You need both. Psalm 119:105 says, "Your Word is a lamp to guide me and a light for my path." The Bible says, "Guide my steps by Your word, so I will not be overcome by evil." God's will is found in God's Word. Stop looking for a vision apart from the Word and start looking for a verse in the Word. Stop looking for a sign apart from Scripture and start looking for what Scripture says. The Spirit of Truth will lead you into truth, and that truth is found in the Bible.

### Step 12: Expect His Guidance in Faith

If you are not expecting the Holy Spirit to give you specific direction, why should He give it to you? God responds to your faith, not to your complaints. God responds to your faith, not to your gripes. James 1:5 says that if you want to know what God wants you to do, ask Him, and He will give you specific wisdom. He is always ready to give wisdom to all who ask Him in faith. But you must genuinely expect Him to answer. If you do not ask in faith, do not expect God to give you clear direction. Trust God's guidance because He is never wrong in what He reveals.

### Step 13: Wait for God's Response

Do not get in a hurry. Do not rush ahead. If God has not given you specific direction yet, do not start that business, do not say yes to that engagement, and do not make that decision on your own. The Bible says God does speak, through Scripture, through circumstances, through the inner witness of the Holy Spirit, and through other believers. If you wait, the uncertainty will become clearer. Galatians 5:5 says, "We are led by the Spirit to wait." Wait in the confident hope of righteousness through faith. The Spirit will say go at the right time, and the Spirit will say wait at the right time. Trust that He is working everything together for your good.

## Examples

### Example 1: Choosing the Right Hospital

Rick and Laura needed brain surgery for cancer. One hospital said the surgery was inoperable. Another hospital said they could and would do the surgery. Rick had four hospitals to choose from and did not know which one to select. He felt consistent internal peace about one particular hospital. Every person he met, every doctor he talked to, even the check-in nurse, felt right. The Holy Spirit had given him multiple specific nudges. He did not need anyone else to pray about it because he already had his answer. He went to that hospital, the surgery was successful, and the doctor was elated with the outcome. This demonstrates how the Holy Spirit gives repeated nudges and confirmations to guide you into the right decision.

### Example 2: Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch

Philip was walking down a dusty road when the Holy Spirit gave him a specific instruction: "Go catch a ride with that chariot." Philip did not know who was in the chariot or why he was supposed to go, but he obeyed immediately. The Ethiopian leader was reading the book of Isaiah and did not understand it. Philip explained the passage and told him about Jesus. The man was baptized, and he took Christianity back to Ethiopia. Because one man listened to a specific nudge, the Ethiopian church was established within a few years and became one of the earliest Christian communities in Africa. This demonstrates how the Holy Spirit will give you a specific direction and a specific message if you are listening and obeying.

## Best Practices

- Read God's Word every day so the Holy Spirit has specific truth stored in your mind to remind you of when a future need arises
- Act immediately when you feel a specific impression to do good, because each act of obedience strengthens your ability to hear the Holy Spirit
- Do not delay when you feel prompted to speak, serve, give, or pray, because delay weakens your sensitivity to the Holy Spirit's voice
- Sit in quiet solitude for at least five to ten minutes every day so you can hear the Holy Spirit's voice clearly
- Ask for guidance with genuine humility, not pride or entitlement
- Say yes to God's will before you know the specific instruction, because the specific direction comes after you commit to obey
- Trust God's timing and do not rush ahead, because delays are preparations for what God has planned
- Rely on the Holy Spirit's power rather than your own willpower when facing temptation
- Write down insights and verses so you can recall them when the Holy Spirit brings them to your mind at a future moment
- Expect God to speak specifically, because He is always ready to give wisdom to those who ask in faith

## Keep In Mind

- The Holy Spirit is God Himself dwelling within every believer, which is why Jesus said His departure was actually better for us—because the Holy Spirit can be with all believers everywhere at the same time, not limited to one physical location
- The Holy Ghost and the Holy Spirit are the same Person. The word "ghost" comes from a 1611 English translation in which "ghost" meant "spirit," not a disembodied dead person. The Greek word is pneuma, which means breath, air, or spirit. Using "ghost" today causes confusion because the modern meaning of ghost is a deceased person's spirit
- You will grow in recognizing the Holy Spirit's specific voice through consistent time in God's Word and prayer, just as a spouse or close friend's voice becomes recognizable after years of relationship
- If you are not experiencing specific promptings from the Holy Spirit, the primary cause is that you are not spending sufficient time in God's Word and in quiet prayer where you can hear Him
- The Holy Spirit regularly speaks through simple, everyday thoughts that come to your mind, not through dramatic visions, audible voices, or supernatural phenomena
- You must genuinely choose to be led by God; if you have decided to be your own boss and make all your own decisions without consulting God, you will not hear His voice

## Security & Safety Notes

- If a thought or impression contradicts the Bible, it is not from the Holy Spirit
- If a thought is based in fear, anxiety, or panic, it is not from the Holy Spirit, because God has not given us a spirit of fear
- Test every impression against Scripture, because God's Word and God's will never contradict each other
- The Holy Spirit will never ask you to do something that violates the clear teaching of the Bible
- Evil spirits can also give thoughts, so discernment through God's Word is essential

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to create your own opportunities instead of following the specific opportunities God is already creating
  **Solution:** Stop forcing outcomes and start listening for the Holy Spirit's specific promptings. God creates the specific opportunities; your role is to recognize them and step into them when He confirms the timing is right.

- **Problem:** Rushing ahead of God's timing and starting a vision before you have received all three parts: what God is doing, how God will do it, and when God will do it
  **Solution:** Wait until you have received specific direction on all three parts. God's way of doing something will differ from your natural expectation. God's timing is the part you must wait for, and it is the most important part of all.

- **Problem:** Delaying when you feel a specific prompting to do good, which weakens your ability to hear the Holy Spirit in future moments
  **Solution:** Act immediately on every specific impression to serve, speak, give, or pray. Each act of obedience trains your mind to recognize the Holy Spirit's voice more quickly in the future.

- **Problem:** Relying on willpower instead of the Holy Spirit's power to overcome temptation and addiction
  **Solution:** Stop trying to resist temptation in your own strength and start relying on the Holy Spirit's specific power. The Holy Spirit's power is stronger than your natural desires. Ask Him specifically for the strength to say no in the moment of temptation.

- **Problem:** Making decisions without first consulting the Holy Spirit and then wondering why you missed God's specific direction
  **Solution:** Make it a practice to consult the Holy Spirit before every significant decision. A significant decision includes marriage, career changes, relocation, major purchases, ministry direction, and any choice that will affect your life or the lives of others for an extended period. Never make a significant choice without His specific guidance.

- **Problem:** Expecting dramatic, audible voices from God instead of listening for simple nudges and impressions
  **Solution:** The Holy Spirit regularly speaks through quiet, ordinary thoughts and internal promptings. Learn to recognize His specific voice through consistent time in the Word and by obeying the promptings you already recognize.
