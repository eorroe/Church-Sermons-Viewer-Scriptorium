# God Can Bring You Through It

## Overview

This sermon from 1 Chronicles 14:8-16 reveals how God empowers His people to overcome overwhelming circumstances by depending on Him as their source of strength. It teaches that when enemies rise against you, the first response must be to inquire of God rather than rely on your own strategy or intellect. The message emphasizes that God can bring you through any situation when you seek Him, obey His instructions, and give Him glory for the victory.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing a new challenge or enemy that seems insurmountable
- When you've been promoted or blessed and opposition suddenly arises
- When you're tempted to rely on your own strength, talent, or resources instead of God
- When the same problem keeps returning and you don't know what to do
- When you need to remember that God's power, not your own, brings breakthrough
- When you want to learn how to respond to God's instructions with immediate action
- When you need encouragement that God is still with you even in uncertain times

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize Who Your Source of Strength Is

When facing issues, first understand that God is your source of strength, not your own abilities, intellect, or resources. Acknowledge that everything you have comes from Him and that He is the one who fights your battles. Stop assuming you have to answer every question or solve every problem on your own. Humble yourself and pray, seeking God's face and acknowledging that your power comes from Him alone.

### Step 2: Inquire of God Before Taking Action

When you see the enemy coming against you, do not immediately react with your own strategy. Instead, pray and ask God what to do. David, a warrior and king, did not rely on his military experience alone. He inquired of God first, and God gave him specific instructions. Do not forget that God responds to your prayers. He is mighty and powerful, yet He makes time for you. Ask God for direction before you make a move.

### Step 3: Act on What God Tells You

Once God answers, move immediately. Do not stay on your knees when God has told you what to do. David received an answer from God and then went up to Baal Perazim, where God delivered the Philistines into his hand. Many people are comfortable just hearing from God but are not comfortable acting on what He said. Get up and do something. Apply for the opportunity, seek wise counsel, care for your health, give faithfully, serve others. Whatever God has told you to do, step out in faith and do it.

### Step 4: Give God Glory for the Victory

When God breaks through for you, do not take credit for the victory. David said, "God has broken through my enemies by my hand like a breakthrough of water," acknowledging that the power came from God. Give God the glory for every victory, every promotion, and every blessing. Tell others that it was God who did it, not your own strength or intelligence. Acknowledge God's hand in your life publicly so that others can see His faithfulness.

### Step 5: When the Enemy Returns, Go Back to God

When the same enemy or problem shows up again, do not assume that what worked before will work now. David did not take the same approach the second time. Instead, he went back to God and inquired again. God gave him a different strategy: circle around, wait, and listen for the sound of marching in the mulberry trees. The enemy had regrouped and was three times stronger, but God had a new plan. Do not rely on past victories or old methods when God wants to do something new. Go back to Him and wait for fresh instructions.

### Step 6: Wait on God's Timing and Direction

Sometimes you must wait for God's signal before you move. David and his army waited until they heard the rustling of leaves in the mulberry trees. That was God's signal that He had gone before them. Be careful who you listen to while you are waiting on God. People will try to push you to move before God's timing is right. Be steadfast and unmovable. When you learn to wait on God, He will show up at the right time and give you the victory. Trust that the leaves are rustling even when you cannot see them yet.

### Step 7: Trust That God Will Make a Way

No matter what you are facing, God can bring you through it. Whether it is a corporate issue, a family problem, a financial struggle, or a health concern, trust that God is in control. He has given you enough power and enough access to Him to see you through. Do not use God as a last resort. Seek Him consistently, and He will honor your faith. He will make a way somehow, even when you cannot see how.

## Examples

### Example 1: David and the Philistines at Baal Perazim

David had just been anointed king over all of Israel when the Philistines heard about it and came up to fight him. Instead of immediately gathering his army to battle, David inquired of God, saying, "Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will You deliver them into my hand?" God answered, "Go up, for I will deliver them into your hand." David went and defeated them at Baal Perazim, and the place was named "God breaks through." David did not rely on his warrior skills alone. He asked God for direction, and when God gave it, he acted and won a complete victory. The Philistines even left their gods on the battlefield, and David had them burned.

### Example 2: David and the Philistines in the Valley of Rephaim

After David's first victory, the Philistines came back again, this time three times stronger and regrouped. If David had used the same strategy as before, he might have failed. But he did not rely on what worked yesterday. He went back to God and inquired again. God gave him a new plan: circle around the enemy, wait behind the mulberry trees, and move when he heard the sound of marching in the tops of the trees. David obeyed, and God went before him to strike the camp of the Philistines. This teaches us that when the enemy returns, we must go back to God for fresh instructions and trust His new strategy for our new situation.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Acknowledge God as your source of strength before facing any challenge
- ✅ Pray and inquire of God before making major decisions or taking action
- ✅ Act immediately when God gives you clear instructions
- ✅ Give God all the glory for every victory and blessing
- ✅ Go back to God when the same problem returns instead of relying on old solutions
- ✅ Wait on God's timing and listen for His signal before moving
- ✅ Trust that God is working even when you cannot see the outcome
- ❌ Do not rely on your own intellect, strategy, or strength alone
- ❌ Do not take credit for victories that God has given you
- ❌ Do not assume that what worked once will work every time
- ❌ Do not move ahead of God's timing because people are pushing you
- ❌ Do not use God as a last resort only when problems become overwhelming
- ❌ Do not become complacent and forget how good God has been to you

## Keep In Mind

- God is always in control, even when leadership fails or circumstances look bleak
- Every new level of promotion or blessing will bring new opposition
- Your greatest strength is knowing that God is your source, not your own abilities
- God responds to your prayers even in the midst of His busy work over the world
- It is not enough to hear from God; you must also act on what He says
- When God answers prayer, you can begin to praise Him for the breakthrough before it arrives
- The same God who brought you through yesterday is the same God who will bring you through today
- You are never too dirty, too unqualified, or too imperfect for God to use you
- Waiting on God is not passive; it is actively trusting Him while staying ready to move
- God's strategies may change, but His promise to bring you through never changes

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share intimate details of your personal struggles publicly without wisdom and discernment
- Protect your spiritual health by not comparing your journey to others
- Guard your heart against bitterness when facing repeated challenges
- Do not make major life decisions solely based on emotion; always seek God's word and wise counsel
- Avoid isolation during difficult times; stay connected to a community of believers who can support you

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Relying on your own strength and strategy instead of seeking God first
  **Solution:** Before taking action, pause and pray. Ask God for direction and acknowledge that He is your source of strength. Trust that His way is better than your way.

- **Problem:** Taking credit for victories that God has given you
  **Solution:** When you experience a breakthrough, immediately give God the glory. Tell others that it was His power, not your own, that brought you through. This keeps you humble and opens the door for more of His favor.

- **Problem:** Using the same old strategy when the enemy returns
  **Solution:** Go back to God and ask for fresh instructions. God may have a new plan for a new situation. Do not assume that what worked yesterday will work today. Be willing to change your approach based on God's leading.

- **Problem:** Moving ahead of God's timing because people are pushing you
  **Solution:** Stay steadfast and unmovable. Listen for God's signal, not the pressure of others. David waited until he heard the rustling of leaves before he moved. Trust that God's timing is perfect, even when it feels delayed.

- **Problem:** Using God as a last resort only when problems become overwhelming
  **Solution:** Develop a consistent habit of seeking God daily, not just during crises. Build your relationship with Him before the trouble comes so that you know how to hear His voice when you need it most.

- **Problem:** Forgetting how good God has been to you
  **Solution:** Keep a record of God's faithfulness in your life. Remember past victories and breakthroughs. When new challenges come, remind yourself of how God brought you through before. This builds your faith and trust in Him for the future.
