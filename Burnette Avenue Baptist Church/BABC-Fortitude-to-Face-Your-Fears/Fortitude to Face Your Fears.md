# Fortitude to Face Your Fears

## Overview

Fear is the "culprit of faith" because being inundated with fear prevents you from possessing the faith you need. Using the parable of the talents (Matthew 25), the preacher shows how fear caused the servant with one talent to bury it instead of investing it. The key message is that God has given believers "fortitude to face your fears" through three gifts from the Holy Spirit: Power (Dunamis), Love (Agape), and a Sound Mind. You cannot have a sound mind without sound theology, and you cannot have sound theology without being sound in the scripture.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are facing fear or anxiety in your life
- When you feel overwhelmed by circumstances and need faith to act
- When you need courage to use your talents and gifts for God

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize that fear is the culprit of faith

Understand that fear prevents you from possessing the faith you need. Being inundated with fear keeps you from acting on what God has placed in your hands.

### Step 2: Remember God has not given you a spirit of fear

Hold onto 2 Timothy 1:7: "For God has not given us the spirit of fear, but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind." This is a foundational truth to combat fear.

### Step 3: Receive the three gifts from the Holy Spirit

Accept the power, love, and sound mind that God has given you through the Holy Spirit. Power (Dunamis) is supernatural dynamite power to deal with the devil. Love (Agape) is unconditional love that casts out fear. A Sound Mind is the ability to remain cool, calm, and collected when everyone else is panicking.

### Step 4: Develop sound theology through scripture

You cannot have a sound mind without sound theology, and you cannot have sound theology without being sound in the scripture. Study the Word to build a solid theological foundation.

### Step 5: Act on your faith despite fear

Don't bury your talents like the servant in Matthew 25. Use what God has given you, remembering that the Holy Spirit will give you the fortitude to face your fears.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Parable of the Talents (Matthew 25)

The master gave one individual five talents, another two, and another one. After a season, the master returns to have an accounting. The one with five and the one with two both invested and multiplied their talents. But the one with one talent buried it in the earth because he was afraid, saying, "I knew that you are a hard man and so I dug your talent and put it in the earth because I was afraid." Fear caused him to act irresponsibly with what God had given him.

### Example 2: The Little Girl on the Plane

During a time of great turbulence on a plane, everyone else was frantic and running around, but a little girl sat calmly. When asked why she wasn't afraid, she said, "I'm not afraid because my daddy is the pilot and my daddy loves me too much to let anything happen to me." This shows how perfect love casts out all fear (1 John 4:18), allowing you to remain calm when everyone else is panicking.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Remember that God has given you power, love, and a sound mind through the Holy Spirit
- ✅ Be sound in the scripture to develop sound theology and a sound mind
- ✅ Remain cool, calm, and collected in fearful situations
- ✅ Love others unconditionally even when they don't mean you well
- ❌ Don't bury your talents or gifts out of fear
- ❌ Don't act irresponsibly with what God has given you
- ❌ Don't have "cotton-candy theology" that tastes good but can't hold you in adversarial winds of life
- ❌ Don't look down on others who are afraid - all of us are afraid of something

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Fear causes you to act irresponsibly with what God has given you (like the servant burying the talent)
  **Solution:** Remember God has given you power, love, and a sound mind through the Holy Spirit to face your fears

- **Problem:** Having "cotton-candy theology" that can't hold you when adversarial winds of life blow
  **Solution:** Be sound in the scripture to develop sound theology, which gives you a sound mind

- **Problem:** Running around depressed and disenchanted because you don't have sound theology
  **Solution:** Ground yourself in scripture to build a solid theological foundation

- **Problem:** Being afraid of what's happening in the world or politics
  **Solution:** Keep your focus on God, not on earthly circumstances. Lift up your eyes to the hills from where your help comes.
