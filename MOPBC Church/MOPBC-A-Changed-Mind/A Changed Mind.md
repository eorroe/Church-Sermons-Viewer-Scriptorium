# MOPBC Sunday service - A Changed Mind

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers to maintain a transformed mind focused on Jesus Christ, drawing from Romans 12:1-2. It emphasizes keeping your eyes on the spiritual board rather than worldly distractions, trusting that God looks beyond your faults to meet your needs, and living as part of a community that provides resources for youth, gives cheerfully to God's work, and prays for one another. The message calls for a changed mind that refuses anxiety, depression, and fear, while fixating on the eternal prize of salvation through Christ.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel overwhelmed by worldly distractions, politics, or social pressures
- When you need encouragement that God sees your need despite your flaws
- When preparing for a new school year or educational season
- When the church is called to consecrate leaders or elevate members to new roles
- When seeking to build a habit of cheerful giving and community support
- When battling anxiety, depression, or suicidal thoughts

## Steps

### Step 1: Keep Your Eyes on the Board

Fix your gaze on Jesus Christ as the ultimate prize and purpose of your life. Do not allow distractions such as classmates, material possessions, political circumstances, or worldly noise to pull your focus away from your God-given calling as a believer. Like a student staring at the teacher's board, your eyes must remain on what God is teaching you and where He is leading you.

### Step 2: Trust That God Looks Beyond Your Faults

Believe that God sees past your mistakes, imperfections, and failures to meet your every need. Even when you are still struggling and not yet perfect, He supplies every one of your needs. This truth alone is worthy of your praise and thanksgiving.

### Step 3: Renew Your Mind Daily

Commit to a changed mind by refusing to conform to the patterns of this world. Allow the Holy Spirit to transform your thinking so that you can discern and walk in God's good, pleasing, and perfect will. This renewal is the foundation for living a life that honors God.

### Step 4: Intercede for Students and Educators

Pray specifically for children, youth, and all students heading back to school, as well as for teachers, administrators, and school staff. Ask God to place a hedge of protection around them, dispatch angels to watch over them, and give them strength to shine His light in their classrooms and friendships.

### Step 5: Reject Destructive Spirits

Speak against and come against spirits of anxiety, depression, and suicide. Declare that these spirits have no place in your life or in the lives of those you love. Stand in the authority of Jesus to remove anything that contradicts God's character from your home, school, and social circles.

### Step 6: Give Cheerfully and Support Youth

Participate in cheerful giving to the church and to the community, especially when it comes to providing school supplies, backpacks, and resources for children and youth. If you cannot give materially, give your time, encouragement, and prayers. Pay forward the kindness that God and this church have shown to you.

### Step 7: Greet and Love One Another

Actively greet people in the house of God and let them know they are loved and appreciated. Extend the love of Christ to visitors, newcomers, and every person God places in your path. Show love not only to fellow church members but to everyone you encounter in your daily life.

### Step 8: Honor and Respect Church Leadership

Respect and reverence those whom God has called and elevated to leadership positions, such as deaconesses and mothers in the church. Recognize that their leadership roles are God-ordained callings, and support them through prayer and encouragement.

## Examples

### Example 1: Keeping Focus During School

A student preparing to return to elementary, middle, high school, or college can apply this sermon by refusing to worry about what classmates have, what shoes they wear, or what material items they carry. Instead, they keep their eyes on their education, their purpose, and Jesus, knowing that the same God who is with them at home is also present in their classroom.

### Example 2: Supporting a Child's Education

A parent or guardian who cannot physically be with their child at school every minute can apply this sermon by praying for God's hedge of protection, asking Him to dispatch angels, and trusting that their child will return home safely. They can also support the church's backpack and school supply drive to help other children who may be in need.

### Example 3: Consecrating a Leader

A church member witnessing the elevation of two sisters to the office of deaconess can apply this sermon by offering congratulations, respecting their new titles, and continuing to pray for their leadership. They recognize that God set them aside for such a time as this and commit to supporting them in their new roles.

### Example 4: Overcoming Anxiety and Depression

An individual struggling with anxiety, depression, or suicidal thoughts can apply this sermon by speaking against these spirits in Jesus' name, refusing to allow them to take root, and declaring that God's plan for their life is good. They can seek support from the church community, prayer, and professional help while standing on God's promises.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Keep your eyes fixed on Jesus and the eternal prize, not temporary circumstances
- ✅ Trust that God looks beyond your faults and meets your needs even when you are still a work in progress
- ✅ Pray for students, teachers, and school staff before the new school year begins
- ✅ Give cheerfully and support youth through donations, school supplies, and encouragement
- ✅ Speak life and truth over yourself and others, rejecting spirits of fear and despair
- ✅ Greet newcomers and show genuine love to everyone in the church
- ✅ Honor church leadership and pray for those consecrated to serve
- ✅ Silence cell phones and refrain from unnecessary movement during worship

## Keep In Mind

- You are not perfect, and you are still making mistakes, but God still supplies your needs
- God is the God who redeems, revives, and restores, not the enemy who steals, kills, and destroys
- The youth are the church of today, not just the church of tomorrow
- Your support of a child's education may be the difference between their success and struggle
- What you do for children in secret, without seeking public recognition, will be rewarded by God
- Outside of the church walls, the world may not care about them, but God does
- There may be a future millionaire or billionaire sitting among the youth in the congregation
- When you consecrate someone, you are confirming what God has already done in heaven

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Getting distracted by worldly things such as politics, material possessions, or social status.
  **Solution:** Keep your eyes on the spiritual board by reminding yourself daily of your eternal purpose and fixing your thoughts on Jesus.

- **Problem:** Believing you must be perfect before God can use you or meet your needs.
  **Solution:** Trust that God looks beyond your faults and sees your needs. He meets you right where you are, imperfections and all.

- **Problem:** Failing to pray for students, educators, and youth going back to school.
  **Solution:** Make it a point of priority to intercede for children and educators before the school year starts, covering them in prayer and providing tangible support.

- **Problem:** Allowing anxiety, depression, or suicidal thoughts to take root without speaking against them.
  **Solution:** Come against these spirits in Jesus' name, seek support from the church community, and remember that God has not given you a spirit of fear but of power, love, and a sound mind.

- **Problem:** Neglecting to show up for church or participate in the church community.
  **Solution:** Recognize that you could have chosen any church, but God led you there. Show up, greet others, and be an active part of the body of Christ.
