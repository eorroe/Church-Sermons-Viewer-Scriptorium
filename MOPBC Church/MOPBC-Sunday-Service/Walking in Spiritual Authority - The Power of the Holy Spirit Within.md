# Walking in Spiritual Authority: The Power of the Holy Spirit Within

## Overview

This sermon, delivered by Elder Dr. R.D. Towns at Mount Olive Primitive Baptist Church, explains that every believer has spiritual authority and power through the indwelling Holy Spirit. The message teaches Christians to recognize their God-given authority in Jesus' name, to rebuke the devil, to exercise that authority in your daily conversations, decisions, and spiritual conflicts, to put on the whole armor of God described in Ephesians 6, and to pray at least 20 minutes every morning and evening, and pray whenever you face a difficulty, temptation, or decision throughout the day. It challenges listeners to stop allowing the devil control and to exercise the authority already given to them by Christ.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel spiritually powerless or defeated by the enemy
- When you need to remember that Satan has power but no authority over you
- When facing spiritual opposition or conflict at work, fear of losing your job, negative comments from coworkers, or spiritual attack on your mind, such as intrusive thoughts of fear, doubt, or condemnation that oppose God's word
- When you want to strengthen your prayer life and intercede for others
- When preparing to minister or lead others in spiritual warfare

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That You Have Power and Authority

Read Luke 10:19 and acknowledge that the devil has power but no authority over you. As a believer in Jesus Christ, you have both power and authority in Jesus' name. Do not allow the devil control in your life. If you grant Satan access through unconfessed sin, fear, or disobedience, he will seek to dominate you. Granting Satan access means allowing sinful thoughts or actions to remain in your life without repentance. Speak and act in the name of Jesus, such as making decisions that honor God, treating others with kindness, and standing against injustice by refusing to participate in gossip, speaking up when you witness unfair treatment, and making choices that honor God even when others disagree. When you sense fear rising, such as when your heart races, your mind races with worst-case scenarios, or you feel the urge to avoid a situation, immediately rebuke it by saying, "Fear, I rebuke you in the name of Jesus." When negativity or attack comes against you, respond immediately by speaking aloud with the authority of Jesus' name—say, "I rebuke this in the name of Jesus," or "I speak peace over this situation in Jesus' name." What matters is that you speak with intentional faith, meaning you choose your words carefully, believe they will accomplish what God has promised, and speak them out loud with confidence. For example, when faced with fear, say "I reject this fear in Jesus' name and I receive peace from God."

### Step 2: Clean Up and Invite the Holy Spirit Inside

Confess your sins to the Lord and ask Him to cleanse you from the inside out. After you confess your sins and receive deliverance from sin or unclean spirits through prayer, confession, and including the laying on of hands by a trusted spiritual leader when the person requests prayer and a trusted spiritual leader who agrees with the practice is present, pray this specific prayer: "Lord Jesus, I ask You to send Your Holy Spirit to come dwell inside me. Be my leader, guide, and direction. Convict me when I do wrong and help me live a holy life." The Holy Spirit will then guide your daily decisions. When you are considering a decision, bring it to God in prayer. If after praying you still feel anxious, the decision is not from God. If you experience God's peace, it is God's leading. He brings Scripture to your mind when you need direction. He convicts you when you sin as a deep inner knowing that something is wrong, often accompanied by a desire to make it right; distinguish it from regular guilt by checking whether the conviction aligns with Scripture—if the feeling contradicts God's word, it is condemnation rather than conviction. Without the Holy Spirit dwelling inside you, Luke 11:24-26 warns that unclean spirits can return to a person's life and make their condition worse than before.

### Step 3: Put On the Whole Armor of God

Each morning, intentionally put on each piece of the armor described in Ephesians 6:10-18 by verbally declaring that you put on truth, righteousness, peace, faith, salvation, and the word of God. Stand firm by:
- Girding your loins with truth: Choose to speak only truth and reject lies
- Putting on the breastplate of righteousness: Live according to the moral standards found in the Ten Commandments and the teachings of Jesus in the New Testament and allow Christ's righteousness to cover you
- Taking the shield of faith: Trust God's promises, such as Psalm 23, Romans 8:28, Philippians 4:19, and Isaiah 41:10, to quench every fiery dart of doubt, fear, and attack from the wicked one
- Wearing the helmet of salvation: Guard your mind by focusing on your eternal security in Christ
- Wielding the sword of the Spirit: Use the word of God as your offensive weapon by speaking Scripture aloud against temptation, depression, fear, and every attack of the enemy
Remember that you are not fighting against people, but against principalities, powers, and spiritual wickedness in heavenly realms (Ephesians 6:12).

### Step 4: Pray at Least 20 Minutes Every Morning and Evening

Set aside at least 20 minutes each day to pray with all supplication in the spirit. "In the spirit" means to pray in the language you use to speak to God in your daily life, whether that is your native language or in tongues. Write down your thoughts, actions, and reactions each evening. Review the journal weekly to see patterns God is revealing, such as recurring sins, repeated conflicts, or consistent responses that reveal heart issues, and bring those patterns to God in prayer. Ask God to reveal hidden sin by praying Psalm 139:23-24, "Search me, O God, and know my heart; try me and know my anxieties; and see if there be any wicked way in me." God reveals sin through Scripture that you read and suddenly understand applies to you, through a sermon that speaks directly to your situation, or through the counsel of a mature believer. When He reveals sin, confess it immediately (1 John 1:9) and turn away from it. Meet with an accountability partner once a week or once every two weeks. Share your struggles, sins, and decisions. Ask them to pray for you and to speak truth to you when you are in error. Examine your reactions to stressful situations by asking whether you responded with anger, fear, or unbelief. Then repent and choose to respond with faith. Pray specifically, asking God to guard your heart and mind in Christ Jesus (Philippians 4:7), to keep your feet from slipping, to protect you from accidents and harm, and to preserve your soul for eternity. Expand your prayer beyond yourself: pray for all the saints, for your pastor, for the deacons, for the choir, and for every member of your church. Intercede for those going through trials, tribulations, and bereavement. "Watch and pray" means staying spiritually alert and continuing to pray for the entire body of Christ even after your own needs are met. Do not stop praying once your own need is met—continue to watch and pray for the entire body of Christ.

### Step 5: Walk Like You Have Power

Exercise your spiritual authority daily in every area of life. Walk and talk in the name of Jesus by greeting people with kindness, speaking truth in love, and refusing to use profanity or gossip. Make decisions based on what honors God by asking "Would this choice bring glory to God? Would I be comfortable praying about this decision?" before acting. Evaluate your friendships by asking: Does this person encourage me to pray, attend church, read the Bible, and make godly choices? Or do they mock my beliefs, invite me to compromise situations, or make me feel guilty for setting godly boundaries? Then speak kindly, act with integrity, and refuse to compromise your values even when no one is watching. Speak truth instead of gossip, choose kindness over retaliation, and set boundaries that protect your peace. For example, if a person drains you—making you feel spiritually depleted, anxious, or tempted to sin after spending time with them—set a specific limit such as one hour per week or no private meetings, and stick to it. A request compromises your faith if it requires you to lie, cheat, steal, violate your conscience, or disobey a clear command of Scripture. Before responding, ask: "Does this honor God? Does this align with God's word?" If the answer is no, decline politely but firmly. A conversation leads to sin if it involves gossip, sexual immorality, hatred, or rebellion against God. When you recognize such a conversation, say "I don't think we should talk about this." Then remove yourself from conversations that lead to sin. Do all of this while declaring that you operate under Jesus' authority. When you face opposition at work, rebuke it in Jesus' name. When you face difficult situations at home or in public, stand in your authority. If people think you are extreme for following Christ, continue walking in the authority He has given you. Do not be ashamed.

## Examples

### Example 1: Rebuking Fear at Work

Before you enter your workplace each morning, pray aloud: "In the name of Jesus, I rebuke every spirit of fear that will try to attack me today." When a coworker begins sharing gossip or negativity, speak up immediately: "I don't receive that in the name of Jesus." Replace negative words with Scripture. When someone speaks death over you, respond with a life-giving verse such as Philippians 4:8 or Proverbs 18:21. If the negativity continues, limit your exposure to that person or environment. Maintain your spiritual authority throughout the workday by speaking life and truth in Jesus' name.

### Example 2: Anointing Your Home

Walk through each room of your house while holding anointing oil. In each room, pray aloud: "I anoint this home in the name of Jesus. I declare that no weapon formed by Satan shall prosper here. This home belongs to God." Continue to exercise your authority by refusing to allow fear, anxiety, or negative influences to remain in your household. If using your spiritual authority causes friends who do not share your faith to distance themselves, allow them to leave by releasing them with a blessing, not with bitterness. For example, say "I release you in Jesus' name" and then focus on relationships that build you up. Your walk with Christ and your spiritual authority are more important than maintaining relationships that oppose your faith.

### Example 3: Interceding for a Sick Loved One

When someone is in the hospital or going through a difficult trial, pray specifically for them. Say: "Lord, I pray for [name]. I ask You to dispatch Your angels to stand guard at their door. I ask for healing, deliverance, and strength in their life. Lord, I believe You can do exceedingly abundantly above all we can ask or imagine according to Your power at work within them." Pray in the spirit, which is praying in the language you use to speak to God in your daily life, whether that is your native language or in tongues, and trust God for their complete restoration.

## Best Practices

- Acknowledge daily that you have both power and authority in Jesus' name
- Ask the Holy Spirit to dwell inside you each day through prayer
- Rebuke the devil before situations start, not after damage is done
- Put on each piece of the armor of God every morning through intentional prayer
- Pray with all supplication in the spirit for yourself, your church, and others
- Walk and talk with authority, even when others think you are extreme
- Thank God for His keeping power and goodness in every situation

## Keep In Mind

- The devil has power but no authority over you unless you grant him access through unconfessed sin, fear, or disobedience
- The Holy Spirit does not force you to shout or dance, but He leads by giving you peace or a sense of wrongness about decisions; distinguish it from regular anxiety by testing it against Scripture and prayer—if the wrongness persists after prayer and fasting, it is God's warning. He guides by bringing Scripture to your mind when you need direction. He convicts you as a deep inner knowing that something is wrong, often accompanied by a desire to make it right; distinguish it from regular guilt by checking whether the conviction aligns with Scripture—if the feeling contradicts God's word, it is condemnation rather than conviction.
- If you change your outward behavior to stop sinning but do not invite the Holy Spirit to dwell inside you, you remain vulnerable to spiritual attack
- You are not fighting against people, but against spiritual forces in heavenly realms as described in Ephesians 6:12
- Prayer is not optional. Set aside at least 20 minutes each day to pray with all supplication in the spirit for yourself and others

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not engage in spiritual warfare without first ensuring you have confessed your sins and received forgiveness through Jesus Christ (1 John 1:9) and are filled with the Holy Spirit by praying for the baptism of the Holy Spirit, surrendering your life to Christ, and walking in obedience to God's word
- Rebuke the devil boldly, but always in the name of Jesus, not in your own name or by your own strength
- Do not try to face spiritual battles without putting on the whole armor of God each day
- Do not stop praying once your own need is met—continue to watch and pray for all the saints

## Common Pitfalls

- Problem: Letting the devil gain a foothold in your life and then trying to remove him later
  Solution: Rebuke the devil at the first sign of attack. Do not let him start, and do not let him stay. Exercise your authority immediately in the name of Jesus.

- Problem: Cleaning up your outward behavior while neglecting the Holy Spirit on the inside
  Solution: After asking for deliverance from sin, specifically pray and ask the Holy Spirit to come inside and dwell within you. The outward change is only the beginning.

- Problem: Fighting against people instead of spiritual forces
  Solution: Remember Ephesians 6:12—your fight is not against people. Stand firm in your God-given authority, put on the armor daily, and pray against the spiritual forces behind the conflict.
