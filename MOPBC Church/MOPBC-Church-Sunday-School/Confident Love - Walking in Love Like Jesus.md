# Confident Love - Walking in Love Like Jesus (1 John 3:11-24)

## Overview

This sermon, delivered at Mount Olive Primitive Baptist Church (MOPBC) during Sunday school, explores the concept of "confident love" based on 1 John chapter 3, verses 11 through 24. The lesson teaches that love is not merely a feeling but an action word — a commandment from God that must be obeyed, lived out, and shown through tangible deeds toward others. The central message is that true discipleship is proven by loving one another unconditionally, sacrificially, and actively, just as Christ loved us. Believers are called to walk in confident love, free from guilt and fear, knowing that God's love has been poured into their hearts through the Holy Spirit.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand what it truly means to love like Jesus
- When struggling with feelings of guilt, unworthiness, or spiritual discouragement
- When facing conflict, hatred, or unkindness from others and need guidance on how to respond
- When seeking to grow in your daily walk with Christ and obey His command to love one another
- When you want to assess whether your love for others is genuine and actionable or merely words

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand That Love Is a Commandment, Not a Choice

Recognize that Jesus did not say "if you feel like it" — He commanded us to love one another. When Jesus commands something, it is not optional. It is something we are required to do, strive for, and obey. Make the decision to obey this commandment regardless of how you feel, because true love is rooted in obedience to God.

### Step 2: Learn the Characteristics of True Love

Study the characteristics of love as described in 1 Corinthians 13:4-9: love suffers long, is kind, does not envy, does not vaunt itself, is not puffed up, does not behave unseemly, does not seek its own, is not easily provoked, thinks no evil, does not rejoice in iniquity but rejoices in the truth, bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, and endures all things. Understand that love is unconditional (applies to everyone regardless of who they are, what they've done, or where they come from), compassionate, active, and sacrificial.

### Step 3: Do Not Be Like Cain — Reject Hatred and Jealousy

Do not compare yourself to other believers. God made each person differently, and comparing yourself breeds jealousy and envy. Cain was evil, and his brother Abel was righteous — Cain's jealousy led him to murder. Do not let hatred, envy, or comparison take root in your heart toward any brother or sister in Christ. A person who hates another is a murderer in their heart, and murderers do not have eternal life within them.

### Step 4: Walk in Confident Love Through Obedience

Know that your actions will show whether you belong to the truth. When you obey God's commandments and love others, you can stand before God with confidence — unashamed and unguilty. Even when you feel guilty because you have sinned, remember that God is greater than your feelings. Do not stay stuck in guilt — repent, turn away from the sin, and return to God in boldness.

### Step 5: Pray to God for the Ability to Love Others

Acknowledge that you cannot love others on your own. The flesh is selfish, and human effort falls short. Pray daily: "Lord, I want to love like You. Help me." Realize that God's love has been poured into your heart by the Holy Spirit — not something you generate yourself. Start your day in prayer, even while still in bed, because your thoughts shape your actions throughout the day.

### Step 6: Put Love Into Action With Tangible Deeds

Love is not a word — it is action. When you see a brother or sister in need and you have the means to help, do not simply say, "I hope it goes well for you" and walk away. If you have money, give. If you have food, pack a basket. If you have a vehicle, offer a ride. If you have no material resources, pray for them, send groceries through a delivery service, or direct them to someone who can help. Always leave a person better than you found them.

### Step 7: Show Love in the Church Community

Promote love and unity within the church by: greeting people with a genuine smile; offering hugs and handshakes; speaking encouraging and affirming words; praying for others; acknowledging that every person matters, even strangers you do not know. Your attitude and actions set the tone for everyone who walks through the church doors.

### Step 8: Respond to Offenses With Forgiveness and Grace

When someone is unkind or says something hurtful, take a deep breath, count to ten (or one hundred), and consider walking away rather than arguing. Do not argue with a fool — you will become a fool by doing so. Do not dwell on the offense or seek revenge. Instead, immediately say: "Lord, forgive them. I forgive them." Keep moving forward. Do not let someone else's response to you become your problem.

### Step 9: Obey God's Will in All Things

When you pray, ensure that your requests align with God's will — not your own desires. Submit to God's will, saying, "Not my will, but Yours be done." When you obey His commandments and do what pleases Him, you remain in fellowship with God. Sin separates you from Him, but repentance and obedience restore that fellowship. God does not change — you do. Remain in fellowship by walking in obedience.

### Step 10: Love Yourself as God Loves You

You cannot truly love others until you love yourself the way God loves you. Recognize that God loves you despite your flaws, and thank Him for transformation. When you accept God's love for you, you can extend that same love to others. Do not mistreat yourself, and do not mistreat others based on how you feel about yourself.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Foreigner Song — "I Want to Know What Love Is"

The sermon references the 1980s song by Foreigner titled "I Want to Know What Love Is." The singer was longing for a genuine, meaningful relationship — not something fleshly or sensual. As believers, we already know what love is and who love is: Jesus Christ. While the secular world searches for love in the wrong places, we have been given the answer. Jesus is not only love itself — He calls us to demonstrate His love to everyone around us, not just those who are easy to love.

### Example 2: The Three Hebrew Boys — God in the Midst of the Fire

Pastor Elder Marcus Wright Jr. shared a personal testimony: he said he should have been gone a long time ago, counted out and thrown in the towel. But God stepped into the midst of his situation and showcased His love, just as God was with the three Hebrew boys in the fiery furnace. They did not come out smelling like the smoke they had been in — because God was in the middle of their situation. This illustrates that God's love is not distant. He steps into your pain, stands with you in the fire, and brings you through transformed.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Treat love as a commandment to obey, not a feeling to wait for
- ✅ Start each day in prayer and in the awareness of God's love for you
- ✅ Show love through action — give time, attention, money, food, or resources to those in need
- ✅ Smile, hug, and speak encouraging words to everyone you encounter, especially in church
- ✅ Repent quickly when you sin and do not stay stuck in guilt or condemnation
- ✅ Pray for people who have wronged you and forgive them immediately
- ✅ Hold tightly to God's commandments with obedience and expectation
- ✅ Remember that God's love is poured into your heart by the Holy Spirit — receive it daily
- ✅ Walk in confidence before God by obeying His word and loving others
- ✅ Align your prayers with God's will, not your own selfish desires
- ✅ Love others "in spite of" — not because they deserve it, but because God commands it

## Keep In Mind

- Love is an action word — it is defined by what it does, not by how it feels
- God did not say "like" one another — He said "love" one another. "Like" is fickle and changes with feelings and circumstances. "Love" is a choice that remains regardless of how someone treats you
- Love does not mean you have to be someone's best friend or agree with everything they do — it means you treat them with the love Christ has shown you
- Being called "brother" or "sister" in church is not offensive — it is an expression of familial love in Christ. Titles like "deacon" or "mother" are positions of responsibility, not measures of worth
- Greater love is a result of your relationship with Jesus — you cannot genuinely love others if you do not first love and know Christ
- The world's definition of love is often fleshly, sensual, or self-serving. As a believer, your definition of love is Jesus Christ Himself
- Even when people are not nice to you, you must respond with love — not because they deserve it, but because you belong to Christ
- Your daily actions and choices — not your feelings — show whether you are walking in confident love

## Security & Safety Notes

- Guard your heart against hatred, envy, and jealousy — these attitudes are spiritual dangers that can lead to destruction, as seen in the story of Cain
- Do not allow the devil to use guilt, condemnation, or past failures to keep you from approaching God in repentance and prayer
- Be aware that sin separates you from God — do not become comfortable or unrepentant in your disobedience
- Protect your fellowship with God by confessing your sins regularly and walking in obedience to His commandments
- Do not let the media, news, or worldly influences cause you to doubt the value and power of Christian love
- When someone harms you, do not internalize the offense in a way that breeds resentment or desire for revenge — guard your heart by immediately forgiving and praying for them
- Be cautious about aligning your prayers or desires with things that are not God's will — submit every request to His purposes
- Never use your position or title in the church as a justification for treating others with less love or respect — God sees all believers as His children equally

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting to love others until you "feel like it."
  **Solution:** Love is a choice and a commandment, not a feeling. Obey God's command to love regardless of your emotions. Feelings will follow as you act in obedience.

- **Problem:** Comparing yourself to other believers and becoming jealous of their blessings, position, or spiritual growth.
  **Solution:** Remember that God made you uniquely. Comparison steals your joy and breeds the same spirit that caused Cain to kill Abel. Celebrate others and focus on your own walk with God.

- **Problem:** Staying stuck in guilt and condemnation after sinning, feeling too unworthy to pray or approach God.
  **Solution:** God is greater than your feelings. Confess your sin, repent, and return to Him with boldness. Do not let the enemy convince you that you cannot come to God when you have failed.

- **Problem:** Telling people "I love you" but never showing it through your actions.
  **Solution:** Love is an action word. Ask yourself daily: What have I actually done to demonstrate love to someone today? Let your actions, not just your words, speak love.

- **Problem:** Responding to offenses with anger, revenge, or the silent treatment.
  **Solution:** Take a breath, count to ten (or one hundred), and walk away if needed. Forgive immediately. Do not argue with a fool — you will only lower yourself to their level. Pray for those who wrong you.

- **Problem:** Only loving people who are easy to love or who can benefit you in some way.
  **Solution:** God's love is unconditional and extends to everyone. Love "in spite of" — regardless of what someone has done, where they come from, or how they treat you.

- **Problem:** Neglecting daily communion with God and expecting to love others from your own strength.
  **Solution:** You cannot love on your own. Start your day in prayer, commune with God daily, and ask the Holy Spirit to pour God's love through you to others.

- **Problem:** Praying for things that are not aligned with God's will.
  **Solution:** Always pray with submission to God's will. Say, "Lord, if it is Your will, do this." Trust that God knows what is best even when it is not what you want.
