# Winning the War in Your Mind

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the mind is a battlefield where most of life's battles are won or lost through our thoughts. Pastor Craig Groeschel explains how the devil creates strongholds of deception through repeated lies, but God's Word has divine power to demolish those strongholds and renew the mind with truth. The message provides a practical framework for identifying your dominant negative thought patterns, naming the biblical truth that counters each lie, and actively taking every thought captive to make it obedient to Christ.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel trapped by negative and unhealthy thinking patterns
- When you battle between thoughts of faith and thoughts of fear
- When you want to understand how lies create mental strongholds
- When you need practical steps to renew your mind with God's truth
- When you are ready to stop repeating destructive thought cycles

## Steps

### Step 1: Conduct a Thought Audit

Pause and evaluate the character of your typical thoughts across three categories. Ask yourself: Are my thoughts characterized by worry and panic, or by peace that goes beyond human understanding? Do my thoughts drift toward the negative and critical, or toward the positive and faith-filled? Are my thoughts consumed with worldly temporary things such as what I have, what I wear, what I look like, who liked my post, how many followers I have, and what everybody thinks about me, or do they drift toward eternal matters such as the life God has given me to steward, the spiritual gifts He has given me to use, and what I have to invest in making a difference in the lives of the people around me? Be honest about what dominates your mind, because what you think about determines the direction of your life.

### Step 2: Identify Your Biggest Stronghold

Choose one dominant lie that the enemy has used to hold you back. This is not the time to address every issue at once. Focus on the single strongesthold that has the most impact on your daily life. Examples include: I am not good enough, my past is too bad for God to use me, I will never succeed, I will always be broke, I will never have a good marriage, or I am nothing but a victim. Name it clearly, because you cannot defeat what you do not define.

### Step 3: Name the Truth That Demolishes the Stronghold

For every lie the enemy tells you, there is a truth from God's Word that sets you free. Find the specific Scripture that directly counters your identified lie. If your stronghold is I am not enough, the truth is 2 Peter 1:3: God's divine power has given us everything we need for a godly life. If your stronghold is I will always be alone, the truth is Hebrews 13:5: He will never leave you nor forsake you. If your stronghold is I am just a victim, the truth is Revelation 12:11: You are an overcomer by the blood of the Lamb and by the words of your testimony.

### Step 4: Take Every Thought Captive

Actively reject lies the moment they enter your mind and replace them with truth. This is an offensive spiritual discipline. Use the Word of God as your weapon, because it is sharper than any double-edged sword and cuts away the lies of the enemy. When a lie surfaces, do not entertain it. Speak the truth aloud, write it down, or memorize it so you can recall it in the moment. You are not a prisoner to your thoughts; you have authority to take them captive and make them obedient to Christ.

### Step 5: Forge a New Neural Pathway

Science confirms what Scripture teaches: every thought you repeat creates a neural pathway in your brain. The more you think a lie, the easier it becomes to believe it. Conversely, the more you think and speak truth, the stronger that new pathway becomes. When you feel the urge to walk the old path of negativity, intentionally choose a different response by replacing the old habit with a God-honoring action such as praying, reading Scripture, taking a walk, or speaking truth aloud. If you normally reach for ice cream when you feel bad, take a walk instead. If you normally scroll social media when you are bored, read the Bible app instead. Stay off the old path and walk the new one until the new path of truth becomes your default.

## Examples

### Example 1: Pastor Kevin and the Unlocked Door

Pastor Kevin was locked in a closet during a game of capture the flag, but he believed a lie that an unlocked door held him prisoner. He spent the day whining to be let out instead of simply opening the door. This illustrates how people can remain trapped by lies even when the door to freedom is unlocked. Jesus has already set you free through His truth; you do not have to stay locked behind an unlocked door of deception.

### Example 2: The Pastor's Stronghold of Never Being Enough

Pastor Craig Groeschel shared his personal struggle with the lie I am never enough. He would feel he could never preach well enough, lead the church well enough, or be present enough for his family. The truth that demolished this stronghold was 2 Peter 1:3: God's divine power has given us everything we need for a godly life. When he stopped relying on his own strength and rested in God's sufficient power, the lie lost its hold.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Focus on one stronghold at a time rather than trying to fix everything at once
- ✅ Write down your identified stronghold and the truth that counters it so you can review it daily
- ✅ Speak truth aloud when lies surface, because confession and declaration strengthen new neural pathways
- ✅ Use the Bible app or Scripture cards to renew your mind during moments of boredom or stress
- ✅ Remember that both science and Scripture agree: repeated thoughts create lasting pathways in the brain
- ❌ Do not entertain lies even when they contain a small amount of truth, because the lie is what holds you prisoner
- ❌ Do not stay on the old path of negative thinking hoping it will change on its own; you must actively forge a new one
- ❌ Do not rely solely on willpower; rely on the power of God's Word and the Holy Spirit to transform you

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace professional counseling for mental health conditions such as clinical anxiety or depression.
- Stop and ask for clarification if you are unsure whether a thought pattern is a spiritual stronghold or a medical condition that requires professional help.
- The process of renewing your mind takes time. Lies often take years to form, so be patient and persistent as you forge new pathways of truth.
- Do not use this sermon as a substitute for licensed mental health care when dealing with severe trauma, suicidal ideation, or diagnosed mental illness.
- If you are in crisis, contact a qualified mental health professional or emergency services immediately.
- Guard your heart against counterfeit truths that sound positive but contradict Scripture.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You try to attack all of your strongholds at once and become overwhelmed.
  **Solution:** Pick one stronghold, define it clearly, and replace it with one truth. Master that before moving to the next.
- **Problem:** You believe a lie because it contains a small amount of truth, making it harder to recognize as deception.
  **Solution:** Separate the partial truth from the lie. Acknowledge what is true, then reject the false conclusion that the enemy wants you to draw from it.
- **Problem:** You expect instant change and become discouraged when old thought patterns resurface.
  **Solution:** Renewing your mind is a gradual process. Each time you choose truth over a lie, you weaken the old pathway and strengthen the new one.
- **Problem:** You try to overcome strongholds through willpower alone instead of through God's Word.
  **Solution:** The weapons we fight with are not the weapons of the world; they have divine power. Submit your thoughts to Christ and let His Word do the demolishing work.
