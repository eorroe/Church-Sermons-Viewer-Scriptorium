# Trusting God When You Don't Understand

## Overview

This sermon teaches how to trust God during difficult seasons when His plans and timing are unclear. Using Proverbs 3:5 and the story of Hannah from 1 Samuel 1, it explains that true trust is a pre-decision made before trials arrive, and that God can handle our honest anguish and questions. It emphasizes that our trust grows not in good times but in trials, and that worship and surrender are acts of faith even when circumstances remain unchanged.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are going through a prolonged trial and do not understand why God has not answered your prayers
- When worry, anxiety, or fear feels stronger than faith
- When you need to rebuild trust in God after disappointment or unanswered prayers
- When you want to learn how to worship and trust God even when your situation has not changed

## Steps

### Step 1: Pre-Decide to Trust God

Make the decision in advance that no matter what happens, what you see, or what God does or does not do, you will trust in the Lord with all your heart. Trust is not just a feeling; it is a pre-decision that must be made before the trial arrives.

### Step 2: Identify Where You Lean on Your Own Understanding

Recognize that what you worry about most often reveals where you trust God the least. Instead of leaning on your own understanding of how things should be, acknowledge God in that area and surrender your timeline and expectations to Him.

### Step 3: Cry Out to God in Honest Anguish

When you do not understand, cry out to God honestly. He can handle your pain, questions, and emotions. Running to Him in honest anguish is better than turning away from Him. Pour out your heart to Him as Hannah did at the temple.

### Step 4: Make Your Request and Surrender the Outcome

Bring your specific request to God and, if needed, make a vow or commitment to surrender the outcome to Him. Trust that He hears your prayer, even before He answers. Like Hannah, you can find peace not because the circumstance changed, but because you chose to trust.

### Step 5: Worship God Even When Nothing Has Changed

Choose to worship God even when your situation remains exactly the same. This is a profound act of faith. Do not wait for your circumstances to improve before you praise Him. Worship Him for who He is, not just for what you see Him doing.

### Step 6: Keep a Record of God's Faithfulness

Document your prayer requests and, over time, look back to see how God was faithful. This builds confidence that He can be trusted with your future because He has proven Himself faithful in the past.

## Examples

### Example 1: Hannah's Unanswered Prayer

Hannah could not conceive, and her rival Peninnah taunted her year after year. Instead of turning away from God, Hannah pre-decided to trust Him, cried out to Him in anguish, made a vow to dedicate her son to God, and eventually worshiped the Lord once more even though she still had no child. In due time, God remembered her and gave her a son, Samuel.

### Example 2: The Pastor's Personal Testimony

The speaker shared a moment several weeks back just before church when he was overcome with worry and sobbing. Nothing had changed in his situation, but he and his wife chose to worship the Lord once more. Their faith was not in changing circumstances, but in the unchanging character of God.

## Best Practices

- Pre-decide to trust God before the crisis hits
- Be honest with God about your pain and confusion
- Worship God for who He is, not just what He does
- Keep a journal or record of answered prayers and God's faithfulness
- Cry out to God rather than turning away from Him when you do not understand

## Keep In Mind

- Trusting God when you do not understand is a choice, not a feeling
- What you worry about most reveals where you trust God the least
- God's ways are higher than our ways, and His timing is not our timing
- Sometimes God is doing a deeper work in you before He moves in your circumstances
- Our trust grows in trials, not in good times

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting for your circumstances to improve before you trust or worship God.
  **Solution:** Trust and worship are choices you make independent of your situation. Hannah was sad no longer even though she still did not have a baby.

- **Problem:** Believing that unanswered prayer means God does not care or is not real.
  **Solution:** God's silence does not equal absence. Keep crying out to Him and continue to seek Him honestly.

- **Problem:** Comparing your situation to others and feeling like God is unfair.
  **Solution:** Trust that God has a bigger picture and a purpose for your trial that you may not yet see.

- **Problem:** Trying to figure everything out on your own instead of leaning on God's understanding.
  **Solution:** Pre-decide to trust God and acknowledge Him in all your ways, even when you do not understand.
