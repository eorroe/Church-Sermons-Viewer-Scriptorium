# Struggling with faith? How to get closer to God (5 steps)

## Overview

This sermon addresses the struggle of doubting faith and feeling spiritually disconnected from God. It teaches that faith is a concrete choice to trust a person—not a feeling, ideology, or body of evidence—and that grace is required to make that leap. Through five actionable daily practices, it guides listeners to open themselves to God's grace, deepen their trust, and move from uncertainty to a firm, lived faith.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When prayer feels empty and you wonder if you are "praying to nobody"
- When you have questions, doubts, or feel like you lack evidence to believe
- When you want to build a real, daily relationship with God but don't know how to start

## Steps

### Step 1: Accept Your Divine Adoption

Accept and internalize that through grace you are 100% adopted as a beloved son or daughter of God. Picture the father's love as powerful, lifegiving, eternal, and deeper than anything you have ever experienced. Realize that the door to this love is not somewhere out there—it is inside you, and He is waiting outside the door right now. The question is not whether God is real, but whether you can trust Him.

### Step 2: Read Scripture Daily as the Divine Teacher

Every morning, read a little scripture or a good spiritual book—just a paragraph or two. Before reading, ask: "What is it you want to show me today?" Approach the words as if the Divine Teacher who inspired them is sitting right there with you. Read it a few times, let Him unlock the meaning, and chew on whatever phrase or thought sticks out for the rest of the day.

### Step 3: Practice Prayer as the Breathing of Faith

Prayer is not about getting results, feelings, or wondering "am I doing this right." Picture it as sitting alive in the Holy Spirit who wants to share Himself with you. Let go of everything else, shut off your brain, and just be there. You do not have to say a word. Let go of expectations and simply rest in His presence.

### Step 4: Take a Daily Mental Inventory

At the end of each day, review your thoughts and actions. Ask where you were more like Christ and where you were less like Him. Notice what opportunities were missed. Go over it with the Spirit—not in a guilty, judgmental way, but in a loving, encouraging way. Come up with a game plan: what will you do better tomorrow? Be disciplined and obedient to what you learn.

### Step 5: Clean Up Sin Through Confession

Sin obstructs grace just as clogged arteries block blood flow. To go deeper in faith, keep going to His mercy. Confess all your sins at His feet humbly, no matter what they are. God cannot resist a humble and contrite heart. Like the prodigal son returning home, honest confession leads to instant restoration and the recovery of all that was lost.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Adoption Birthday

Imagine a child at his birthday party who looks out of place until the family reveals he is being adopted. They bring out a huge cake that says "Welcome home, our new son—we love you." At first he is in disbelief, but then it hits him, his face wells up with tears, and he flies across the room into his new father's arms. This is what happens at our baptism: we are 100% officially adopted by the God of the universe. Just as that child must come to trust his new father, so must we come to trust our divine Father.

### Example 2: The Prodigal Son

When the prodigal son was out wasting his father's inheritance, he could have kept shouting, "Where are you, Dad? Give me more money. Do you even exist?" But it was not until he returned, fell down, and confessed, "Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you," that he was instantly restored and given back everything his father had. Humble confession—not performance, not pride, not waiting for a feeling—is the key to returning to God's embrace.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Make a firm, concrete "yes" choice for faith—not a "maybe" or "we'll see"
- ✅ Do the homework and make an informed, reasonable choice based on the person of Christ
- ✅ Participate actively in the master/apprentice relationship with God
- ✅ Approach scripture as sitting with the Divine Teacher and ask what He wants to show you
- ✅ Sit in prayer without expecting results, feelings, or wondering if you are doing it right
- ✅ Take your daily inventory in a loving, encouraging way—not in guilt or judgment
- ✅ Remember that feelings may follow faith, but they should never confirm it

## Keep In Mind

- Faith is not about a feeling. Feelings are deceitful, fleeting, and come and go. Real faith is grounded in knowing God is real.
- Faith is based on a person, not evidence or ideology. The question is simply: do I trust this person or not?
- Grace takes two: it is the harmony between your loyalty as a good apprentice and the gift of the Divine Master.
- The earliest disciples and thousands of saints did not die for Christ because of a feeling. Mother Teresa said, "I feel nothing," and she did it anyway.
- It is far better to know God's presence than to merely feel it. The strongest faith is saying, "I don't really feel You, God, but I know You are there."
- The enemy will bring heavy doubts the closer you get to real faith. See them for what they are—temptation—and throw them out.
- Blessed is the one who believes without seeing, hearing, feeling, or touching, but by trusting.
- Intellectual doubts are real and should be addressed—there are 2,000 years of answers available. Find them.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting for a feeling, sign, or evidence before committing to faith
  **Solution:** Faith is based on the person of Christ, not on shifting emotions or proofs. Make the choice to say "I know" and trust Him more than anyone else in the universe.
- **Problem:** Treating faith as a passive receipt of blessings
  **Solution:** Grace is not passive. Like an apprentice, you must show up, participate, and put what the Master says into practice. The Master gives freely, but you must receive and respond.
- **Problem:** Being lazy or disobedient in daily spiritual habits
  **Solution:** Be disciplined with at least daily scripture, prayer, and inventory. Obedience to the person of Christ—not to doctrines or rules—is what keeps grace flowing.
- **Problem:** Feeling guilty and judgmental during daily inventory
  **Solution:** Review your day with the Spirit in a loving, encouraging way—not in condemnation. The goal is growth, not shame.
