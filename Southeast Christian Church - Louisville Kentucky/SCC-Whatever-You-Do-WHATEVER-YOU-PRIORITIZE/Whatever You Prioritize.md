# Whatever You Do Week 3: Whatever You Prioritize

## Overview

This sermon teaches that Jesus must be the priority of our lives. Based on Colossians 3:1-17, it calls believers to set their hearts and minds on things above, put to death earthly sinful behaviors, take off the old self and put on the new self, clothe themselves with Christ-like virtues, and do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus. The message emphasizes that our beliefs must be the foundation for our behavior, and that we need the Holy Spirit to transform us from the inside out.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel like Jesus is being crowded out by other priorities in your life
- When you are struggling with old sinful patterns and need to make a change
- When you want to understand what it truly means to surrender your life to Christ

## Steps

### Step 1: Set your hearts and minds on things above (Colossians 3:1-2)

Set your heart on things above where Christ is seated at the right hand of God. Your heart is the core of your being and represents your priority. Set your mind on things above, not on earthly things, because thoughts are actions in rehearsal. Remember that you have died to your old life and your life is now hidden with Christ in God. You have a heavenly purpose and different priorities than this world.

### Step 2: Put to death earthly sinful behaviors (Colossians 3:5-9)

Put to death whatever belongs to your earthly nature: sexual immorality, impurity, lust, evil desires, and greed, which is idolatry. Also rid yourselves of anger, rage, malice, slander, and filthy language. Do not lie to each other. To put these things to death, you can starve them, expose them to the light, and ask for help from the Holy Spirit who lives inside you. Remember that you used to walk in these ways, but that is your past - you are now a new creation in Christ.

### Step 3: Take off the old self and put on the new self (Colossians 3:9-10)

Do not lie to each other since you have taken off your old self with its practices and have put on the new self which is being renewed in knowledge in the image of its creator. This is a change of clothes - you must actively take off the old sinful behaviors and put on the new identity in Christ. It is not enough to remove negative things without replacing them with the positive. Satan knows you by your name but calls you by your sin; Jesus knows you by your sin but calls you by your name.

### Step 4: Clothe yourself with Christ-like virtues (Colossians 3:12-14)

As God's chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience. Bear with each other and forgive one another. If any of you has a grievance against someone, forgive as the Lord forgave you. These virtues are the new uniform of the Christian, showing your identity and purpose to the world.

### Step 5: Let love bind everything together (Colossians 3:14)

Above all, put on love, which binds all these virtues together in perfect unity. Your love for Christ is so paramount that you begin to love others with His love. Love is the uniform of the Christian - when people see it, they know who you belong to.

### Step 6: Do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus (Colossians 3:17)

Whatever you do, whether in word or deed, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through him. This is the crux of the matter and the summary verse for the entire book of Colossians. Jesus is not a decoration, He is the foundation. He is not the seasoning, He is the savior and the main course. He wants to be the priority of your life - not just a resident, but the president.

## Examples

### Example 1: Lauren/Lolli's Story

Lauren (formerly known as Lolli) was an exotic dancer for five years after being kicked out of her home at 18. She dropped out of high school and stumbled into dancing to pay for a motel. Through the Scarlet Hope ministry, she had a major spiritual breakthrough and left the adult entertainment industry. She said, "I buried Lai over a month ago," preferring to be called by her given name, Lauren. This demonstrates the wardrobe change that happens when you surrender to Christ - you take off the old identity and put on the new. Lauren was baptized and is still wearing those new clothes.

### Example 2: The Pilot Story

Tammy Joe Schultz, one of the first female fighter pilots in the US Navy, safely landed a Boeing 737 after the engine exploded mid-flight. Debris flew through a window, killing a passenger and creating a hole in the side of the airplane. The plane went into a nose dive and was limping sideways through the air. In the midst of the crisis, she got on the intercom and announced they would land safely in Philadelphia. The passengers later said that hearing they had a destination changed everything for them. Having a destination gave them hope. This illustrates the Christian's hope - while living on earth, your eternal home is in heaven, and that should give you confidence and purpose.

### Example 3: The Dirty Clothes Shower Analogy

Imagine working outside on a hot day, getting sweaty and dirty, then taking a shower and feeling completely clean. But then, instead of putting on clean clothes, you reach down and put those same filthy, sticky, dirty clothes back on. That is what many Christians do - they get washed clean by Jesus but then revert back to their old sinful ways. God has cleaned you up and given you a new wardrobe. Don't go back to the dirty, smelly rags of your old life.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do set your heart and mind on things above, not on earthly things
- ✅ Do put to death sinful behaviors with the help of the Holy Spirit
- ✅ Do take off the old self and put on the new self in Christ
- ✅ Do clothe yourself with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience
- ✅ Do forgive others as the Lord has forgiven you
- ✅ Do let love bind all your virtues together
- ✅ Do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father
- ❌ Don't try to change your life in your own strength or willpower
- ❌ Don't remove negative habits without replacing them with positive ones
- ❌ Don't go back to your old sinful patterns after being saved
- ❌ Don't let good things crowd out the best thing (Jesus)
- ❌ Don't allow your past to determine your future
- ❌ Don't be deceived by Satan who calls you by your sin instead of your name

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon complements personal Bible study and regular spiritual disciplines, but does not replace them.
- Seek wise counsel and community accountability as you apply these teachings to your life.
- The transformation described in this sermon is a lifelong process through the Holy Spirit, not a one-time event.
- Remember that God's grace covers you when you stumble - keep returning to Christ in repentance and faith.
- This sermon is intended for spiritual growth and encouragement, not as a substitute for professional counseling, mental health care, or pastoral oversight.
- If you are struggling with deep-seated sin patterns, trauma, or mental health concerns, please seek help from a qualified Christian counselor, pastor, or mental health professional.
- Do not use the teachings in this sermon to judge or condemn others - we are all in different stages of sanctification.
- Guard against spiritual pride; receiving this teaching carries the responsibility to extend grace to others.
- If you feel unsafe or are in an abusive situation, reach out to trusted authorities and support organizations immediately.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to change in the flesh rather than through the Holy Spirit
  **Solution:** Surrender to the Holy Spirit and allow Him to change you from the inside out
- **Problem:** Removing negative things without replacing them with positive things
  **Solution:** Take off the old self AND put on the new self - don't leave a vacuum
- **Problem:** Letting good things crowd out the best thing (Jesus)
  **Solution:** Evaluate your priorities and ensure Christ is truly first in your life
- **Problem:** Going back to old sinful patterns after being saved
  **Solution:** Remember your new identity in Christ and don't put on those old dirty clothes
- **Problem:** Thinking the old nature is completely gone without a battle
  **Solution:** Recognize that there is still a battle at hand and rely on the Holy Spirit daily
- **Problem:** Using circumstances as an excuse for not following Christ
  **Solution:** Remember that God specializes in doing what is impossible
