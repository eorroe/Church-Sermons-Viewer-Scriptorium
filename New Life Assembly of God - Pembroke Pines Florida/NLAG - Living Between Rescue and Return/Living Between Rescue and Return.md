# Countdown to Christ's Coming: Living Between Rescue and Return

## Overview

This sermon explores three critical end-time events from God's prophetic calendar: the rapture, the tribulation, and the second coming of Christ. Using the story of Baby Jessica trapped in a well as a powerful analogy, Pastor Maria illustrates how just as rescue crews broke through the earth to save a toddler trapped underground, God will break through the clouds to rescue His people. The message calls believers to live rapture-ready every day, anchored in faith, actively sharing the gospel with urgency, and encouraging one another with the blessed hope of Christ's imminent return.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When studying end-times prophecy, the rapture, and Christ's return
- When you need encouragement that God has a plan for the future and is in control
- When feeling complacent or spiritually stagnant and need a call to urgency
- When seeking to understand the difference between the rapture, tribulation, and second coming
- When preparing your heart to share the gospel with others before Christ returns
- When facing fear, uncertainty, or despair about world events

## Steps

### Step 1: Place Your Faith in Jesus Christ and Repent of Your Sins

The first and most essential step to being ready for Christ's return is to personally surrender your life to Jesus. Repentance means turning away from your sinful life and turning to Christ in faith, confessing that you are a sinner and asking Him to forgive you. This is not a one-time event but the beginning of a lifelong journey of following Jesus. If you cannot say with 100% certainty that you are ready to meet Christ if He returned today, this is your day to place your faith in Him.

### Step 2: Live Rapture-Ready Every Day

Jesus could return at any moment—there are no prophecies that must be fulfilled before the rapture happens, and it will occur suddenly, in the blink of an eye. Live each day with the expectancy that the trumpet could sound and you could be caught up to meet the Lord in the air. This means staying spiritually alert, avoiding complacency, and letting the hope of Christ's return shape your priorities, relationships, and use of resources. Invest in eternal things rather than temporary pursuits.

### Step 3: Stay Anchored in Scripture, Prayer, and Faith

The coming tribulation will be a time of unprecedented deception, suffering, and divine judgment. While God promises to rescue believers before this period, the urgency of the times calls us to be deeply rooted in God's Word and prayer now. Anchor yourself in Scripture so that you will not be deceived, strengthen your prayer life so you can stand firm, and build your faith so you can endure whatever comes. A believer who is anchored in Christ will not be shaken when the world around them convulses.

### Step 4: Share the Gospel with Urgency and Boldness

The rapture will be followed by the tribulation, a seven-year period when evil will reach its climax on earth and people will face the choice of loyalty to Christ or compromise with the Antichrist. This makes the work of reaching the lost more urgent than ever. Every conversation, every act of kindness, every prayer, and every invitation to church could be someone's turning point before the door of opportunity closes. Share Jesus with your family, friends, neighbors, and coworkers now while there is still time.

### Step 5: Encourage One Another and Persevere in Hope and Holiness

The second coming of Christ will bring an end to evil, sin, and injustice. Christ will return with His church to establish His righteous reign on earth, and every wrong will be made right. Until that day, encourage fellow believers with these truths. When you are discouraged, remember the King is coming. When you are downcast, remember Jesus is coming to take you to Himself. Let holiness flow from hope, and let the expectancy of Christ's return motivate you to keep loving, serving, witnessing, and doing the work of the Lord.

## Examples

### Example 1: Baby Jessica - Rescue Comes Suddenly and Unexpectedly

Pastor Maria shares the story of Baby Jessica, an 18-month-old who fell into an abandoned well in Midland, Texas, and was trapped 22 feet underground for nearly 60 hours. Just as rescue crews broke through the earth to bring her to safety, God will break through the clouds to rescue His people. This story illustrates how rescue can come suddenly, without warning, and when we least expect it. It reminds us that we must be ready at all times because Christ's return for the church will happen in an instant—in the blink of an eye.

### Example 2: Abdul Salam Dar - The Urgency of Rescuing the Perishing

The pastor tells of Abdul Salam Dar, a Kashmiri fisherman known as "the diver" who has saved hundreds of drowning people from rivers and floods despite the danger and poverty he faces. When asked why he keeps diving into dangerous waters, he says, "Because lives are at stake." This is the same urgency Christ has called us to in reaching the lost. The nearness of Christ's return means every person's eternity depends on hearing the message of Jesus, and we cannot be content to simply look forward to going to heaven while others are left behind to face the tribulation.

### Example 3: Ministering on the Airplane - God Calls You to Divine Appointments

Pastor Maria shares a personal story of being prompted by God to minister to a young man on an airplane who was crying after discovering his boyfriend had been unfaithful. Though exhausted from three days of ministering at a conference, she obeyed and shared the love of Christ. The young man's grandmother had been praying for him for years, and Pastor Maria became the answer to her prayer. This example demonstrates that God places people in your path for a purpose and calls you to be ready to share the gospel in unexpected moments.

### Example 4: Pastor's Testimony of Healing - God Is Still Jehovah Rafa

During the service, Pastor Maria shares that she personally experienced God's healing power after being diagnosed with an aggressive, rare stage three cancer nearly ten years ago. The doctors gave her little hope, but God healed her, and she stands before the congregation today as a living testimony. This personal testimony reinforces the sermon's message that the same God who promises to rescue believers at the rapture is also a healer, and it encourages the congregation to stand in faith for miracles, including the healing prayer offered for a member recently diagnosed with cancer.

- ✅ Stay anchored in God's Word, prayer, and faith so you will not be deceived
- ✅ Share the gospel with urgency, boldness, and compassion while there is still time
- ✅ Encourage fellow believers with the hope of Christ's return
- ✅ Use your resources, time, and talents for eternal purposes and God's kingdom
- ✅ Persevere in holiness, love, and service even when the world grows darker
- ✅ Pray for others to come to Christ and be the answer to someone's prayer

- ❌ Don't live in fear of the tribulation if you are in Christ—He has promised to rescue you
- ❌ Don't focus so much on prophecy debates that you neglect the present mission
- ❌ Don't be ashamed or afraid to speak the name of Jesus to others
- ❌ Don't place your hope in earthly things that will be left behind
- ❌ Don't compromise your faith to avoid ridicule or pressure from the world

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon reflects a pre-tribulation rapture position, which is one of several views held by evangelical Christians. Other believers interpret the timing of end-time events differently.
- The core call to live ready, stay anchored, share the gospel, and encourage one another applies to all Christians regardless of eschatological viewpoint.
- No one knows the exact day or hour of Christ's return—not even Jesus' disciples knew when He was on earth. The emphasis should be on readiness, not date-setting.
- The promised rescue of the rapture is for those who have placed their faith in Jesus Christ. If you have not yet surrendered your life to Him, today is the day to do so.
- God's timing is perfect, and while His clock may tick slowly to us, it never stops and never misses. Trust His timing even when world events seem chaotic.
- The sermon teaches that believers will be rescued from the tribulation through the rapture, not left to endure the full outpouring of God's judgment on earth.
- The mark of the beast (666) represents a future worldwide system of economic and religious control that will force people to choose loyalty to Christ or compromise with evil. Accepting this mark means renouncing Christ and aligning with the Antichrist.
- The Antichrist will perform lying signs and wonders using supernatural power and possibly advanced technology such as AI to deceive many people. Stay anchored in Scripture so you will not be misled.
- The tribulation will be a time of intense persecution for those who refuse the mark of the beast—martyrs will be killed for their faith. Standing for Jesus now in the small pressures of life prepares you to stand for Him then.
- The power of the name of Jesus is greater than any sickness, disease, financial need, or problem you may face. Speak His name in faith and trust in His power to heal, deliver, and provide.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Becoming spiritually complacent and assuming there is plenty of time before Christ returns
  **Solution:** Live each day with intentional expectancy, knowing the trumpet could sound at any moment without warning
- **Problem:** Being paralyzed by fear of the tribulation or end-times events
  **Solution:** Remember that Christ has promised to rescue believers from the wrath to come—you will be taken out before the tribulation begins if you are ready
- **Problem:** Becoming so focused on prophecy debates and timelines that you neglect the present mission
  **Solution:** Balance your study of end-times events with urgent action to share the gospel and serve others now
- **Problem:** Refusing to share your faith out of fear of ridicule or rejection
  **Solution:** Remember that if you cannot stand for Jesus now in small ways, you will not be able to resist the pressure to bow to the Antichrist during the tribulation
- **Problem:** Placing your hope, security, and identity in earthly things like money, career, or relationships
  **Solution:** Keep your eyes fixed on eternity—when Christ returns, you will leave all earthly attachments behind, so invest in things that last forever
- **Problem:** Waiting for a "better time" to surrender your life to Christ
  **Solution:** Today is the day of salvation—if you cannot say with certainty that you are ready to meet Jesus, place your faith in Him now while there is still time
