# THE OVERCOMING LIFE - Overcoming in the Struggle Against Sin

## Overview

This sermon teaches that every believer faces an inner conflict between the desire to do what is right and the sinful nature that remains after coming to Christ. It explains that deliverance from sin does not come through human effort alone, but through a personal relationship with God who writes his law on your heart, forgives your sins, and fights your battles. The message calls listeners to stop trying harder in their own strength and instead surrender their struggles to God, receive his forgiveness, and walk in the freedom that Christ provides.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are struggling with recurring sin and feel trapped in a cycle of failure
- When you are trying to overcome sin through willpower and self-effort without lasting change
- When you feel burdened by guilt, unforgiveness, pride, anger, envy, or hidden sins
- When you want to understand the difference between the old covenant of rules and the new covenant of relationship with God
- When you need encouragement that God can intervene suddenly and set you free from long-standing struggles
- When you are ready to surrender control to God and let him transform your desires from the inside out

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge the Universal Reality of Sin

Every person is born with a sin nature, and this nature does not vanish immediately when you come to Christ. Admit that you have an inner conflict between wanting to obey God and being drawn to sin. Recognize that this struggle is normal for every believer, not a sign that you are not truly saved.

### Step 2: Stop Trying to Overcome Sin Through Human Effort Alone

Do not rely on willpower, promises, or self-discipline to break sinful patterns. Human effort is insufficient and will only lead to frustration and repeated failure. Surrender the idea that you can fix yourself through harder trying, stricter rules, or personal determination.

### Step 3: Draw Near to God Through a Covenant Relationship

Come closer to God instead of trying harder to stop sinning. Cultivate a daily relationship with him through prayer, worship, and reading his word. Understand that true deliverance comes from God changing your heart from the inside out, not from you gritting your teeth to behave better. Let God write his truth on your heart and shape your desires.

### Step 4: Receive God's Forgiveness for Past and Present Sin

Confess your sins to God and receive his forgiveness freely. God's forgiveness has the power to untwist the most tangled, stuck, and impossible situations in your life. When you receive his forgiveness, you are freed from the guilt and shame that have bound you. Trust that God forgets your sins and remembers them no more.

### Step 5: Let God Fight the Battles You Cannot Win

Release the need to defend yourself, fight your own battles, or overcome every temptation in your own strength. God actively draws near to you, lifts you up when you are weak, and confronts the enemy on your behalf. Picture yourself reaching for God's hand in a crowded, confusing world, and let him pull you close and walk with you through the struggle.

### Step 6: Surrender Your Specific Struggles to God

Bow your head and surrender whatever sin or stronghold you are facing. It may be pride, anger, unforgiveness, envy, lust, greed, or any other struggle. Say to God, "Lord, I can't. I need you." Hand over the situation to him and trust that he will set you free, change your heart, and give you the desire and power to live a life that pleases him.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Alcoholic Freed Suddenly

A man had been addicted to alcohol for over 15 years and repeatedly broke promises to his family. After crying out to God in desperation one night, he experienced a sudden, powerful encounter with Christ. He woke up the next morning sober, joyful, and feeling as if his past struggles were a bad dream. This shows that God can intervene instantly and set you free from long-standing bondage when you surrender to him.

### Example 2: The Pastor Delivered From Depression

A pastor fell into a dark depression lasting three years after a well-liked worship leader was removed from ministry and spread lies about him. Despite leading services and ministering to others, virtually no one knew his suffering. One Sunday morning, God told him to go into his office, kneel, and pray. As he prayed in other tongues, the chains of depression suddenly broke. This demonstrates that God can deliver you from deep emotional struggles when you obey his prompting and continue to seek him.

### Example 3: The Husband Who Surrendered His Drug Addiction

A husband was trapped in drug addiction and repeatedly promised his wife he would change, only to relapse within a week. The cycle continued until one day he finally surrendered to God's power and said, "God, I can't do this. I need you." His life turned around not because he tried harder, but because he gave up and gave the struggle over to God. Deliverance came through surrender, not self-effort.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Acknowledge the sin nature and the inner conflict instead of pretending you have no struggle
- ✅ Come closer to God through relationship rather than trying harder to stop sinning through willpower
- ✅ Receive God's forgiveness freely and trust that he forgets your sins
- ✅ Surrender specific struggles to God and say "Lord, I can't. I need you"
- ✅ Continue to pray and seek God even when deliverance seems delayed
- ✅ Allow God to fight your battles instead of trying to defend yourself or overcome every temptation alone
- ❌ Do not rely on human effort, willpower, or promises to yourself as the primary means of overcoming sin
- ❌ Do not try to fix your sinful nature by gritting your teeth and trying harder to be better
- ❌ Do not underestimate God's ability to act suddenly in your life
- ❌ Do not carry guilt and shame after receiving God's forgiveness

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to overcome sin through self-effort, willpower, and making promises to yourself or others.
  **Solution:** Surrender your struggles to God and let him change your heart from the inside out. Deliverance comes through relationship with Christ, not through human effort.
- **Problem:** Believing that your sin nature disappears the moment you come to Christ, so any ongoing struggle means you are not truly saved.
  **Solution:** Understand that every believer faces an inner conflict between the sinful nature and the Holy Spirit. This struggle is normal and does not nullify your salvation.
- **Problem:** Carrying guilt and shame for past sins even after confessing and receiving God's forgiveness.
  **Solution:** Receive God's forgiveness completely and trust that he remembers your sins no more. When God forgives, he untwists the burden and sets you free to live again.
- **Problem:** Underestimating God's ability to intervene suddenly in your situation after years of struggle.
  **Solution:** Continue to pray, seek God, and take steps of faith. God often works suddenly and in unexpected ways when you least expect it.
- **Problem:** Trying to fight spiritual battles in your own strength instead of letting God fight for you.
  **Solution:** Draw near to God, reach for his hand, and let him confront the enemy on your behalf. God fights for those who surrender to him.
