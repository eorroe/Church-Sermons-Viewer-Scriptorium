# New Oportunities - Part 1 - Isaías 43:18-19 - Pastor Elvis Sánchez

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God is a God of new opportunities who can transform any adversity, defeat, or broken circumstance into a new beginning for those who turn to Him. Drawing from Isaiah 43:18-19, 2 Kings 20 (the story of King Hezekiah), and the book of Ruth, Pastor Elvis Sánchez reveals that every season of pain, loss, or waiting can become a doorway to something greater when we humble ourselves before God, seek Him with all our heart, and embrace the opportunities He places before us.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you have experienced defeat, failure, or loss and need hope for a new beginning
- When you are struggling with illness, hardship, or broken circumstances
- When you feel stuck in past mistakes or old wounds and need to move forward
- When facing a new season, year, or chapter and want to walk in God's purpose
- When you need encouragement that God is still in control and can make a way where there seems to be no way
- When seeking to understand how to receive and act on the opportunities God is giving you
- When you need to make a decisive commitment to follow God and honor Him in your daily walk

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge That God Is a God of New Opportunities

Declare with your mouth that God is a God of new opportunities. Affirm that He is an expert at creating new opportunities in your life, even in the midst of adversity, brokenness, and a fallen world. Do not allow past defeats — such as the losses of 2020 — to define your future. Instead, recognize that God was present in your pain and that He is now preparing something new for you.

### Step 2: Release the Past and Do Not Look Back

Follow the instruction from Isaiah 43:18-19: do not remember the former things, do not bring old circumstances or past failures to memory. Let go of what is behind you. God is doing a new thing, and clinging to the past will prevent you from seeing and receiving it. Make a conscious decision to set aside old wounds, old disappointments, and old ways of thinking so that God's new work can be established in your life.

### Step 3: Turn Your Face to the Lord and Humble Yourself in Prayer

When faced with a devastating situation, follow the example of King Hezekiah in 2 Kings 20. Turn your face to the Lord, pray with a sincere and contrite heart, cry out to Him, and acknowledge that He alone has the power to change your circumstances. Be honest before God about your pain and your need for Him. Humility and genuine repentance open the door for God to intervene on your behalf.

### Step 4: Believe That God Hears Your Prayers and Sees Your Tears

Hold fast to the promise that God hears your prayers and sees your tears. Even when you do not see immediate answers, trust that God is recording every cry and every tear. The season of waiting is not the season of abandonment. Remain faithful in prayer, knowing that God will respond at the right time and that your sowing in tears will be harvested with joy.

### Step 5: Receive God's Healing, Restoration, and New Assignment

When God responds, receive what He is giving you with faith. Like Hezekiah, who was given 15 additional years of life and deliverance from his enemies, accept the new opportunities, restoration, and breakthrough that God is placing before you. Do not shrink back from the new assignment or the new season God is calling you into. Step forward in confidence, knowing that God's mercies are new every morning.

### Step 6: Walk in Humility and Guard Your Heart in the New Opportunity

Once you receive your new opportunity, guard your heart against pride, arrogance, and self-sufficiency. Hezekiah ultimately fell into pride after receiving such a great miracle from God. Stay humble, remain dependent on the Lord, and continually acknowledge that every good thing comes from Him alone. Humility is what keeps you anchored in the new season God has given you.

### Step 7: Choose to Follow God Wholeheartedly, No Matter the Cost

Follow the example of Ruth. Make a decisive, unwavering choice to follow God and His people, even when it requires leaving behind familiarity, comfort, or the approval of others. Say with Ruth: "Your God will be my God, your people will be my people." Honor God in your decisions, your relationships, and your daily walk. A committed, surrendered heart is what positions you to fully receive the opportunities God has for you.

### Step 8: Seize the Opportunity With Diligence and the Right Attitude

When God opens a door, walk through it with urgency and diligence. Ruth did not wait idly — she humbled herself, went to glean in the fields, and worked with perseverance from morning until evening. Do not let opportunities slip away. Step into them with a willing heart, a diligent spirit, and the expectation that God is orchestrating every detail for your good.

### Step 9: Invite Others to Receive Christ and Enter Into God's New Opportunities

Share the message of new opportunities with those around you. If you are watching or listening and have not yet given your life to Jesus Christ, recognize that the greatest opportunity of all is standing at the door of your heart. Open the door and invite Jesus to be your Lord and Savior. He will forgive your sins, wash you with His blood, and write your name in the book of life — the ultimate new beginning.

## Examples

### Example 1: King Hezekiah — From a Death Sentence to 15 Extra Years of Life

King Hezekiah received a devastating prophecy from Isaiah: he would die and not live. Instead of giving up, he turned his face to the wall, humbled himself before God, and cried out with a sincere heart. God heard his prayer, saw his tears, and reversed the decree — healing him and adding 15 years to his life. He also delivered his city from the enemy. This demonstrates that no situation is too far gone for God to intervene and create a brand-new opportunity.

### Example 2: Ruth and Naomi — From Loss and Desolation to a Place in God's Royal Lineage

After the death of her husband and sons, Naomi was left destitute and bitter in a foreign land. Her daughter-in-law Ruth chose to leave everything behind and go with Naomi to the land of Israel, declaring her allegiance to Naomi's God. In Bethlehem, Ruth seized the opportunity to glean in the fields — and God providentially directed her to the field of Boaz, a wealthy relative. Through this chain of new opportunities, Ruth was brought into the lineage of King David and ultimately into the genealogy of Jesus Christ. She became part of God's redemptive story because she chose to follow Him and acted on the opportunity before her.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Acknowledge daily that God is a God of new opportunities and declare it over your life
- ✅ Release past hurts, failures, and disappointments — do not let them anchor you
- ✅ Turn to God in prayer and humility whenever you face a difficult situation
- ✅ Trust that God hears your prayers and is working even when you cannot see the answer yet
- ✅ Receive God's promises and new beginnings with faith and gratitude
- ✅ Guard your heart against pride after God blesses you
- ✅ Make a decisive, wholehearted commitment to follow God in every area of your life
- ✅ Act with urgency and diligence when God opens a door for you
- ✅ Share the message of Jesus Christ and new opportunities with those who have not yet received Him

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal Bible study, prayer, or guidance from the Holy Spirit in your specific situation.
- God's promises are personal — what He did for Hezekiah and Ruth, He can do for you, but always seek His will and timing for your life.
- Stop and ask God for clarity if you are uncertain about what new opportunity He is calling you to step into.
- New opportunities from God often come through sacrifice, humility, and obedience — not through comfort or ease.
- Guard your heart against spiritual pride after experiencing God's blessings or breakthroughs in your life.
- Do not use God's promises as a guarantee of material prosperity or the absence of all hardship — God's new opportunities are primarily about spiritual growth, alignment with His will, and eternal purposes.
- Remain grounded in God's Word and community so that you can rightly discern whether an opportunity is truly from God.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Clinging to past failures, wounds, or losses and allowing them to define your identity and future.
  **Solution:** Release the past through prayer and deliberate choice. Meditate on Isaiah 43:18-19 and declare that God is making something new.
- **Problem:** Receiving a miracle or breakthrough and then falling into pride, believing you accomplished it on your own.
  **Solution:** Stay humble before God continually. Remember that every good thing comes from Him. Guard your heart as Hezekiah failed to do.
- **Problem:** Recognizing that God has opened a door but failing to act with urgency and diligence when the opportunity is before you.
  **Solution:** Step forward in faith. Like Ruth, seize the opportunity God has placed in your path with a willing heart and a spirit of perseverance.
- **Problem:** Making a conditional or half-hearted commitment to follow God, like Ruth's sister-in-law who chose to stay in Moab.
  **Solution:** Make a complete, decisive commitment to God. Say "Your God will be my God" with your whole heart, and watch how God transforms your life and calls you into His eternal story.
