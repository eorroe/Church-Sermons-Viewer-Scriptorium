## Verdict

**No true duplicates found.** The two In Touch Ministries sermons (*Your Convictions About Money* and *God and Our Money*) share the highest topic overlap — they cover the same stewardship/tithing framework with nearly identical scripture references — but the new Life.Church sermon is distinct in its central question ("how much is enough?"), its $2.15/day global poverty comparison, its explicit "rich toward God" framing, and its emphasis on investing time/talent/money in eternal purposes. These differences are substantive enough to justify keeping all three.
