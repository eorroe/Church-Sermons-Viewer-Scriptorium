# What You're Praying For Is Waiting on You

## Overview

This sermon teaches that your future and God's blessings for your life are already placed in your imagination, and acting on that vision through faith, a written vision board, and bold action is what brings those blessings into reality. Steve Harvey shares his personal testimony of writing down at age 10 that he wanted to be on TV, keeping that paper until age 27, and finally achieving it at 38—proving that what you consistently imagine and declare can manifest when you combine belief with action.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stuck, unsure of your next move, or waiting on God to answer a long-held prayer
- When you have a dream or vision others cannot see or do not believe in
- When fear is keeping you from taking the step that God has been showing you
- When you want to activate faith and align your actions with the future God has placed in your imagination

## Steps

### Step 1: Write Down What You Want

Take a pen and paper (or use your phone) and write down everything you want out of life—your career goals, relationships, health, finances, and calling. Be specific and honest. Do not hold back or downplay your desires. Write them as if they are already possible and yours.

### Step 2: Create a Vision Board

Collect those written desires and turn them into a vision board. Review and update it regularly as your goals evolve. Steve Harvey kept his original written note from age 10 until age 27, and he has changed his vision board three times in four years. Keep it visible and alive.

### Step 3: Read Your Vision Every Morning and Night

Look at your vision board or written list first thing in the morning and again before bed. Speak over it. Internalize it. This repetition trains your heart and mind to believe that what you have written is possible and coming.

### Step 4: Believe It Can Happen for You

Faith is the belief in things you cannot see. If you do not believe it can happen for you, it will not come back to you. Convince yourself that you are worthy of your vision and that God has already placed it in your imagination for a reason.

### Step 5: Recognize What God Has Put in Your Imagination

Everything God has for you is communicated through your imagination. Albert Einstein said imagination is the preview of life's coming attractions. Steve Harvey emphasizes that when you keep imagining something consistently, it is not random—it is God showing you a preview of what He has for you. That vision is real and was given to you on purpose.

### Step 6: Act on the Vision—Faith Without Works Is Dead

Do not just pray and wait. If God has shown you a vision and also shown you the work required to get there, you must act. Ask yourself: What would you do if God showed you what to do? Are you willing to quit the job, apply, take the risk, or make the move? Taking the step is where your faith becomes real.

### Step 7: Move Through Fear

Fear is the barrier between where you are and everything you want. Steve Harvey states plainly: everything you ever wanted and the best things in life are on the other side of fear. If you are scared, move anyway. Taking the jump—even when it looks crazy to everyone else—is how your vision becomes reality.

### Step 8: Keep Believing Even When No One Else Sees It

People may not understand your vision. They may even mock it. Steve Harvey notes that when he was a 10-year-old Black boy in 1968 saying he wanted to be on TV, no one saw it but him. That is because God did not show it to them—He showed it to you. Their inability to see it does not make it unreal. Protect your vision and do not let others' doubt cancel your faith.

### Step 9: Stay Faithful Through Setbacks

The path to your vision may include hardship, homelessness, or failure. Steve Harvey experienced three years of homelessness after quitting his job to pursue comedy. He emphasizes that God sometimes breaks you down to get your attention and that all the teachings from your upbringing will start making sense when you have nothing else but Him. Stay faithful and keep moving.

### Step 10: Expect God to Reward Your Faith

God will bless you and keep blessing you as you stop playing around with your calling. Ask for forgiveness when you fall off track, get back up, and keep walking. Your consistency and willingness to act on what God has shown you will result in the manifestation of your prayers and the fulfillment of your vision.

## Examples

### Example 1: Steve Harvey's Television Career

At age 10, Steve Harvey wrote on a piece of paper that he wanted to be on TV. He kept that paper for 17 years. At age 27, he won amateur night at a comedy club in October 1985 and quit his job the next day to pursue comedy full-time. He experienced homelessness for three years, but eventually got back on his feet, bought his first car at 38, and received a call from the Apollo Theater. He got on TV at 38 and has remained on television ever since. His mother lived to see his success, and he was able to bless her with a house, a remodeled home, and a Cadillac for his father.

### Example 2: A Career Change Backed by Faith

If you keep dreaming about a different career or job, that is God showing you a possible future. The action step is to apply, interview, and if necessary, quit your current job and make the move. Steve Harvey says, "Have you applied? Are you willing to quit and go over there and get the job?" Dreaming alone is not enough—you must act on the vision God placed in your imagination.

### Example 3: Overcoming Homelessness Through Faith

After quitting his job to pursue comedy, Steve Harvey became homeless. During that season, he re-engaged with the Bible and the teachings his mother, a Sunday school teacher, had given him. He credits that hardship with turning his life around because it forced him to listen to God with undivided attention. His story shows that even when a decision leads to catastrophe, staying faithful and humble can position you for eventual breakthrough.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Write your vision down and read it every morning and night
- ✅ Update your vision board as your goals and understanding evolve
- ✅ Believe that your vision is real and possible, even if no one else sees it
- ✅ Take action immediately after God confirms a direction or opportunity
- ✅ Stay humble and return to God's word when life falls apart
- ✅ Ask for forgiveness and keep moving when you stray from the path
- ✅ Surround yourself with people who will support or at least not attack your vision

## Keep In Mind

- Faith is available to everyone—you do not need a degree, an agent, or the right connections to possess it
- God does not show your future to other people; He shows it to you. Their disbelief does not invalidate your calling
- Everything in the world started in someone's imagination before it became real
- Your imagination is not hocus pocus—it is God giving you a preview of what He has planned for you
- Being "normal" is not the goal. If you do not enjoy being ordinary, take a chance on the extraordinary
- God may break you down to get your full attention. Hardship is not the end—it can be the catalyst for your breakthrough
- Faith without works is dead. Prayer and vision without action will not produce results
- This sermon does not replace professional career counseling, mental health support, or financial planning. Pursue wise counsel alongside your faith journey.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You write down your vision but stop reading it or forget it exists.
  **Solution:** Set a specific morning and evening reminder to review your vision board or written list daily.

- **Problem:** You pray and wait but never take action because you are waiting for a feeling or a sign.
  **Solution:** Understand that God has already shown you the work. Faith without works is dead—start applying, practicing, or moving in the direction of your vision now.

- **Problem:** You share your vision with people who tear it down and then abandon it because of their doubt.
  **Solution:** Remember that God did not show your future to them. Protect your vision and do not let their inability to see it affect your belief.

- **Problem:** Fear paralyzes you and you stay in a job, relationship, or situation you already know is not for you.
  **Solution:** Recognize that everything you want is on the other side of fear. Take one small step today in the direction of your vision, even if it feels scary.

- **Problem:** You experience setbacks and conclude your vision was never real.
  **Solution:** Setbacks are part of the process. Steve Harvey was homeless for three years before his breakthrough. Stay faithful, return to God's word, and keep moving.

- **Problem:** You judge your own faith harshly and feel like a failure when you stumble.
  **Solution:** Steve Harvey says he is "about a 70" and just hoping that is a low A. Perfection is not the standard. Ask God for forgiveness, get back on the line, and keep walking.
