# GOD Created You to Have a Life of Abundance

## Overview

Steve Harvey shares his personal testimony of how God saved him from trouble and gave him a specific gift—comedy and entertainment. He emphasizes that success is not about finding a passion but discovering the gift God already placed inside you, then pursuing it with persistence, faith, and daily prayer despite setbacks, haters, and the false comparisons of social media.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stuck in a job that doesn't align with your natural abilities
- When you're comparing your journey to others' highlight reels on social media
- When you need encouragement to pursue the gift God gave you instead of chasing fleeting passions

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify Your God-Given Gift

Your gift is what you do best with the least amount of effort. Steve Harvey realized he was a natural entertainer and comedian, not a teacher, carpenter, or mathematician. Sit down and honestly assess what you do effortlessly well—what people naturally come to you for. That is your gift, and it was placed inside you before you were born.

### Step 2: Pursue Your Gift, Not Just a Passion

Passion can fade, but your gift is permanent. Steve tried many jobs—about 11 different jobs—before realizing comedy was the only profession that fit him. Do not settle for work that requires you to be someone you are not. If your current job interferes with your gift, you must be willing to leave it and step out in faith.

### Step 3: Commit to the Process and Hustle

Success takes a long time. It is a process, not an overnight phenomenon. Steve Harvey highlights examples like Colonel Sanders, who achieved success late in life, and ordinary people like a landscaper named Cutter and a barber whose simple gifts became extraordinary livelihoods. Set clear goals, put in the work, and understand that dreams are free but the grind is sold separately.

### Step 4: Protect Your Mindset

Do not compare yourself to others' highlight reels on Instagram or social media. Ignore haters—they are either jealous or the devil opposing your God-ordained progress. Talk to God daily; it makes the journey easier. When you step out in faith, God will take care of you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Steve Harvey's Career Journey

Steve Harvey held approximately 11 different jobs before finding his true calling in comedy. He was fired from many of them because he was not a great employee in roles that did not match his gift. Once he stepped into comedy—the one thing he could do naturally and effortlessly—his life changed. He emphasizes that comedy is not something you can learn in school; it is a gift.

### Example 2: Colonel Sanders and the Ordinary-to-Extraordinary Gifts

Steve Harvey points to Colonel Sanders as proof that it is never too late to pursue your God-given gift. Sanders achieved massive success later in life. Harvey also mentions everyday examples: a landscaper named Cutter and a barber whose simple, God-given talents became thriving livelihoods. These examples show that ordinary gifts, when pursued faithfully, can produce extraordinary abundance.

- ✅ Identify your gift by noticing what you do effortlessly well, not by chasing trendy passions
- ✅ Talk to God daily and seek His guidance for your journey
- ✅ Set goals and commit to the long-term grind, knowing success is a process
- ✅ Ignore social media comparisons and focus on your own path
- ✅ Step out in faith even when leaving a stable job feels risky
- ❌ Do not confuse passion with your permanent God-given gift
- ❌ Do not compare your daily life to others' highlight reels
- ❌ Do not give up when haters or the devil oppose your progress
- ❌ Do not expect overnight success—embrace the process

## Keep In Mind

- Your gift was given to you by God at birth; it is already inside you waiting to be discovered and used
- You cannot take a lesson for comedy, acting, or singing in the same way you can take a lesson for piano or drums—your gift is innate
- Dreams are free, but the grind is sold separately; you must put in the work
- It is never too late to pursue what God has for you, as demonstrated by Colonel Sanders
- God will take care of you when you step out in faith and pursue your gift

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You confuse your gift with a temporary passion or trend
  **Solution:** Sit down and honestly assess what you do effortlessly well and what people naturally seek you for—that is your gift
- **Problem:** You compare your daily life to others' social media highlight reels and feel inadequate
  **Solution:** Remember that social media shows only the best moments; your process is unique and valid
- **Problem:** Haters and opposition discourage you from pursuing your God-ordained path
  **Solution:** Recognize that haters are either jealous or the devil opposing your progress; ignore them and keep moving forward
- **Problem:** You expect overnight success and give up when it takes longer than anticipated
  **Solution:** Understand that success is a long-term process; stay committed to the grind and trust God's timing
- **Problem:** You stay in a job that interferes with your gift because of fear
  **Solution:** Step out in faith—God will take care of you when you pursue what He created you to do
