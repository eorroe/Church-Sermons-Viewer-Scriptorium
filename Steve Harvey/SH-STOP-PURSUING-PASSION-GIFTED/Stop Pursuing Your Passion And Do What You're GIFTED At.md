# Stop Pursuing Your Passion And Do What You're GIFTED At

## Overview

This sermon teaches that talent and passion are not the same as one's God-given gift. It explains that true success comes from identifying and pursuing your unique gift rather than chasing what you are merely talented or passionate about. Through personal stories and biblical references, it shows how focusing on your gift leads to fulfillment and prosperity.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stuck pursuing a talent or passion that isn't producing results
- When you are unsure what career or life path to pursue
- When you need encouragement that your gift is within you and can change your life

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the difference between talent and gift

Talent is not the same as your gift. Your talent has nothing to do with your gift. Be very careful with what you are talented and passionate about because they sound like the same thing but are not. Talent and passion can be distractions from your true gift.

### Step 2: Identify your gift

Your gift is the thing you do the absolute best with the least amount of effort. It is the quest you must pursue to find your greatest chance for success. Every living soul God created has a gift at birth. Young people, you have to identify your gift. Therein lies your greatest chance for success.

### Step 3: Stop pursuing passion, pursue your gift

Stop focusing on your talent. Stop running away from your gift. Your gift will make room for you. Do not waste time pursuing your passion. You are wasting your time pursuing your passion. The Bible does not mention passion. It mentions your gift. What are you gifted at and do that?

### Step 4: Trust that your gift will provide

Your gift has made room for others and will make room for you. If you do your gift better than anyone else, you should be doing it. People make money doing simple things like frying chicken or cutting hair because they found their gift. Go do that. Do your gift. Don't worry about which one of them talents.

### Step 5: Have faith through difficult times

Everything you ever thought you would not make it through, you got past it. Now, if you currently going through something right now, guess what? You going to get past that, too. Faith is the substance of things hoped for. That if you hope hard enough, one day you ought to get smart and turn all that hoping into belief. And what is belief? Nothing but faith. And what is faith? Faith is a belief in things that you cannot see. Every trial and tribulation you go through prepares you for what you asked God for.

### Step 6: Practice forgiveness and stay focused

Forgiveness is not for the other person. Forgiveness is you. Your unwillingness to forgive another person is like you sipping the poison waiting on them to die. You can't sip the poison of unforgiveness and expect them to die. Forgiveness is for you. You can't drive your car looking in the rearview mirror. If you keep looking in the rearview mirror, you going to keep crashing your car. The rearview mirror is this big and the windshield is this big. That's a reason for. Cuz all the rearview mirror does is allows you to see what you passed. And to prevent what you passed from coming up on you again. That's all the rearview mirror is for. The windshield is your future. It's where you going.

### Step 7: Live in your gift before you die

Before you die, you should do what God created you to do. You really should, man. If your job makes you sick to go to, if you unhappy with waking up to go to where you got to go, it's because you ain't living in your gift. You should Before you die, you should do what God created you to do. If you living in your gift, you cool. It's just if you ain't. Telling jokes was a gift. If you paint, you should be painting. If babysitting is what you really do, you should be babysitting. You doing anything else, when that alarm clock go off in the morning, you ain't going to like it.

## Examples

### Example 1: Clipper - The Grass Cutter

A friend who only cut grass as a young man. He charged $2 for the front and $2 for the back. People called him Clippers because he was so gifted at cutting grass. He built a landscaping company in Cleveland, Ohio making $4 million a year with 38 trucks and crews. When winter came, he added snow plows and got all the snow removal contracts. He now makes $4 million a year and all he does is cut grass.

### Example 2: Marie Callender - The Pie Baker

A single mother who worked at a diner that was going out of business. She asked the owner to let her bake a pie to help make money. They sold every slice of the first pie. Demand grew so much she eventually installed a commercial oven in her house. The diner stopped selling hamburgers and only sold her pies. She eventually opened more than 120 restaurants. She started with just one pie.

### Example 3: Steve Harvey's Barber

A barber who cut Steve Harvey's hair for $10 in 1986. When Steve got on TV, he hired the barber and paid him $300 per haircut. Over the years, the barber earned up to $1,500 per haircut and made $6,000 a week. After Steve stopped performing, he paid the barber severance. The barber now owns four salons and two barber colleges making $3.6 million a year. All he does is cut hair.

## Best Practices

- Identify your gift as the thing you do best with the least amount of effort
- Stop focusing on talent and pursue your gift
- Do not compare your gift to others' gifts
- Have faith that your gift will make room for you
- Practice forgiveness so it does not consume you
- Live in your gift rather than staying in a job that makes you miserable

## Keep In Mind

- Your gift is not necessarily what you are passionate about
- Talent and passion can be distractions from your true gift
- Your gift has already been given to you by God at birth
- You may already be doing your gift without realizing it
- Your gift will make room for you if you stay committed to it

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not confuse talent with gift
- Do not let other people's opinions of your gift discourage you
- Do not let unforgiveness poison your life

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Pursuing a talent instead of your gift
  **Solution:** Identify your gift as the thing you do best with the least effort and pursue that instead

- **Problem:** Staying in a job or path because of passion rather than gift
  **Solution:** Stop pursuing passion and focus on your God-given gift

- **Problem:** Letting unforgiveness and past hurts consume you
  **Solution:** Practice forgiveness for your own benefit and keep your eyes on the future, not the past

- **Problem:** Giving up when times are hard
  **Solution:** Remember that faith is the substance of things hoped for and hard times are preparing you for your destiny
