# You Must Talk To God Every Day! | Steve Harvey

## Overview

This sermon teaches the importance of maintaining a daily relationship with God through prayer and asking for His guidance in all areas of life. Steve Harvey emphasizes that faith does not remove difficulties but gives us the strength to endure them, and that true success and happiness come from aligning with God's purpose rather than pursuing worldly achievements like wealth or formal education alone.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel overwhelmed by life's challenges and need strength to endure
- When you are chasing success but feel something is missing
- When you doubt your worthiness or ability to fulfill your God-given purpose
- When you need reminders that prayer and faith are accessible to everyone, regardless of background

## Steps

### Step 1: Talk to God Every Day

Make prayer a daily habit, regardless of your perfection or current circumstances. You do not need to belong to a specific faith or use specific language—just communicate with God consistently. Prayer changes things, and maintaining this connection is essential for spiritual strength.

### Step 2: Ask God for What You Want

Understand that you have not because you ask not. If you desire happiness, success, or provision, ask God for it. When you change what you ask God for, He responds by adjusting what He provides in your life. This principle is available to everyone, not just a select few.

### Step 3: Identify Your God-Given Gift

Your gift is the thing you do best with the least amount of effort. Write it down and recognize it as a divine assignment. Your gift will make room for you and take you places you never imagined. Do not ignore or suppress it.

### Step 4: Use Your Gift

Do not waste your God-given gift by settling for work that does not align with it. Your gift back to God is the use of the gift. If you are unhappy in your job, it is likely because you are not operating in your gift. Pursuing your gift leads to a life you will not believe.

### Step 5: Chase Your Dreams

Do not let diplomas or other people's expectations dictate your path. A diploma signifies the ability to complete a task, but real life and true fulfillment come from pursuing what keeps you up at night and what you were born to do. Bishop Jake warns that it is sad to die having never done the thing you were born to do—do not let that be you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Surviving Bad Days

Someone sent Steve Harvey a plaque that read: "On the days that I feel like I'm not going to make it, on the days that it feels like I can't endure anymore, I think back on my track record for surviving all my bad days. And so far, surviving all my bad days, my track record is 100%." This illustrates that God gives us the strength to endure, even when life feels impossible.

### Example 2: Education vs. Success

Steve Harvey points out that Mark Zuckerberg and Bill Gates, two of the richest men in America, do not have college degrees. He shares that he flunked out of school and knows people with master's degrees and PhDs who are not working. His message is clear: you do not need formal education to be successful—your relationship with God and your obedience to your gift are what truly matter.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Talk to God every day, even on days when you feel far from perfect
- ✅ Ask God boldly for the desires of your heart
- ✅ Identify and operate in your God-given gift
- ✅ Chase the dreams that keep you up at night
- ✅ Do not be ashamed or too proud to pray
- ❌ Do not tie your entire success to formal education
- ❌ Do not let a diploma or others' opinions stop you from pursuing your dreams
- ❌ Do not accept unhappiness in your job because you have a degree
- ❌ Do not ignore your gift just because you have stable employment

## Keep In Mind

- Faith does not eliminate life's difficulties, but it provides the strength and purpose to persevere through them.
- Money can turn emergencies into minor inconveniences, but it cannot buy true happiness or spiritual fulfillment.
- Your track record for surviving bad days is 100 percent so far.
- The Bible says you have not because you ask not.
- When you increase your faith and asking, God responds with greater blessings and opportunities.
- Your gift back to God is the use of the gift.
- The Bible says your gift will make room for you.
- Bishop Jake said: "I would hate to die and never do the thing I was born to do."

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Believing that you must be perfect before you can talk to God.
  **Solution:** God invites you to talk to Him often, even when you are not perfect. Consistency matters more than perfection.

- **Problem:** Thinking that formal education is the only path to success.
  **Solution:** Many successful people, including Mark Zuckerberg and Bill Gates, achieved greatness without degrees. Trust your God-given gift and relationship with God over worldly credentials.

- **Problem:** Staying in a job or career path that makes you miserable because you already invested in a degree.
  **Solution:** A diploma is a piece of paper, not a life sentence. Chase the dream that keeps you up at night, because anything else will lead to lifelong disappointment.

- **Problem:** Being too proud to pray or feeling ashamed to seek God.
  **Solution:** Prayer changes things, and no one is too important or too flawed to talk to God. Humility in prayer opens the door to God's guidance and peace.
