# GOD Is Closer Than You Think

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God is always present and available, regardless of how long it has been since you last spoke to Him. Steve Harvey emphasizes that the enemy uses the lie of unworthiness to keep people from prayer, but God already knows your situation and is waiting for you to simply talk to Him. The message also covers the importance of gratitude, renewing your mind, and using your God-given gifts to walk in purpose and blessing.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel distant from God or guilty for not having prayed in a long time
- When you are struggling with ungratefulness or constantly chasing the next milestone without enjoying the journey
- When you need encouragement to pursue the purpose and gifts God has placed inside you

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize and Reject the Enemy's Lie

When you feel like you should not talk to God because of past mistakes or time away, recognize that this is a tactic of the enemy. God already knows what you are going through and is not surprised by your absence. Do not let guilt or shame keep you from turning back to Him.

### Step 2: Start Talking to God Right Where You Are

You do not need to know how to pray or have the right words. Simply start talking to God honestly. He is available at all times. Consistency matters more than perfection, so establish a consistent habit of regular communication with Him and keep showing up.

### Step 3: Practice Gratitude for Every Level of Blessing

Make a habit of thanking God for what you have right now instead of only focusing on what you lack. Gratitude positions you to receive more grace and prevents you from becoming bitter or discontent. Thankfulness opens the door for God to take you to the next level.

### Step 4: Renew Your Mind and Speak Life Over Your Situation

Your thoughts and words shape your reality. Speak positively about your life, your future, and your abilities. Replace negative self-talk and limiting beliefs with declarations of faith. What you project from your mind is what you will receive back.

### Step 5: Identify and Pursue Your God-Given Gift

Do not waste time chasing passions that do not align with your God-given gift. Your gift is what will open doors, provide provision, and establish your purpose. Commit to developing the gift you were born with and let it lead the direction of your life.

## Examples

### Example 1: Overcoming Homelessness Through Faith

Steve Harvey shares his personal testimony of being homeless and struggling to survive, even being run off people's property while trying to fish for food and facing arrest. Despite these hardships, he maintained his faith and eventually experienced God's grace in ways he never imagined, including receiving eight television shows when he only asked God for one. This demonstrates that God is with you in your lowest moments and can turn your situation around beyond what you dreamed.

### Example 2: The Cup of Sugar Analogy

Steve Harvey illustrates the importance of gratitude through the analogy of someone who comes to your house every day asking for a cup of sugar but never says thank you. Eventually, the homeowner stops giving. In the same way, if you constantly ask God for blessings without gratitude, you risk losing His favor. But if you show thankfulness, God may surprise you with even greater blessings, such as giving you a five-pound bag of sugar so you do not have to keep coming back.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Talk to God regularly, even if you do not have the perfect words or feel worthy
- ✅ Keep a gratitude mindset and thank God for incremental progress
- ✅ Renew your mind daily by speaking positive, faith-filled declarations
- ✅ Pursue your God-given gift with discipline and focus
- ✅ Write down your vision and place it where you will see it every day
- ❌ Let the enemy's guilt keep you from praying after a time of absence
- ❌ Chase success without including God in your plans
- ❌ Focus only on distant goals and miss the joy in your current season
- ❌ Pursue passions that are not aligned with your God-given gift

## Keep In Mind

- God is always closer than you think and does not require perfection to relate to you
- You cannot fix yourself; only God can transform you from the inside out
- Your gift, not your education or passion alone, is what makes room for you in life
- Gratitude is not optional; it is a key principle for receiving more from God
- Your thoughts and words are like a remote control, projecting what you will receive back into your life
- Being imperfect does not disqualify you from having a powerful relationship with God
- This sermon is intended for personal spiritual growth and reflection. The principles shared are rooted in faith and personal testimony. Readers should discern how these teachings apply to their own lives and relationship with God.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Feeling too guilty or ashamed to pray after being away from God for a long time, which causes you to avoid Him even more.
  **Solution:** Remember that the enemy uses guilt as a trick to keep you silent. God is already waiting for you, so simply turn around and start talking to Him again. No explanation or apology is required to begin.

- **Problem:** Chasing success and bigger goals without showing gratitude for current blessings, leading to constant dissatisfaction.
  **Solution:** Pause and reflect on how far God has already brought you. Celebrate the small wins and thank God for each level of progress before asking for more. Gratitude keeps your heart in the right posture to receive grace.
