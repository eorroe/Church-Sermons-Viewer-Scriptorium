# You Have To Start Believing In Your Imagination

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God places divine visions and desires in your imagination as a preview of your future. It emphasizes that imagination is not fantasy but a spiritual preview of life's coming attractions, and you must believe it, write it down, pray about it, and take action to manifest it.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you have a vision or dream that others have discouraged you from pursuing
- When you feel stuck in your current circumstances and need to reconnect with your purpose
- When you struggle with doubt, discouragement, or negative influences from others
- When you need to understand the connection between imagination, faith, and action

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize Your God-Given Imagination

Understand that God has placed specific visions and desires in your imagination. These are not random thoughts but divine previews of what He has planned for you. When you repeatedly imagine opening a business, graduating, buying a house, or achieving wealth, God is showing you His plan for your life.

### Step 2: Protect Your Vision From Naysayers

Stop telling your visions to people who cannot see what God has shown you. God did not reveal your vision to your family, friends, or critics — He revealed it to you. When you share your dreams with people whose vision for your life is smaller than God's, they will shoot them down because they cannot see what God has placed in your imagination.

### Step 3: Write Down Your Vision and Pray About It

Write everything you imagine down. Take those written visions and pray about them, asking God for them. "But you have not because you ask not." If you see the vision but don't ask God for it, you cannot receive it. Faith requires both belief and action.

### Step 4: Take Action Through Faith and Work

Faith without works is dead. Once you have recognized your vision, written it down, and prayed about it, you must put in the work. God created you with the power of choice. If you decide to be rich, no one can stop you except yourself. Start where you are with what you have.

### Step 5: Combat Discouragement With Gratitude

When you feel discouraged or focused on what is not happening, shift your focus to gratitude. Joy and depression cannot reside in the same space. List everything you are grateful for each morning. By focusing on what God has already done, you immediately straighten out your perspective and overcome discouragement.

### Step 6: Identify Your Gift and Pursue Your Dream

Identify your God-given gift and go do what you are gifted at. You do not need a degree to be successful. Many of the world's most successful people built extraordinary lives by operating in their God-given gift rather than following a traditional academic path. Your gift is what God uniquely equipped you with. Regular people make regular income, but exceptional people make exceptional income because they operate in their unique, God-given assignment. People pay for expertise, excellence, and uniqueness.

### Step 7: Keep Doing the Work Over and Over

Take the idea God has instilled in you — no matter how small it seems — and develop it, multiply it, and do it over and over. Do not wait for a million-dollar idea. Consistency and repetition build success. Steve Harvey kept telling the same jokes over and over for decades, steadily growing his audience and his platform until he could command $250,000 per show. Your success comes from sustained effort and faithful repetition, not a single breakthrough.

## Examples

### Example 1: Steve Harvey's Personal Journey

Steve Harvey imagined himself on stage in front of 10,000 people as a young boy. He used to sing "A Song for You" and act out his life on stages with thousands watching. When he told his family he was quitting his job to be a comedian, everyone shot it down - his mother, ex-wife, friends, and siblings. But he didn't listen because God had put it in his imagination. He won amateur night on October 8, 1985, earned $50, quit his job the next day, and eventually became one of the original Kings of Comedy. Today he has performed in front of 44,000 people and commands $250,000 per show.

### Example 2: The Power of Consistent Effort

Steve Harvey kept doing the same jokes over and over for decades. He built his career on repetition, not on constantly creating new material. Nike started with one pair of shoes and just kept making shoes over and over until they became a billion-dollar company. People selling sugar cane in Ghana and cocoa beans make millions by doing the same thing consistently. Success comes from multiplying what you already have, not waiting for a perfect idea.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Write everything you imagine down
- ✅ Pray about your visions and ask God for them
- ✅ Put in the work - faith without works is dead
- ✅ Combat negativity with gratitude
- ✅ Identify your God-given gift and pursue it
- ✅ Focus on what you have, not what you lack
- ✅ Keep doing the work consistently over time
- ❌ Don't tell your vision to people whose vision for your life is smaller than God's
- ❌ Don't let others define your route to success
- ❌ Don't wait for a million-dollar idea before starting
- ❌ Don't focus on what is not happening - it causes discouragement
- ❌ Don't let your mother's or anyone else's limited vision override God's plan for you

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon is for spiritual encouragement and does not replace personal discernment, pastoral counsel, or wise guidance from trusted mentors.
- Pause and seek God in prayer if you are unsure whether a vision or desire is from Him or rooted in selfish ambition.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Protect your visions and dreams by sharing them only with trusted, supportive individuals who align with your spiritual journey.
- Be cautious about who you share your business or life plans with, as negative input can kill your faith and vision before it has time to grow.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Telling your vision to people who don't understand or support it
  **Solution:** Keep your vision private between you and God, sharing only with those who have earned the right to speak into your life
- **Problem:** Getting discouraged when things don't happen immediately
  **Solution:** Combat discouragement with gratitude by focusing on what God has already done in your life
- **Problem:** Waiting for a "million-dollar idea" before starting
  **Solution:** Take the $10 idea God has already given you and multiply it through consistent action
- **Problem:** Letting others define your success path
  **Solution:** Recognize that God created you to be different, and your route to success may not look like anyone else's
