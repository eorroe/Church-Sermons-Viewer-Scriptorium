# God Always Has a Gift Behind Every Moment of Pain

## Overview

This sermon teaches that every moment of pain and adversity in life carries both a lesson and a blessing from God. Steve Harvey explains that grace is God's unmerited favor — receiving what you do not deserve — and that aligning your will with God's plan leads to a life far greater than anything you could design for yourself. The message challenges listeners to stop worrying about tomorrow, embrace gratitude, take calculated risks, and maintain faith through every hardship because God always has a gift behind every moment of pain.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are going through a painful season and need hope that something good will come from it
- When you feel stuck, worried about the future, or unable to control outcomes in your life
- When you are pursuing a dream, goal, or career and need encouragement to keep going despite setbacks
- When you struggle with comparison, entitlement, or dissatisfaction with where you are in life
- When you need to shift your perspective from obligation to gratitude

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Nature of Grace

Grace is God's unmerited favor. He gives you things you do not deserve. Stop believing that you are entitled to anything. Instead, recognize that every good thing in your life is a gift of grace, not something you earned. Shift your mindset from expecting blessings to receiving them with humility.

### Step 2: Align Your Will With God's Plan

You can want and plan all you want, but God's plan for you is always better than anything you could design. Surrender your own vision by praying "thy will be done" and leave room for God to exceed your expectations. Do not limit your future to what you can see or imagine.

### Step 3: Stop Worrying About Tomorrow

Worrying about tomorrow does not add a single hour to your day. You have no control over the future, so release the anxiety. Instead, ask God only for your daily bread — just for today. Focus your energy on making the best decision for this moment and trust that tomorrow will take care of itself.

### Step 4: Take a Chance and Stop Playing It Safe

Success is not sustainable without faith, and faith requires risk. If God brought you through everything up to this point, He will not stop caring for you now. Take the job, start the business, pursue the dream, and walk through the door that God opens. When a door closes in your face, treat it as a sign to walk up the hall to something better.

### Step 5: Keep the Faith Through Every Setback

Your track record for surviving hardships is 100 percent. Every disappointment, every loss, and every setback has already been survived by you. Do not quit when things get hard. Keep the faith even when you cannot see the outcome, because faith is the belief in things you cannot yet see.

### Step 6: Change Your Words to Shift Your Perspective

Replace "I have to" with "I get to." Saying "I get to go to work" or "I get to eat right" removes obligation and replaces it with gratitude. This one-word shift changes your perspective on life and opens the door for additional blessings.

### Step 7: Practice Gratitude Every Single Day

Gratitude is the number one principle of success. Start each day by thanking God for what you already have — your breath, your clothes, your job, your family. The more grateful you are, the more things God will give you to be grateful for. You do not need more money to start this practice. You can begin today.

### Step 8: Project Your Desired Future With Faith

Your mind is the biggest remote control ever created. Speak and think toward the life you want, just as you would press a button on a remote to change the channel. Whatever you project with faith and gratitude will eventually come back to you. Write your vision, speak it, and believe it.

### Step 9: Recognize That Pain Always Leaves a Gift

Every moment of adversity contains a lesson and a blessing. When a relationship ends, a job fails, or a dream is delayed, do not wallow in misery. Wait on the lesson, trust the process, and expect the blessing that follows. A breakup can be the very thing that positions you for the right relationship. A closed door can lead you to a better opportunity.

### Step 10: Develop a Daily Devotional Practice

Spend at least 20 minutes each day with God. Read your Bible, journal what you learn, and ask God to show you what you should learn that day. Put what you learn into action. This daily habit keeps you grounded, grateful, and connected to the source of all success and peace.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Super Bowl Commercial After Hardship

After enduring personal and professional losses, Steve Harvey received a call from T-Mobile to appear in a Super Bowl commercial. That single opportunity paid him ten times what he earned from Miss Universe, earned him praise from 14 countries, and resulted in 14 villas around the world for him and his wife. The pain he went through was not in vain — it positioned him for a gift he never could have planned.

### Example 2: The Remote Control Mindset

Steve Harvey explains that your mind works like a TV remote. If you press the button for Sports Center, Sports Center comes on. If you press the button for HBO, HBO comes on. Whatever you project with your mind — poverty or prosperity, fear or faith — is what will show up in your life. He projected rich thoughts, and richness came to him because he was created in the image of God, who said "Let there be light" and it was so.

### Example 3: The Middle East Dinner Table

At a dinner with billionaires and sheiks, a British guest kept bragging about Jeff Bezos being the richest man in the world. A young sheik shut him down by revealing that his uncle made a billion dollars a day. Steve Harvey realized in that moment that his own thinking had been small. He apologized to his wife for failing her miserably, and from that point on, he committed to thinking bigger because a man is as he thinketh.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You feel entitled to blessings and become bitter when they do not arrive on your timeline
  **Solution:** Replace entitlement with gratitude. Recognize that everything good in your life is grace, not something you earned. Thank God daily for what you already have.

- **Problem:** You worry so much about tomorrow that you miss the opportunities and peace of today
  **Solution:** Ask God only for your daily bread. Focus on making the best decision for today. Worrying does not add a single hour to your day, so release the future into God's hands.

- **Problem:** You play it safe and never pursue your dreams because you fear failure or loss
  **Solution:** Remember that your track record for surviving hardships is 100 percent. God brought you through everything before, and He will not stop caring for you now. Take the chance.

- **Problem:** You wallow in misery after a painful event instead of looking for the lesson and blessing
  **Solution:** Remember that pain always leaves a gift. Every moment of adversity contains a lesson and a blessing. Wait on both, expect both, and do not let one bad chapter make you close the whole book.

- **Problem:** You compare your beginning to someone else's middle and feel discouraged
  **Solution:** Understand that success takes time. The person you admire today went through years of hardship you did not see. Keep taking steps, inch by inch, and trust the process.

- **Problem:** You stay at a closed door, begging for something God has already redirected
  **Solution:** When a door closes in your face, walk up the hall. There are more doors ahead. Quit knocking on a door that is already closed and trust that God has something better waiting.
