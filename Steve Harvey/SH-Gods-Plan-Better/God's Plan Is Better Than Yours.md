# God's Plan Is Better Than Yours

## Overview

Steve Harvey shares how God's plan is always better than your own plan, illustrated through his personal experience of losing everything in 2005 and subsequent restoration. He explains that God gives us the power of choice and will let us work our own plans, but when we surrender and ask God for His plan, He will blow our minds. The sermon also teaches how to condition your mind for success through the law of attraction.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to surrender your own plans and seek God's direction
- When working through rock bottom moments or major life setbacks
- When the user asks about aligning their mindset with higher achievement

## Steps

### Step 1: Surrender Your Own Plan to God

Recognize that God's plan is always better than your own plan. When you hit rock bottom and have no more plans, surrender to God and ask Him what He has for you. Understand that God may let you work your plan to a point, but will ultimately redirect you when you ask for His plan.

### Step 2: Condition Your Mind for Higher Achievement

Give your partner credit and let them handle areas you cannot see, acknowledging that there are no great men without great women supporting them. Condition your mind for success by experiencing higher standards, such as first class travel or luxury items. Use the law of attraction: once you experience a higher level, your mind will attract ways to return to that level.

## Examples

### Example 1: Rock Bottom Restoration

Steve Harvey lost everything in 2005, including going through a divorce and being down to $1,700. When he surrendered and asked God for His plan, God restored him and took him to places he never thought he'd go, demonstrating that God's plan is always better.


### Example 2: Law of Attraction in Action

Buying expensive items like Louis Vuitton purses or first-class seats programs your subconscious to produce thoughts that attract success. Once you experience a higher level, your mind will subconsciously attract ways to return to that level, showing how conditioning your mind through experiences produces desired outcomes.
