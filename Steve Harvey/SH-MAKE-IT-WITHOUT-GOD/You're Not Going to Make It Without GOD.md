# You're Not Going to Make It Without GOD

## Overview

Steve Harvey delivers a powerful message on the necessity of prayer and dependence on God, drawing from his personal journey from homelessness and repeated failure to national success. The sermon teaches that God is always available to those who call on Him—regardless of their past mistakes, current struggles, or unconventional path—and that taking a leap of faith, even when circumstances seem impossible, is the only way to true breakthrough.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel too ashamed or too proud to pray
- When you're waiting for the "right time" to make a move
- When you've been trying to figure everything out on your own and it's not working
- When you need encouragement that God can work with you exactly as you are
- When you feel like giving up on your dreams or purpose

## Steps

### Step 1: Don't Forget to Pray

Prayer is the foundational step. Steve emphasizes that prayer changes things no matter how dark your situation looks, what people have said about you, what the verdict is, or what the haters are saying. Make prayer a daily practice, not just something you do when you're in crisis.

### Step 2: Don't Be Ashamed or Too Proud to Pray

Go somewhere by yourself and tell God you need help. Don't worry about who's looking or what they'll think. God already knows your problems, and He would love to hear from you. Steve challenges listeners to stop playing with prayer and stop being ashamed about needing God.

### Step 3: Acknowledge That You Need God

Quit trying to make it on your own. Steve says, "You not going to make it without God." If you've tried to do it your way so far, he asks, "Tell me how that's working out for you? It sucked, don't it? You need God." Admit that you need Him and that you're tired of trying to figure it out for yourself.

### Step 4: Talk to God Even When You Don't Need Anything

Steve shares that he likes talking to God even when he doesn't really need anything, so that when he does need something, he doesn't have to introduce himself to Him. Build a relationship with God through regular conversation, not just emergency prayers.

### Step 5: Take Your Leap of Faith

Don't wait for the right time to make your move—the ducks will never line up in a row. God requires an exchange between you and Him called faith. Sometimes the leap isn't a leap; sometimes it's a push, and God kicks you off the cliff. You just have to go after it. Stop trying to figure out the "how to"—that's none of your business. The Bible says, "Ask, believe, receive."

## Examples

### Example 1: From Homelessness to Comedy Success

Steve grew up on a block where you had to be something to make it out, and many people from his block are no longer alive. He was told he would never be nothing, flunked out of school, went through three marriages, lost everything he owned twice, and lived homeless in a car for three years. Despite all of this, he kept faith and kept going. He eventually got a call to do Showtime at the Apollo, became the permanent host of Amateur Night in 1991, and turned his life around. Today he owns luxury cars because he refuses to ever be homeless again—he jokes that if he ever goes bad again, he's living in his Rolls-Royce.

### Example 2: The Amateur Night Decision That Changed Everything

At 27 years old, Steve was lost and tired of trying to figure life out. A girl named Gladys Jacob dared him to go to an amateur night at a comedy club on October 8th, 1985. He had never even heard of a comedy club—he was writing jokes for a comedian named A.J. Jamal for $10 a joke. Gladys picked him up, took him to the club, he signed up, went up last, won the clap-off, and got paid $50. The next day, October 9th, 1985, he quit his job and has done nothing but tell jokes ever since. That single leap of faith changed the entire trajectory of his life.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do pray regularly, even when you don't need anything, to build your relationship with God
- ✅ Do go somewhere private to talk to God without worrying about who's watching
- ✅ Do acknowledge that you need God in every area of your life
- ✅ Do take action in faith without waiting for perfect timing or circumstances
- ✅ Do keep going even when you feel like giving up
- ✅ Do focus on your own walk with God instead of comparing it to others
- ❌ Don't let shame or pride keep you from praying
- ❌ Don't wait for the "right time" or for all the ducks to line up
- ❌ Don't try to figure out the "how to" before taking your leap
- ❌ Don't expect God to line everything up perfectly for you—He requires faith

## Keep In Mind

- God is never too busy for you, no matter what you're going through
- God actually knows who you are and created you to converse with Him
- Everyone's walk with God is different, and that's okay—just get your own version
- God works with you where you're at, not where you think you should be
- God can save anybody who wants to be saved, no matter their past
- If God lined everything up perfectly for you, you wouldn't need faith
- Your breakthrough may come after a season of struggle, but God will come through
- Listeners should seek guidance from their own spiritual leaders and personal faith community
- The speaker's unconventional approach to Christianity reflects his personal version of being saved and may differ from traditional religious interpretations
- Spiritual journeys are deeply personal—what works for one person may not work for another

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting for the perfect time or for all the ducks to line up in a row before making a move
  **Solution:** Take your leap of faith now—God doesn't set things up perfectly because He wants to see if you really believe
- **Problem:** Feeling ashamed or too proud to pray, especially around other people
  **Solution:** Go somewhere by yourself and tell God you need help; He already knows your problems and would love to hear from you
- **Problem:** Trying to figure out the "how to" before taking action
  **Solution:** Stop trying to control the process—ask, believe, and receive; the "how to" is none of your business
- **Problem:** Comparing your spiritual walk to others and feeling like you're not doing it right
  **Solution:** Focus on your own relationship with God; everyone's path is different, and God works with you exactly as you are
- **Problem:** Giving up during difficult seasons
  **Solution:** Keep faith and keep going—Steve lived in a car for three years before his breakthrough came
