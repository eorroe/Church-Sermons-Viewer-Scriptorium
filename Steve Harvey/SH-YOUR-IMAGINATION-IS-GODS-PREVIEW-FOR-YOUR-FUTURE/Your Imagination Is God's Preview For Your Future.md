# Your Imagination Is God's Preview For Your Future

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God gave you imagination as a preview of your future. You are encouraged to ask God for big things, walk in faith, take action, and stop letting small-minded people or your own doubts block your blessings. It challenges listeners to stop trying to figure everything out on their own and instead trust that God has an abundant plan for their lives.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stuck in a cycle of mediocrity or lack
- When you are afraid to ask God for something big
- When you have been trying to figure everything out on your own
- When doubt, past failures, or other people's limited vision are holding you back
- When you need encouragement to start walking with God today
- When you want to understand the connection between faith, imagination, and God's plan for your life

## Steps

### Step 1: Ask God for Big Things Instead of Small Stuff

Stop wasting God's time with small requests and start asking for the big things He wants to give you. Whether it is financial freedom, a business, a home, or abundant life, ask God for it. You have not because you ask not, so begin asking for the mortgage instead of just the rent. God is not limited by your current circumstances, so do not rule yourself out before you even ask.

### Step 2: Stop Trying to Figure Everything Out Yourself

You were not the creator of your life, so stop trying to figure it all out. The how-to is none of your business. Instead, follow the simple principle: ask, believe, and receive. When you stop trying to control every detail and trust God with the outcome, you make room for Him to work on your behalf.

### Step 3: Have Faith and Take Action

Faith without works is dead. Once you ask God for something, you must believe it and put in the work required to receive it. Trust that God is working behind the scenes while you stay consistent, prayerful, and diligent. Expect great things from God each day, and do not give up before the appointed time. Your faith will carry you through until you receive everything God has for you.

### Step 4: Trust Your God-Given Imagination as a Preview

Your imagination is not random. It is God showing you a preview of a coming attraction He has for you. If you keep imagining a business, a promotion, a second home, or a richer life, that is God speaking to you. Do not play your imagination off as meaningless. Attend to it, nurture it, and act on it, because what you imagine is often what God is calling you into.

### Step 5: Do Not Let Others' Lack of Vision Limit You

If you want to kill a big dream, tell it to a small-minded person. People often do not believe in your dream because God did not show it to them the way He showed it to you. Do not let their doubt discourage you. You only need permission from God, not from people who cannot see what He has placed in your heart. Stay focused on the vision He gave you.

### Step 6: Start Your Walk with God Today Without Needing Anyone's Permission

You do not need anyone's approval to begin your faith journey or to walk with God. There are plenty of openings, and He is available now. All you have to do is go. Start today. Speak to Him, ask Him for what you need, and claim your place as His child. It is never too late to chase your dreams, and it is never too late to start.

## Examples

### Example 1: Asking for Six Acres and Receiving Sixteen

Steve Harvey asked God for six acres of land while he was still struggling. When he found a piece of land for sale that matched his request, the seller offered him 16 acres for the same price he was prepared to pay for six. This illustrates that when you ask God for something, He can add grace and favor that far exceeds your original request.

### Example 2: Quitting a Job to Pursue Comedy

At 27, Steve Harvey won an amateur night competition, quit his job the next day, and pursued comedy full-time. Everyone in his family thought he was foolish except his father. He was foolish enough to believe in the vision God placed in his imagination, and that decision changed the entire course of his life.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Ask God specifically for what you want
- ✅ Replace small thinking with big, faith-filled requests
- ✅ Trust your imagination as a divine preview
- ✅ Act on your dreams instead of waiting for perfect timing
- ✅ Keep talking to God daily about your vision
- ✅ Stay grateful and expectant regardless of your current situation
- ❌ Do not let small-minded people talk you out of your God-given dream
- ❌ Do not try to figure out the exact "how" before stepping out in faith
- ❌ Do not ask God for things out of fear or lack
- ❌ Do not wait for someone else's permission to start your walk with God

## Keep In Mind

- You have not because you ask not. Asking is the first and most important step.
- God rewards you according to your faith, so believe for big things.
- The imagination God placed in you is real and purposeful. It is a preview of what He has planned.
- It is never too late to chase your dream. God is never late, and He can turn your past mistakes into your purpose.
- Your gift back to God is the use of the gift. Do not bury what He has placed inside you.
- Gratitude changes your attitude, and your attitude changes your altitude.
- This sermon is for spiritual encouragement and personal growth.
- Always seek wise counsel and verify any major life decisions with prayer and discernment.
- Do not replace professional financial, legal, or medical advice with spiritual principles alone.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Asking God for small things out of fear or low expectations.
  **Solution:** Train your mind to think big. God is not limited by your circumstances, so do not limit your requests.
- **Problem:** Telling your dream to people who will shoot it down.
  **Solution:** Share your vision only with those who have faith and vision, or keep it between you and God until it is time.
- **Problem:** Giving up before the appointed time because the process is hard.
  **Solution:** Remember that God is processing you. Stay faithful, keep working, and trust that your appointed time is coming.
- **Problem:** Trying to figure out every step before taking action.
  **Solution:** Let go of the need to control the "how." Ask, believe, receive, and let God direct your steps.
