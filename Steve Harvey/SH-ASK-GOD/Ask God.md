# Ask God

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the key to receiving blessings, favor, and purpose in life is simply asking God. Steve Harvey explains that grace is God's unmerited favor—something you cannot earn, buy, or work your way into. He emphasizes that God gave you your gifts and abilities at birth, and your job is to identify them, live in them, and ask God for what you need while maintaining faith and gratitude. The message encourages believers to stop overthinking, stop judging others' spiritual walks, and simply follow the biblical pattern: ask, believe, receive.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel stuck or confused about why you haven't achieved your goals or dreams
- When you are struggling with depression, discouragement, or a "dark place" and need to reset your perspective
- When you want to understand how to walk with God in an authentic, imperfect way without comparing yourself to others

## Steps

### Step 1: Ask God for What You Want

Stop struggling to figure things out on your own and go directly to your Maker. God created you, and He wants you to ask Him for the things you need in this world. The vast majority of people do not have what they want because they never take the specific step of asking God for it. As Steve Harvey states, "All you got to do is ask God." This means approaching God in prayer with a specific request, not vague wishes or half-hearted hopes.

### Step 2: Believe That You Will Receive

After you ask, believe that God has heard you and will answer in His timing. Faith is essential to receiving from God. As Steve Harvey states, "I ask, I believe, I get ready to receive." Getting in a posture of expectancy means actively choosing to trust that God is faithful to provide, rather than living in doubt, worry, or resignation while you wait for your answer.

### Step 3: Get Ready to Receive and Put in the Work

Faith without works is dead (James 2:17). After you ask and believe, you must be ready to receive and take action. Do not just sit back and wait passively—prepare yourself for the opportunity when it comes. As Steve Harvey states, the "how" is not your responsibility; your responsibility is to ask, believe, and get ready to receive by doing the practical work of preparing your heart, mind, and actions for what God is going to do.

### Step 4: Identify and Live in Your God-Given Gift

God gave you a gift at birth. It is inside you—the thing you do the absolute best with the least amount of effort. When you are living in your gift, you wake up overjoyed. If you dread going to work, it is a sign you are not walking in what God created you for. As Proverbs 18:16 states, "Your gift will make room for you." Identify your gift, stop wasting time in things you were not made for, and go do it.

## Examples

### Example 1: Steve Harvey's Career and Favor

Steve Harvey shares that he does not have a traditional education, but he has faith and a God-given gift for comedy and entertainment. He tried 11 different jobs and was fired from most of them because he could not find a boss he respected. Everything he has today—his TV shows, radio show, and platform—came from asking God and walking in the gift God gave him. He keeps a vision board (a visual board displaying images and words representing the goals he is asking God for) in his home, demonstrating that he actively relies on divine favor rather than his own credentials.

### Example 2: Overcoming Darkness with Gratitude

Steve Harvey explains that when he finds himself in a dark place or feeling down, he immediately starts making a written or mental list of everything he can think of that he is grateful for. He states, "Gratitude erases depression immediately." He shares a personal story of having a tough morning and then shifting his focus to what God had done for him, which instantly changed his perspective. Joy and depression cannot reside in the same space, so choosing gratitude is a deliberate act that drives out darkness.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Ask God for what you want and need. Do not struggle alone trying to figure everything out yourself.
- ✅ Live in your God-given gift. Identify what you do best with the least effort and build your life around it.
- ✅ Practice gratitude when you feel down. Make a specific list of everything you can be grateful for to push back against depression.
- ✅ Walk your own imperfect walk with Christ. Do not compare yourself to others or judge their faith.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You keep trying to figure out the specific logistics ("how") instead of simply asking God and trusting Him with the details, which keeps you stuck and prevents faith from operating.
  **Solution:** Remember that nowhere in the Bible does it tell you to figure out how to get what you need. Just ask, believe, and get ready to receive.

- **Problem:** You judge other people's spiritual walk or think you are a "perfect 10" Christian, which leads to pride, isolation, and a lack of genuine relationship with God.
  **Solution:** Accept that nobody is perfect. As Steve Harvey states, "He ain't made no 10s." Focus on your own imperfect walk with Christ rather than comparing yourself to others.
