# The Vision God Gave You Is Yours for a Reason

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the dreams and visions God places in your imagination are real previews of His plans for your life. Steve Harvey explains that imagination is not hocus-pocus but a divine preview of coming attractions, and he shares principles for protecting your dreams, taking incremental action, and building generational wealth through faith, hard work, and the law of attraction.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you have a dream or vision but don't know how to pursue it
- When small-minded people have shot down your ideas
- When you're stuck in a comfort zone and afraid to change
- When you need encouragement that success is possible despite setbacks
- When you want to build generational wealth and leave a legacy for your children's children's children
- When you're struggling with faith or doubt about your future

## Steps

### Step 1: Protect Your Imagination

Recognize that God places your dreams in your mind. When a dream comes into your imagination, it is not just your imagination - it is a preview of a coming attraction that God has for you. Don't tell your dreams to everybody, because if you want to kill a big dream, tell it to a small-minded person. They couldn't see it because God didn't show it to them. He showed it to you.

### Step 2: Stop Worrying About the "How"

The specific path to success is not your responsibility to map out. God is in the blessing business. When you have these dreams in your imagination, wake up and take action. The Bible says ask, believe, receive. You do not need to figure out every detail between asking and receiving. You have to be willing to work, but God does not require you to solve the entire puzzle before you begin.

### Step 3: Take Incremental Action

Break your goal down into small increments. Inch by inch, anything's a cinch. Stop trying to figure out how to make a million dollars in one step. You cannot do that. Instead, ask: What can you do right now to earn $10? Can you sell a pie, braid hair, mow a lawn, paint a picture? Do that 10 times and you have $100. Do that 10 more times and you have $1,000. Start somewhere.

### Step 4: Build Generational Wealth

Getting rich is not a magic trick. You just have to learn a few principles of success and be willing to work really, really hard. Before you can build generational wealth, you have to achieve personal financial success first. You cannot build generational wealth without first securing your own finances. So don't skip over the steps. Take care of yourself first, then create generational wealth after you've secured finances for yourself.

### Step 5: Talk to Your Children About Their Dreams

Spend time talking to your children about what they dream, not what you want for them. Because God's plan for them may very well not be the plan you have for them. If you ask a young person what they dream about, you open up a conversation you have never had with them. Ask them: "If you could not fail, what would you be if you just knew you couldn't fail and you could be it?"

### Step 6: Practice the Law of Attraction

I believe in the law of attraction and it works for me. What you put out is what comes back. A simple practice: set aside one-dollar bills and save loose change. Put a $5 bill in every night and roll it up tight. After a while you will need a rubber band on it. Put a $10 in there if you can. After 1 year, take the rubber band off that roll - you will be stunned at how many dollars actually come your way. Focus on abundance and only welcome positive, high-value opportunities.

### Step 7: Embrace the Journey

The joy ain't in the end. The joy is actually in the journey. If you don't make a million, when you get to $200,000, that's kind of cool. If you're broke and you get a $50,000 a year job, that's kind of cool. Quit trying to see your way all the way to the tape and understand that it is a journey that God put you on. Success is hard, but being not successful is hard too. On the road to success, it's hard, but it creates options for you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Steve Harvey's Personal Journey

Steve Harvey was living in his car for 3 years. He had a stuttering problem. He wasn't a good student. He didn't look like a TV personality. But he kept imagining himself on TV. On October 8th, 1985, he won an amateur night at a comedy club for $50. The next day, October 9th, he walked into his insurance company job with that $50 bill in his hand and quit. He had never been in a comedy club before, but he was for sure this was it. He was the only one who was sure - nobody else in his life thought it was a smart move. His brothers wanted him to work for their daddy's construction company. But he had a vision and he acted on it incrementally, eventually building a media empire.

### Example 2: The Big Mama Story

A friend of Steve's from Cleveland was a tough guy - a gangster who never changed his lifestyle. His grandmother, Big Mama, was dying in the hospital. Steve flew to Cleveland to be with his friend. Big Mama looked at him and asked: "Do you know your grandfather's name? Do you know your grandfather's father's name?" He said no. She said, "You know why? Cuz he didn't leave you nothing." She then told him: "I'm finna go see Jesus now. And when I go, Big, I want you to live your life so that your children's children's children will know your name." She died 15 minutes later. This changed Steve's friend's life. Steve changed his own life after hearing this story and started living so his children's children's children would remember his name. This illustrates the principle: old men plant trees under which they know they will never sit in the shade.

### Example 3: Michael Jordan

Michael Jordan took 946 career game-winning shot attempts. He made only 134 of them. You don't hear about the misses, but you do hear about the makes. All you need is the makes. You will miss many times. The average professional baseball player succeeds only three out of 10 times at bat. Teams pay him millions of dollars per year to succeed 30% of the time. They don't pay for the misses - they pay for the hits. All you need is one breakthrough success. Steve Harvey got on Showtime at the Apollo and turned that 2:00 AM show into a ratings success. They kept giving him shows because he demonstrated he could turn one opportunity into another.

- ✅ Trust that God placed your vision in your imagination
- ✅ Break big goals into small, actionable increments
- ✅ Celebrate progress along the journey
- ✅ Ask, believe, receive - don't try to figure out the entire path
- ✅ Build for future generations after securing your own finances first
- ✅ Focus on what you can control and ignore negativity
- ✅ Spend time talking to your children about their dreams, not yours
- ✅ Have a relationship with God that helps in day-to-day living
- ❌ Don't tell your dreams to small-minded people who will shoot them down
- ❌ Don't try to figure out the entire journey from A to Z
- ❌ Don't skip steps in pursuit of generational wealth
- ❌ Don't let your comfort zone keep you from success
- ❌ Don't focus only on the destination and miss joy in the journey
- ❌ Don't let other people's opinions control your decisions
- ❌ Don't quit listening to people who haven't achieved what you're trying to achieve

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Telling your dreams to people who don't understand them and getting discouraged when they shoot you down.
  **Solution:** Keep your vision close. Remember that God showed it to you, not to them. Your imagination is real.

- **Problem:** Trying to figure out the entire path to success before taking action.
  **Solution:** Focus on the next small step. You can't figure out the whole journey. Ask, believe, receive. Break it down into increments.

- **Problem:** Comparing yourself to others or feeling like you should already be at your destination.
  **Solution:** Celebrate incremental progress. The joy is in the journey, not just the end. Celebrate making $50,000, then $100,000, then $150,000.

- **Problem:** Letting fear and comfort keep you stuck in the same place.
  **Solution:** Step out of your comfort zone. Everything you want is on the other side of fear. You have to change.

- **Problem:** Negativity from others affecting your confidence and focus.
  **Solution:** Other people's opinions are not your business. You can't stop what God has for you. Focus on the love in your life, which is greater than hate.

- **Problem:** Feeling like success is impossible because of past failures or current circumstances.
  **Solution:** Your track record for surviving bad days is 100%. If God didn't bring you through everything, why would he not get you through the rest? Michael Jordan missed 812 game-winning shots but made 134. You only need one hit.

- **Problem:** Wanting to build generational wealth before securing your own finances.
  **Solution:** You can't build generational wealth and not hit it yourself. Take care of yourself first, then build for future generations.

- **Problem:** Staying in a situation that is making you unhappy because you feel like you wasted time.
  **Solution:** You didn't waste those years - you learned lessons. If you stay, you will just have more of the same. It is time to go.
