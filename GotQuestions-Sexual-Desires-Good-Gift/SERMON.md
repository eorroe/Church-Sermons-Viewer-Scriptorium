# Are Sexual Desires Inherently Sinful? — Sexual Desires: A Good Gift

## Overview

Sexual desires are a God-given gift, integral to our personhood as those made in God's image, but they do not define your entire identity—a believer's true identity is rooted in being made in God's image (Genesis 1:27) and in being reconciled to God through faith in Christ (2 Corinthians 5:17). This sermon teaches that sexual desires are not inherently sinful but must be mastered and channeled within the boundaries of God's moral law, specifically within the covenant of marriage between one man and one woman. When followed correctly, sexuality yields the blessing God intended (Genesis 1:28); when distorted by false teaching—including the claim that sexual desire defines personal identity, the claim that any consensual sexual expression is permissible, and the claim that gender is entirely self-determined apart from biological sex—that rejects God's created order, it leads to destruction (Romans 6:23).

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When seeking to understand whether sexual desires are inherently sinful
- When wrestling with questions about the role of sexuality in the Christian life
- When needing biblical clarity on God's design for human sexuality
- When addressing temptations or cultural lies about sexual identity and expression

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge Sexuality as a God-Given Good

Sexual desires are not inherently sinful. God designed sexuality as the means by which creation perpetuates, and for human beings it includes emotional, spiritual, and psychological dimensions. Recognize that your sexuality is an integral part of who you are, but it does not define your entire identity.

### Step 2: Master Your Desires, Do Not Be Mastered by Them

God calls you to exercise self-control over your sexuality, governing your desires by His Word rather than allowing every sexual impulse to dictate your behavior. Bring your sexual desires under subjection to God's moral law (1 Corinthians 7:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:27). When sexual desires determine your lifestyle or dominate your behavior, you are following every sexual impulse apart from the self-control the Holy Spirit provides (Galatians 5:22-23), rather than exercising the self-control God calls us to as His image-bearers.

### Step 3: Keep Your Body in Subjection to Your Spirit

You were created in God's image with the immaterial part of your being that will live forever, either with God or apart from Him (Genesis 1:27). To live in continual communion with Him, you must keep your physical body in subjection to that spirit. Do not allow sexual desires to override the spirit-led life God has called you to live.

### Step 4: Align with God's Design for Sexuality

God's design for human sexuality is marital: one man and one woman united in a lifelong covenant becoming "one flesh" (Genesis 2:21–24; Matthew 19:5–6). Sexual desires are to be expressed within this God-established boundary. Violating this design through premarital sex, adultery, or any sexual act outside the marriage covenant leads to spiritual destruction: separation from God in this life through broken fellowship and, if unrepented, eternal separation from Him, plus broken relationships with God and others and the loss of the blessing God intends—a life of sexual purity, peace with God, and joy in obeying Him for those who walk in His ways.

### Step 5: Reject Satan's Perversion of God's Good Gift

False teaching and sinful human hearts twist whatever God creates as good, redirecting it into ways He did not intend. Satan gains control of sexuality by convincing people that their desires should determine the course of their lives. Recognize that distortions of God's created order include: rejecting the male/female binary God established in Genesis 1:27; rejecting the marriage covenant between one man and one woman established in Genesis 2:24; and embracing any sexual practice God has forbidden in Scripture, including premarital sex, adultery, homosexuality, bestiality, and incest (Leviticus 18:22; 1 Corinthians 6:9)—these are not God's design but the result of Satan's exploitation of selfish human hearts.

### Step 6: Direct Sexual Desires Only Toward God's Design for Marriage

Sexual desires must be directed only toward God's design for marriage between one man and one woman. Channel your desires only within the avenues God designed. When you direct your desires toward your spouse within marriage, you experience the good God intended. When you violate His design, spiritual destruction follows—separation from God, broken relationships with God and others, and loss of the good life God intends—a life of sexual purity, peace with God, and joy in obeying Him (Romans 6:23). God's gifts always come with instructions.

### Step 7: Follow God's Instructions and Experience His Good

God's design is not restrictive but protective and purposeful. When you follow His instructions for sexuality, you experience the good He intended. Submit your sexual life to Christ, obey His commands, spend time in His Word, pray, and fellowship with other believers, letting His Word shape your understanding and practice of sexuality.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Married Couple Guarding Their Hearts

A husband and wife, committed to Christ, choose to keep their sexual intimacy within the bounds of their marriage covenant. They do not allow outside influences: media that depicts sexual activity outside the marriage covenant; peer pressure from friends or culture to compromise biblical purity; cultural norms that reject biblical boundaries for sexuality; and relationships that tempt them to compromise sexual purity, including dating relationships outside of marriage or close friendships with someone they are attracted to outside of the marriage covenant between one man and one woman. Instead, they see their sexuality as a God-given gift to be cherished and protected, resulting in a deep bond that honors both each other and God.

### Example 2: The Single Person Walking in Purity

A single believer acknowledges their sexual desires as good but chooses to live celibately, in line with God's instruction (1 Corinthians 7:8–9). They refuse to let culture or their own urges determine their identity, instead finding their worth in being created in God's image. They keep their body in subjection to their spirit, walking in purity and awaiting God's timing for marriage or singleness.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Affirm that sexual desires are a good gift from God, not inherently sinful
- ✅ Master your sexuality; do not allow it to master you
- ✅ Keep your body in subjection to your spirit in continual communion with God
- ✅ Express sexuality only within the biblical covenant of marriage between one man and one woman
- ✅ Recognize Satan's lies and reject cultural distortions of God's design
- ✅ Follow God's instructions for His gifts to experience the blessing He intended

## Keep In Mind

- Sexuality is part of your personhood but does not define you entirely
- You are created in God's image and called to live in continual communion with Him
- God's boundaries for sexuality are for your protection and flourishing (Deuteronomy 6:24; Proverbs 4:20-22).
- Sinful human hearts, influenced by false teaching, actively work to misuse every good gift God has given
- God's gifts always come with instructions—follow them

## Security & Safety Notes

- Guard against cultural narratives that redefine sexuality outside of God's design
- Do not normalize or accept sexual activity outside marriage between one man and one woman, including adultery, fornication, homosexuality, bestiality, incest, or any sexual activity outside the marriage covenant—do not treat these as morally equivalent to God's design for sex within marriage
- Be cautious of influences that seek to make sexual desire the primary definer of personal identity
- Protect yourself and others from spiritual destruction—separation from God, broken relationships with God and others, and loss of the good life God intends—a life of sexual purity, peace with God, and joy in obeying Him (Romans 6:23)

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Believing that sexual desires are inherently sinful or shameful
  **Solution:** Remember that God designed sexuality as a good gift; the problem is sin, not the desire itself

- **Problem:** Allowing sexual desires to determine your identity or lifestyle
  **Solution:** Your identity is rooted in being created in God's image (Genesis 1:27), not in your sexuality or desires

- **Problem:** Violating God's design and suffering the natural consequences
  **Solution:** Channel your sexual desires only within the boundaries God established—marriage between one man and one woman. It is better to marry than to burn with passion (1 Corinthians 7:8-9) — marriage is the God-designed context for sexual expression.

- **Problem:** Falling for Satan's lie that following God's design is restrictive
  **Solution:** Understand that God's boundaries are for your protection and the key to experiencing the good life God intends for you—a life of sexual purity, peace with God, and joy in obeying Him
