# Don't Leave Your Family Behind

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers to reject the enemy's strategy of dividing families and to pursue unity in worshiping Jesus Christ as a complete family unit. Drawing from the book of Exodus, Pastor Anna explains how Pharaoh (a type of Satan) repeatedly tried to separate Israel's men from their women, children, and livestock—and how Moses refused every such offer. The message calls parents to be spiritual authorities in their homes, to bring their children to church, to plant God's Word in their children's hearts from an early age, and to refuse any pressure to marry outside God's will.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When the enemy is attacking your marriage or family unity
- When you are a parent who wants to raise godly children
- When you are single and facing pressure to marry outside God's will
- When your teenager has drifted away from the Lord
- When your church is struggling with generational or gender division
- When you want to understand why family unity matters to your spiritual freedom

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Enemy's Strategy to Divide Your Family

Satan uses the same tactics Pharaoh used against Israel: he offers a version of freedom that leaves your family behind. He pressures spouses, parents, and children to worship God separately or not at all. Do not be deceived by offers that sound spiritual but exclude part of your family.

### Step 2: Refuse Any Offer That Separates Your Family From Worship

Like Moses, who refused Pharaoh's offer to let only the men leave Egypt, you must refuse anything that divides your household from Christ. If an opportunity, relationship, or system requires you to leave your spouse, children, or family behind, it is not from God.

### Step 3: Rise as the Spiritual Authority in Your Home

Fathers and mothers are the spiritual leaders of their homes. You are responsible before God for what happens under your roof. Model Christ-like character, speak words that heal, and lead your family in prayer and Bible study.

### Step 4: Bring Your Children to Church and Get Them Connected

Do not leave your children at home while you attend church alone. Bring them with you and get them involved in children's ministry, youth ministry, and the life of the church. This plants a seed of faith that will produce fruit in their lives.

### Step 5: Plant God's Word in Your Children Early

Start teaching your children the Word of God when they are young. Pray with them, read the Bible with them, and take them to church. Isaiah 55:11 promises that God's Word will always accomplish what He sends it to do—even if it takes time to see the fruit.

### Step 6: Pray Without Ceasing for Wayward Children

If your son or daughter has drifted from the Lord, do not give up. Continue to pray, declare God's Word over their life, and trust that the seed you planted will prosper in God's timing.

### Step 7: Refuse to Marry Outside of God's Will

If you are single, do not yield to pressure to marry an unbeliever or someone not walking with Christ. Marrying outside God's will gives Satan a foothold to divide your future family. Trust God's timing and wait for a God-centered marriage.

### Step 8: Work Hard at Unity in Your Marriage

Marriage is a gift from God, but it requires intentional effort. Forgive quickly, serve your spouse, control your speech, and put Christ at the center of your home. Unity in marriage brings freedom; division brings bondage.

### Step 9: Reject Division in the Church

Do not accept lies that separate generations, genders, or ethnic groups in the body of Christ. There is no retirement from serving Jesus. The older and younger generations must worship and serve together. Gender does not disqualify anyone from ministry.

### Step 10: Stand Together as a Family Unit

Make a covenant as a family: you will go to church together, pray together, study the Bible together, and serve your community together. Declare with your household: "Me and my house will serve the Lord."

## Examples

### Example 1: A Family Choosing Church Together

A father who previously attended church alone decides to bring his wife and children every Sunday. He leads family devotions before bed and prays with his children. Over time, his children develop a personal relationship with Christ, and his marriage grows stronger because they are walking in God's will as a unit.

### Example 2: A Single Person Waiting for God's Will in Marriage

A single Christian woman faces pressure from family and friends to marry a non-believer. She chooses to honor God's Word instead, praying for a husband who loves Christ. In God's timing, she meets a godly man, and their marriage becomes a testimony of unity and freedom rather than division and compromise.

### Example 3: Parents Praying for a Wayward Teenager

A mother's teenage son has left the church and rejected the faith she taught him. Instead of giving up, she continues to pray, speak life over him, and model Christ's love. After two years of persistent prayer, her son returns to the Lord, fulfilling the promise of Isaiah 55:11 that God's Word always produces fruit.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Bring your whole family to church—do not leave anyone behind
- ✅ Be the spiritual leader your home needs by praying and studying the Word with your family
- ✅ Plant seeds of God's Word in your children's hearts while they are young
- ✅ Pray without ceasing for family members who have strayed
- ✅ Refuse any relationship or marriage that is not centered on Christ
- ✅ Forgive quickly and speak words that heal in your marriage
- ✅ Serve together as a family in your church and community
- ✅ Reject generational, gender, or racial division in the body of Christ
- ❌ Do not accept offers that separate you from your family in worship
- ❌ Do not leave your children at home while you attend church alone
- ❌ Do not marry outside God's will to escape loneliness
- ❌ Do not give up on praying for your children or your marriage
- ❌ Do not allow bitterness, anger, or frustration to divide your home
- ❌ Do not accept the worldly lie that older believers should retire from serving God

## Keep In Mind

- Satan's primary strategy is division—he attacks marriages, families, and churches because unity produces spiritual authority
- True freedom in Christ includes the freedom of your entire household
- The best inheritance you can leave your children is a relationship with Jesus Christ, not money or education alone
- God's Word never returns void—it will always produce fruit in your family's lives, even if it takes time
- You are a spiritual parent to the generation around you, whether or not you have biological children
- Unity in the family and church is not automatic; it requires intentional effort, prayer, and sacrifice

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share personal family struggles publicly in ways that could expose your children to harm or exploitation
- When praying for your family online or in public, avoid disclosing sensitive details that could compromise their safety
- If you are experiencing abuse in your marriage or home, seek help from trusted church leadership and professional authorities—this sermon does not condone staying in abusive situations
- Guard your children's identities and privacy when posting about them on social media or church platforms

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Leaving children at home while attending church alone, believing they are too young or will attend later
  **Solution:** Bring them with you now. The Holy Spirit can work in their hearts at any age, and your presence teaches them that worship is a family priority.
- **Problem:** Giving up on a wayward child because years have passed without visible change
  **Solution:** Continue praying and speaking God's Word. Isaiah 55:11 guarantees that God's Word will produce fruit in His timing, not yours.
- **Problem:** Marrying an unbeliever out of loneliness or desperation
  **Solution:** Trust that God's plan for your life includes a God-centered marriage. Fellowship with other believers, serve the church, and wait on the Lord rather than settling for less than His will.
- **Problem:** Believing that only men should lead or serve in the church and family
  **Solution:** This is a divisive lie from the enemy. God has called men and women, old and young, to worship and serve together in unity.
- **Problem:** Allowing generational retirement in the church, believing older believers no longer have a role
  **Solution:** There is no retirement in the kingdom of heaven. Older generations must mentor and walk with younger generations, and both must worship together.
- **Problem:** Accepting the world's standards for marriage and family instead of God's design
  **Solution:** Make Jesus the center of your home and let the Word of God be your authority. Marriage and family work when they are done God's way.
