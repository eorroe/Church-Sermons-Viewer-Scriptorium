# Don't Leave Your Family Behind

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers to reject the enemy's strategy of dividing families and to pursue unity in worshiping Jesus Christ as a family unit. Drawing from the book of Exodus, Pastor Anna explains how Pharaoh, who opposed God's people, represents the enemy. He tried to separate Israel's men from their women, children, and livestock—and Moses refused the offers that would have separated the people. The message calls parents to be spiritual authorities in their homes and all believers to be spiritual parents to the generation around them, to bring children to church, to plant God's Word in young hearts from an early age, and to refuse any pressure to marry outside God's design for marriage as described in the Bible.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are facing the enemy's opposition to your marriage or family unity
- When you are a parent or spiritual parent—a believer who mentors and guides the younger generation, someone God has entrusted with children or teenagers even if you have no biological children—who wants to raise children who follow Christ.
- When you are single and facing pressure to marry outside God's design for marriage as described in the Bible—specifically, to marry someone who is not serving God or who is not someone who follows Christ
- When your teenager has drifted away from the Lord
- When your church is struggling with generational or gender division
- When you want to understand why family unity matters to your freedom in Christ

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Enemy's Strategy to Divide Your Family

Satan uses the same tactics Pharaoh used against Israel: Satan offers freedom that leaves your family behind. He pressures family members to worship God in Egypt while other family members remain behind. Do not be deceived by offers that sound spiritual but exclude part of your family.

### Step 2: Refuse Any Offer That Separates Your Family From Worship

Like Moses, who refused Pharaoh's offer to let only the men leave Egypt, you must refuse anything that divides your household from Christ. If an opportunity, relationship, or system requires you to leave your spouse, children, or family behind, it is not from God.

### Step 3: Rise as the Spiritual Authority in Your Home

Fathers and mothers are the spiritual leaders of their homes. You are responsible before God for what happens in your home. Believe and honor the gospel in every area of your life, speak words that honor Christ, lead your family in prayer and Bible study, go to church as a family, and serve your community together.

### Step 4: Bring Your Children to Church and Get Them Connected

Do not leave your children at home while you attend church alone. Bring them with you and get them involved in children's ministry, youth ministry, and the life of the church. This plants a seed of faith that will produce fruit in their lives.

### Step 5: Plant God's Word in Your Children Early

  Start teaching your children the Word of God while they are children. Pray with them, read the Bible with them, and take them to church while they are children.

### Step 6: Pray Without Ceasing for Wayward Children

If your son or daughter has drifted from the Lord, do not give up. Continue to pray, declare God's Word over their life, and trust that the seed you planted will prosper in God's time, even if it takes time, trials, or difficult circumstances before they open their hearts to the Lord again.

### Step 7: Refuse to Marry Outside of God's Will

If you are single, do not yield to pressure from family, friends, or culture to marry someone who does not follow Christ. Marrying someone who does not follow Christ is marrying outside of God's design for marriage, and that is how Satan brings division into the family unit and causes you to give up your freedom in Christ.

### Step 8: Work Hard at Unity in Your Marriage

  Marriage is a gift from God, but it calls for action. Forgive, serve your spouse, control your speech, and put Christ at the center of your home. Unity in marriage brings freedom; division brings bondage.

### Step 9: Reject Division in the Church

  Do not accept lies that separate generations, genders, or people based on nationality, language, or skin color in the body of Christ. There is no retirement from serving Jesus. The older and younger generations must worship and serve together. Gender must not separate anyone from serving in the body of Christ.

### Step 10: Stand Together as a Family Unit

Make an agreement as a family: you will go to church together, pray together, study the Bible together, and serve your community together. Declare with your household: "Me and my house will serve the Lord."

## Examples

### Example 1: A Family Choosing Church Together

  A father who previously attended church alone decides to bring his wife and children every Sunday. He leads family devotions and prays with his children. As they continue attending church, his children choose to follow Christ, and his marriage grows stronger because they walk together in faith.

### Example 2: A Single Person Waiting for God's Will in Marriage

 A single Christian woman faces pressure from family and friends to marry someone who does not follow Christ. She chooses to honor God's Word instead, praying for a husband who loves Christ. She meets a man who follows Christ, and their marriage becomes a testimony of unity and freedom rather than division and compromise.

### Example 3: Parents Praying for a Wayward Teenager

  A mother's teenage son has left the church and rejected the faith she taught him. Instead of giving up, she continues to pray, speak God's Word over him, and model Christ's love. After continuing to pray, during which he goes through trials and difficult circumstances, her son chooses to return to the Lord, fulfilling the promise of Isaiah 55:11 that God's Word always produces fruit.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Bring your whole family to church—do not leave anyone behind
- ✅ Be the spiritual leader your home needs by praying and studying the Word with your family
- ✅ Plant seeds of God's Word in your children's hearts while they are children
- ✅ Pray without ceasing for family members who have strayed
- ✅ Refuse any relationship or marriage that is not centered on Christ
- ✅ Forgive and speak words that honor Christ in your marriage
- ✅ Serve together as a family in your church and community
- ✅ Reject generational division, gender-based division, and division based on nationality, language, or skin color in the body of Christ
- ❌ Do not accept offers that separate you from your family in worship
- ❌ Do not leave your children at home while you attend church alone
- ❌ Do not marry outside God's design for marriage to escape loneliness
- ❌ Do not give up on praying for your children or your marriage
- ❌ Do not allow bitterness, anger, or frustration to divide your home
- ❌ Do not accept the lie from the enemy that older believers should retire from serving God

## Keep In Mind

- According to the sermon, the enemy uses division as a tactic—he attacks marriages, families, and churches because division keeps you from walking in the authority God gives believers
- Freedom in Christ includes the freedom of your entire household
- The sermon teaches that the greatest thing a father or mother can leave their children is a relationship with Jesus Christ, not a bank account, a degree, or education alone, because those things eventually fade away
- Isaiah 55:11 promises that God's Word will accomplish all He sends it to do and will prosper every word He sends—it will accomplish what God intends in your family's lives, even when the results are not immediate
- You are a spiritual parent to the children and teenagers in your church and community, whether or not you have biological children
- Unity in the family and church is not automatic; it calls for action: thinking about others, being willing to serve others, being patient and merciful with others, forgiving when offended, asking for forgiveness when you offend, and working hard at all relationships in your life

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share personal family struggles publicly in ways that could identify your children or compromise their safety
- When praying for your family online or in public, avoid disclosing sensitive details that could compromise their safety
- Guard your children's identities and privacy when posting about them on social media or church platforms

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Leaving children at home while attending church alone, believing they will attend later
  **Solution:** Bring them with you now. Plant the seed of God's Word in their hearts while they are children, and your presence teaches them that worship is a family priority.
- **Problem:** Giving up on a wayward child because time has passed without visible change
  **Solution:** Continue praying and speaking God's Word. Isaiah 55:11 says God's Word will accomplish all He sends it to do and prosper every word He sends—it will produce fruit in God's time, not yours, even if it takes time, trials, or difficult circumstances.
- **Problem:** Marrying someone who does not follow Christ out of loneliness or desperation
  **Solution:** Honor God's Word and do not yield to pressure to marry someone who does not follow Christ or someone not serving God or not someone who follows Christ. Do not allow loneliness, desperation, fear, or similar emotional pressure to lead you outside of God's design for marriage.
- **Problem:** Believing that only men should lead or serve in the church and family
  **Solution:** This is a lie from the enemy. The Bible teaches that God has called men and women, old and young, to worship and serve together in unity.
- **Problem:** Allowing the idea that older believers should stop serving in the church, believing older believers no longer have a role
  **Solution:** The Bible teaches that there is no retirement from serving in the kingdom of heaven. Older generations are called to mentor and walk with younger generations, and both are called to worship together.
- **Problem:** Accepting the world's standards for marriage and family instead of God's design
  **Solution:** Make Jesus the center of your home and let the Word of God be your authority. Marriage and family work when they are done according to God's design for marriage and family as taught in the Bible: when Jesus is the center of the home and the family, and when the Word of God is the authority in the marriage.
