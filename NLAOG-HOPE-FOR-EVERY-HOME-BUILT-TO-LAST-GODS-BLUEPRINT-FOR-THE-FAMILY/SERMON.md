# God's Blueprint for the Family

## Overview

This sermon teaches God's original design for marriage and family based on Matthew 19, Ephesians 5, and Genesis 2. It explains that a marriage built on covenant commitment — a daily choice to serve and prioritize your spouse through prayer, patience, self-sacrifice, and perseverance, rooted in commitment to God rather than personal comfort — remains united through hardship without separation or divorce. It presents three foundational principles: when a married couple follows God's design for marriage, their home has a lasting future; when a married couple rejects God's design for marriage, their marriage becomes vulnerable to separation or divorce; and when a marriage damaged by sin or broken trust is surrendered to God for healing, God can restore it. Regardless of whether you are married, single, divorced, or navigating pain or loss, Jesus offers hope and a divine blueprint for every family that follows God's design.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are experiencing conflict marked by hostility or withdrawal occurring at least once per week for three or more consecutive months, or family crisis involving addiction, abuse, or destructive conflict, or a state in which no constructive conversation occurs between partners, and you desire to restore your marriage
- When you want to build a home where Christ is the central influence in daily decisions and relationships and that home can withstand life's hardships and challenges
- When you or a spouse, child, or parent is navigating pain, loss, or a relationship that has experienced separation, abandonment, or estrangement and you need to know that God can heal and restore broken relationships

## Steps

### Step 1: Build Your Home on God's Word

Begin each week on Sunday with 10 to 15 minutes of shared Scripture reading and prayer as a family when all family members are present. Read a passage of scripture together, discuss how it applies to each family member present, and pray together. Make Sunday worship a non-negotiable priority for your family. Invite Jesus to be the guiding influence in your home, not just a guest.

### Step 2: Protect Your Marriage Daily

A covenant marriage requires daily intentional choices. Be present and engaged with your spouse daily. Choose to act with love even during conflict or frustration. Choose forgiveness even during conflict or frustration. It is the daily intentional choices that demonstrate love which maintain and strengthen your marriage.

### Step 3: Soften Your Heart and Make the First Move

When shutting down during conflict in your relationship, examine your own heart before God. Selfish pride, unresolved bitterness, and stubborn refusal to listen damage and destroy relationships. Do not wait for the other person to take the first step toward reconciliation — you go and seek to restore the relationship except when abuse or safety concerns are present. Do not let ongoing disagreement or tension prevent you from participating in worship with a clear conscience before God — first go and seek to restore the relationship with the other person, then return to worship.

### Step 4: Trust God for Restoration

If your marriage has exhausted professional counseling and pastoral guidance and cannot be restored through human effort, and only God can bring healing, remember that God is able to restore your broken marriage. Bring your specific incidents of betrayal, neglect, or loss to God in prayer. Repent of your own sin and failures, ask for His healing, and surrender the outcome of the situation to Him. No past adultery, abandonment, abuse, or addiction disqualifies you from God's future when you place your faith in Jesus.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Couple Married 40+ Years

A couple in their 60s, whom we will call Robert and Mary, shared their testimony at a marriage retreat. By the 22nd year of their marriage they were prepared to file for divorce — he had lost his job, she battled depression, and they had stopped meaningful communication except during conflicts. But during a church service, they heard a message about covenant love and renewed their commitment to each other and to God through prayer and covenant. They prayed together each morning, attended counseling, and asked another couple to mentor them. They now serve as marriage coaches in their church giving hope to other couples facing serious marital difficulties.


### Example 2: The Counseling Story About Positive Texts

A pastor once counseled a couple in his church where the wife had repeated critical remarks toward her husband that was eroding her husband's self-confidence and their marriage. He recommended she find one positive aspect of her spouse each day and text it to him or leave him a note. But at their follow-up counseling session two weeks later, she admitted she had written ten or more grievances she felt he was doing wrong on a list and left the list on his car's steering wheel instead. This story illustrates how naturally we can fall into patterns of focusing on faults, and how deliberately we must choose to speak words that encourage and affirm our loved ones rather than criticize them.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Build your marriage and family on God's word, not on cultural trends that contradict biblical principles or personal comfort
- ✅ Show up every day with prayer, patience, self-sacrifice, and verbal affirmations, handwritten notes, or small gifts and weekly acts of service
- ❌ Don't treat marriage as a contract you can walk away from when the marriage faces conflict marked by hostility or withdrawal lasting three or more consecutive months, or when the marriage involves withholding affection and communication
- ❌ Don't let working hours that prevent family meals or quality time three or more days per week, children's schedules that prevent family meals or quality time three or more days per week, or career advancement take priority over your relationship with Christ and each other

## Keep In Mind

- Your past adultery, addiction, abuse, or neglect do not prevent God from working in your life — God can rebuild relationships that have been broken when you surrender to Him
- A house built on God's word can withstand seasons of severe marital tension, ongoing family conflict, or significant personal suffering; what sustains you is 10–15 minutes each day in personal prayer, humble obedience to God's word, and daily acts of love and forgiveness

## Security & Safety Notes

- Hard-heartedness and pride are the greatest dangers to a marriage or family, can be more damaging than financial or external pressures — allow God to examine and change your heart before the relationship damage deepens.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** When persistent conflict involving emotional withdrawal or contempt arises, or when infidelity occurs, or when withholding affection and emotional connection for three or more consecutive weeks arises, seeking an exit from the marriage through divorce or emotional withdrawal, neglect, or desertion instead of seeking restoration through God's blueprint for marriage
   **Solution:** Instead of asking "How can I end this marriage?" ask "Lord, how can you restore my marriage? How can I become more Christlike through this experience?" Repent of stubbornness and refusal to forgive, take the first step toward reconciliation, and ground your relationship in God's word.
