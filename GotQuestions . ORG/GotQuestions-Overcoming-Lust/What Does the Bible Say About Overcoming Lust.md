# What Does the Bible Say About Overcoming Lust?

## Overview

Based on GotQuestions.org's biblical teaching, this sermon explains how James 1:14-15 teaches that sinful lust progresses from temptation to sin and death, and lists five practical steps Christians can take to resist and conquer lustful desires through Christ. It teaches that while temptation itself is not sin, we must actively reject evil desire, flee temptation immediately, and surrender our hearts to God's glory to overcome lust.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When struggling with lustful thoughts or sexual temptation
- When you need to understand the biblical progression of sin from temptation to death
- When seeking practical, scriptural steps for maintaining sexual purity
- When learning how to surrender your desires to Christ and take every thought captive

## Steps

### Step 1: Reject Evil Desire Immediately

When evil desire introduces itself, you have a choice. Reject it immediately and refocus on the path God has set before you, following Jesus' example of resisting temptation with Scripture. Temptations may come, but you do not have to dwell on them or let them take root in your heart. This means you do not have to dwell on tempting thoughts or let them grow into sinful desire in your heart.

### Step 2: Flee Temptation Without Hesitation

Second Timothy 2:22 says, "Flee youthful lusts." To "flee" means to run from the situation immediately, just as Joseph fled from Potiphar's wife. Do not stick around to consider your options. When you encounter temptation, immediately reject it and physically remove yourself from the situation, as Joseph did when tempted by Potiphar's wife.

### Step 3: Do Not Make Provision for the Flesh

Hesitation gives the tempting thought a foothold in your mind. Romans 13:14 calls hesitation "making provision for the flesh—the fallen, sinful nature within every person that desires what opposes God (Galatians 5:17)." When we hesitate, we give the flesh—the fallen, sinful nature within every person that desires what opposes God (Galatians 5:17)—the opportunity to choose evil. Do not entertain tempting thoughts, savor fantasies, or imagine sinful scenarios, believing they will delight and fulfill you.

### Step 4: Take Every Thought Captive to Christ

Allow the Holy Spirit to keep your thoughts where He wants them to be. Second Corinthians 10:5 says, "Take every thought captive to the obedience of Christ." Intercept lust at the very first tempting thought before it grows into a strong desire that is difficult to resist.

### Step 5: Surrender to the Lord and Pray Daily

Surrender your heart to the glory of God so your deepest needs for love, acceptance, and significance are satisfied in your relationship with Him (Psalm 42:1-2) rather than through sinful desire. Pray daily the words of Psalm 19:14: "Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in your sight, O LORD, my rock and my redeemer." When your heart's desire is to please God more than yourself, you can keep lust from gaining control over you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Joseph's Resistance to Temptation

Joseph immediately fled from sexual temptation when Potiphar's wife attempted to seduce him, demonstrating that the proper response to lust is instant rejection and physical removal from the situation (Genesis 39:11-12). He did not hesitate or consider his options.

### Example 2: Samson's Defeat by Lust

Samson was a physically strong man, yet he was no match for his own lust (Judges 16:1). Despite his strength, Samson fell into sin and lost his strength when he hesitated to flee from Delilah's manipulations, showing that even strong people are vulnerable when they entertain evil desire.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Reject evil desire immediately and refocus on God's path when temptation arises
- ✅ Flee temptation immediately without hesitation or consideration of options
- ✅ Take every thought captive to the obedience of Christ through the Holy Spirit
- ✅ Pray daily for God to accept your thoughts and meditations
- ✅ Surrender your heart to the glory of God so your needs are met in Him
- ❌ Do not entertain or dwell on tempting thoughts or sinful fantasies
- ❌ Do not hesitate when faced with temptation
- ❌ Do not believe that sinful desire will delight or fulfill you
- ❌ Do not think that God has not provided all you need for true fulfillment and joy.

## Keep In Mind

- Being tempted is not sin; the sin begins when you choose to entertain evil desire
- Lust begins as a tempting thought that can grow into a strong desire if you do not resist it and remove it from your mind.
- The downward progression described in James 1:14-15 is: temptation → desire conceives → sin → spiritual death—the eternal separation from God that results from unrepented sin (Romans 6:23).
- Your heart is God's domain; allowing evil to grow there defiles the believer's body, which is the temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 3:16; 6:19)
- Jesus made it clear that lust is sin even if you do not physically act on it (Matthew 5:27-28)

## Security & Safety Notes

- Sexual temptation can lead to sin that defiles the body as the temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19)
- Lustful thoughts are sin in themselves, even without physical action (Matthew 5:27-28)
- Making provision for the flesh (the fallen, sinful nature within every person that opposes God's will, Galatians 5:17) through hesitation opens the door to destructive sin
- The tenth commandment forbids coveting, which means lusting for something that is not yours (Deuteronomy 5:21; Romans 13:9)

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Hesitating when temptation arises, which "makes provision for the flesh"—giving sinful thoughts room to grow in the fallen, sinful nature within you that opposes God's will (Galatians 5:17)
  **Solution:** Flee immediately without considering your options, as Joseph did
- **Problem:** Allowing evil desires to germinate and grow in your heart
  **Solution:** Take every thought captive to the obedience of Christ and reject lust at its earliest seed
- **Problem:** Believing sinful desire will fulfill a need that God has not met
  **Solution:** Surrender to the Lord and find your needs met in a relationship with Him
- **Problem:** Thinking you are helpless against temptation
  **Solution:** Remember that you are not helpless; you can choose to give in or to resist, and the Holy Spirit enables you to overcome (1 Corinthians 10:13)
