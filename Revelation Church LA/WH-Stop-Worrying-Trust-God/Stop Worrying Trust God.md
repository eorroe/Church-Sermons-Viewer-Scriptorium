# Stop Worrying and Trust God Will Handle What You Can't See

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers to stop worrying and instead trust God to handle what they cannot see. It explains that laughter is a powerful spiritual weapon against the enemy, joy is an internal state based on God's revelation rather than external circumstances, and that every action has spiritual consequences. The message encourages consistent faith, prophetic living, and not waiting for human approval.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are struggling with worry and anxiety
- When facing attacks from the enemy
- When you need to understand the power of laughter as a spiritual weapon
- When you feel like giving up
- When you are seeking joy that is not dependent on circumstances

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand Laughter as a Spiritual Weapon

Laughter is not just a reaction to humor; it is a prophetic statement that disrupts what the devil plans against you. When God laughs in the Bible, it is always because He is giving someone victory. Laughter is a weapon of warfare.

### Step 2: Stop Worrying and Trust God

Your problem when the devil attacks is that you pray instead of laughing. Stop taking too long to laugh because laughter is a prophetic sign. When the enemy rises against you, God laughs and their plans break apart.

### Step 3: Choose Joy Over Happiness

Happiness is external and never satisfied - you will always want the next thing. Joy is internal and comes from the revelation of what God has already established. The joy of the Lord is your strength because anyone operating in joy has already seen the future.

### Step 4: Be Consistent (Meek)

Meekness does not mean being humble; it means being consistent. The consistent shall inherit the earth. Everything everyone else is working for is actually yours because the wealth of the wicked is stored up for the righteous.

### Step 5: Don't Wait for People's Approval

When God reveals something to you, He reveals it to you, not to everybody. Don't wait for people to applaud you to receive courage, joy, or to laugh. God told you directly - it is between you and God.

### Step 6: React Spiritually to Attacks

Your reaction should contradict what the devil is throwing at you. When the devil provokes you to react physically, just laugh. A merry heart does better than medicine. Laughing in God can even bring physical healing.

## Examples

### Example 1: Sarah's Laughter

When God told Abraham that Sarah would be pregnant, Sarah laughed. She laughed prophetically before it happened. She was already laughing during her pregnancy because she understood God's promise. Don't prolong your battles by taking too long to laugh.

### Example 2: Paul's Reaction to the Snake

When a snake bit Paul, he treated it as insignificant. The people waited to see if he would swell up or die, but he was unharmed. His consistent reaction of faith meant the snake's bite had no effect on him.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Choose to laugh when the devil attacks
- ✅ Operate in joy based on God's revelation, not circumstances
- ✅ Be consistent in your faith
- ✅ Don't wait for human approval
- ✅ React spiritually rather than physically to attacks
- ✅ Remember that every act has spiritual consequences
- ❌ Don't cry like the world cries
- ❌ Don't pursue happiness as an external thing
- ❌ Don't wait for people to affirm what God told you

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon is for spiritual growth and encouragement.
- Apply biblical principles with discernment and pastoral guidance.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting for people's approval before you act on God's revelation
  **Solution:** Remember that God reveals things to you directly - don't wait for others to affirm what God has told you.
- **Problem:** Pursuing happiness through external things
  **Solution:** Understand that joy is internal and comes from revelation of what God has already established.
- **Problem:** Reacting physically when the devil attacks
  **Solution:** Learn to laugh at the enemy's attacks - a merry heart does better than medicine.
