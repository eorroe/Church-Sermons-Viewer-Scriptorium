# The Deception of Procrastination

## Overview

This sermon teaches that procrastination is a spiritual deception that causes believers to delay specific, Spirit-directed acts of obedience God has clearly called them to perform. Joyce Meyer explains that good intentions are not the same as obedience, and when God calls you to act, there is an anointing on that specific timing. Delaying God's instructions leads to missed opportunities, harder circumstances, and spiritual stagnation.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are delaying a specific decision, action, or habit God has placed on your heart
- When you've been putting off surrendering fully to Christ
- When you're struggling with unforgiveness toward others
- When you're waiting for perfect conditions before obeying God
- When you feel nagging conviction about a specific decision, action, or habit God has called you to begin or end

## Steps

1. Recognize procrastination as deception - understand that saying "I'll do it later" when God says "now" is disobedience
2. Respond promptly to unmistakable Holy Spirit convictions that align with Scripture and godly counsel; recognize that God's appointed time for action is His specific will for that moment, not your preferred convenience
3. Stop making excuses - like the man at the Pool of Bethesda who waited 38 years making excuses
4. Take initiative like Zaccheus - climb the tree to see Jesus despite obstacles
5. Turn obstacles into opportunities like the donkey in the well - shake off the dirt and use it to climb out
6. Forgive quickly - don't hold unforgiveness which shuts down your prayer life
7. Have faith now - don't delay trusting God for another day

## Examples

### Example 1: The Man at the Pool of Bethesda (John 5)

A man who had been lying by the Pool of Bethesda for 38 years waiting for someone to put him in the water when it was stirred. He made excuses about why he couldn't get healed himself, but Jesus asked him "Do you really want to get well?" This illustrates how we can make excuses for our circumstances instead of taking personal responsibility and initiative.

### Example 2: Zaccheus (Luke 19)

A short tax collector who wanted to see Jesus. Instead of making excuses about his height, he climbed a tree to get a better view. Jesus saw his determination and invited Himself to Zaccheus's house. This shows the power of taking initiative and not letting obstacles stop you from seeking God.

### Example 3: The Donkey in the Well

A farmer's donkey fell into a well. The farmer decided it wasn't worth saving and began shoveling dirt in to bury the donkey. Instead of giving up, the donkey shook off each shovel of dirt and used it to climb higher until he walked out of the well. This illustrates how obstacles in life can actually become stepping stones if we refuse to stay stuck.

## Best Practices

- Respond promptly to unmistakable Holy Spirit convictions that align with Scripture and godly counsel
- Don't wait for perfect conditions before obeying
- Don't make excuses for problems you can address yourself
- Forgive quickly to keep your spiritual life open
- Cast your cares on God and enter His rest
- Take every opportunity to bless others
- Be decisive - today is the day for salvation and obedience

## Keep In Mind

- God's appointed time for action is His specific will for that moment, not your preferred convenience
- Good intentions without action are not obedience
- The longer you put off a decision, the harder it gets
- Faith must be exercised now, not tomorrow
- Every act of obedience is a seed that will produce a harvest
- God will find someone else to do His work if you refuse the opportunity

## Security & Safety Notes

- Delaying spiritual decisions can have eternal consequences (missing salvation)
- Unforgiveness poisons your spirit and blocks your prayers
- Procrastinating on medical treatment, routine check-ups, or necessary lifestyle changes can lead to life-threatening consequences
- Remaining in abusive, unrepentant, or clearly toxic relationships because you refuse to confront or leave is spiritually dangerous

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking good intentions equal obedience
  **Solution:** Act immediately on what God tells you to do; intentions without action are meaningless
- **Problem:** Waiting for perfect conditions before obeying
  **Solution:** Obey now; Ecclesiastes 11:4 says waiting for every condition to be favorable means you'll never act
- **Problem:** Making excuses like the man at Bethesda who waited 38 years
  **Solution:** Take personal responsibility and initiative; stop waiting for others to do what you can do
- **Problem:** Holding onto unforgiveness
  **Solution:** Forgive quickly to keep your spiritual life free and your prayers unblocked
- **Problem:** Attempting to accomplish every major task and carry every burden in your own strength instead of partnering with God
  **Solution:** Take the practical steps within your control, and release the outcomes beyond your control to Go
