# Battlefield of the Mind

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the mind is the battlefield where you win or lose the spiritual war with Satan, and that persistent wrong thinking can keep you trapped in difficult seasons far longer than God intends. Joyce Meyer explains how the Israelites spent 40 years wandering in the wilderness on what should have been an 11-day journey from Egypt to Canaan because of ten wrong mindsets, including grumbling, complaining, fault-finding, and entitlement. She provides seven practical, Bible-based strategies for renewing the mind. The core message is that you can choose to interrupt wrong thoughts by speaking God's Word aloud, maintain a godly attitude during suffering, and learn to live by what you know is true in your heart rather than by surface-level feelings and doubts.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you find yourself stuck in a prolonged difficult season, repeating the same struggles, and wondering why you have not moved forward
- When you are going through unjust suffering, unfair treatment, or painful circumstances and need to maintain the right attitude
- When you are battling constant doubt, negative thoughts, or self-doubt that tempt you to give up on God's promises

## Steps

### Step 1: Interrupt Wrong Thoughts with God's Word

When Satan puts a wrong or condemning thought in your mind, do not keep it. Immediately open your mouth and say what the Bible says. For example, if the enemy tells you that you are worthless, say aloud: "God loves me, and I am the righteousness of God in Christ." Speaking truth interrupts the wrong thought and redirects your thinking back to what God says about you.

### Step 2: Keep a Book of Remembrance

Record the specific ways God has shown up for you in a dedicated journal or notebook. Write down answered prayers, unexpected blessings, and specific moments when God showed up for you. When you are having a difficult day, read through this book to remind yourself of God's faithfulness instead of focusing only on what you lack.

### Step 3: Choose to Be Thankful and Grateful

When you feel irritable, grumbly, or self-pitying, get a piece of paper and write down at least ten specific things you can thank God for right now. Refuse to focus so much on what you do not have that you lose sight of everything God has already provided. Make a conscious decision to think about the goodness of God and recall His specific blessings.

### Step 4: Maintain a Godly Attitude During Suffering

Understand that your suffering does not glorify God by itself, but a godly attitude in the middle of suffering does. Decide that no matter what you are going through personally, you will continue to do what is right. Your primary responsibility is to keep doing the right thing no matter what anybody else does to you or for you.

### Step 5: Go Deeper Than Your Thoughts and Feelings

When doubt attacks your mind, take a moment to pause and examine what you truly believe in your heart rather than accepting every thought as truth. Ask yourself, "Do I really believe that God is going to abandon me?" If you have had any genuine experience with God, you must admit that He has proven Himself faithful. Do not live by random thoughts or feelings; live by what you know is true in your heart.

### Step 6: Dare to Believe God for Big Things

Because of your faith in Jesus, you can approach God with boldness, courage, and confidence. Do not let your mistakes or past failures keep you from asking God for significant needs. Feed your faith by reading and speaking the promises of God, and do not be satisfied with weak faith when God wants you to trust Him for more.

### Step 7: Trust God Even When You Do Not Understand

You will never have all the answers, and God will not always explain why you are going through what you are going through. But you have a clear choice: trust God or live in misery. Decide to trust Him anyway because refusing to trust leads to inner turmoil and wasted time. Live life forward as it happens, but look back later to see how God was working everything out for your good.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Israelites in the Wilderness

The Israelites spent 40 years wandering in the wilderness on what should have been an 11-day journey from Egypt to Canaan because of ten wrong mindsets, primarily grumbling, fault-finding, and complaining. They complained about the miracle bread (manna) God provided, spoke against God and Moses, and were attacked by fiery serpents because of their incessant complaining. Their wrong thinking kept them out of the promised land even though God had already provided victory. This shows how dangerous complaining is and how it can prolong your wilderness season.

### Example 2: Abraham Hoping Against Hope

Abraham was about 100 years old and his wife Sarah was barren. Human reason for hope was completely gone, but he did not weaken in faith. He took a clear look at his circumstances, acknowledged the impossibility of his situation, and chose to believe that God was greater than his circumstances. He grew strong in faith as he gave praise and glory to God, and he became the father of many nations as promised. This demonstrates how to handle doubt by choosing to believe God's Word above your circumstances.

### Example 3: Joyce Meyer Giving Her Favorite Coat to the Woman Who Took Her Job

After a woman lied about Joyce and got her fired from her ministry position, God directed Joyce to give that same woman her favorite coat. It seemed unjust, and every time she saw the woman wearing it, she felt pain. But she obeyed God anyway, and that obedience made her more like Jesus than anything else she could have done. This illustrates maintaining a godly attitude during unjust suffering and doing the right thing even when it hurts.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Interrupt wrong thoughts immediately by speaking what the Bible says out loud
- ✅ Keep a book of remembrance and read it regularly, especially on difficult days
- ✅ Write down specific things you are thankful for when you feel grumpy or discouraged
- ✅ Choose to believe God's Word even when your circumstances say otherwise
- ✅ Go deeper than your feelings and ask yourself what you truly believe in your heart
- ✅ Feed your faith with the specific promises of God rather than focusing on your problems
- ❌ Do not keep wrong thoughts just because the enemy put them in your head
- ❌ Do not complain about your circumstances, especially about blessings God has provided
- ❌ Do not live by how you feel or by every random thought that rushes through your mind
- ❌ Do not pretend to have faith you do not have; be honest with God about your doubts

## Keep In Mind

- The mind is the battlefield where you win or lose the spiritual war with Satan; guard your thoughts carefully
- You only have one job: no matter what is going on around you, keep doing what is right according to God's Word
- God will never allow more to come on you than you can bear, and He always provides a way to endure it (1 Corinthians 10:13)
- Everything you go through here is temporary compared to eternity
- Suffering produces compassion; without it, you cannot truly minister to others who are hurting
- When you obey God in difficult situations, you grow in Christlikeness and increase your capacity to help others
- Fact comes first, then faith, then feelings - in that order always
- Jesus understands your suffering because He suffered unjustly too, yet He never sinned

## Security & Safety Notes

- Be careful when you think you are hearing from God; test every impression against Scripture because false impressions can lead to harmful actions (1 John 4:1)
- Do not ignore parts of the Bible that do not fit your current understanding; study the whole counsel of God rather than selecting only the parts you prefer
- Beware of complaining - it is a destructive habit that can open the door to further hardship, as it did for the Israelites who fell dead in the wilderness because of persistent grumbling (Numbers 16:41-49)
- Do not let self-doubt keep you from asking God for help; God meets you where you are without condemnation, fault-finding, or shaming you for your past

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Getting stuck in a wilderness season for years because of wrong mindsets like grumbling and complaining.
  **Solution:** Recognize that complaining and fault-finding are wrong mindsets that keep you from the good plans God has for you. Choose the right mindset by purposely focusing on what you have, keeping a book of remembrance, and in everything you do without grumbling.

- **Problem:** Living by feelings and surface-level thoughts instead of going deeper, which leads to giving up when circumstances seem impossible.
  **Solution:** When doubt attacks, pause and ask yourself what you truly believe in your heart. Remember your past experiences with God, feed your faith with His specific promises, and choose to believe God's Word above your current circumstances. Do not bow down to how you feel.

- **Problem:** Thinking you must have perfect, doubt-free faith before God can use you or before you can trust Him.
  **Solution:** Be honest with God about your doubts. God will meet you where you are, just as He met Thomas after the resurrection. The more experience you have with God, the stronger your faith will become. Dare to believe God even with imperfect faith, and know that God can use you even when you doubt.

- **Problem:** Having an entitlement mindset that demands immediate results and refuses to wait on God's timing.
  **Solution:** Understand that patience is not just waiting; it is choosing the right attitude and actions while you wait. Your responsibility while waiting is to keep doing what is right. Believe that God's timing is perfect, and that when it is the right time, nobody can stop Him from fulfilling His promises.
