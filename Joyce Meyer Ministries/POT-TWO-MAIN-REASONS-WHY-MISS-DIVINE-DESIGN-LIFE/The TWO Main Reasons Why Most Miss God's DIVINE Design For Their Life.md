# The TWO Main Reasons Why Most Miss God's DIVINE Design For Their Life

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the two main reasons people miss God's divine design for their lives are people-pleasing behavior driven by fear of rejection, and a failure to love and accept oneself as God's unique creation. Joyce Meyer explains that God created each person intentionally and uniquely, and when we try to be like someone else or constantly seek approval from others, we cannot fulfill the specific purpose God has for us. The message emphasizes learning to separate your identity ("who you are") from your actions ("what you do"), pursuing holiness, and using your faith to be free from guilt and condemnation so you can give to others what God has given you.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you find yourself constantly seeking approval from others at the expense of following God's will
- When you struggle with self-hatred, guilt, or condemnation that prevents you from embracing who God created you to be
- When you are making decisions based on what people will think rather than what you know God wants you to do

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the insult to your creator

Understand that not liking yourself is an insult to God. He created each one of us uniquely and intentionally, taking time to knit us together in our mother's womb. The Amplified Bible says God created us "carefully and intricately." God knows exactly what He is doing, and He does things on purpose. Disliking the person God created you to be shows a lack of respect for His creative work.

### Step 2: Stop trying to be like somebody else

We cannot make use of the uniqueness that God has given us if we keep trying to be like somebody else. This applies to men as much as women. Whether it's complaining about your voice, your appearance, or your talents, trying to mimic others prevents you from using the unique gifts God specifically gave you.

### Step 3: Separate your "who" from your "do"

Learn the difference between who you are and what you do. You may not like everything you do, but you can still love yourself. God doesn't like everything we do, but He loves us. We are holy and sanctified positionally through Christ, even when we fail. This distinction frees you from guilt and condemnation.

### Step 4: Stop people-pleasing out of fear

Identify when you are doing things to keep people from getting mad at you or to avoid rejection. This fear of man leads to inauthentic behavior where you complain while doing things you don't want to do. When you people-please, your works are not pure, and while you may be saved, you will lose rewards in heaven.

### Step 5: Pray instead of manipulate

When you want something, such as a promotion or a position at church, pray and ask God to make it happen rather than pretending, manipulating, and being phony to win people over. If you buy friendships by doing everything others want, you will never be free to follow the Holy Spirit's leadership.

### Step 6: Pursue holiness and work out your salvation

Use your faith to be free from guilt and condemnation. Pursue holiness without which no man will see the Lord. Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, not in your own strength but with God helping you. Water the seeds of God's nature in your spirit with His word so they manifest in your life.

### Step 7: Give away what you have received

God gives you forgiveness so you can give forgiveness to others. He gives you mercy so you can be merciful. He loves you so you can love others. But you cannot give away what you do not have. If you hate yourself, you cannot love everyone through that. Learn to receive God's love so you can overflow to others.

## Examples

### Example 1: Church attendance and job security

Joyce Meyer shares that when she first had an opportunity in public ministry, she worked at a church where the man in charge reminded her of her father - strong and intimidating. Because she so wanted to minister and didn't want to lose her job, she did many things she shouldn't have, such as being at church too much when she should have been home with her family. This demonstrates how fear of losing position causes people to compromise their priorities and authenticity.


### Example 2: Manipulating for church status

While Joyce and her husband Dave were still in the Lutheran church, there was a board of elders that held all the influence. Joyce wanted her husband to be an elder because those were the important people. She worked, manipulated, and pretended to get into a certain group of people, realizing that however you get your friends is how you have to keep them. As soon as she was filled with the Spirit and God called her to teach, those same people abandoned her because they wanted her to follow them, not God.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Recognize that God created you carefully and intricately for a unique purpose
- ✅ Separate your identity from your actions - who you are is different from what you do
- ✅ Pray and seek God's will rather than manipulating situations or people
- ✅ Pursue holiness and work out your salvation with God's help
- ✅ Receive God's love so you can give it to others
- ✅ Use faith to be free from guilt and condemnation
- ❌ Don't people-please to avoid rejection or gain approval from others
- ❌ Don't compare yourself to others or try to be like someone else
- ❌ Don't pretend or be phony to get ahead in business or church
- ❌ Don't compromise your morals for promotions or status
- ❌ Don't let fear of man control your decisions
- ❌ Don't hate yourself or focus only on your faults

## Keep In Mind

- Your "who" is different from your "do" - don't confuse your identity with your actions
- God knows exactly what He is doing and created you on purpose
- What others think of you is not as important as what you think of yourself
- The longer you let somebody control you, the harder it is to break that control
- You cannot give away what you do not have
- There are rewards in heaven for pure works done with the right motive
- Works done out of wrong motives will be burned up, though you yourself will be saved

## Security & Safety Notes

- Be cautious about letting anyone control you, as it becomes increasingly difficult to break free the longer it continues
- Watch for the subtle trap of people-pleasing that often begins with seemingly good motives but leads to inauthentic living
- Guard against compromising your morals in business or church settings to gain status or promotions
- Be aware that guilt and condemnation from past hurts (such as childhood abuse) can be used by the enemy to keep you from God's design

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Doing things for the wrong motives to keep people from getting mad or to avoid rejection
  **Solution:** Pause and pray before acting. Ask God if this is what He wants you to do, rather than automatically saying yes to people.
- **Problem:** Trying to be like somebody else and missing your unique God-given design
  **Solution:** Focus on your own gifts and calling. God created you intentionally, and comparing yourself to others steals your purpose.
- **Problem:** Manipulating situations and pretending to be someone you're not to gain friendships or status
  **Solution:** Be authentic. Let God open doors for you rather than forcing them open through manipulation.
- **Problem:** Hating yourself and focusing on faults, which prevents you from loving others
  **Solution:** See yourself in Christ. Understand that your "who" is different from your "do," and receive God's love for you.
- **Problem:** Doing spiritual exercises while living in compromise
  **Solution:** Get your life in order first. Don't fast for spiritual fruit while actively living in sin. Pursue holiness before pursuing spiritual disciplines.
