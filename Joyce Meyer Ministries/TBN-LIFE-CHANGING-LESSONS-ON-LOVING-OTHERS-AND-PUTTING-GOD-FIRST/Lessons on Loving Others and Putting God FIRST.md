# Joyce Meyer: LIFE-CHANGING Lessons on Loving Others and Putting God FIRST

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the Christian life is about putting God first and loving others genuinely, not about pursuing personal success, position, or recognition. Joyce Meyer explains that pride is the root of many spiritual problems and that true spirituality is measured by how we treat people who can do nothing for us. The message covers humility, contentment with your God-given gifts, radical generosity, forgiving those who have hurt you, and serving without expectation.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you struggle with pride, self-righteousness, or jealousy of others
- When you want to learn how to genuinely love difficult people
- When you feel called to serve but don't know where to start
- When you need to understand the difference between spiritual gifts and spiritual fruit
- When you want to overcome evil with good in your relationships
- When you need to forgive someone who has deeply hurt you

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand That All Gifts Come From Heaven

A man can receive nothing except it has been granted to him from heaven. Every ability you have is a gift from God. Be content with the specific gift you have been given, whether it is cleaning toilets or preaching to thousands. Never be jealous of another person's gift, because God gave them that gift for you to enjoy, not for you to envy. Even if others describe your gift as unusual or "flawed," God made you exactly as you are in your mother's womb, and you are not a mistake.

### Step 2: Prioritize Knowing God Over Doing for God

Paul's determined purpose was to know Christ, not to have a big position or prosperity. Move beyond merely knowing about Jesus to truly knowing Him personally. Count everything as loss compared to the priceless privilege of knowing Christ. For His sake, be willing to lose everything, because when you give your all to God, you will never lose out. Anything good you are able to do is only possible because of the goodness of God working through you.

### Step 3: Practice Humility Daily

Humble yourself under the mighty hand of God, and He will exalt you. If you refuse to humble yourself, God will arrange circumstances that will humble you, but it will be much more painful. Take the lowest place rather than seeking positions of honor. When you are invited to a wedding feast, do not take the place of honor, for a more distinguished person may have been invited. If you take the lowest place, the host will honor you in front of all the guests. Admit when you are wrong and apologize freely, even if the other person did not notice your mistake.

### Step 4: Love People Who Cannot Benefit You

Look for people who are alone, hurting, or downtrodden. Pay attention to people who nobody else notices, such as the person cleaning the bathroom in a department store. Bless those who cannot repay you. When your daughter forgot her credit card at the grocery store, the cashier paid for her groceries. Be prepared to bless someone every day. Real love is undeniable when the world sees it. If every Christian did one thing for one person every day, the world would change.

### Step 5: Forgive Those Who Have Hurt You

Forgiveness is for your freedom, not their benefit. Even if the person does not deserve your forgiveness, you deserve peace. When you forgive somebody, you set yourself free. Do good to your enemies to break the power of the devil. Overcome evil with good. If your enemy is hungry, give them food to eat. If they are thirsty, give them something to drink. Love is not a feeling; it is how you treat people.

### Step 6: Be a Radical, Generous Giver

Make helping the poor a priority in your life. A church cannot rightfully call itself a church if it is not helping the poor. Jesus said that if you have done it unto the least of these His brethren, you have done it unto Him. Give generously without keeping score or expecting anything in return. Find a single mom who has never had enough money to take her kids out to lunch and give her 50 dollars. Come to church to give to others, not just to get something in return.

### Step 7: Watch for the Fruits of Pride

Be aware of proud thoughts and behaviors. Do not interrupt people when they are talking just to share what you already know. Avoid comparing yourself to others or seeking special recognition. When you listen to a teaching, do not wish someone else were there to hear it instead of receiving it yourself. Be willing to look honestly at yourself and ask God to show you ways you exhibit pride. Humility is the hardest virtue to come by and the hardest one to maintain, and it requires regular prayer.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Grocery Store Blessing

Joyce Meyer's daughter forgot her credit card at the grocery store. The cashier spontaneously paid for her groceries. Joyce Meyer's daughter had been taught by her mother to be prepared to bless somebody every day, and she was ready. This demonstrates being prepared to do good without being asked and blessing someone who cannot repay you.

### Example 2: Caring for Abusive Parents

Joyce Meyer shares that God called her to bring her parents to St. Louis, buy them a nice house, and take care of them until they died. Her father had sexually abused her for about 15 years, and her mother knew but was too afraid to do anything. She did not want to do it, but she obeyed. She visited her mother in the nursing home every two weeks for 13 years, even when she did not want to go. This shows that love is how you treat people, not how you feel about them. You do not have to want to do what is right to do it.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Be content with your God-given gift and never be jealous of others' gifts
- ✅ Practice humility by doing small tasks you would rather not do
- ✅ Bless people who cannot repay you
- ✅ Cast your cares on God instead of worrying about fixing everything yourself
- ✅ Forgive others to set yourself free, even if they do not deserve it
- ✅ Give generously and randomly to those in need without keeping score
- ✅ Look for people who are lonely or downtrodden and pay attention to them
- ✅ Admit when you are wrong and apologize freely
- ✅ Remember that how you treat people is how you treat Jesus
- ✅ Do not interrupt people when they are sharing their thoughts with you

## Keep In Mind

- God loves you and wants to promote you, but you cannot maintain promotion if you get full of yourself
- God chose the weak and foolish people of the world to confound the wise so that no mortal man can glory in His sight
- You are exactly what God wants you to be, and nothing about you is a mistake
- If you exalt yourself, you will be humbled; if you humble yourself, you will be exalted
- The higher you go in success, the more danger you are in of pride
- Sometimes God allows trouble in your life to keep you from being excessively exalted
- If you refuse to humble yourself, God will do it for you, and it will be public and painful
- You do not have to feel like doing what is right to do it
- God knows the why behind the what when people act out of pain or brokenness

## Security & Safety Notes

- This sermon is for personal spiritual growth and should not replace professional counseling for trauma or abuse
- Be careful not to judge others' spiritual journeys based on outward behavior or appearance
- Remember that God knows the "why" behind the "what" when people act out of pain or brokenness
- Do not use the teaching on humility as an excuse to tolerate ongoing abuse or sin
- We are responsible to judge sin but not to judge people

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Thinking you are too important to do small tasks
   **Solution:** Intentionally do tasks you do not want to do to practice humility and remember you are not too important to serve
- **Problem:** Being jealous of others' gifts or success
  **Solution:** Remember that God gave them their gift for you to enjoy, not to be jealous of, and focus on developing your own gift
- **Problem:** Expecting repayment when you give
  **Solution:** Give freely without keeping score or looking for recognition, because real love is undeniable
- **Problem:** Thinking spirituality means avoiding certain people or situations
  **Solution:** Real spirituality is found in serving people who can do nothing for you, not in avoiding them
- **Problem:** Refusing to humble yourself until God forces it
  **Solution:** Humble yourself voluntarily in private rather than having God humble you publicly through painful circumstances
- **Problem:** Judging people instead of judging sin
  **Solution:** See people as God sees them - broken and in need of grace - while still recognizing that sin is wrong
