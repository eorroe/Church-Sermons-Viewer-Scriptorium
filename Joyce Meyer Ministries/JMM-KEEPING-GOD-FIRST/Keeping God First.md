# Keeping God First | Joyce Meyer | Enjoying Everyday Life Teaching

## Overview

This sermon teaches how to keep God first in every area of life by prioritizing His will over your own desires, schedules, and relationships while practicing gratitude, forgiveness, generosity, and boundary-setting. Joyce Meyer shares practical steps for breaking free from people-pleasing patterns through honest confession and saying no, and explains how praying "nevertheless, your will be done" aligns your heart with God's plans. You will learn that seeking God's kingdom first—through prayer, obedience, mercy, and believing before seeing—brings His provision and peace.

## When to Follow This Sermon


- When you feel too busy to spend time with God and sense He is not first in your life
- When you struggle with people-pleasing, manipulation, or controlling relationships
- When you are holding onto anger, bitterness, or unforgiveness toward someone
- When you want to make wise decisions but are torn between your own desires and God's will
- When you need to set boundaries and learn to say no without guilt
- When you desire to please God in your conduct, dress, and daily choices
- When you want to understand the biblical principle of sowing and reaping in your spiritual life
- When you are ready to surrender to Christ or recommit your life to Him

## Steps

### Step 1: Evaluate Your Priorities and Thank God Daily

Ask yourself honestly if God is truly first in your life. Make a habit of thanking God for your blessings, even small ones like being able to get out of bed, having clean water, or having people to eat with. Write down or speak three specific things you are grateful for each day to shift your focus from what you lack to what God has already provided.

### Step 2: Pray "Nevertheless, Your Will Be Done"

When you deeply desire something—such as marriage, a promotion, or a changed circumstance—pray honestly about it and then add "nevertheless, your will be done, not mine." Follow Jesus' example in the Garden of Gethsemane by submitting your desires to God's plan. Tell God you can be happy with just Him, and mean it, so that your joy is not dependent on getting what you want.

### Step 3: Seek God's Kingdom First (Matthew 6:33)

Make seeking God's kingdom and His righteousness your primary aim above career goals, relationships, or personal ambitions. Live to please the Lord in all decisions rather than living to please yourself or other people. Read Colossians 1:10 and ask God to guide your choices so they are fully pleasing to Him.

### Step 4: Set Boundaries and Learn to Say No

If you have allowed yourself to be manipulated or controlled by others, commit to breaking that pattern. When asked to do something that conflicts with God's will or your responsibilities, say no clearly and kindly. Explain your reasons without over-apologizing. Accept that some people may get mad or disappointed, but remember that true friends will respect your boundaries and that people-pleasing will prevent you from walking in God's will.

### Step 5: Get Out of a People-Pleasing Mess

If you are already trapped in commitments or relationships that came from people-pleasing, take these actions:
1. Pray and ask God what to do and how to get out of the situation.
2. Sit down with the person and confess that you have been insecure and have taken actions you did not feel right about because you wanted to maintain the relationship.
3. Tell the truth about your limits and the changes you need to make.
4. Expect that they may not like it, but remain firm. Truth is the only way to adjust anything and break free from manipulation and control.

### Step 6: Forgive Others and Let It Go

If you have anything against anyone—small or large, justified or not—forgive them and let it drop. Pray for the person who hurt you and put them in God's hands. Release the hatred, bitterness, and resentment so you can get on with your life. Remember that staying angry changes you and poisons your life; it does not change the other person. God will be your Vindicator if you let Him handle the justice.

### Step 7: Be Merciful Even When It Is Undeserved

Choose to show mercy to people who do not deserve it. Cut them a break, believe the best about them, and drop offenses instead of keeping score. Do not keep mentioning past wrongs after you have forgiven someone. Keep your mouth shut and do what is right without announcing your mercy to others. Mercy is kindness beyond what is reasonable, and when you give it, you open the door to receive mercy from God.

### Step 8: Examine Your Own Faults Before Criticizing Others

Do not ignore your own faults while pointing out the faults of others. First, remove the beam from your own eye so you can see clearly to take the speck out of your brother's eye (Matthew 7:5). Humble yourself under the mighty hand of God, and He will help you change. Only those who are perfected should criticize anyone else.

### Step 9: Pray First and Give First

Before you make plans, pray about everything and acknowledge God in all your ways so He can direct your path (Proverbs 3:6). Do not plan first and then ask God to make it work. Bring the whole tithe, the tenth of your income, into the storehouse first, and prove God by seeing if He will open the windows of heaven and pour out blessings that you cannot contain (Malachi 3:10). Give good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over, because the same measure you use will be dealt back to you (Luke 6:38).

### Step 10: Believe First and Then Receive

Choose to believe in your heart before you see the result. Keep speaking God's promises and His Word over your situation while you wait for the breakthrough, rather than confessing your circumstances or doubts. Do not wait until you can see the result to believe it, because in God's economy you must believe first and then you will see. Stand where you are, keep your head bowed, and trust God until the breakthrough comes suddenly.

### Step 11: Receive Jesus Christ as Your Savior

If you are not sure that you are right with God or know that you would go to heaven if you died today, surrender your life to Christ. Pray: "Father God, I believe in You. I know that I am a sinner and I deserve punishment, but I am sorry for my sins. I believe that You died for me on the cross, took all my sin upon Yourself, and are willing to forgive me. Jesus, I receive You today as my Savior and my Lord. I give myself to You. Please take me just the way I am and make me what You want me to be. I am now saved, born again, loved, forgiven, and on my way to heaven." If you prayed this prayer, contact Joyce Meyer Ministries to request a free book on how to begin your life with Christ.

## Examples

### Example 1: A Single Woman Praying for Marriage

A single woman who has never married deeply desires a husband. She brings her desire to God in prayer, honestly expressing how much she wants to be married. She then adds "nevertheless, I can be happy with just You, and Your will be done, Lord." She chooses to make God the center of her joy rather than basing her happiness on getting married. As a result, she experiences peace regardless of her relationship status, and if marriage is God's will for her, she will walk into it without anxiety or desperation.


### Example 2: Breaking Free from a Controlling Friendship

A person agrees to help a close friend move apartments even though they already have a full schedule and feel it is not wise. They say yes out of fear of losing the friendship or making the friend mad. Afterward, they feel overwhelmed and realize they have been manipulated. They pray and ask God what to do, then sit down with the friend and confess: "I have been insecure and have taken many actions you wanted me to do that I did not feel right about. God is dealing with me about this, and I have to make some changes." The friend may be disappointed, but if the friendship is healthy, it will respect the boundary. If not, the person is free to walk in God's will without guilt.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Thank God daily for both big and small blessings
- ✅ Pray "nevertheless, your will be done" when desires arise so your joy is not tied to outcomes
- ✅ Read and meditate on Scripture so you can renew your mind and think like God thinks
- ✅ Say no kindly but firmly when requests conflict with God's will or your responsibilities
- ✅ Forgive quickly, pray for your enemies, and let offenses drop
- ✅ Show mercy even when someone does not deserve it
- ✅ Examine your own heart and faults before criticizing or correcting others
- ✅ Pray about everything before making plans or decisions
- ✅ Tithe and give first, trusting God to provide
- ✅ Believe in your heart before you see the physical evidence
- ❌ Don't say yes to people just to avoid conflict or being disliked
- ❌ Don't hold onto anger, bitterness, or resentment, because it damages you more than the other person
- ❌ Don't announce your mercy or forgiveness to make yourself look good
- ❌ Don't plan first and then ask God to bless your plans
- ❌ Don't wait to believe until you can see the result with your natural eyes
- ❌ Don't criticize others while ignoring your own faults

## Keep In Mind

- If you do not have time for God, He is not first in your life.
- Anger itself is not sin, but staying angry and acting on it is sin.
- Love is a decision about how you will treat and talk about people, not a feeling.
- Unforgiveness does more damage to the body of Christ and to your own life than almost any other sin.
- People who get mad at you for setting boundaries probably do not care about you as much as you think.
- God is your Vindicator; you do not have to take matters of justice into your own hands.
- Mercy is given to people who do not deserve it—that is the very definition of mercy.
- You cannot walk in the will of God if you are not willing to occasionally make somebody mad.
- The measure you use when you deal out to others is the same measure that will be dealt back to you.
- You reap what you sow; a farmer who sows no seed has no right to be upset about having no harvest.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share deeply personal struggles or sins with people who are not spiritually mature or trustworthy; seek wise, godly counsel instead.
- If you are in an abusive or controlling relationship, prioritize your physical and emotional safety and seek help from authorities, pastors, or counselors before confronting the person alone.
- Guard your heart against people who consistently manipulate, guilt, or control you into taking actions that violate your conscience or God's will.
- Do not use grace or forgiveness as an excuse to remain in harmful situations; boundaries are biblical and necessary for healthy relationships.
- Protect your peace by limiting exposure to toxic conversations, social media comparisons, or environments that stir up anger and resentment.
- When forgiving someone who has seriously harmed you, it is okay to also establish distance or protection while still releasing them to God's justice.
- Do not base your identity or worth on other people's approval; your value comes from being a child of God.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You say yes to every request because you fear conflict or losing people's approval, and you end up overwhelmed and unable to fulfill your own God-given responsibilities.
  **Solution:** Practice saying no in low-stakes situations to build your confidence. Pray before responding to requests, and remember that God calls you to live to please Him, not people.

- **Problem:** You have been hurt by someone and justify staying angry because you feel your anger is righteous or deserved.
  **Solution:** Recognize that the feeling of anger is not sin, but staying angry and acting on it is sin. Forgive the person, pray for them, and let it go so you can be free and so God can forgive you as you forgive others.

- **Problem:** You criticize others while ignoring your own obvious faults and sins, which blinds you to what God needs to change in you.
  **Solution:** Humbly examine your own heart first and repent of your own sins before addressing anyone else's faults. Ask God to remove the beam from your own eye so you can see clearly to help others.

- **Problem:** You plan your own way and then ask God to bless it, or you wait to believe until you see the result with your natural eyes.
  **Solution:** Pray first and let God direct your path. Believe in your heart before you see, keep a good confession while waiting, and trust that God will bring His promises to pass in His timing.

- **Problem:** You give to others grudgingly or reluctantly, expecting specific repayment, and you become bitter when people do not meet your expectations.
  **Solution:** Give freely, cheerfully, and without keeping score. Remember that mercy and kindness are for people who do not deserve it, and God will deal with others according to His own justice.

- **Problem:** You have already said yes to too many commitments and feel trapped in obligations that came from people-pleasing, and you do not know how to get out.
  **Solution:** Pray and ask God for wisdom and courage, then sit down with those involved and honestly confess your mistake. Tell the truth about your limits and the changes you need to make, even if it is uncomfortable.
