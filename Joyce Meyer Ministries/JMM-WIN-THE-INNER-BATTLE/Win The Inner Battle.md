# Win The Inner Battle

## Overview

This sermon teaches that the most important part of a victorious walk with Christ is winning the battle of the mind. Joyce Meyer explains that our thoughts shape who we become, referencing Proverbs 23:7 and Philemon 1:16 to show that what we think and speak over ourselves has power. Through personal testimony and biblical examples, she reveals that negative thinking opens doors to defeat and torment, while renewing the mind through God's Word and speaking life-filled confessions over yourself leads to complete victory and transformation in every area of life.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are struggling with negative thoughts or self-criticism
- When you want to overcome fear, doubt, or feelings of condemnation
- When you are ready to renew your mind and align your words with God's Word

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify and Stop Negative Self-Talk

Pay attention to the words you speak about yourself and your circumstances. Recognize when you are saying things like "I'm stupid," "I'm no good," or "I can't do this." The devil uses negative words to torment you and reinforce lies. Stop agreeing with negative thoughts and refuse to speak them over yourself.

### Step 2: Speak God's Word Over Your Life

Replace negative thoughts and words with the truth of God's Word. Confess scriptures out loud such as "I am the righteousness of God in Christ," "I am a new creature in Christ," and "I can do everything through Christ who strengthens me." The more you speak God's Word, the more powerful your faith becomes, and angels hearken to the Word of God to help you.

### Step 3: Get Into Agreement With God

Align your thoughts and words with what God says about you. When you acknowledge every good thing in you in Christ, your faith becomes effective. Surround yourself with positive, faith-filled people who encourage you, and distance yourself from those who speak doubt and unbelief over your life.

### Step 4: Trust God With Your Circumstances

Look your circumstances in the face and say that they are not greater than your God. Like Abraham, grow strong in faith as you give praise and glory to God ahead of time. Fan the flame of faith inside you, remember that God is the author and finisher of your faith, and believe that all things are possible with Him.

## Examples

### Example 1: Joyce Meyer's Personal Battle

Joyce Meyer shares that 45 years ago, she was sitting home alone after an argument with her husband, speaking negatively about herself out loud. She felt a demonic presence come into the room. When she began confessing "I am the righteousness of God in Christ" repeatedly, the spirit backed out of the room and left. This shows the tangible power of speaking God's Word over negative thoughts and how negative self-talk can open doors to spiritual attack.

### Example 2: The Woman With the Issue of Blood

In Mark 5, a woman who had been bleeding for 12 years touched the hem of Jesus' garment and was healed. She kept saying to herself, "If I can only touch the hem of his garment, I know I shall be healed." Her faith was stirred by what she said to herself. When Jairus' daughter died while Jesus was helping this woman, Jesus told Jairus not to fear, but only believe. This demonstrates that your words affect your faith, and you must keep believing even when circumstances look impossible.

### Example 3: Overcoming Fear and Doubt

Joyce Meyer explains that doubt is like baby fear - it is the beginning of tormenting fear if left unchecked. When doubt comes, recognize that the enemy is trying to steal your faith. Fan the flame of your faith by speaking God's Word and praising Him ahead of time for the answer. Surround yourself with people of faith who will encourage you, and remove negative influences from your circle.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Speak life-filled confessions and scriptures out loud every day
- ✅ Renew your mind by replacing negative thoughts with God's Word
- ✅ Surround yourself with positive, faith-filled people
- ✅ Acknowledge every good thing in you in Christ Jesus
- ✅ Stop doubting yourself and trust that God believes in you

## Keep In Mind

- Proverbs 23:7 says, "As a man thinks in his heart, so is he" - your thoughts shape your reality.
- Philemon 1:16 says your faith becomes effective by acknowledging every good thing in you in Christ Jesus.
- You have the mind of Christ (1 Corinthians 2:16), and you can do everything through Christ who strengthens you.
- Faith is the title deed to what you hope for but have not seen yet.
- The devil is against you, but you do not need to agree with him - get into agreement with God instead.
- You are not a surprise to God; He knew everything about you before you were born and loves you unconditionally.

## Security & Safety Notes

- The content reflects Joyce Meyer's personal interpretation and teaching style, which may differ from other Christian traditions or denominations.
- If you are experiencing severe mental health challenges, seek professional help alongside your faith practice.
- Do not use negative self-talk or self-criticism as a form of motivation or accountability.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Continuing to speak negatively about yourself, which opens doors to demonic torment and reinforces lies.
  **Solution:** Start speaking God's Word out loud and refuse to agree with negative thoughts. Use scriptures like "I am the righteousness of God in Christ" to counter lies.

- **Problem:** Surrounding yourself with negative, doubtful people who steal your faith and peace.
  **Solution:** Get rid of negative influences and surround yourself with people of faith who encourage you and believe God with you. Like Jesus, remove doubt and unbelief from your immediate circle when facing desperate situations.

- **Problem:** Allowing fear and doubt to control your decisions and prevent you from stepping out in faith.
  **Solution:** Recognize that doubt is the beginning of fear. When doubt comes, fan your faith by speaking God's Word and praising Him ahead of time. Look your circumstances in the face and say they are not greater than your God.
