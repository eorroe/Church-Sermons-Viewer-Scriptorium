# Suffering A Faith Failure

## Overview

This sermon teaches about the danger of suffering a faith failure, using the graphic example of the Israelites in Numbers 13-14 who refused to enter the Promised Land due to fear, unbelief, and rebellion against God. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that trusting God is the foundational step in every situation, and that rebellion against God's will brings severe, unavoidable consequences. The sermon exists to warn believers about the cost of disobedience and to show how to overcome faith failures through trust, obedience, and remembering God's past faithfulness.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing a challenge or trial that triggers fear and doubt
- When you've been trusting God but feel like giving up
- When you're at the brink of a blessing but afraid to move forward
- When you've been forgetting God's past faithfulness in your life
- When you're struggling with rebellion against God's will
- When you need encouragement to trust God despite unclear circumstances

## Steps

### Step 1: Trust God as the First and Foundational Step

When confronted with any situation or circumstance, the first step is to trust God. Dr. Stanley emphasizes that God's answer to our problems is often simply "Trust Me." This is not the final step, but it is the foundation of everything else He will say thereafter. When we trust God, we honor Him by acknowledging who He is, recognizing our need, and having absolute confidence that He loves us enough to help us in every situation.

### Step 2: Remember the Power of God in Your Past Experiences

Look back on your life and recall how many times God has blessed you, provided for you when you didn't know where the provision was coming from, and done wonderful works on your behalf. We often take credit for what God has done, but the truth is that God is the one who has blessed us abundantly over and over again. Do not suffer from memory loss regarding God's past faithfulness.

### Step 3: See Things from God's Perspective, Not Your Own

The Israelites failed because they looked at the giants and walled cities from a human perspective and became convinced they could not succeed. They forgot that God had already given them the land—it was a done deal. Instead of looking through the eyes of deity, they saw themselves as grasshoppers. When you face challenges, remember that God's perspective is that your promises are already fulfilled. Believe that you have what you desire when you pray, for it is as good as done.

### Step 4: Meditate on the Word of God

Get in the Word of God and ask Him to give you His promises. Dr. Stanley shares how his mother gave him Joshua 1:8-9 before his first sermon: "Have I not commanded you to be strong and courageous, do not tremble or be dismayed for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go?" Meditate on the Word of God day and night, and He will make your way successful. Plant Scripture in your heart and the hearts of your children, for it will protect, guide, encourage, enable, and challenge them throughout their lifetime.

### Step 5: Remember the Awesome Things God Has Done for You

Consciously recall the specific ways God has intervened in your life. Remember that He saved you from Egyptian bondage—from a life of sin—and that He continues to bless you. Do not forget what He saved you from. When you keep His blessings at the forefront of your mind, it becomes easier to trust Him with new challenges.

### Step 6: Keep Your Focus on Him

Maintain your focus on God no matter what you're facing in life. When you keep your eyes on Him, you can walk through any difficulty with confidence. He is with you wherever you go, and He will never leave you nor forsake you. Let Him be your shield, your provider, and your guide.

### Step 7: If You Are Not a Christian, Surrender Your Life to Jesus Christ

If you have never trusted Jesus Christ as your Savior, you are rebelling against God. The only way to stop floundering in life is to ask God to forgive you for your rebellion, ask Him to forgive you of your sins based on what Jesus Christ did at the cross by dying and paying your sin debt in full, and surrender your life to Him. Tell Him that you don't want any more wilderness living—you want to get into the Canaan He has for you. Everything will change when you surrender to Him.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Israelites in the Wilderness (Numbers 13-14)

The nation of Israel had been delivered from Egyptian bondage, crossed the Red Sea, received God's law at Sinai, and were now on the very border of the Promised Land. God had promised this land to them through Abraham. Moses sent twelve spies to scout the land, and they returned with an overwhelming report: the land was indeed flowing with milk and honey, but the people were strong, the cities were fortified, and they saw the descendants of Anak—the Nephilim—who made them feel like grasshoppers in their own sight. Ten of the twelve spies gave a bad report, saying they were not able to go up against the people. The congregation wept, grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and wanted to return to Egypt. They even wanted to stone Joshua and Caleb, the only two spies who encouraged them to take the land. Because of their rebellion, God declared that every man over the age of twenty would die in the wilderness—forty years of wandering, fighting, grumbling, and dying. They lost the blessing that was right before them because of their faith failure.

### Example 2: Caleb's Expression of Faith

While the other spies focused on the size of the enemies and the strength of their cities, Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, "We should by all means go up and take possession of it, for we will surely overcome it." Caleb saw the same land, the same giants, and the same fortified cities, but he saw them through the eyes of God. He trusted that God would give them the victory. His faith stood in stark contrast to the fear and unbelief of the other spies. Because of his faith, Caleb was one of the two men (along with Joshua) who was allowed to enter the Promised Land.

### Example 3: Personal Application—Missing God's Blessings Through Fear

Dr. Stanley asks the listener to examine their own life: How many times have you missed one of God's blessings because you were afraid? Afraid to fail, afraid of what somebody would say, afraid you couldn't live up to your own expectations. Fear is a paralyzer, a destroyer, and a cheater. It deprives us of the very best that God has to give us. When you back off from what God is calling you to do, you are essentially saying, "I know that's what God says, but I'm an exception to the laws of God, the rules of God, therefore, it won't work for me." This is a faith failure that costs you God's best.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Trust God as the first and foundational step in any situation, even when you don't have all the answers
- ✅ Meditate on the Word of God day and night, planting Scripture in your heart and the hearts of your children
- ✅ Remember the awesome things God has done for you in the past
- ✅ See your circumstances from God's perspective, knowing that His promises are as good as done
- ✅ Be strong and courageous, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go
- ✅ Keep your focus on Him no matter what you are facing in life
- ❌ Don't put a "but" after acknowledging God's promises—this is a sign of rebellion
- ❌ Don't forget the blessings of God who has blessed you abundantly over and over again
- ❌ Don't let fear paralyze you and keep you from the best that God has for you
- ❌ Don't rebel against the will of God by wanting things your own way
- ❌ Don't look at your circumstances from only a human perspective, focusing on the size of the problem rather than the power of God

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon provides spiritual guidance on faith and obedience to God. It does not replace personal Bible study, prayer, or counsel from mature believers.
- Stop and ask God for guidance through prayer and Scripture if you are unsure whether a thought or decision aligns with His will.
- Rebellion against God opposes the person of God, the power of God, the work of God, the will of God, and the ways of God. The more God wants to bless you, the more severe the consequences when you choose to rebel against Him.
- When you backslide on God, you are going back to the wilderness. There is no way to experience peace, contentment, or joy living in rebellion toward God.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Rebellion against God brings consequences that are inevitable and inescapable in the life of individuals as well as the life of a nation.
- Fear is a paralyzer, a destroyer, and a cheater. It deprives you from the very best that God has to give you.
- The laws of God are not suggestions. They are the promises of God for good and the promises of God that concern evil.
- If you refuse God's Son, Jesus, you are rebelling against God. He is the Son of God whom the Father sent to die on the cross that you may be forgiven of your sins. Rejecting Him means you do not believe in the one true God.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Fear of failure paralyzes you and keeps you from entering the Promised Land God has for you
  **Solution:** Trust God and remember that He has already provided everything you need to conquer the land. He is with you wherever you go.

- **Problem:** Forgetting God's past faithfulness when facing new challenges
  **Solution:** Consciously recall how God has blessed you, provided for you, and delivered you in the past. Write down His faithfulness so you can look back on it when doubt arises.

- **Problem:** Looking at circumstances from a human perspective instead of God's perspective
  **Solution:** See situations through the eyes of faith. When God says a promise is yours, it is a done deal. Believe that you have what you desire when you pray, and walk in that faith.

- **Problem:** Rebellion against God's will by wanting things your own way
  **Solution:** Surrender your life to Almighty God and to His Son, Jesus Christ. Follow Him and watch Him work in your life in awesome ways. Submit to His will rather than your own.

- **Problem:** Putting a "but" after acknowledging God's promises, making yourself an exception to His laws
  **Solution:** Remove the "but" and choose to trust God completely. When you acknowledge God's promises, stand upon them without reservation. True, genuine faith starts with trusting God even when you don't have all the answers.
