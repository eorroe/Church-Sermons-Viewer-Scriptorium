# Your Convictions About The Holy Spirit

## Overview

In this sermon from In Touch Ministries, Dr. Charles Stanley teaches that the Holy Spirit is not an impersonal force or "it" but the third Person of the Trinity who indwells every believer at salvation. The Holy Spirit is our Helper and Great Enabler who empowers, convicts, guides, and comforts us so we do not have to live the Christian life alone. Through surrender, confession, and repentance, we can experience the fullness of God's divine power and authority working within us for godly living and fruitful service.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel burned out, discouraged, or desperate while trying to serve God
- When you need to understand who the Holy Spirit is and what He does in your life
- When you feel alone in your Christian walk and need reassurance of God's presence
- When you want to learn how to surrender your life to the Holy Spirit and experience His power
- When you are struggling with sin and need to understand the Holy Spirit's convicting work
- When you need encouragement that you are fully equipped by the Holy Spirit to face life's challenges

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Holy Spirit as a Person of the Trinity

Understand that the Holy Spirit is not an impersonal force or "it." He is the third Person of the Trinity, co-equal with God the Father and God the Son. At creation, God said, "Let Us make man in Our own image" (Genesis 1:26), revealing the plurality of the Godhead. The Holy Spirit is a Person who dwells within every believer.

### Step 2: Acknowledge the Holy Spirit's Indwelling Presence

When you trusted Jesus Christ as your Savior and confessed with your mouth that He is Lord and believed in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you were saved. At that moment, the Holy Spirit came to dwell within you permanently. He is sealed in you and will never leave. You do not have to earn His presence or try to keep Him. He is already there.

### Step 3: Understand the Holy Spirit as Your Helper and Enabler

Jesus called the Holy Spirit "another Helper" (John 14:16-18) who is with you forever. The Holy Spirit is the Great Enabler who gives you the capacity to do everything God wants you to do at every age and in every circumstance of life. He is the source of your knowledge, wisdom, and power to be and to do what God calls you to do.

### Step 4: Receive the Power of the Holy Spirit

The power of the Holy Spirit is defined as: God's divine energy and authority released in the life of the believer for the purpose of godly living and fruitful service. This power is not for self-promotion or worldly gain but to enable you to live for God and accomplish His will. When you face challenges, remember that you are not fighting battles alone. He who has indwelt you is your guardian and source of strength.

### Step 5: Surrender Your Life Through Confession and Repentance

To experience the fullness of the Holy Spirit, you must be willing to:
- Confess your sins, which means agreeing with God that they do not belong in your life
- Repent of your sins and turn away from them
- Yield your life to the Holy Spirit
- Surrender your life to Him and acknowledge that He owns you
- Follow His will and do what He wants you to do

When you fully surrender yourself to Him, the Spirit of God comes into your life in total fullness.

### Step 6: Live in Continuous Awareness of the Holy Spirit

Wake up each morning in the awareness that the Holy Spirit is with you. Throughout the day, remember that you do not have to fret, worry, or be anxious because He is in you, with you, and upon you, enabling you to carry out your responsibilities and fulfill whatever God wants you to do. Respond to situations out of your spirit, not your feelings, because emotions come and go, but the Spirit's power is constant.

### Step 7: Experience More of the Holy Spirit by Giving More of Yourself

The more of yourself you give to the Holy Spirit, the more of Him you will experience. Do not settle for merely confessing sins and going through the motions. Fully surrender every area of your life to Him so that you can experience His fullness and walk in the uninterrupted, continuous presence and power of the Holy Spirit.

### Step 8: Trust That You Are Not Alone

You do not face life alone. God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit are with you in every circumstance. You are fully equipped by the presence of the Holy Spirit to face anything, everything, and all things. It does not mean life will get easy, but it means whatever you face, you never face it alone. You and the whole Trinity face them together, and you cannot lose.

## Examples

### Example 1: Overcoming Burnout in Service

You feel burned out and discouraged while trying to serve God. Instead of relying on your own strength, acknowledge that the Holy Spirit lives within you and is your Helper. Confess any sin that may be hindering your relationship with God, surrender your weariness to Him, and ask Him to enable you to serve with His strength. As you walk in awareness of His presence, you will find the energy and joy to continue your service.

### Example 2: Facing a Difficult Conversation

You are about to have a confrontation with someone about the gospel or a difficult personal situation. Before you speak, claim the presence, power, and word of the Spirit of God. Trust that He will be your defender, your proclamation, and the speaker of truth through you. You may find yourself saying things you had not even thought about, and Scriptures will come to your mind that you had not thought about in a long time. This is the Holy Spirit working through you.

### Example 3: Struggling with Temptation

You are tempted to harbor sin or fall into a pattern of negative thinking. The Holy Spirit convicts you of sin and reminds you that you do not have to stay trapped. Agree with God that the sin does not belong in your life, confess it, repent, and turn away from it. Trust that because Jesus paid your sin-debt in full, God is faithful and just to forgive you and cleanse you. Then yield that area of your life back to the Holy Spirit and walk in the freedom He provides.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do this
- ✅ Recognize and speak of the Holy Spirit as a Person, not an impersonal force or "it"
- ✅ Acknowledge the Holy Spirit's constant presence within you throughout the day
- ✅ Confess your sins daily and agree with God that they do not belong in your life
- ✅ Surrender your will and yield to the Holy Spirit's guidance in every circumstance
- ✅ Trust that the Holy Spirit is your source of power, wisdom, and strength
- ✅ Respond to situations out of your spirit, not your fluctuating emotions
- ✅ Remember that you are sealed by the Holy Spirit and He will never leave you
- ❌ Don't do this
- ❌ Do not refer to the Holy Spirit as "it" or treat Him as an impersonal force
- ❌ Do not try to live the Christian life in your own strength, saying "I'll just do the best I can"
- ❌ Do not ignore the convicting work of the Holy Spirit in your life
- ❌ Do not act as if you are alone in your struggles
- ❌ Do not harbor sin or think you must handle guilt on your own

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon is a spiritual guide and does not replace personal Bible study, prayer, or accountability within a local church community.
- The experience of the Holy Spirit's fullness varies by individual and circumstance. Stop and ask for clarification from a trusted spiritual leader if you have questions about your own spiritual journey.
- The Holy Spirit's work in your life is a lifelong process. Receiving the Holy Spirit is instantaneous at salvation, but living it out is a lifelong journey of growing in surrender and dependence on Him.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating the Holy Spirit as an impersonal force or "it" rather than a Person of the Trinity.
  **Solution:** Remember that the Holy Spirit is a Person who indwells you, convicts you, guides you, and comforts you. Speak of Him and relate to Him as a Person.

- **Problem:** Trying to live the Christian life in your own strength by saying, "I'll just do the best I can."
  **Solution:** Acknowledge that you have the Holy Spirit living within you who enables you to do everything God calls you to do. Depend on His power, not your own.

- **Problem:** Feeling anxious, fearful, or alone when facing difficulties.
  **Solution:** Remember that you are never alone. The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are with you in every circumstance. Walk in the awareness of the Holy Spirit's continuous, uninterrupted presence and power.

- **Problem:** Refusing to confess sin or surrender areas of your life to the Holy Spirit.
  **Solution:** Understand that confession means agreeing with God that your sin does not belong in your life. Repent, turn away from sin, and yield your life to the Holy Spirit. The more of yourself you give to Him, the more of Him you will experience.

- **Problem:** Being misled by feelings and emotions rather than responding in the Spirit's power.
  **Solution:** Emotions come and go, but the Holy Spirit's presence is constant. Choose to respond out of your spirit, not your feelings, so that you can respond in the energy, power, and anointing of the Spirit of God.
