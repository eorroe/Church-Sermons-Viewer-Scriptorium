# The Will of God in Your Life

## Overview

This sermon teaches how to seek and follow God's will in every area of your daily life. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that God has a specific plan for each person and that surrendering to His direction brings fulfillment, peace, and purpose. The message emphasizes that no part of our lives should be lived independently of our wise and loving Creator.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need guidance for daily decisions
- When working through uncertainty about relationships, career, or life direction
- When seeking to discern God's plan for your life

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That God Has a Plan for Your Life

Understand that God has a specific plan for you, including your relationships, career, and daily decisions. Acknowledge that your life is not random and that God cares about every detail. Accept that His will is what He approves and determines to bring about for your good.

### Step 2: Stop Making Decisions Independently of God

Identify areas where you have been living without consulting God. People make choices based solely on their own understanding, ignoring His guidance. Confess this independence and commit to involving Him in every decision, no matter how small.

### Step 3: Ask God for Direction Daily

Make it a practice to ask God each day, "Lord, what would you have me to do?" Seek His guidance before taking action. Follow the example of Jesus, who prayed "not my will but yours be done" before the cross, demonstrating complete dependence on the Father.

### Step 4: Surrender Your Will to God

Submit your desires, plans, and future to God's will. Surrendering does not mean losing control—it means trusting that God loves you and wants the best for you, regardless of past mistakes. Allow Him to turn any mess into something awesome through His forgiveness and grace.

### Step 5: Teach Others About God's Will

If you are a parent or grandparent, teach children about God's will for their lives. Help them understand early that seeking God's direction is the foundation for a meaningful and purposeful life.

## Examples

### Example 1: Daily Decision Making

Before making a significant choice at work or in a relationship, pause and ask God for His direction. Wait for His guidance rather than rushing into decisions based only on your own reasoning.

### Example 2: Overcoming Past Mistakes

If you have been running from God through unhealthy substitutes like addiction or destructive relationships, return to Him. Confess your independence, accept His forgiveness, and allow Him to redirect your life toward His good plan.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Seek God's direction every single day
- ✅ Surrender specific areas of your life to God's will
- ✅ Follow Jesus' example of dependence on the Father
- ✅ Teach children and young people about God's will
- ❌ Do not make major life decisions without seeking God first
- ❌ Do not believe that past mistakes disqualify you from God's plan
- ❌ Do not try to fill the emptiness of life without God with temporary substitutes

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal Bible study, prayer, or wise counsel from mature believers.
- Stop and ask God to show you His will if you are unsure about specific decisions or how to hear His voice in your life.

## Security & Safety Notes

- This message encourages personal responsibility and spiritual growth through seeking God's will.
- Those struggling with addiction or mental health issues should seek professional help alongside spiritual guidance.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to understand God's will without surrendering your own
  **Solution:** Surrender your plans and desires to God before seeking His direction. Trust that His plan is better than your own.
- **Problem:** Believing your past mistakes disqualify you from God's plan
  **Solution:** Remember that God can forgive any sin and turn any mess into something awesome. His love is not based on your perfection.
- **Problem:** Making decisions independently of God and then asking for His blessing afterward
  **Solution:** Seek God's guidance before making decisions, not after. Daily dependence on Him prevents costly mistakes.
