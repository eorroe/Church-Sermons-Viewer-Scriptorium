# Experiencing Inner Peace – Dr. Charles Stanley

## Overview

This sermon teaches that true inner peace is a gift from God, available to all who surrender their lives to Jesus Christ and walk in His ways. Dr. Charles Stanley outlines eight foundational truths that, when believed and practiced, produce a deep, abiding peace that surpasses worldly understanding. This peace is not dependent on circumstances but on a genuine relationship with God through trust, obedience, and surrender.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel anxious, fearful, or unsettled about the future
- When you are struggling with guilt, a troubled conscience, or inner conflict
- When you feel empty, purposeless, or dissatisfied with life
- When you are wrestling with feelings of rejection, inadequacy, or low self-worth
- When you need reassurance of God's love, control, and provision in your life
- When you want to experience the peace that Jesus promised to those who follow Him

## Steps

### Step 1: Place Your Trust in Jesus Christ as Your Personal Savior

Settle once and for all where you will spend eternity by asking the Lord to forgive your sins, surrendering your life to Him, and yielding yourself to Jesus as your Savior and Lord. This is the foundation of all genuine peace.

### Step 2: Believe That God Is in Sovereign Control of All Things

Embrace the truth that God is in control of all things—He causes, allows, or prevents events according to His perfect will. This removes fear and anxiety for your personal safety and future, knowing that nothing happens outside of His knowledge and purpose.

### Step 3: Trust the Lord to Meet All of Your Needs

Choose to trust that God will supply all your needs according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus. Release your daily worries about not having enough, understanding that worry takes upon yourself a weight and load that God intends to carry for you.

### Step 4: Maintain a Clear Conscience

Regularly examine your heart and deal with anything that violates your conscience. A clear conscience shields and protects your peace with yourself. Do not ignore or suppress convictions, but bring them to God for forgiveness and cleansing.

### Step 5: Accept Yourself as God Made You

Guard against trying to change something over which you have no control. God has gifted each person uniquely, and fighting against how He made you only leads to unhappiness and inner conflict. Be satisfied with His design and purpose for your life.

### Step 6: Cultivate a Sense of Purpose in Life

Seek God's purpose for your life and walk in it. You are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works which He prepared beforehand. Purpose protects you from the plague of emptiness, and the closer you walk with Christ, the more peace you will experience.

### Step 7: Develop a Sense of Competency and Adequacy

Recognize that God has equipped you with spiritual gifts and the Holy Spirit's power to accomplish His will. He would never call you to do something He would not equip you to do. Let go of feelings of inadequacy and rest in His sufficiency.

### Step 8: Embrace Your Sense of Belonging to God

Remember that you belong to Jesus Christ. When you feel rejected, unwanted, or unloved, look to the cross and remember how valuable you are to God—so valuable that He sent His Son to die for you. This shields you from the painful feelings of rejection and gives you a secure identity in Him.

## Examples

### Example 1: Anxiety About the Future

A person facing uncertainty about their career or health can apply Step 2 by choosing to believe that God is sovereign over all circumstances. Instead of being consumed by worry, they can remind themselves of Romans 8:28 and rest in the truth that God works all things together for good for those who love Him.

### Example 2: Struggling with Guilt and a Troubled Conscience

Someone carrying guilt from past mistakes can apply Step 4 by bringing their sin before God in confession, receiving His forgiveness, and making restitution where necessary. As they maintain a clear conscience, they experience the protective shield of inner peace.

### Example 3: Feeling Empty or Unfulfilled

A person who feels empty despite achieving worldly success can apply Step 6 by seeking God's purpose for their life through prayer, Bible study, and obedience. As they walk in the good works God prepared for them, the emptiness is replaced by the fullness of Christ's presence and peace.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Write down the eight statements and review them regularly
- ✅ Practice the truth daily—peace comes from walking in obedience to God
- ✅ Bring any guilt or conviction to God immediately through confession
- ✅ Choose trust over worry in every circumstance
- ✅ Remember that peace is a gift, not a feeling to be manufactured
- ❌ Do not ignore a troubled conscience—it will eventually trap you
- ❌ Do not compare yourself to others or strive to be someone you are not
- ❌ Do not rely on worldly solutions to fill the emptiness only Jesus can fill
- ❌ Do not try to control what God has called you to surrender to Him

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon provides spiritual guidance rooted in Christian faith and is not a substitute for professional counseling, medical advice, or mental health treatment when needed.
- If you are struggling with severe anxiety, depression, or trauma, seek support from a qualified Christian counselor or pastor alongside your faith practice.
- Peace with God through Jesus Christ is the foundation; without it, the other steps cannot produce lasting inner peace.
- Stop and ask God for clarification through prayer and Scripture if you are uncertain about any of these steps or how to apply them to your life.

## Security & Safety Notes

- This sermon is intended for personal spiritual growth and reflection within the context of Christian faith.
- Do not use the teaching to condemn yourself or others; God's purpose is to bring freedom, healing, and peace through Christ.
- Guard against legalism—peace comes through faith and surrender, not through perfection or self-effort.
- Share these truths with gentleness and love, respecting each person's journey and relationship with God.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trying to manufacture peace through worldly distractions, substances, or relationships
  **Solution:** Return to the source of peace—Jesus Christ. Peace that passes all understanding comes only from Him, not from temporary or external sources.

- **Problem:** Believing that peace means the absence of trouble or difficulty
  **Solution:** Understand that God's peace prevails even in the midst of hardship, trials, and heartache. It is not dependent on circumstances but on your trust in Him.

- **Problem:** Comparing yourself to others and feeling inadequate or dissatisfied with how God made you
  **Solution:** Accept that God created you uniquely with specific gifts and purposes. Comparing yourself steals your peace; contentment in Christ restores it.

- **Problem:** Carrying guilt and avoiding confession, hoping to forget past sins
  **Solution:** Bring everything to God in confession. A clear conscience is essential for inner peace, and God is faithful to forgive and cleanse when you come to Him in humility.

- **Problem:** Trying to be in control of your life instead of surrendering to God
  **Solution:** Yield your life to Christ daily. True peace comes when you acknowledge that God is in control and trust Him with every area of your life, including your eternity, safety, needs, identity, purpose, and relationships.
