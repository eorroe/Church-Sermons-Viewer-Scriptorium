# To Whom Are You Listening?

## Overview

In this sermon, Dr. Charles Stanley challenges listeners to examine who they are truly listening to in their daily lives. Drawing from Hebrews 1:1-2 and John 14:26, he explains that God desires to speak to every believer through His Word and the Holy Spirit, yet many people miss His voice because their ears are tuned to the world instead. The sermon outlines why people fail to listen to God, how to identify His voice, the protection and direction that come from listening, and the eternal consequences of refusing to hear Him. It concludes with a clear call to accept Jesus Christ as Savior for those who have never done so.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel disconnected from God and unsure if He is speaking to you
- When making important life decisions and need to discern God's will
- When struggling with doubt about whether an inner direction is truly from God
- When you sense that worldly distractions are crowding out your spiritual life
- When you need encouragement to prioritize quiet time and Bible reading
- When you want to understand the consequences of ignoring God's voice
- When sharing the gospel with someone who has never trusted Christ as Savior

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge That God Wants to Speak to You

Recognize that God, the Sovereign Creator of the universe, desires to communicate with you personally. From the moment you trust Christ as your Savior, the Holy Spirit indwells you and is available to guide, direct, and speak to your heart morning, noon, and night (Hebrews 1:1-2; John 14:26). Ask yourself: How much time do you spend in any given day being quiet and listening to God?

### Step 2: Understand Why People Do Not Listen to God

Identify the barriers that prevent people from hearing God's voice:
- **No relationship with Him**: Unsaved people cannot hear God because they have no relationship with Him through Christ.
- **Unexpectation**: Many people do not expect God to speak to them and assume He remains silent.
- **Worldly noise**: The messages, music, and activities of the world drown out God's voice.
- **Ignoring the Word of God**: When you neglect reading and meditating on Scripture, the world grows larger in your thinking while God's voice fades.
- **Harboring sin**: Unconfessed sin deafens the ears of a child of God and blocks His voice.
- **Rebellious spirit**: A refusal to submit to God's authority keeps people from listening.

### Step 3: Learn to Identify the Voice of God

Develop discernment through these three indicators:
1. **Consistency with Scripture**: God's voice will always align perfectly with the Word of God. He never contradicts Himself or His written Word.
2. **Challenge to your faith**: God often calls you to do things that conflict with your own ability, confidence, or understanding. If a direction requires nothing of you, it may not be from God.
3. **Quiet certainty**: God often speaks in silent, ordinary moments rather than through dramatic experiences. His voice comes with clarity and peace, not confusion.

### Step 4: Quiet Yourself and Listen

Intentionally set aside time to be still before God. Turn off the radio, the television, the music, and the electronic devices that fill your mind. Sit in silence and invite God to speak. Prayer is not only about talking; it is also about listening. After praying, pause and wait to see if God wants to answer or direct your thoughts.

### Step 5: Compare What You Hear Against the Bible

Whenever you sense God speaking or directing you, verify it against the Scriptures. Open your Bible and ask: Does this match what God has said? God will never ask you to do anything that violates His Word. If what you are hearing conflicts with Scripture, it is not from God.

### Step 6: Act on What God Says

When you recognize God's voice, obey immediately. Do not delay or rationalize away His direction. Dr. Stanley shares that God may ask you to do things that seem beyond your ability, but He will always equip you for what He calls you to do. Obedience is the pathway to spiritual growth and the fulfillment of God's will for your life.

### Step 7: Make the Decision to Follow Christ (If Unsaved)

If you have never trusted Jesus Christ as your personal Savior, the most important decision in your life is to receive Him now. Stop listening to the world, stop looking to other people for validation, and ask: "What is the mind of God?" Salvation comes by faith alone: confess with your mouth that Jesus Christ is Lord, believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, and place your trust in His death on the cross as payment for your sins.

## Examples

### Example 1: Choosing a Career Path

A believer is considering two job opportunities and is uncertain which direction God wants. Instead of relying solely on salary, location, or personal ambition, they spend time in prayer and quiet reflection before God. They read relevant Scripture and wait for God's peace and direction. If one opportunity requires them to step out in faith and trust God for provision, while the other is comfortable but unrelated to their spiritual calling, the challenging option may be God's direction. They compare the inner leading against the Bible to ensure it does not conflict with God's commands, then obey.

### Example 2: Overcoming a Sinful Habit

A Christian realizes they have been struggling with a persistent sin that has become a pattern in their life. They feel distant from God and sense that their prayers are not being answered. After hearing this sermon, they understand that harboring sin deafens the ears of a child of God. They confess the sin, turn away from it, and recommit to daily time in the Word. As they quiet themselves and listen, they begin to sense God's guidance again and experience the restoration of their relationship with Him.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Start each day with quiet time before God before turning on the radio, television, or social media.
- ✅ Read the Bible daily to keep your mind filled with God's truth so you can recognize His voice.
- ✅ Ask God to speak to you and then wait patiently in silence for His direction.
- ✅ Compare every inner leading or impression against the clear teaching of Scripture.
- ✅ Obey promptly when you know God is speaking, even if it challenges your comfort or ability.
- ✅ Confess known sin quickly so it does not block your ability to hear God.
- ✅ Remember that God speaks primarily through His Word; let Scripture be your primary guide.
- ❌ Do not rely on feelings, emotions, or circumstances alone to confirm God's will.
- ❌ Do not expect God to speak in contradiction to the Bible.
- ❌ Do not assume that because God has not spoken in the way you expected, He is silent.
- ❌ Do not allow the noise of the world to become the default soundtrack of your day.

## Keep In Mind

- God speaks to every believer, not just a select few. If you are a Christian, God desires to communicate with you.
- Listening to God requires intentionality. It will not happen accidentally in the midst of a busy, noisy schedule.
- God's voice may challenge your faith, but He will never ask you to do anything that is outside of His will or beyond His enabling.
- The Holy Spirit was given to indwell every believer and to guide you into all truth. He is your Helper and Teacher.
- Listening to God is not a one-time event; it is a daily practice that grows stronger with consistency.
- Even if you have been neglecting God, He is still willing to speak to you. Turn back to Him today.

## Security & Safety Notes

- No spiritual practice or method of discerning God's voice should replace the authority of the Bible. Always test what you hear against Scripture.
- Be cautious of claims or impressions that contradict the clear teachings of the Word of God. God does not contradict Himself.
- Never make major life decisions based solely on an emotional feeling or a fleeting impression without confirming it through prayer and the Bible.
- If you are experiencing confusion or distress, seek wise counsel from a mature, Bible-believing Christian leader or pastor.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Pitfall: Assuming God is silent because He has not spoken in the way you expected.**
  - **Solution:** God often speaks in quiet, ordinary moments. Be still, read the Word, and listen. Do not demand fireworks or dramatic experiences to confirm His voice.

- **Pitfall: Confusing personal desires or worldly advice with God's voice.**
  - **Solution:** Always test what you hear against the Bible. God's voice will be consistent with Scripture and will often challenge your human nature, not simply affirm it.

- **Pitfall: Allowing sin to block your ability to hear God.**
  - **Solution:** Confess known sin immediately and turn away from it. Unconfessed sin creates a barrier between you and God and dulls your spiritual sensitivity.

- **Pitfall: Being too busy or too distracted to listen.**
  - **Solution:** Purposefully set aside quiet time each day. Turn off the noise of the world so you can hear the voice of God speaking to your heart.

- **Pitfall: Putting off the decision to follow Christ.**
  - **Solution:** If you have never trusted Jesus as your Savior, do not delay. The consequences of rejecting Christ are eternal. Confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, and accept His payment for your sins today.
