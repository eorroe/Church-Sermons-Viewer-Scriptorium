# The Rocky Road to the Will of God

## Overview

One of the most important subjects in the Bible is the will of God. Unbelievers scoff at it and believers often ignore it, but every single day we either live in or out of the will of God. This sermon identifies the obstacles that keep Christians from discovering and obeying God's will, including self-will, the influence of other people, ignorance of God's principles, willful sin, doubt, feelings of unworthiness, busyness, fear of criticism, and backsliding. Dr. Stanley teaches that the Christian life is not easy, but God provides everything we need through the Holy Spirit to walk in His will step by step.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to understand why following God's will feels difficult
- When working through obstacles that keep you from obeying God
- When you are seeking to discern God's will for your life

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize Self-Will as the First Obstacle

Self-will is a matter of pride and selfishness. We want our own way and often ask God to bless our plans instead of seeking His will. None of us is sufficient within ourselves to live out the will of God without His guidance.

### Step 2: Avoid the Wrong Influence of Other People

Other people are quick to tell you how to live, but they cannot know God's specific plan for your life. When someone says "if I were you," remember they are not you and do not know what God is doing in your life. Place God's opinion above human opinion.

### Step 3: Learn God's Principles for Walking in His Will

You cannot walk in the will of God if you do not know His principles. Key principles include: learning to wait on God's timing, learning to trust God and leave all consequences to Him, and remembering that God will supply all your needs when you are walking in His will. Walk one step at a time, trusting God even when you cannot see around the curve.

### Step 4: Remove Willful Known Sin From Your Life

Sin deafens your ears to God's voice, blinds your eyes to God's vision, hardens your heart to God's awareness, and deadens your conscience to God's Word. When you are out of the will of God, you sense something is not right. Look within your own heart to see what is going on.

### Step 5: Overcome Doubt by Remembering God's Faithfulness

Doubt leads to wrong decisions. When you doubt God, you are doubting that He has a will for you personally, that He will make His will known, that you can hear from God, and that you can do what He requires. God loves you individually, has a plan for your life, will walk you through that plan, and will provide everything you need.

### Step 6: Do Not Underestimate What God Can Do Through You

Do not make decisions based on how you think. Make decisions based on what God has called you to do. Do not say "I can't" or "I'm unworthy" like Moses did. God is there to help you become the person He wants you to be.

### Step 7: Confess and Move Forward When You Sin

There is no place in the Bible where God says that if one of His children sins, that is the end. If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us and cleanse us from all unrighteousness. Do not live in the muck of disobedience. Ask God to forgive you sincerely, and He will pick you up and move you forward.

### Step 8: Do Not Use Busyness as an Excuse

Busyness is a tool of the devil. We live in a world opposed to who we are as Christians, and we can all claim busyness as an excuse for sin. Spend time with God in His Word and in prayer. It takes time to be a godly person, to walk with the Lord, and to know His will.

### Step 9: Do Not Fear What God May Require, Failure, or Criticism

A loving Father will only require what He knows you are capable of doing with His help. The Holy Spirit has sealed you as a child of God and dwells within you to enable you to do anything God requires. You will fail at times, but if you confess your sin, God will forgive you. When you live a godly life, you will be criticized, but you will also be blessed.

## Examples

### Example 1: Moses and Feelings of Unworthiness

When God came to Moses, Moses responded with feelings of inadequacy: "I can't speak, I can't do this." Many of God's servants step out of His will early in life because they tell God they cannot do what He calls them to do. Do not underestimate what God thinks you can do. He is there to help you accomplish His will.

### Example 2: The Apostle Paul and Difficulty

Look at the Apostle Paul and all the major characters of Scripture. They went through great difficulty, all of which God used to accomplish His purpose and mold them into godly men and women. God's goal is not to please you or give you everything you want, but to fashion you into the likeness of Christ.

### Example 3: Backsliding and God's Forgiveness

Many people backslide by choosing to return to sin they were delivered from because they do not think they can be the person God wants them to be. You cannot sin so badly that God will not retrieve you, but you can sin so badly that you will wish you hadn't because of God's chastening hand. He is loving you back into His will, not casting you out.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Walk in the will of God by choosing obedience each day
- ✅ Ask God to show you His will and guide your steps
- ✅ Trust God with the consequences and walk by faith
- ✅ Spend time in God's Word and in prayer to know His principles
- ✅ Confess sin immediately and sincerely when you fall short
- ✅ Rely on the Holy Spirit within you for strength and guidance
- ✅ Remember that God will never require more than He enables you to do
- ❌ Do not let self-will, pride, or selfishness control your decisions
- ❌ Do not place other people's opinions above God's will for your life
- ❌ Do not allow willful sin to deafen your ears, blind your eyes, or harden your heart
- ❌ Do not doubt that God has a plan and purpose for your life
- ❌ Do not use busyness as an excuse for neglecting your relationship with God
- ❌ Do not fear criticism, failure, or what God may require of you

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal Bible study, prayer, or pastoral guidance.
- Stop and ask God for clarity if you are uncertain about His will for your life.
- The Christian life is a journey, not a destination. Keep moving forward in obedience.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Self-will and pride cause you to pursue your own way instead of God's way.
  **Solution:** Humble yourself before God and ask Him to align your desires with His will.
- **Problem:** Listening to wrong voices from other people instead of listening to God.
  **Solution:** When someone gives advice, test it against God's Word and seek God's specific guidance for your life.
- **Problem:** Willful sin blinds you to God's will and hardens your heart.
  **Solution:** Confess your sin immediately, ask God to forgive you, and turn away from that sin.
- **Problem:** Doubt causes you to step out of God's will or ahead of His timing.
  **Solution:** Remember God's faithfulness, love, and plan for your life. Trust Him even when you cannot see the whole picture.
- **Problem:** Using busyness as an excuse for neglecting time with God.
  **Solution:** Prioritize your relationship with God through daily Bible study and prayer, no matter how busy you are.
- **Problem:** Fear of what God may require, failure, or criticism keeps you from obeying.
  **Solution:** Trust that God will provide everything you need and that He is with you. The safest place is in the center of God's will.
