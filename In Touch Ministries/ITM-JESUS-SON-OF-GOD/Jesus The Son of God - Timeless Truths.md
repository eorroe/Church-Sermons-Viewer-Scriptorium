# Jesus: The Son of God | Timeless Truths

## Overview

This sermon by Dr. Charles Stanley addresses the most important question anyone can ask: "Who is this man called Jesus?" Rather than relying on popular opinion or personal speculation, the message calls believers to examine the evidence from Scripture and arrive at an informed, personal conclusion about Jesus' identity. By tracing His birth, life, death, and resurrection, the sermon reveals why Jesus is uniquely the Son of God and why getting this answer right changes everything.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are seeking clarity on who Jesus truly is and want to move beyond cultural assumptions
- When you feel uncertain about your own answer to the question "Who do you say Jesus is?"
- When you want to share the evidence for Christ's identity with someone who is searching or skeptical

## Steps

### Step 1: Examine the Birth of Jesus

Jesus' birth was not a normal birth. He was born of a virgin, conceived by the Holy Spirit, and announced by angels. He was born in Bethlehem but raised in Nazareth — into a world He Himself created. Begin by recognizing that His birth was supernatural, prophesied, and purposeful. This is not the beginning of a mere man, but the arrival of the eternal Son of God entering human history.

### Step 2: Examine the Life of Jesus

Jesus lived a life of sinless perfection. He existed before His birth — preexistent with the Father — yet grew up as a child in a carpenter's household. During His ministry, He preached repentance and the confession of sin, calling people to turn back to God. John the Baptist identified Him as "the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world." No one could find fault in Him, and His enemies could only destroy Him because His life was absolutely flawless.

### Step 3: Examine the Death of Jesus

Jesus died on a cross at a place called Golgotha (Calvary) in Jerusalem. This was no ordinary execution — it was the crucifixion of the living God clothed in human flesh, walking among men. Born of a virgin, raised in Nazareth, and now nailed to a cross for the sins of humanity. His death was the fulfillment of God's redemptive plan from the beginning to the end of the age.

### Step 4: Examine the Resurrection of Jesus

On the third day, Jesus rose again from the dead. After His resurrection, He revealed Himself to hundreds of people so that nobody would ever have a realistic reason to doubt. The resurrection is the definitive proof that He is who He claimed to be — the Son of the living God. It confirms that His sacrifice was accepted and that He has power over life and death.

### Step 5: Make a Decision About Jesus

You must answer the question for yourself: "Who do you say that I am?" This is not a question you can defer or ignore. You cannot accept the word of God while rejecting Jesus — the two are inseparable. If you accept Him, you accept His word; if you accept His word, you accept Him. Answer the way Peter did: "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God." Place your trust in Him today.

## Examples

### Example 1: Peter's Confession at Caesarea Philippi

When Jesus asked His disciples, "Who do people say that I, the Son of Man, am?" they offered various opinions — John the Baptist, Elijah, Jeremiah, or one of the prophets. But when Jesus turned the question personally to them, Simon Peter answered boldly: "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God." This confession became the bedrock of Christian faith and shows the kind of personal, convinced response Jesus is calling every person to make.

### Example 2: Thomas and the Reality of the Resurrection

After His resurrection, one of His disciples, Thomas, said, "I've got to see. I've got to see the..." Thomas struggled to believe without tangible evidence. Yet Jesus graciously revealed Himself after His resurrection to hundreds of people — not just the disciples — so that no one would have a legitimate reason to doubt. The resurrection appearances were widespread, public, and undeniable, giving every generation a firm basis for faith in the risen Christ.

- ❌ Don't rely on popular opinion, cultural assumptions, or secondhand descriptions of who Jesus is
- ❌ Don't separate acceptance of Jesus from acceptance of His word — the two are inseparable

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Treating the question "Who is Jesus?" as unimportant or irrelevant to daily life
  **Solution:** Recognize that your answer to this question shapes your eternal destiny and every choice you make. Take time to examine the evidence seriously and form a personal conviction.

- **Problem:** Accepting Jesus intellectually but not receiving Him personally in faith
  **Solution:** Move beyond knowledge to trust. Acknowledging facts about Jesus is not the same as accepting Him as Lord and Savior. Pray, place your faith in Him, and follow Him daily.

- **Problem:** Relying on the opinions of others — family, culture, or social media — instead of examining Jesus for yourself
  **Solution:** Others' beliefs do not determine yours. Study the Scriptures, examine the evidence of His birth, life, death, and resurrection, and come to your own informed and personal conclusion.
