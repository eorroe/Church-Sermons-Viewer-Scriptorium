# Finding Clear Guidance

## Overview

This sermon teaches how to find clear guidance from God when facing important decisions in life. Dr. Charles Stanley presents six simple but vital principles that walk you through the entire process of seeking and recognizing God's will. The message affirms that God is willing to give clear direction to every believer and provides a practical plan for discerning His voice above the noise of the world, personal desires, and external pressures.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing a major life decision and seeking God's will
- When feeling confused, anxious, or pressured about which path to take
- When you have prayed but have not yet received clear direction
- When external opinions or internal fears are clouding your judgment

## Steps

### Step 1: Clear the Pathway

Before asking God for direction, you must remove anything that hinders your ability to hear Him clearly. This means dealing with two things: known sin and strong personal desires. Confess and repent of any known sin in your life, because sin introduces confusion and clouds your ability to discern God's voice. Then bring your natural desires, dreams, and goals before God and yield them to Him. The goal is to reach a place of "submissive neutrality"—where you can sincerely say, "Lord, not what I want, but what You want." A clean heart and pure motives form the foundation of clear discernment.

### Step 2: Exercise Patience

Patience means refusing to rush ahead of God. Do not force a decision simply because you feel time is running out or because circumstances are pressing you. Remember that time never runs out on God. Waiting upon the Lord is not passive Marking of time; it is an active, confident expectation that God has heard your prayer and will answer in His perfect timing. He may be preparing you, changing your attitude, or working behind the scenes before He reveals His direction. Do not move until you know you have heard from God.

### Step 3: Be Alert to Pressure

Whenever you are seeking guidance, expect pressure to arise—and resolve not to let it push you into a premature decision. There are two primary forms of pressure to watch for. External pressure comes from people who offer advice based on their own limited perspective, or from circumstances that seem to demand an immediate answer. Internal pressure comes from your own fears, anxieties, and exaggerated expectations—feelings like "I must," "I should," or "I ought to." These pressures are not from God. Stay sensitive to them and refuse to let them dictate your choice.

### Step 4: Persistence in Prayer

Do not stop praying once you have presented your request. As you continue to pray, God begins a hidden work in your heart. He sifts your desires, brings hidden attitudes and prejudices to the surface, and eliminates alternatives until His clear direction remains. This is what it means to "pray without ceasing" (1 Thessalonians 5:17). Even when you see no outward change, keep praying. Trust that God is at work in you, shaping your desires and preparing you to receive His answer. Do not abandon prayer because the answer has not come as quickly as you expected.

### Step 5: Rest in God's Promises

While you wait, anchor yourself in the promises of God found in His Word. The Bible is the ultimate resource for discovering how God thinks and how He acts. Meditate on Scripture regularly, allowing God's truth to filter your thinking and align your desires with His will. A passage that seems ordinary today may become a specific word of direction when you face a future decision, because the Holy Spirit brings it to your mind at just the right moment. The Word of God builds your faith and reminds you of His faithfulness.

### Step 6: Wait for the Peace

The final confirmation of God's direction is His peace. Colossians 3:15 says, "Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts." This peace is an inner harmony and oneness with God that surpasses all understanding. It means your mind and God's mind are in agreement about the decision. The turmoil, anxiety, and uncertainty are gone, replaced by a deep, unwavering certainty. Do not move forward until you experience this peace. It is God's seal of approval on the direction He has given you.

## Examples

### Example 1: Choosing a Career Path

A believer is torn between two job offers—one that pays well but requires compromising values, and another that aligns with their faith but offers less financial security. After clearing their heart of any sinful ambition or fear of lack, they pray persistently over several weeks. As they meditate on Scripture, a verse about God's provision stands out. Eventually, a deep, unshakable peace settles on the second offer, confirming it as God's direction even though the first option seems more attractive.

### Example 2: Making a Major Relocation Decision

A family feels pressure from relatives to move closer to them, but the couple is uncertain whether it is God's timing. They confess any selfish motives, submit their desires to the Lord, and refuse to be rushed by family expectations or internal fears of missing out. They continue praying without ceasing, resting in God's promise that He will guide them. Over time, the anxiety fades and is replaced by a confident peace about staying put for another season—until a clear, God-ordained opportunity arises.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Begin every decision with a clean heart—confess sin and surrender your desires to God
- ✅ Write down the six phrases and review them throughout your decision-making process
- ✅ Meditate on Scripture daily, allowing God's Word to shape your thinking
- ✅ Wait patiently for God's peace before taking action
- ❌ Do not ignore known sin and expect to hear God clearly
- ❌ Do not rush a decision because of time pressure or people's expectations
- ❌ Do not rely solely on advice from others without seeking God's direction first
- ❌ Do not mistake anxiety, excitement, or stubbornness for God's leading

## Keep In Mind

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always verify that your discernment aligns with the teachings of Scripture.
- Be cautious of internal desires or external voices that contradict the Word of God.
- If peace is absent, do not proceed—wait until Christ's peace confirms your direction.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Moving ahead before receiving clear direction because of external pressure or internal anxiety
  **Solution:** Remember that time never runs out on God. Refuse to act until you have His peace confirming your decision.

- **Problem:** Mistaking personal desire or opinion for God's will
  **Solution:** Bring your desires to a place of submissive neutrality and wait for the peace of Christ to rule in your heart as confirmation.
