# Living Wisely Or Foolishly

## Overview

This sermon teaches the critical difference between godly wisdom and human wisdom. Godly wisdom is the capacity to view life from God's viewpoint and respond according to the principles of Scripture, while human wisdom is earthly, natural, and demonic. True wisdom comes from seeking God's will in every decision, trusting in His omniscience and perfect guidance rather than relying on our own limited understanding.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When making life decisions and need to discern God's will
- When choosing between wisdom and folly in daily choices
- When seeking God's guidance before any major or minor decision

## Steps

### Step 1: Ask "Is this the wisest thing to do?" before every decision

Before making any decision — whether about marriage, finances, health, or everyday choices — ask yourself: "Is this the wisest thing to do?" This question should be asked consistently, for both major and minor decisions.

### Step 2: Define godly wisdom as viewing things from God's viewpoint

Godly wisdom is the capacity to see things from God's perspective and respond to them based on what the Word of God teaches. It is not about human reasoning or personal preference, but about aligning your thoughts and actions with God's truth.

### Step 3: Consult God's Word as the source of all wisdom

Turn to Scripture as the ultimate source of wisdom. God's Word contains the principles and guidance needed for every decision. A person who neglects the Bible is choosing to live a foolish life and will suffer consequences they never imagined.

### Step 4: Recognize human wisdom is earthly, natural, and demonic

Human wisdom is limited to what man can reason and accomplish apart from God. It is also natural — rooted in our fallen sin nature that desires our own will over God's will. At its deepest level, human wisdom is demonic, originating from Satan's lies that tell us we don't need God.

### Step 5: Choose to live wisely by obeying God

Obedience to God is always the wisest choice. Living wisely means submitting your decisions, plans, and desires to God's authority. You can never go wrong by obeying God — even when you don't fully understand why.

### Step 6: Walk step by step trusting God's guidance

God does not reveal the entire future at once. Instead, He guides you step by step, showing you the next right move as you trust Him. Walk obediently one step at a time, seeking His direction for each decision along the way.

## Examples

### Example 1: Unwise Decisions

A city with a strong economy goes bankrupt due to years of unwise decisions. A family buys a home they cannot afford, driven by desire rather than wisdom. A person marries only for sexual fulfillment without considering deeper compatibility. Someone racks up credit card debt beyond their means to pay. In each case, the decision was made without seeking God's will — and the consequences were devastating.

### Example 2: Wise Living Through Trusting God's Guidance

A believer facing an uncertain future chooses to seek God's direction rather than follow their own understanding. Step by step, God provides guidance for each decision — even when the full path isn't visible. This person walks through difficult valleys with confidence, knowing that God's wisdom is pure, peaceable, gentle, and unwavering. They demonstrate that obeying God is always the wisest path, regardless of what the world may say.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Ask "What is the wisest thing to do?" before every decision
- ✅ Turn to God's Word as your source of wisdom
- ✅ Seek God's will rather than your own preferences or emotions
- ✅ Trust that God's wisdom is pure, peaceable, and without hypocrisy
- ✅ Walk step by step, following God's guidance one decision at a time
- ❌ Don't make decisions based solely on feelings or what others think
- ❌ Don't leave God out of your decision-making process
- ❌ Don't rely on human wisdom, worldly reasoning, or natural desires
- ❌ Don't assume you are smart enough to live wisely apart from God

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon is for spiritual guidance and personal reflection — it is not a substitute for professional financial, legal, or medical advice.
- Always consult qualified experts when making decisions with significant real-world consequences (e.g., financial commitments, legal matters, health choices).
- Do not use this sermon as the sole basis for decisions that affect the safety or well-being of yourself or others.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Leaving God out of decision-making leads to unwise, costly choices.
  **Solution:** Make it a habit to ask "What is the wisest thing to do?" and seek God's Word before every decision.

- **Problem:** Following worldly, human wisdom that is earthly, natural, and demonic.
  **Solution:** Recognize that human reasoning is limited and fallen — test every thought against Scripture and God's perspective.

- **Problem:** Trusting in your own intellect, position, or resources instead of God.
  **Solution:** Remember that God is omniscient, omnipotent, and omnipresent — He knows everything and loves you enough to guide you. Obedience to Him is never a mistake.

- **Problem:** Believing the devil's lie that you don't need God to handle life.
  **Solution:** Understand that Satan's voice will tell you "You can handle it on your own" — this is demonic. True wisdom begins with reverencing God and submitting to His will.
