# Be Careful When All Is Well

## Overview

This sermon warns believers about the spiritual danger of becoming complacent and lowering their guard when life is going well. Dr. Charles Stanley uses the story of Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden to illustrate how prosperity and ease can lead to spiritual carelessness, temptation, and disobedience to God. The message emphasizes that the devil's tactics have not changed and that Christians must remain vigilant and obedient regardless of their circumstances.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you are experiencing a season of prosperity and success
- When you feel tempted to relax your spiritual disciplines
- When you need a reminder to stay vigilant against the devil's schemes

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize the Danger of Complacency During Good Times

Understand that when everything is going well—when you have enough money, a good job, and extra time—it is natural to want to relax and enjoy life. However, this is exactly when the devil launches his most deceptive attacks. Be aware that prosperity can lull you into spiritual carelessness.

### Step 2: Study the Example of Adam and Eve in Genesis 2

Read Genesis 2 and examine how Adam and Eve lived in the perfect Garden of Eden. They had everything they needed: a beautiful home, perfect relationship with God, and only one restriction. Notice how Satan tempted Eve by contradicting God's word and offering something forbidden. Adam failed to lead and protect, and both ate the fruit.

### Step 3: Understand the Devil's Unchanging Tactics

The devil uses the same tactics today that he used with Adam and Eve. He lies about God's word, denies consequences, and makes sin seem desirable and harmless. He tells you "you won't surely die" and "a little bit won't hurt." Recognize these lies for what they are.

### Step 4: Maintain Spiritual Sensitivity and Guard Your Heart

Do not allow prosperity to make you less sensitive to sin. Stay aware of what made you successful in the first place—your obedience to God, your prayer life, and your sensitivity to the Holy Spirit. Continue to read the Word of God every day and talk to the heavenly Father daily.

### Step 5: Choose Godly Influences and Obey the Holy Spirit

Be careful about who your friends are, what you listen to, what you look at, and what you desire. When the Holy Spirit warns you about something in your life, obey immediately without arguing or discussing. Say "no" to the devil and "yes" to the Living God.

## Examples

### Example 1: Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden

Adam and Eve had everything perfect in the Garden of Eden, yet they disobeyed God's one command when tempted by the devil. Their story serves as a timeless warning that even in perfect circumstances, human beings can fall into sin when they drop their guard.

### Example 2: Modern Believers Tempted by Prosperity

Today, many Christians who are blessed with financial success, good health, and comfortable lifestyles become careless in their spiritual walk. They may stop praying fervently, neglect reading the Bible, or compromise their values. The devil offers them small pleasures that eventually lead to great destruction.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Do stay vigilant in your spiritual life regardless of circumstances
- ✅ Do read the Word of God every day and talk to the Father daily
- ✅ Do be careful about your relationships and influences
- ✅ Do obey the promptings of the Holy Spirit immediately
- ❌ Don't assume that prosperity means you can relax your faith
- ❌ Don't underestimate the devil's schemes during good times
- ❌ Don't believe Satan's lies that "a little bit of sin won't hurt"
- ❌ Don't argue with God's warnings or have discussions with the Holy Spirit about sin

## Keep In Mind

- The wages of sin is death—this is God's unchanging law that applies to all people
- The Holy Spirit within believers warns them when they are in danger of sinning
- Salvation through Jesus Christ is the only remedy for sin and its consequences

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Dropping your guard when life is going well and becoming complacent in your spiritual walk
  **Solution:** Stay sensitive to the Holy Spirit's warnings and maintain daily spiritual disciplines like prayer and Bible reading
- **Problem:** Believing Satan's lie that a little sin won't hurt or that you won't face consequences
  **Solution:** Remember that a little bit of sin multiplies into greater disobedience and that the wages of sin is death—God's law has not changed
- **Problem:** Choosing friends and influences that lead you away from God during times of prosperity
  **Solution:** Be careful about who you listen to, who you look at, and what you desire; choose godly influences that draw you closer to Christ
