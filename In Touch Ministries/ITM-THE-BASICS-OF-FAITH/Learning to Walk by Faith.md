# Learning to Walk by Faith: The Basics

## Overview

This sermon teaches the foundational principles of living by faith as described in Second Corinthians chapter five. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that faith is a confident conviction that God is who He says He is and will do exactly what He promises, and that walking by faith means making every decision based on trust in God rather than sight, feelings, or human reasoning. The message provides practical steps for building and strengthening faith through daily surrender, prayer, and engagement with scripture.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you feel uncertain about how to trust God in daily decisions
- When you are struggling with doubt, fear, or anxiety about the future
- When you want to understand what biblical faith actually requires
- When you are new to the Christian faith and need a starting point for spiritual growth
- When you have faltered in your walk with God and need encouragement to continue

## Steps

### Step 1: Begin Each Day by Surrendering to God

When you wake up, consciously affirm your relationship with God and acknowledge that He is in control of the day ahead. Recognize that you will face decisions, people, and tasks, and that your Father desires to walk with you through every one of them. Do not compartmentalize your faith for only certain areas of life. Instead, enter the day with the mindset that Jesus Christ will dominate your thinking, decisions, and actions from the moment you begin.

### Step 2: Trust God for Every Single Need and Decision

Throughout the day, rely on God for everything rather than turning first to your own understanding. Before acting, ask for His guidance and direction. Trust Him to meet every need you face, whether practical, relational, or spiritual. When you face criticism, praise, difficult conversations, or uncertainty about what to do, pause and ask, "Father, what would You have me to do?" Replace worry about the future with trust in His sovereignty, knowing that your eternal security is already settled through Christ.

### Step 3: Build Your Faith Through God's Word and Answered Prayers

Strengthen your faith by immersing yourself in scripture to understand the nature and promises of God. As you read, absorb who God is and what He has promised, because knowledge of His character is the foundation of trust. Also, make specific requests to God and watch how He answers. When He answers, thank Him. When He does not answer in the way you expected, trust that He has something better in mind, because His love for you means He will not give you what is not good for you. Each answered prayer and each trusted trial builds another layer of strength on your foundation of faith.

### Step 4: Respond to Failure by Getting Back Up

When you falter, fall, or give in to doubt, do not dwell in self-pity or discouragement. Discouragement will destroy your faith if you allow it to take root. Instead, acknowledge your mistake, thank God for His forgiveness and cleansing, and immediately return to walking by faith. The key to growth is not avoiding failure entirely, but what you do after you fall. Get up, refocus your attention on Christ, and keep moving forward.

## Examples

### Example 1: Trusting God in a Financial Decision

You are considering a major financial choice and feel pressured to rely on your own calculations and reasoning. Instead of leaning solely on your understanding, bring the decision before God in prayer, ask for His direction according to Proverbs 3:5-6, and trust Him to supply your needs according to Philippians 4:19. Act on what He leads you to do, even if the path seems unclear or risky by worldly standards.

### Example 2: Responding to a Relationship Conflict

You receive harsh criticism or face a painful conflict in a close relationship. Your natural response may be to retaliate, defend yourself, or withdraw in hurt. Walking by faith means pausing to ask God how to respond, trusting Him to guide your words and actions, and refusing to let fear or pride control the situation. Thank God for the opportunity to represent Christ in the conflict and trust Him to work all things together for good.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Begin each morning by consciously affirming your dependence on God and inviting Him into the day ahead
- ✅ Seek God's direction before making decisions, rather than reasoning first and inviting God along afterward
- ✅ Stay in the Word regularly so your understanding of God's nature and promises grows stronger
- ✅ Trust God for every area of life, including finances, relationships, health, and the future
- ✅ When you fail, get up quickly, confess your sin, thank God for forgiveness, and keep moving forward
- ✅ View trials and hardships as opportunities for your faith to grow stronger through experience

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal study of the Bible, consistent prayer, or accountability within a local church community.
- Stop and ask for clarification from a pastor or mature believer if a specific instruction or promise is unclear in your current circumstances.
- Faith grows through experience over time, so be patient with yourself as you learn to trust God more deeply.
- Trusting God does not mean ignoring wisdom, counsel, or responsible planning. Seek godly counsel and use the discernment God has given you.
- Do not use faith as an excuse for recklessness or neglect of practical responsibilities. Biblical faith and practical action work together.
- If you are struggling with severe anxiety, depression, or distress, reach out to a pastor, counselor, or trusted Christian leader who can walk alongside you.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trusting God only for certain areas of life while keeping others under your own control
  **Solution:** Surrender every area to Christ and practice relying on Him for all decisions, relationships, and needs.
- **Problem:** Becoming discouraged and giving up after falling into sin or doubt
  **Solution:** Remember that God understands your frailty. Confess your sin, receive His forgiveness, and immediately return to walking by faith.
- **Problem:** Trying to build faith through reading alone without experiencing God's faithfulness in real situations
  **Solution:** Step out in faith by making specific requests to God and trusting Him through difficult circumstances. Experience is what solidifies belief.
- **Problem:** Walking by sight, feelings, or reasoning instead of submitting every decision to God's will
  **Solution:** When any situation arises, make it your first response to ask, "Father, what would You have me to do?" rather than acting on your own impulses.
