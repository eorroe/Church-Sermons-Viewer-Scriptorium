# Patience: Essential to Experiencing God's Best

## Overview

This sermon teaches that patience is a gift from God, not a human effort, and is essential for experiencing His best. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that patience is the will to wait on God's timing, even when circumstances pressure us to act. Through the Holy Spirit, believers are empowered to trust God's protection, guidance, and perfect timing, leading to obedience and the fulfillment of God's purpose.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When facing a difficult decision and unsure whether to wait or act
- When struggling with impatience in relationships, work, or family
- When you have prayed about something and heard no clear answer
- When you need reassurance that God's delays are acts of love and protection

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize that patience is a gift from God

Understand that patience is not something you can work up or manufacture on your own. It is a fruit of the Holy Spirit released in your life when you surrender to Him. Acknowledge that the same Spirit who sealed you as a child of God also empowers you to wait.

### Step 2: Define patience as the will to wait

Adopt the simple definition that patience is the will to wait. When situations arise that require a decision, and you do not know what to do, choose to wait for God's direction rather than forcing your own way.

### Step 3: Trust that God's "no" is an act of protection

When God says no to something you desire, do not interpret it as rejection. Recognize it as God protecting you from harm and withholding what is not of Him. Remain submitted to His will even when it is difficult.

### Step 4: Exercise faith and an obedient spirit

Choose to obey God even when you do not fully understand His timing. Submit your personal desires and opinions to His wisdom, trusting that He knows what is best for you.

### Step 5: Discern God's timing

Ask the Holy Spirit to give you clarity about when to act. Understand that what you are asking for may not be God's will at the moment, but His plan for later. Ask for the grace to wait until He gives you permission.

### Step 6: Love God above all else

Realize that true patience flows from loving God more than yourself, your desires, or other people. If you love God, you will be patient because you trust His heart and His plan for your life.

### Step 7: Wait and watch God at work

Rest in God's perfect timing, and do not try to force His hand. Trust that when you wait on Him, He will bring situations to the right conclusion. Thank Him for the outcome, knowing that waiting was the right choice.

## Examples

### Example 1: Waiting for direction in a major life decision

You are considering a job change, a move, or a relationship commitment. You have prayed and asked God for guidance, but you have not heard a clear answer. Instead of forcing a decision, you choose to wait, trusting that God will direct you at the right moment. When you wait on God, circumstances align or a clear conviction settles in your heart, confirming that waiting was the right path.

### Example 2: Responding to God's "no"

You prayed for something you deeply desired—perhaps a specific relationship, a promotion, or a possession. God did not grant it, and you felt disappointed. Over time, you realized that what you wanted would have led you away from God's best. You later see how God protected you, and you grow in gratitude for His no.

### Example 3: Patience in marriage or friendships

You are frustrated with a spouse or close friend who is not meeting your expectations. Instead of reacting with anger or demanding change, you choose to be patient, remembering that everyone makes mistakes. You extend grace, pray for the other person, and allow God to work in the relationship. As you continue to wait, the relationship deepens because of your patience.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Wait on the Lord and trust that His timing is perfect
- ✅ Pray specifically and then relax, allowing the Holy Spirit to give clarity
- ✅ View delays as opportunities to grow in dependence on God
- ✅ Love God more than your own desires and timeline
- ✅ Surround yourself with godly counsel when making decisions
- ❌ Do not act simply because God has not explicitly told you not to
- ❌ Do not manipulate circumstances to justify your own desires
- ❌ Do not compare your timing to others and become impatient
- ❌ Do not push God to act on your timetable

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal Bible study, prayerful discernment, or wise counsel from mature believers.
- Patience is a process; you may need to practice it daily in small situations before you can trust it in larger ones.
- If you have never trusted Jesus Christ as your Savior, patience in the Christian life begins with surrendering your life to Him first.
- Stop and ask God to show you whether you are waiting on Him or simply procrastinating.

## Security & Safety Notes

- If you are in an abusive or dangerous situation, seeking safety is not impatience. God calls you to protect yourself and others from harm.
- Do not use the concept of waiting as an excuse to remain passive in situations that require action, such as medical needs, legal obligations, or the care of dependents.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You pray about a decision and hear nothing, so you assume it must be okay to proceed.
  **Solution:** Wait for clear confirmation from God rather than assuming silence equals approval.

- **Problem:** You compare your life to others who seem to have what you want, and you become bitter and impatient.
  **Solution:** Remember that God's plan and timing for your life are unique. Trust that He has your best in mind.

- **Problem:** You push ahead with your own plans because you feel you have waited long enough.
  **Solution:** Recognize that God's timing is not measured by your finite clock. If He is not late, then waiting is the right place to be.

- **Problem:** You interpret God's "no" as rejection or punishment.
  **Solution:** Understand that God's "no" is always rooted in love and protection. He withholds what would harm you or lead you away from His best.
