# Looking Deeper Into The Will of God

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God has a specific, personal plan for every individual's life, and that trusting and obeying God are the two essential requirements to fulfill His plan. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that God's sovereignty rules over all things, and that His will can be understood through three major categories: predestined will (things God has already determined that are irresistible and unchangeable), moral will (how we should live, based on the Ten Commandments and Scripture), and desired will (what God wants for us including salvation, baptism, prayer, and obedience). For personal application, God's will is divided into intentional will (what God had in mind for you before you were born), circumstantial will (what God does when you mess up, offering forgiveness and a new start), and immediate will (what God wants you to do right now today). The message encourages listeners to surrender to God, practice saying "if the Lord wills" before making plans, and reject the lie that it is too late to surrender.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need clarity on whether God has a specific plan for your life
- When facing decisions about time, money, relationships, or major life purchases
- When struggling with past mistakes or feeling it is too late to follow God
- When seeking to understand how to discern and walk in God's will daily
- When parents want to teach children about God's will early in life

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand That God Is Sovereign Over All Things

Recognize that God's sovereignty rules over all things (Psalm 103:19), meaning He is in absolute control. God has a specific, personal plan for every individual's life. Trusting and obeying God are the two essential requirements to fulfill His plan. God wants what is best for you, and He is personally interested in every detail of your life.

### Step 2: Learn the Three Major Categories of God's Will

Understand that there are three major categories of God's will. The first is predestined will: things God has already determined that are irresistible and unchangeable. The second is moral will: how we should live, based on the Ten Commandments and Scripture. The third is desired will: what God wants for us including salvation, baptism, prayer, and obedience.

### Step 3: Discover the Three Parts of God's Will for Your Personal Life

For personal application, God's will can be divided into three parts. First, intentional will: what God had in mind for you before you were born, including your unique purpose and design. Second, circumstantial will: when you mess up, sin, or rebel, God forgives and offers a new start, just like the prodigal son. Third, immediate will: what God wants you to do right now today in the daily decisions of your life.

### Step 4: Ask God Before Making Decisions

Make a practice of asking God before making decisions about your time, money, relationships, and purchases. Read and believe the Word of God to know His will. Practice saying "if the Lord wills" before making future plans (James 4:13-17). This habit keeps you dependent on God's guidance rather than your own understanding.

### Step 5: Surrender Your Life to God Today

Confess any sins and commit to obedience. Surrender your life to God today, rejecting the devil's lie that it is "too late" to surrender. Many people live their whole lives without ever asking what God's plan is for them, but God's grace is available now. Parents should teach children about God's will early rather than focusing only on material things.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Prodigal Son

The prodigal son illustrates God's circumstantial will. When the son messed up, rebelled, and wasted his inheritance, the father forgave him, ran to meet him, and offered a new start. This demonstrates that when you mess up, God's will includes forgiveness and restoration through His grace.

### Example 2: Those Who Resisted God's Call Early

The sermon describes people who were called by God at age 17 but refused to obey and rebelled against Him. By the time they were in their 40s and 50s, they finally yielded to God and went to Bible institute. God did not reject them or abandon them. Instead, He forgave them, put the pieces of their lives back together through His grace, and used them to accomplish His purpose.

### Example 3: Those Who Never Asked About God's Plan

People live their whole lives without ever asking what God's plan is for them. They pursue their own desires, make their own plans, and never seek God's will. This example warns of the danger of living a self-directed life without ever discovering the specific purpose God intended for you.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Trust that God has a specific, personal plan for your life
- ✅ Practice saying "if the Lord wills" before making future plans
- ✅ Ask God before making decisions about time, money, relationships, and purchases
- ✅ Read and believe the Word of God to know His will
- ✅ Surrender your life to God today, no matter your age or past mistakes
- ✅ Parents should teach children about God's will from a young age rather than focusing only on material things
- ❌ Don't live your whole life without ever asking what God's plan is for you
- ❌ Don't believe the devil's lie that it is "too late" to surrender
- ❌ Don't ignore God's moral will while claiming to follow His personal will

## Keep In Mind

- God's sovereignty means He is in absolute control and has a plan for every individual
- Salvation through Jesus Christ is the first step in discovering God's desired will
- Always seek confirmation through Scripture and the Holy Spirit when discerning God's will
- Consult with mature spiritual leaders and mentors when making major life decisions

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Living your whole life without ever asking what God's plan is for you
  **Solution:** Start today by reading the Word of God and asking Him to reveal His will for your life. Surrender your plans to Him.

- **Problem:** Believing the devil's lie that it is too late to surrender to God
  **Solution:** Remember that God's grace is available now, regardless of your age or past mistakes. He forgives and offers a new start to all who come to Him.

- **Problem:** Resisting God's will and expecting to avoid consequences
  **Solution:** Understand that disobedience and rebellion come at a price. Trust and obey are the two essential requirements to fulfill God's plan, and obedience leads to fulfillment and purpose.
