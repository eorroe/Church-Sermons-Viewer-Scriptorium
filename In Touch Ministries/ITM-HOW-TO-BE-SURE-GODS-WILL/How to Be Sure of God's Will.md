# How to Be Sure of God's Will

## Overview

This sermon teaches believers how to confidently discern God's will for their lives by understanding the different categories of God's will and applying a series of practical tests to every decision. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that God has a personal plan, purpose, and desire for each person's life, and provides specific questions to ask when seeking confirmation of God's will. The message also addresses what to do when you have missed portions of God's will and emphasizes that trusting God for daily decisions begins with surrendering your life to Jesus Christ.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need to make a significant life decision and want to know God's will
- When you are facing uncertainty about a career, relationship, or moral choice
- When you want to understand the different dimensions of God's will
- When you need practical steps to confirm whether a decision aligns with God's plan
- When you are struggling with past decisions and their consequences
- When you want to grow in your ability to hear and follow God's direction

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand That God Has a Personal Will for Your Life

God has a plan, a purpose, and a desire for your life from the moment you are born. Once you trust Jesus as your Savior, your name is written in the Lamb's Book of Life, and God takes a personal interest in you. He is not only concerned with worldwide matters but also with the intimate details of your life. Ask yourself: "Lord, where am I in Your plan? Where am I in Your purpose? Where am I in Your will for my life?" You can be sure of God's will, and it is for your good and the advancement of His kingdom.

### Step 2: Learn the Categories of God's Will

There are four categories of God's will that apply to every believer:

- **Predestined Will of God**: God's sovereign control over all things. This is what He will accomplish regardless of human opposition. Nobody can stop it.
- **Moral Will of God**: How God intends for us to live. The Ten Commandments are an example. This applies to everyone.
- **Desired Will of God**: What God desires for us in the Christian life from the very beginning of our relationship with Him.
- **Circumstantial Will of God**: God's will for our life when we have strayed or made wrong choices. This is how to respond to the consequences of our decisions.

Every aspect of your life is scrutinized by the will of God because He has already chosen the best route for you, considering all your advantages, disadvantages, opportunities, and upbringing.

### Step 3: Apply the Test Questions to Confirm God's Will

Before making any decision, ask yourself these questions:

1. **Is this decision consistent with or in agreement with the Word of God?**
   Compare your decision against Scripture. If it contradicts the Bible, it is not God's will. Turn to the Word of God and search the index or relevant passages to find what God says about your situation.

2. **Is this a wise decision? What are the consequences?**
   Ask: "If I do this, what will the consequences be?" Not what you would like them to be, but what they actually will be. Every decision has unavoidable consequences. Teach children early that there are always consequences to decisions.

3. **Can I honestly ask God to enable me to achieve whatever I am going through or this decision?**
   Consider whether you can sincerely pray for God's help with this path. If you are making a decision without involving God, you are acting in your own wisdom rather than His.

4. **Do I have genuine peace about this decision?**
   When you are walking in God's will, He gives you a sense of peace—a quietness in your spirit, no irritation, no crowding doubts. You will not need to ask others what they think. You will say, "Yes, Lord, thank You, Jesus." If you do not have this peace, stop and ask God what He wants you to do.

5. **Is this the right timing?**
   It may be God's will to do something, but timing matters. People make costly mistakes by stepping ahead of God or being too late. Wait for God's timing.

6. **Is this consistent with the way God works?**
   Consider whether your action aligns with how God has worked in Scripture and in the lives of other believers. Can you do this and know you have His approval?

7. **Does this fit who I am as a follower of Jesus Christ?**
   Ask whether your action, conversation, conduct, dress, and responses fit your claim to be a follower of Jesus. Your life should be consistent with who you say you are. If you are walking in God's will, things will fit. If not, they will feel wrong.

8. **Will this decision honor God?**
   Can you make this decision and pray at night without any "ruffling, scuffling" in your heart? If a decision keeps you awake at night, ask God why. He is trying to protect you.

9. **Will this decision result in unrest and regret for the rest of my life?**
   Some decisions are made once but have lifetime consequences. Avoid "if I'd-a" thinking by seeking God's will now.

10. **Can I expect God to reward me for this decision?**
    Think seriously: Can God bless you for this choice? Is it something that aligns with His heart and His Word?

### Step 4: Trust God for Daily Decisions as You Trusted Him for Eternity

You already trusted God with your eternity when you were born again. You said, "God, I'm trusting You that when I die, I'm going to be in heaven with You." If you can trust Him for an eternal decision, you can trust Him for daily decisions that are not nearly so important. God has your very best in mind. He works to get you to do the right thing. If you ignore Him, there are consequences.

### Step 5: Surrender Your Life to Jesus Christ if You Have Not Already

If you have never trusted Jesus as your Savior, you cannot expect God to give you wisdom or direction. You must first surrender your life to Him by confessing your sins, repenting, turning away from sin, and accepting Jesus as your Lord and Savior. Then you become a child of God, and He will guide your decisions. Without Jesus, you are on your own, and any success outside of God's will will ultimately require an account before Him.

### Step 6: Pray and Surrender Whatever Needs to Be Surrendered

Say to the Lord: "I want Your will, whatever that requires. I want Your best plan for my life, and I'm willing to surrender whatever needs to be surrendered, willing to change whatever needs to be changed." This is the wisest decision you can make. God will forgive you, direct you, and help you live in His will from that point forward.

## Examples

### Example 1: Choosing a Career Path

You are deciding between two job offers. Apply the test questions:
- Is either job inconsistent with God's Word?
- What are the consequences of each choice for your family and walk with God?
- Can you ask God to enable you to succeed in either role?
- Do you have genuine peace about one or the other?
- Is the timing right for a change?
- Does the role fit who you are as a follower of Jesus?

### Example 2: Responding to a Moral Temptation

Someone at work offers you something that conflicts with your values.
- You should not have to ask God whether to participate because the atmosphere does not fit who you claim to be as a Christian.
- Your response should be immediate and consistent with your identity in Christ.

### Example 3: A Major Life Decision Made Without God

You made a decision years ago that has caused regret. God is not disappointed because He knew you would make that mistake. He is willing to step into your life at any moment to forgive you and give you direction if you are willing to welcome Him and ask Him to straighten things out.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Start every decision by asking God for His will through prayer and Scripture.
- ✅ Ask the test questions before making choices.
- ✅ Teach children early to ask God about decisions so they learn dependence on Him.
- ✅ Wait for God's peace and right timing before acting.
- ✅ Be consistent between who you say you are as a Christian and how you act.
- ✅ Accept that consequences are unavoidable and weigh them honestly.
- ✅ Surrender your life to Jesus if you have not already done so.
- ✅ Trust God for daily decisions as you trusted Him for your eternal salvation.

## Keep In Mind

- This sermon does not replace personal Bible study, prayer, and wise counsel from mature believers.
- Stop and ask for clarification from God if peace, Scripture, or timing is unclear.
- Every decision affects someone else because no one lives in isolation.
- God's will is not a secret; He reveals it to those who genuinely want to know and obey.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Avoid making major decisions while living in unconfessed sin or distant from God.
- Be cautious of claiming peace when Scripture explicitly contradicts a choice.
- Do not ignore persistent conviction from God about a decision; He is trying to protect you.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Making decisions without asking God.
  **Solution:** Develop the habit of bringing every decision to God in prayer and comparing it to Scripture.
- **Problem:** Ignoring consequences.
  **Solution:** Honestly evaluate what will actually happen as a result of your choice, not just what you hope will happen.
- **Problem:** Moving ahead of God's timing.
  **Solution:** Wait for God's peace and confirmation; rushing leads to costly mistakes.
- **Problem:** Living with regret from past "if I'd-a" decisions.
  **Solution:** Confess and surrender past mistakes to God; He can redirect your life at any point.
- **Problem:** Thinking God is only interested in big, worldwide matters.
  **Solution:** Remember that God has a personal, intimate plan for your individual life.
