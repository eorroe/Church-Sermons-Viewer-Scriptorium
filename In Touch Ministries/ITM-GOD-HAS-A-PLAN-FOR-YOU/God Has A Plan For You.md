# God Has A Plan For You

## Overview

This sermon teaches that God is deeply interested in every facet of a believer's life and has a personal, specific plan for each individual. Dr. Charles Stanley explains that God's will includes His moral will for all people, as well as His personal will which encompasses intentional, circumstantial, and immediate aspects. The message encourages listeners to surrender to God's plan, trust Him through hardship, and understand that resisting His will brings consequences, while obedience leads to fulfillment and purpose.

## When to Follow This Sermon

- When you need clarity on whether God has a plan for your life
- When facing difficult decisions about school, marriage, career, or major life choices
- When struggling with past mistakes or feeling you've waited too long to follow God
- When seeking to understand how to discern and walk in God's will daily

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand That God Has a Personal Plan for Your Life

Recognize that God was interested in your life before you were born. He planned your birth, your spiritual gifts, your abilities, your talents, and your personality. God did not birth you and dump you into humanity without a purpose. He allowed you to be born with a plan and a purpose for your life.

### Step 2: Learn the Two Categories of God's Will

Understand that there are two general categories of God's will. The first is God's moral will, which applies to every single person and includes the clear do's and don'ts of Scripture such as not lying, stealing, cheating, or committing adultery. The second is God's personal will, which is specific to each individual and relates to His design, purpose, and plan for your life.

### Step 3: Discover the Three Aspects of God's Personal Will

Under God's personal will, there are three categories. First, His intentional will: what God had in mind for you when you were born, including your vocation, your spouse, and your ultimate purpose to be conformed to the likeness of Christ. Second, His circumstantial will: what God does when you make mistakes, sin, or rebel against Him—He forgives you, picks up the pieces, and works in your life in the light of your current circumstances through His grace. Third, His immediate will: the daily decisions you make, trusting in the Lord with all your heart and acknowledging Him in all your ways so He can direct your path.

### Step 4: Surrender Your Will to God

Confess any areas where you have resisted or rebelled against God's will. Repent of being prideful and arrogant, thinking you know better than He does. Ask God to forgive you and yield your life to Him. Say, "God, from this moment on I yield to You. I want Your will. I'm walking in it no matter what." He will enable you to step right into the center of His will.

### Step 5: Trust God Through Hardship and Pressure

Understand that God may allow pressure in your life to bring you back into His will, just as He sent a whale to bring Jonah to repentance. This pressure is not to harm you but to show you the consequences of disobedience and to cause you to yield to His perfect plan. Endure hardship with confidence, knowing that when you have done the will of God, you will receive what was promised.

### Step 6: Walk in God's Will Daily

Make every decision with the awareness that God is interested in every single facet of your life. Trust Him for the small daily choices as well as the major life decisions. Life at its very best is discovering His will and walking in it. You cannot improve on living in the will of God.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Nation of Israel

God planned to raise up a nation of people through Abraham through whom He would send the Messiah and bless the entire world. He planned the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and His death at the cross before He ever created the world. This demonstrates that God is a planner, not a reactor, and that He has an intentional will for His creation.

### Example 2: Jonah's Resistance

Jonah resisted God's will by fleeing to Tarshish instead of going to Nineveh. God sent pressure in the form of a great storm and a whale to bring Jonah to repentance. Jonah spent three days in the belly of the whale before crying out to God and yielding to His will. This shows that while you can resist God's will, you cannot do so without consequences, and God will use pressure to bring you back.

### Example 3: The Prodigal Return

A pastor in the transcript described men who were called by God at age 17 but refused to obey and rebelled against Him. By the time they were in their 40s and 50s, they finally yielded to God and went to Bible institute. God did not reject them or put them on the shelf. Instead, He forgave them, put the pieces of their lives back together through His grace, and used them to accomplish His purpose. This demonstrates God's circumstantial will at work.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Trust that God has a plan for your life regardless of your age or past mistakes
- ✅ Recognize that God's moral will is clear in Scripture and applies to all believers
- ✅ Surrender your will to God and ask for forgiveness when you resist Him
- ✅ Understand that God's circumstantial will works even when you've made mistakes
- ✅ Make daily decisions trusting in the Lord and acknowledging Him in all your ways
- ❌ Don't assume you're too old or your past disqualifies you from God's plan
- ❌ Don't resist God's will without expecting consequences
- ❌ Don't look at others living outside God's will and assume they are truly happy or at peace

## Keep In Mind

- This teaching is for spiritual guidance and should be applied within the context of a personal relationship with Jesus Christ
- Always seek confirmation through Scripture and the Holy Spirit when discerning God's will
- Consult with mature spiritual leaders and mentors when making major life decisions
- Do not use this teaching to justify reckless decisions or to ignore clear biblical moral standards

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Assuming God doesn't have a plan for your life because of your age or past mistakes
  **Solution:** Remember that God's grace knows no end, and He can use anyone who surrenders their life to Him regardless of their history.

- **Problem:** Resisting God's will and expecting to avoid consequences
  **Solution:** Understand that disobedience always carries consequences, but repentance and yielding to God bring restoration and enablement to walk in His will.

- **Problem:** Living outside of God's moral will while claiming to follow His personal will
  **Solution:** Recognize that God's moral will is non-negotiable and applies to every believer. You cannot selectively obey God and expect to experience His best for your life.
